How to choose a baby bouncer

  1. Age and weight limits: Most bouncers cover 0 to 6 months or up to 20 lbs. Convertible models last longer.
  2. Newborn support: Look for an infant insert, head support or bolster for tiny babies.
  3. Soothing features: Vibration, melodies and sounds can help calm a fussy baby. Check what batteries are needed.
  4. Harness: Every pick has a 3-point or 5-point harness. Use it every time.
  5. Cleaning: A machine-washable seat pad is a must for spit-up and diaper blowouts.
  6. Portability: A bouncer that folds flat is handy for visits to grandparents.

Bouncer safety tips

  • Awake time only: Never use a bouncer for sleep. Move a sleeping baby to a crib or bassinet.
  • Floor only: Keep the bouncer on the floor, away from stairs, tables and counters.
  • Always buckle: Use the harness every time, even for a minute.
  • Stay close: Keep baby within sight and reach.
  • Watch the limits: Stop using it once your baby hits the weight limit or tries to sit up or climb out.
  • Do not carry baby in it: Lift your baby out before moving the bouncer.

How a bouncer fits into your day

A bouncer is a helper, not a babysitter. Short sessions while you make breakfast or take a quick shower work well, but newborns also need plenty of floor time, tummy time and being held. Frequently asked questions

Can a newborn use a baby bouncer?

Yes, most bouncers are made for babies from birth. The Bright Starts, Ingenuity Soothing and Baby Delight picks list 0 to 6 months or up to 20 lbs, and the Ingenuity Keep Cozy includes an infant bolster for newborns.

Can babies sleep in a bouncer?

No. Bouncers are for supervised awake time only. If your baby falls asleep in a bouncer, move them to a firm, flat sleep surface such as a crib or bassinet.

How long can a baby stay in a bouncer?

Keep sessions short and supervised. Many parents use a bouncer for 15 to 30 minutes at a time while they shower, cook or eat, and babies also need floor time for tummy time and movement.

When should a baby stop using a bouncer?

Stop when your baby reaches the maker's weight limit or starts trying to sit up or roll out, whichever comes first. Most limits are around 20 lbs, while the Ingenuity Keep Cozy converts to a toddler seat up to 40 lbs.

Should a bouncer go on a table or counter?

No. Always place a bouncer on the floor, never on a raised surface like a table, counter or bed, and always buckle the harness.
