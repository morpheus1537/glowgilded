How to choose a newborn swaddle

  1. Size by weight: Swaddles are sized by weight and length; a snug fit at the shoulders matters.
  2. Arms up or down: Arms-down calms the startle reflex; arms-up lets babies self-soothe.
  3. Zipper: A two-way or bottom-opening zipper allows diaper changes without unswaddling.
  4. Hip room: Look for a roomy lower half so legs can bend up and out.
  5. TOG and fabric: Match the warmth to your nursery temperature.
  6. Transition plan: Some swaddles convert to arms-out for when baby starts rolling.

Safe swaddling tips

  • Back to sleep: Always place a swaddled baby on their back, on a firm, flat surface with only a fitted sheet.
  • Hips free: The swaddle should be snug at the chest but loose at the hips so legs can bend up and out.
  • Check the fit: You should be able to slip two or three fingers between the swaddle and baby's chest.
  • Avoid overheating: Dress baby lightly and check the back of the neck for sweat. Our winter baby essentials covers safe layering.
  • Stop at rolling: Transition out of the swaddle as soon as baby shows signs of rolling.
  • Keep an eye on things: A reliable monitor helps; see our best baby monitor without Wi-Fi.

When to transition out of a swaddle

Signs it is time include rolling attempts, breaking out every night or startle reflex fading. Move to arms-out swaddling (the HALO and SwaddleDesigns options can do this) and then to a wearable sleep sack.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to swaddle a newborn?

The American Academy of Pediatrics says swaddling can be used for young babies if done correctly. Always place a swaddled baby on their back on a firm, flat sleep surface, keep the swaddle loose enough at the hips and stop swaddling as soon as baby shows signs of trying to roll.

When should I stop swaddling my baby?

Stop when your baby shows signs of rolling over, often around 2 months, and some babies earlier. HALO says to discontinue its swaddle when baby begins rolling over or attempting to escape.

What TOG swaddle does a newborn need?

TOG measures warmth. Love To Dream lists its 1.0 TOG Swaddle Up for rooms between 68°F and 75°F, while HALO lists a 1.5 TOG for cooler rooms. Dress baby lightly underneath and check for sweating or hot skin.

How many swaddles do I need for a newborn?

Most parents find 3-4 swaddles enough, so you have spares for blowouts and laundry days.

Should a newborn sleep with arms up or arms down?

Both are common. Arms-down swaddles help calm the startle reflex, while arms-up designs like the Love To Dream Swaddle Up let babies reach their hands to self-soothe. Many babies show a clear preference within a few nights.
