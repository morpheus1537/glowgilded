How to choose a silicone bib

  1. Pocket depth: A deep, sturdy pocket that stays open catches more food.
  2. Neck fit: An adjustable neck with several settings lets the bib grow with your baby and fit snugly without being tight.
  3. Age range: Check the listed range. Some bibs start at 3 months; others suit older babies and toddlers.
  4. Cleaning: Dishwasher-safe bibs save time. All silicone bibs wipe clean.
  5. Materials: Look for food-grade silicone and BPA-free claims.
  6. Travel: Bibs that roll or fold are easier to pack in a diaper bag.

Mealtime tips for new eaters

  • Supervise every meal: Stay with your baby and keep them seated upright while eating.
  • Check the fit: The neck should be snug enough to keep food out but loose enough to slide a finger underneath.
  • Remove after meals: Take the bib off when eating is done; bibs are not for naps or play.
  • Inspect regularly: Replace bibs with tears, cracks or loose fasteners.
  • Rinse right away: Rinsing before food dries makes cleaning much easier.
  • Keep a spare: One in the diaper bag saves messy outings.

Silicone vs fabric bibs

Fabric bibs are soft and good for drool and bottles, but they soak up purees and need laundering after almost every meal. Silicone bibs do not absorb liquid, rinse clean in the sink and often go in the dishwasher, and their built-in pocket catches food that would otherwise land in your baby's lap. The trade-off is that they can feel stiffer, and some babies take a few meals to get used to them.

Hybrid bibs like the OXO Tot Roll-Up split the difference with a soft fabric body and a silicone pocket. Many families use fabric bibs in the early months and switch to silicone once solids start, then keep a roll-up bib in the diaper bag for meals out.

Frequently asked questions

Are silicone bibs good for starting solids?

Yes. Silicone bibs wipe clean in seconds, do not soak through, and their pocket catches dropped food, which makes them popular once babies start purees and finger foods. Always supervise your baby while eating.

What age can a baby use a silicone bib?

It depends on the bib. Moonkie lists 3 to 36 months, while PandaEar lists 10 to 72 months. Check the adjustable neck fits snugly but comfortably and is not too large for your baby.

Can silicone bibs go in the dishwasher?

Many can. PandaEar, Moonkie and Bella Tunno list their bibs as dishwasher safe. mushie recommends washing with soap and air drying, and the OXO Tot Roll-Up Bib is machine washable.

Are silicone bibs safe for babies?

The bibs here list food-grade silicone, and most state BPA-free. Use them only during supervised meals, remove the bib after eating, never let a baby sleep in a bib, and replace any bib that is torn or worn.

How many bibs do I need?

Many families keep two to four bibs so one can dry while another is in use. Sets like the PandaEar 3-pack or Moonkie 2-pack make rotation easy.
