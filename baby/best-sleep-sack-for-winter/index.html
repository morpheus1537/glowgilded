How to choose a winter sleep sack

  1. TOG rating: Higher TOG is warmer. Match it to the room temperature using the brand's chart.
  2. Fabric: Cotton is breathable, fleece is cozy, and merino wool helps regulate temperature.
  3. Arms: Sleeveless sacks help prevent overheating; swaddle-style wings suit newborns.
  4. Zipper: A two-way zipper makes night diaper changes easier.
  5. Size and fit: Correct sizing keeps the neckline in place and lets hips move freely.

Winter sleep tips for babies

  • Check the room temperature: A simple thermometer takes the guesswork out of TOG.
  • Layer underneath, not on top: Adjust pajamas under the sack instead of adding blankets.
  • Feel the neck or chest: Warm and dry is right; sweaty means too warm.
  • Keep the crib clear: No loose blankets, pillows or bumpers.
  • Watch from another room: A baby monitor without Wi-Fi lets you check in without opening the door.
  • Have a spare: Leaks happen at night; a second sack saves a 2 a.m. scramble.

Frequently asked questions

What TOG sleep sack should a baby wear in winter?

It depends on the room temperature, not the season. Yoofoss lists its 1.5 TOG sacks for rooms around 69 to 73°F, while HALO rates its TOG 3.0 Micro-Fleece for cooler nurseries around 61 to 64°F. Check your nursery with a thermometer and follow the brand's chart.

What should a baby wear under a sleep sack in winter?

Usually a long-sleeve bodysuit or footed pajamas, adjusted to the room temperature. Feel the back of your baby's neck or chest; it should be warm, not sweaty.

Are sleep sacks safer than blankets?

Wearable blankets are widely recommended in place of loose blankets in the crib. HALO describes its Easy Transition SleepSack as a safer alternative to loose bedding that supports safe sleep practices.

When should a baby stop being swaddled?

Many parents move to an arms-free sleep sack once a baby shows signs of rolling. The HALO Easy Transition SleepSack has lightly structured arms to help with that step; ask your pediatrician about timing.

How do I choose the right sleep sack size?

Go by your baby's weight and height, not just age. Yoofoss warns that the correct size keeps the sack from riding up around the face. Woolino's universal size adjusts from 2 months to 2 years with under-arm snaps.
