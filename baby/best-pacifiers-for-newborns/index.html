How to choose a pacifier for a newborn

  1. Age range: Newborn sizes are usually labeled 0-3 or 0-6 months and are smaller and lighter.
  2. Nipple shape: Round, symmetrical nipples work either way up. Orthodontic nipples are flatter and shaped for the palate.
  3. Material: Most pacifiers are silicone, which is durable and easy to sterilize. Natural rubber is softer and more flexible but wears faster.
  4. One-piece design: One-piece pacifiers have no joints for gunk to collect in.
  5. Shield: Look for a curved shield with room for the nose and air holes to help reduce skin irritation.
  6. Night features: Glow-in-the-dark designs make it easier to find a dropped pacifier at 3 a.m.

Pacifier safety and buying tips

  • Expect some trial and error: Many babies prefer one shape, so a couple of different packs can save frustration.
  • Inspect before every use: Throw away any pacifier with cracks, tears or a sticky feel.
  • Never tie it around the neck: Use a short, safety-tested pacifier clip instead of string or ribbon.
  • Clean often: Sterilize before first use, then wash regularly following each maker's directions.
  • Keep spares everywhere: Stash one in the diaper bag, car and nursery. A swaddle for newborns and a pacifier make a calming bedtime pair.
  • Ask your pediatrician: If you have questions about breastfeeding and pacifiers, your pediatrician or lactation consultant can help.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best pacifier for a newborn?

The Philips Avent Soothie is a popular first pacifier because it is made for 0-3 months, is a one-piece medical-grade silicone design and is easy to sterilize. Many babies are picky, though, so trying two or three shapes is normal.

When can you give a newborn a pacifier?

Many parents offer a pacifier from the early weeks, and Philips says you can breastfeed and offer the Soothie. If you are breastfeeding and have concerns, ask your pediatrician or a lactation consultant about timing.

Is a round or orthodontic pacifier better?

Neither is right for every baby. Round, symmetrical nipples like the Soothie, MAM and BIBS can't be put in upside down, while orthodontic nipples like NUK are shaped to fit the palate. Pick the one your baby accepts.

How often should you replace a pacifier?

Check pacifiers before every use and replace them at any sign of cracks, tears or stickiness. BIBS recommends replacing its natural rubber pacifiers every 4 to 6 weeks.

How do you clean a newborn pacifier?

Sterilize before first use and clean often. Philips says the Soothie can go in a sterilizer or be submerged in boiling water, and BIBS says to pour boiling water over its pacifiers, squeeze out the water and let them dry.
