Sleep tips for holiday travel with a baby

  • Keep the routine: Use the same sound, the same bedtime steps and, if possible, the same sleep sack.
  • Place it at a distance: Put the machine away from the crib and keep the volume low and comfortable.
  • Charge every night: Plug in portable machines overnight so they are ready for the next day's naps.
  • Darken the room: A travel blackout shade or a towel over a window helps a baby nap in a bright guest room.
  • Bring a spare cable: USB-C cables go missing on trips.

Buying tips

  • Portable vs plug-in: Rechargeable models are best for cars, strollers and hotels; plug-in models are better for longer stays.
  • Check the battery claim: Compare listed battery life with how long your baby naps and sleeps.
  • Look for a child lock: Curious toddlers love buttons.
  • Consider a night light: Helpful for night feeds in an unfamiliar room.
  • Check the current listing: Colors and bundles vary, and ratings can differ between versions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a portable sound machine for traveling with a baby?

If your baby sleeps with white noise at home, bringing a sound machine helps keep the routine familiar in a new room, a car or a hotel. A portable, rechargeable model is the easiest to use on the go.

How long does the Hatch Go battery last?

Hatch says the Hatch Go's rechargeable battery lasts up to 15 hours and keeps playing while charging at night. It charges with the included USB-C cable.

Where should I put a sound machine for a baby?

Place it away from the crib rather than inside it, and keep the volume low and comfortable. Many pediatric experts recommend keeping sound machines at a distance and at a moderate volume to protect hearing.

Is brown noise or white noise better for baby sleep?

There is no single best sound for every baby. White noise is a steady hiss, while brown noise is deeper and softer. Machines with several options, like the 30-sound plug-in model here, let you try what your baby settles to best.

Can I use a phone app instead of a sound machine?

You can, but a dedicated machine frees up your phone, avoids notifications interrupting sleep and keeps working if your phone battery runs low. A separate machine also makes it easier to keep the routine the same every night.
