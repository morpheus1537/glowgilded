Winter baby tips

  • Dress in layers. A good rule of thumb is one more layer than you are wearing.
  • Skip loose blankets. Use a wearable blanket like the HALO SleepSack for sleep.
  • Check the nursery temperature. Match sleepwear TOG to the room.
  • Moisturize after baths. Apply cream to slightly damp skin to lock in moisture.
  • Remove bulky coats in car seats. Follow your car seat maker's guidance on clothing.

Frequently asked questions

What does a baby need for winter?

A warm, wearable blanket for sleep, an outerwear layer like a fleece bunting for outings, a gentle moisturizer for dry skin, a cool mist humidifier for dry indoor air, and mittens that stay on. Hats and warm socks round out the list.

What TOG sleep sack should a baby wear in winter?

HALO says its TOG 3.0 microfleece SleepSack Swaddle is made for cooler nursery temperatures of 61-64°F and provides warmth without extra layers. Warmer rooms call for a lower TOG.

Can a baby wear a bunting in a car seat?

The Cuddle Club bunting is listed as car seat friendly. Always follow your car seat manufacturer's instructions on clothing and make sure the harness fits snugly. The bunting is not intended as sleepwear.

Is a humidifier good for a baby's room in winter?

Many parents use a cool mist humidifier in winter to add moisture to dry heated air. The Vicks SleepyTime 3-in-1 is a cool mist humidifier with a built-in night light and a filterless design. Follow the manufacturer's cleaning instructions.

How do you keep mittens on a baby?

Choose mittens with a long cuff and a secure wrist closure. N'Ice Caps sherpa mittens use wide elastic openings plus adjustable wrist straps, and thumbless styles for babies are easier to put on.
