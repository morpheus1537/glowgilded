How to choose a diaper bag backpack

  1. Capacity: About 25 liters suits a full day out with one baby. Twins or toddlers plus a baby may need more.
  2. Pockets: Insulated bottle pockets, a wipes pocket and a hidden valuables pocket make life easier.
  3. Opening: A wide opening lets you see everything at a glance.
  4. Changing pad: A wipe-clean pad is essential for public restrooms.
  5. Stroller straps: Hang the bag on the stroller when your back needs a break.
  6. Comfort: Padded straps and a padded back panel help on long days.
  7. Style: A neutral look makes it easy to share between parents.

Diaper bag packing tips

  • Pack a mini kit: Diapers, wipes, a changing pad, a spare outfit, burp cloths and a plastic bag for soiled clothes.
  • Restock nightly: Refill diapers and wipes before bed so you are always ready.
  • Use the insulated pockets: Keep bottles at the right temperature on outings.
  • Add a parent pouch: Keys, phone, wallet and hand sanitizer in one small pouch save digging.

Frequently asked questions

Is a backpack diaper bag better than a tote?

For most parents, yes. A backpack spreads weight across both shoulders and leaves your hands free for a baby, stroller or car seat. Totes can be easier to dig through but are harder to carry for long periods.

What size diaper bag backpack do I need?

Most popular diaper backpacks hold about 25 to 26 liters, like the RUVALINO (25L) and BabbleRoo (26L). That fits diapers, wipes, a change of clothes, bottles and a few parent items for a day out.

What should a good diaper bag include?

Look for a changing pad, insulated bottle pockets, a wide opening and stroller straps. All five bags here include stroller straps or attachments, and most include a changing pad.

Can you wash a diaper bag backpack?

Check the care label. Tenot says its bag is not recommended for machine washing, and most diaper bags are best spot cleaned. Removable changing pads are usually easier to wipe down.

Does a diaper bag backpack work for dads?

Yes. All five bags here come in neutral colors such as black or grey, and several brands specifically describe their designs as suitable for moms and dads.
