Buying tips

Separate display gifts from supervised play

Age guidance applies to each item, not to the basket as a whole. Rings listed for six months and older should not be described as a newborn activity. Keep the ornament, ribbon and gift wrapping out of reach and keep loose toys and books out of the sleep space. Ask parents about readiness, duplicate toys and cleaning preferences. Inspect items regularly and follow the current label. A toy can support shared play without guaranteeing milestones or making a baby develop on a fixed schedule.
