About Glow & Gilded

Glow & Gilded is a small beauty journal for people who love a good ritual: the cleanser that melts away the day, the cream that makes skin feel cared for, the quiet ten minutes that feel like a spa visit at home.

We focus on premium, well-reviewed skincare and beauty tools, and we try to explain why something earns a place in a routine instead of just telling you to buy it. We favor products with large numbers of real buyer reviews, and we re-check availability when we update a guide.

We are not dermatologists, and nothing here is medical advice. If you have a skin condition, are pregnant, or use prescription skincare, check with a qualified professional before adding new actives or devices.
