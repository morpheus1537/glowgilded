## Holiday party food warming tips

- **Hold, do not cook:** Warmers are best for food that is already fully cooked. Cook to a safe temperature first, then move dishes to the warmer.
- **Keep lids on:** Lids trap heat and moisture. Lift them only when serving.
- **Add liquid to saucy dishes:** Meatballs, pulled pork and mashed potatoes stay creamy with a splash of broth or milk.
- **Stagger refills:** Put out half a dish and keep the rest warm in the kitchen, then swap in a fresh tray.
- **Plan your outlets:** Electric warmers draw power, so spread them across different outlets where possible and keep cords out of walkways.
- **Watch the clock:** Food safety guidance generally recommends not leaving perishable food out for long periods. When in doubt, refrigerate leftovers promptly.

## Buying tips

- **Match the warmer to your menu:** Saucy dishes suit slow cookers, flat foods and casseroles suit warming mats, and big trays suit chafing dishes.
- **Count your dishes:** Each hot dish needs its own spot. Write out the menu, then pick a warmer or combination that covers it.
- **Think about storage:** Triple slow cookers and chafers are bulky. A roll-up mat fits in a drawer.
- **Check the current listing:** Colors and bundle options can change, so confirm you are buying the exact item you want.
