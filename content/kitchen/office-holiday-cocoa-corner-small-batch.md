## Buying tips

### Choose a drink workflow before buying a frother

Decide whether you want hot-water cocoa, cocoa made with separately warmed milk, or a drink finished with foam. Those are different workflows, and the two frothers do not perform the same heating job. Keep toppings separate for people managing sweetness or dietary needs. Check all current food labels for allergens and serving directions. Plan a cleanup point before you start, particularly with dairy, and keep hot liquids and electrical cords out of children’s reach.
