## How to choose a mini food processor

1. **Capacity:** 3 to 3.5 cups for everyday family cooking; 1.5 cups for small, quick jobs.
2. **Power:** Listed motors here range from 150 to 350 watts. More power helps with nuts and hard cheese.
3. **Controls:** Pulse-only models are simple; multiple speeds give finer control.
4. **Liquids:** Oil holes or a drizzle basin make dressings and hummus easier.
5. **Cleanup:** Look for dishwasher-safe bowl, lid and blade.

## Buying tips for mini choppers

- **Cut food into chunks first:** Even pieces about an inch across chop more evenly.
- **Pulse, do not hold:** Short pulses give a chop; holding turns things into paste.
- **Do not overfill:** Stay below the max line, especially with liquids.
- **Add liquids slowly:** Use the oil holes or drizzle basin for dressings and hummus.
- **Know the limits:** Mini choppers are not for kneading dough or slicing; a full-size processor or [hand mixer](/kitchen/best-hand-mixer-for-baking-cookies/) handles those jobs.
- **Pair with soup season:** Chopped onions, carrots and celery are the start of most recipes in our [winter soup making essentials](/kitchen/winter-soup-making-essentials/) guide.
