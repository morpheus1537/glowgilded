### Why we like it

The cocoa is the star of the bar, so it is worth choosing a mix people actually look forward to. Ghirardelli's Rich Chocolate envelopes come in a pack of 15, and you simply add hot water for a foolproof cup. ## How to set up a hot cocoa bar

1. **Pick a spot.** Use a corner of the counter or a sideboard near an outlet for a kettle or milk steamer.
2. **Set up the base.** Cocoa envelopes, hot water or hot milk, and mugs.
3. **Add toppings.** Mini marshmallows, crushed candy canes, chocolate chips, whipped cream and cinnamon in small jars.
4. **Add the tools.** A frother, spoons and stirrers, napkins, and a dish for used spoons.
5. **Label it.** A small sign or chalkboard tells guests how to build their cup.

## Buying tips

- **Plan quantities:** count one or two cups per guest for an evening party.
- **Offer one non-dairy option:** keep a carton of oat or almond milk on hand for guests who skip dairy.
- **Keep it warm:** a kettle or milk steamer near the bar keeps drinks hot all night.
- **Watch the kids:** keep hot water and steamers out of small hands and let an adult pour.
- **Check the current price on Amazon:** cocoa and holiday treats often go on sale and sell out in December.
