## How to choose your first cast iron skillet

1. **Size:** 10 to 12 inches covers most cooking. Bigger pans are heavier.
2. **Pre-seasoning:** Choose a pan seasoned at the factory so you can cook right away.
3. **Handles:** A helper handle or dual handles make a heavy pan easier to lift.
4. **Lid:** A lid lets you braise, simmer and steam.
5. **Weight:** Cast iron is heavy. If wrist strength is a concern, start smaller.
6. **Care:** Every pan here needs hand washing, drying and a light coat of oil.

## Cast iron care tips for beginners

- **Cook fatty foods first:** Bacon, burgers and shallow-frying help build seasoning.
- **Clean while warm:** Rinse, scrub with a brush or sponge, and use a little soap if needed.
- **Dry completely:** Water causes rust. Dry with a towel, then warm the pan on the stove for a minute.
- **Oil lightly:** Wipe a thin layer of oil over the surface while the pan is warm.
- **Skip the dishwasher and soaking:** Both strip seasoning and cause rust.
- **Use a handle holder or mitt:** Cast iron handles get hot in the oven and on the stove.
- **Go easy on long acidic simmers at first:** Tomato sauces can wear new seasoning.
