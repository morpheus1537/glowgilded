Carving the turkey is the moment everyone watches, and it is also where many Thanksgiving dinners go wrong. A dull chef's knife tears the breast meat, the slices come out uneven and the cook ends up sawing at the bird while the sides go cold. An electric carving knife fixes most of that. Two serrated blades move back and forth at high speed, so you guide the knife and let the motor do the cutting.

## How to carve a turkey with an electric knife

1. **Rest the bird.** Let the turkey rest loosely covered so the juices settle before carving.
2. **Set up a stable board.** Use a large board with a groove for juices, and put a damp towel underneath so it does not slide.
3. **Remove legs and wings.** Pull each leg away from the body and cut through the joint. Do the same with the wings.
4. **Take off each breast.** Cut down along the breastbone, then follow the ribcage to remove the whole breast in one piece.
5. **Slice across the grain.** Lay the breast flat and slice it crosswise with the electric knife, using light pressure and letting the blades move.
6. **Arrange and serve.** Fan slices on a warm platter with the dark meat alongside.

## Buying tips

- **Look for a safety lock:** It prevents the knife from starting by accident, especially while you are changing blades.
- **Think about storage:** A case or tray keeps the sharp blades together and protected between holidays.
- **Check cleanup:** Some blades are dishwasher safe; others should be hand washed. Always unplug first.
- **Cord length matters:** A 5-foot cord makes it easier to carve at the table or on an island.
- **Use it beyond turkey:** All four knives list bread too, and several mention foam for crafts.
