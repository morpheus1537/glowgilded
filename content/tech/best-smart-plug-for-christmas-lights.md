## How to choose a smart plug for Christmas lights

- **Indoor or outdoor:** Any plug that sits outside should have a weather rating such as IP44 or IP64. Indoor plugs are fine for trees and window displays.
- **Individual control:** Multi-outlet outdoor plugs either control each outlet separately (Kasa EP40, meross) or all together (BN-LINK). Separate control lets you set different schedules.
- **Sunset scheduling:** Dusk-based scheduling means you never have to adjust timers as the days get shorter.
- **Your ecosystem:** Check that the plug works with your assistant: Alexa, Google Assistant or Apple HomeKit.
- **Wi-Fi reach:** Outdoor plugs need a solid 2.4GHz signal at the outlet. Test your phone's Wi-Fi at the spot before you buy.
- **Load:** Add up the wattage of everything you'll plug in and stay within the plug's rating. LED lights use far less power than incandescent strings.

## Smart plug safety tips

- Keep outdoor connections off the ground and out of puddles, and use covers on unused outlets.
- Use only outdoor-rated extension cords outside.
- Don't daisy-chain multiple power strips into one smart plug.
- Unplug and inspect lights with frayed wires or cracked sockets.
