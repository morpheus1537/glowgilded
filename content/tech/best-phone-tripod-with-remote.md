## How to choose a phone tripod

1. **Height:** 60 inches or more lets the camera reach eye level for group shots.
2. **Phone fit:** Check the clamp width against your phone in its case.
3. **Remote range:** Listed ranges here run from 30 to 100 feet.
4. **Stability:** Aluminum legs and a wider stance resist tipping, especially outdoors.
5. **Portability:** Selfie-stick tripods fold small; full camera tripods are steadier but bulkier.
6. **Extras:** A cold shoe adds a light or mic; a 1/4-inch screw fits cameras.

## Tips for better holiday photos with a tripod

- **Set the camera at eye level:** It is more flattering than shooting up from a table.
- **Use the timer and the remote:** Burst mode plus a remote gives you a few shots to choose from.
- **Mind the light:** Face windows or warm lamps; our [video call lighting setup](/office/video-call-lighting-setup/) guide covers simple fixes.
- **Spread the legs fully:** Wider legs mean less wobble, especially on carpet.
- **Keep the phone charged:** Video drains batteries fast; a [portable charger for iPhone](/tech/best-portable-charger-for-iphone/) helps on long shoots.
- **Pair the remote first:** Connect it in Bluetooth settings before everyone gathers.
