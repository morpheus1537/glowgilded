## How to choose a keyboard for your iPad

1. **Compatibility:** Check your iPad model against the listing, especially for keyboard cases that fit only certain generations.
2. **Standalone or case:** Standalone keyboards work with many devices; cases add protection and a stand but fit one model.
3. **Size and weight:** Ultra-slim keyboards travel well; desk keyboards with a cradle hold the tablet for you.
4. **Power:** AAA batteries mean no charging; Smart Connector cases draw power from the iPad.
5. **Shortcut keys:** iPad hotkeys for volume, brightness and media save time.
6. **Multi-device switching:** Handy if you also type on a phone or computer.

## Tips for typing on an iPad

- **Learn the shortcuts:** Hold the Command key on a connected keyboard to see available shortcuts in many apps.
- **Use a stand:** If your keyboard has no cradle, a folio case or tablet stand keeps the screen at eye level.
- **Add a mouse:** iPadOS supports Bluetooth mice, which helps with editing documents.
- **Keep spare AAAs:** Battery-powered keyboards can die mid-trip; pack a spare pair.
- **Check your iPad model:** Find it under Settings, General, About before buying a keyboard case.
- **Mind your posture:** For long sessions, see our [best ergonomic keyboard for typing all day](/office/best-ergonomic-keyboard-for-typing-all-day/) guide.

## Separate keyboard or keyboard case?

A separate Bluetooth keyboard is flexible. You can use it with your phone, a laptop or your next iPad, and it costs less to replace if it is lost. A keyboard case is more convenient day to day because it is always attached and ready, adds a trackpad and protects your screen, but it only fits specific iPad models. If you switch iPads often, go standalone; if you use one iPad as a laptop replacement, a case like the Combo Touch makes sense.
