Stocking stuffers are tricky. Candy disappears in a day and novelty gifts end up in a drawer. Small tech gifts are the exception: a good charger, speaker or tracker gets used every single day, long after the wrapping paper is gone.

These five tech stocking stuffers all fit in a standard stocking, and each one is rated 4.4 stars or higher by thousands of shoppers. ## Best for music: JBL Go 4 Portable Bluetooth Speaker

### Why we like it

The JBL Go 4 is tiny but, according to JBL, packs its Pro Sound with punchier bass. It plays for up to 7 hours on a charge, and a Playtime Boost button adds up to 2 more hours. It is rated IP67 waterproof and dustproof, so it can handle the shower, the pool or the beach. You can pair two Go 4s for stereo sound, or connect several JBL Auracast-enabled speakers for a bigger sound. JBL also says it uses recycled plastic and fabric.

### Who it's for

Teens, college students, and anyone who plays music in the shower or on the go.

### Pros

- IP67 waterproof and dustproof
- Up to 7 hours plus 2 with Playtime Boost
- Pair two for stereo
- Over 20,000 ratings

### Cons

- A small speaker will not fill a big room
- Battery life is modest compared to larger speakers

## Best charger: Anker 511 Charger (Nano Pro) 20W USB-C

### Why we like it

Many people still charge their iPhone with an old 5W cube. Anker's Nano Pro delivers full-speed 20W charging and, according to the listing, charges an iPhone to 50% in about 25 minutes, up to 3 times faster than a 5W charger. It is 45% smaller than an original 20W charger, and Anker's ActiveShield safety system monitors temperature and adjusts power output. It comes with an 18-month warranty and has more than 25,000 ratings at 4.8 stars.

### Who it's for

iPhone users, and anyone who keeps a spare charger in a bag or at the office.

### Pros

- Fast 20W USB-C charging
- Very compact
- Temperature monitoring
- 4.8 stars, the highest in this guide

### Cons

- Cable not included
- One port only

## Best smart home starter: Kasa Smart Plug Ultra Mini

### Why we like it

A smart plug is the easiest smart home upgrade there is. Plug it in, set it up in the Kasa app over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, and you can switch a lamp, fan, humidifier or Christmas lights on and off with your voice through Alexa or Google Assistant, or from anywhere with your phone. You can also set schedules and countdown timers, which is perfect for tree lights. The listing says it is UL certified and comes with a 2-year warranty.

### Who it's for

Anyone curious about smart home tech, and anyone who wants Christmas lights on a timer.

### Pros

- Works with Alexa and Google Assistant
- Schedules and timers
- Compact plug design
- Over 40,000 ratings

### Cons

- Needs 2.4GHz Wi-Fi
- Controls on and off only, not dimming

## Best for forgetful people: Life360 Tile Pro

### Why we like it

Tile Pro is Tile's most powerful tracker, according to the listing. Attach it to keys, a bag or a golf bag, and ring it or track it in the free app when it goes missing. It works in reverse too: press the Tile to make your phone ring, even on silent. It also works with the Life360 family app to show your things and your people on one map, and it has a discreet SOS feature.

### Who it's for

Anyone who misplaces their keys, wallet or bag, on iPhone or Android.

### Pros

- Rings your phone, even when silenced
- Works on iOS and Android
- Life360 integration
- SOS feature

### Cons

- Some features work best inside the Life360 app

## Best for TV: Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Plus

### Why we like it

A streaming stick is a stocking stuffer that upgrades a whole TV. The Fire TV Stick 4K Plus streams in 4K Ultra HD with Dolby Vision, HDR10+ and Dolby Atmos support, and Wi-Fi 6 keeps streaming smooth when other devices are on your network. The Alexa Voice Remote controls the TV's power and volume and finds shows by voice, and Amazon says the stick works with apps like Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and HBO Max, plus free ad-supported apps. Subscriptions may apply. It is the most rated pick here, with more than 116,000 ratings.

### Who it's for

Anyone with an older or non-smart TV, and families who want a second TV for streaming.

### Pros

- 4K with Dolby Vision and HDR10+
- Wi-Fi 6
- Alexa Voice Remote with TV controls
- Huge number of ratings

### Cons

- Best on a 4K TV
- Some apps need paid subscriptions

## How to choose tech stocking stuffers

- **Solve a daily annoyance.** Slow charging, lost keys and an old TV are everyday problems; a gadget that fixes one gets used.
- **Check compatibility.** The Anker charger is built for iPhone fast charging, the Kasa plug needs 2.4GHz Wi-Fi and the Fire TV Stick needs an HDMI port.
- **Add the extras.** Throw in a USB-C cable with the Anker charger.
- **Mix with practical gifts.** Pair a smart plug with new string lights, or a tracker with a key ring.
