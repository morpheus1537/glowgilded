You plug it in once, and new photos of the grandkids keep arriving all year. The catch is choosing a frame that is simple for them and easy for the rest of the family to send photos to.

## Best for email sharing: Skylight Digital Picture Frame, 10-Inch

### Why we like it

Skylight's big advantage for grandparents is how family members send photos. Each frame gets its own Skylight email address, so anyone can email a photo to it, and the listing also mentions loading photos from a phone. Setup is described as plug-and-play: plug it in, connect to WiFi and choose the email address.

The 10-inch color touchscreen has a 1280 x 800 resolution and uses cloud-based storage, so there is no SD card to manage. Two gift-friendly features stand out: Gift Mode lets you preload and personalize the frame without unboxing it, and offline mode keeps showing existing photos when WiFi is down.

### Who it's for

Families where some relatives are not comfortable with apps, and grandparents with spotty home WiFi who still want photos on display.

### Pros

- Unique email address makes sharing easy for anyone.
- Gift Mode for preloading before the holidays.
- Offline mode keeps showing existing photos.

### Cons

- New photos still require WiFi.
- Some features may be tied to the brand's app or plans; check the listing.

## How to set up a digital frame for grandparents

1. **Preload before wrapping.** Use the brand's gift or preload feature so the frame shows familiar photos the moment it is plugged in.
2. **Invite the whole family.** Add siblings, cousins and grandkids to the app or share the frame's email address so photos keep arriving.
3. **Set a sleep schedule.** Turn the screen off overnight so it does not light up the room.
4. **Pick the right spot.** Near an outlet, at eye level from a favorite chair, and away from glare from windows.

## Buying tips

- **Think about who sends photos:** If relatives are not app users, Skylight or Nixplay's email option helps.
- **Check WiFi at their home:** All four frames need WiFi to receive new photos. If the connection is unreliable, prioritize offline display or local storage.
- **Look at fees before buying:** Read the current listing and app details for any optional subscription.
- **Keep it simple for the grandparent:** The best setup is one where they never need to touch the settings.
