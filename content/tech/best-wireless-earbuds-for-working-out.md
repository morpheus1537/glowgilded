## How to choose wireless earbuds for working out

1. **Fit:** Ear hooks give the most security for running and jumping. Small, light buds work well for lifting, yoga and walking.
2. **Sweat and water resistance:** Look for an IPX rating. IPX7 handles heavy sweat and rain.
3. **Controls:** Physical buttons are easier with sweaty fingers. Touch controls are lighter but can trigger by accident.
4. **Battery:** Check playtime per charge and total playtime with the case. Long runs and multiple weekly workouts need more.
5. **Awareness:** For outdoor runs, an ambient mode or a modest seal lets you hear traffic.
6. **Extras:** Apps with EQ presets, noise cancelling and battery displays on the case are nice bonuses.

## Workout earbud tips

- **Try every ear tip size:** A proper seal improves both fit and bass. The right size may differ for each ear.
- **Wipe after every session:** Sweat and skin oils build up on tips and charging contacts.
- **Dry before charging:** Let wet earbuds air dry before putting them back in the case.
- **Use awareness modes outside:** Keep noise cancelling for the gym and use an ambient mode or lower volume on roads.
- **Carry a backup charge:** A small [portable charger](/tech/best-portable-charger-for-iphone/) keeps your phone and earbuds going on long days.
- **Replace worn tips:** Silicone tips loosen over time, and fresh tips can make old earbuds fit like new.

## In-ear or ear hooks?

Small in-ear buds like the TOZO A1 and JLab Go Air Pop+ are light, discreet and easy to pocket, and they work well for strength training, cycling classes, walking and yoga, where your head does not bounce much. Ear-hook designs like the occiam, Aptkdoe and Boean add a loop over the ear that holds the bud even when the seal shifts, which makes them a safer choice for running, jumping, HIIT and sports. If you wear glasses or a hat, try ear hooks on first, since they can crowd the space around your ear.
