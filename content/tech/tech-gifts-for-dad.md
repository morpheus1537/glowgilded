Dads are famously hard to shop for. Ask what he wants and you will hear "nothing" or "socks". But most dads will happily use a well-made gadget every day, as long as it solves a small, real annoyance: the book that is too heavy on vacation, the phone that dies by mid-afternoon, the radio in the garage that sounds like a tin can.

We chose four tech gifts that fit that bill. Each one is easy to set up, well-reviewed by tens of thousands of buyers, and useful for years, not just on Christmas morning. We checked each product's star rating, rating count and stock status on its Amazon page on the date above. Prices change often during the holiday season, so check the current price on Amazon before you buy.

Shopping for the whole house? Our guide to [smart home gadgets worth buying](/tech/smart-home-gadgets-worth-buying/) has more ideas.

## Best for readers: Amazon Kindle Paperwhite 16GB (2024)

### Why we like it

For a dad who reads, a Kindle Paperwhite is the gift that keeps on giving, and it is one of the most-loved e-readers on Amazon. The 2024 model has a 7-inch glare-free display with a high contrast ratio and fast page turns, according to the listing, so the pages stay sharp whether he is on a sunny porch or reading in bed.

The details that make it a great gift for dad are the simple ones. It does not have social media, notifications or distracting apps, so it is just for reading. A single charge over USB-C lasts up to 12 weeks, and the display can shift from white to amber for comfortable reading in the dark. It is also waterproof, so the pool, the bath or the beach is fair game. The 16GB storage holds a big library of books.

### Who it's for

Readers, frequent travelers, and dads who have started holding paper books at arm's length. It is also a great gift for retirees with time to read again.

### Pros

- 7-inch glare-free display with fast page turns
- No social media, notifications or distracting apps
- Up to 12 weeks of battery life on a single charge
- Adjustable warm light and waterproof design

### Cons

- Black-and-white screen, so it is not for comics or photo-heavy books
- Books are bought separately or borrowed through a subscription or library
- Not a tablet: no web browsing, streaming or games

## Best for music anywhere: JBL Flip 6 Portable Waterproof Speaker

### Why we like it

If dad is the one with music playing in the garage, on the grill or out on the boat, the JBL Flip 6 is an easy upgrade. It is a 2-way speaker system: the listing says a racetrack-shaped woofer handles lows and mids while a separate tweeter handles the highs, with dual passive radiators for deeper bass. That is a more serious design than many small Bluetooth speakers.

It is built for the outdoors, with an IP67 waterproof and dustproof rating, and it plays for up to 12 hours on a single charge. It can stand upright or lie on its side, and JBL's PartyBoost feature lets you pair it with other compatible JBL speakers for bigger sound. With a 4.7-star average from tens of thousands of buyers, it is one of the safest speaker gifts you can pick.

### Who it's for

Grill masters, garage tinkerers, campers, anglers and any dad who likes a soundtrack while he works.

### Pros

- 2-way speaker design for clear highs and solid bass
- IP67 waterproof and dustproof
- Up to 12 hours of playtime
- Stands vertically or horizontally
- PartyBoost pairing with other compatible JBL speakers

### Cons

- Not designed to fill a large room the way a home speaker would
- 12 hours is generous, but it still needs regular charging
- Basic controls, which some dads will see as a plus

## Best for the always-low phone: Anker Zolo Power Bank 10,000mAh 30W

### Why we like it

Every family has one: the dad whose phone is on 9% by lunchtime. This compact Anker power bank fixes that without adding a tangle of cables, because the 5.4-inch USB-C cable is built right in. Anker says that cable is tested to survive over 10,000 bends.

According to the listing, the 10,000mAh battery delivers up to 2 full charges for an iPhone 15, with up to 30W output for fast charging and 20W input when recharging the power bank itself. It measures just 4.32 x 2.58 x 0.98 inches, small enough for a jacket pocket, glove box or golf bag. Anker includes an 18-month warranty.

### Who it's for

Commuters, travelers, golfers, and anyone who uses his phone for maps, photos and podcasts all day. It also makes an easy stocking stuffer.

### Pros

- Built-in USB-C cable, so nothing to forget
- Up to 2 full charges for an iPhone 15
- 30W output for fast charging
- Pocket-friendly size
- 18-month warranty

### Cons

- The built-in cable is USB-C, so older Lightning iPhones need their own cable
- One built-in cable means charging one device at a time from it
- Not meant for heavy laptop charging on the go

## Best for walks and calls: Soundcore by Anker P20i True Wireless Earbuds

### Why we like it

Earbuds are one of those gifts dads rarely buy for themselves, and then use every day once they have them. The Soundcore P20i is a well-rated, no-fuss pair with more than a hundred thousand ratings on Amazon. The listing highlights oversized 10mm drivers with boosted bass and a companion app that lets him customize the controls and choose from 22 EQ presets.

Battery life is strong: up to 10 hours on a single charge, extended to 30 hours with the case, and a quick 10-minute charge gives about 2 hours of playtime. There is also a "Find My Earbuds" feature in the app, which can make a lost earbud play a sound. The case is compact with a lanyard attached, so it clips onto keys or a bag. Two built-in microphones and an AI algorithm help pick up his voice on calls.

### Who it's for

Dads who walk the dog, go to the gym, commute or spend a lot of time on the phone. Also a good pick for dads who keep losing earbuds, thanks to the lanyard and Find My Earbuds.

### Pros

- Long battery life with the case
- Quick 10-minute charge for 2 hours of listening
- App with 22 EQ presets and Find My Earbuds
- Compact case with a lanyard
- Two mics for clearer calls

### Cons

- The listing does not advertise active noise cancelling
- Fit is personal, so some ears may need a different tip size
- The app is optional but needed for the EQ presets and Find My Earbuds

## How to choose a tech gift for dad

- **Start with his routine:** what does he already do every day? Read, walk, drive, work in the garage? Pick the gadget that improves that.
- **Keep setup simple:** a gift he can use within five minutes of unwrapping is more likely to be used for years.
- **Check compatibility:** know his phone's charging port before buying a power bank or cable.
- **Think durability:** if he is outdoors a lot, waterproof ratings like IP67 matter more than extra features.
- **Bundle it:** a Kindle plus a gift card, or a speaker plus a power bank, makes a bigger present without a big jump in price.

## The bottom line

If dad reads at all, the **Kindle Paperwhite** is the stand-out gift on this list. For the outdoorsy or handy dad, the **JBL Flip 6** is a durable crowd-pleaser. The **Anker Zolo power bank** and **Soundcore P20i earbuds** are budget-friendly, practical picks that work well as stocking stuffers or add-ons.
