## Wireless stand or multi-port charger?

- **Wireless 3-in-1 stands** charge a phone, watch and earbuds without plugging anything in. They are tidy and great at the bedside, but slower than a cable.
- **Multi-port desktop chargers** power several devices through USB-C and USB-A cables. They are faster and can charge laptops, but you still need a cable for each device.
- **Mix and match:** Many homes use a wireless stand on the nightstand and a multi-port charger at the desk.

## Tips for choosing a charging station

- **Count your devices:** List everything you charge daily, then pick a station with enough spots.
- **Check your phone:** Magnetic stands work best with iPhone 12 or newer or a magnetic case.
- **Look at wattage:** Laptops need high-wattage USB-C ports; earbuds need very little.
- **Check what is in the box:** Some stations include the adapter and cable; others do not.
- **Plan the cables:** Pair a desktop charger with short cables and clips. See our [desk cable management ideas](/office/desk-cable-management-ideas/).
- **Mind the bedroom:** If indicator lights bother you, choose a stand where you can turn the light off.
