## How to choose a USB-C charger for a laptop and phone

1. **Wattage:** 65W covers many thin-and-light laptops. Choose 100W for larger laptops, and 30W if you only charge phones, tablets and earbuds.
2. **Number of ports:** A single port is smallest. Three ports let you charge a laptop, phone and earbuds together, but power is split between ports when several are in use.
3. **Port types:** Two USB-C ports plus one USB-A port covers newer devices and older cables.
4. **Size and plug:** GaN chargers are much smaller than old laptop bricks, and a foldable plug makes them easier to pack.
5. **Cable:** Some chargers include a USB-C cable and some do not. For laptops, use a cable rated for the charger's wattage.
6. **Safety features:** Look for temperature monitoring and protection against over-voltage, over-current and short circuits.

## Tips for charging a laptop and phone from one charger

- **Check your laptop's charger rating:** Match or exceed the wattage of the charger that came with your laptop for full-speed charging.
- **Use the right cable:** A cable rated for 60W or 100W is needed to get full laptop charging speed.
- **Know how power is shared:** On multi-port chargers, the laptop gets less power when phones and earbuds are plugged in too.
- **Pack one charger, not three:** A single multi-port charger and a [universal travel adapter](/travel/best-universal-travel-adapter-for-international-travel/) cover most trips abroad.
- **Carry a backup:** For days away from outlets, pair your charger with a [portable charger for iPhone](/tech/best-portable-charger-for-iphone/).
