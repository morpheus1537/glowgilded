The catch is that rowers are long, and some types are loud. This guide focuses on compact rowers that fit small spaces and use quiet magnetic or hydraulic resistance. ## How to fit a rower into a small apartment

- **Measure length first:** The rower's length is what matters most. Add a little room behind the seat for your arms.
- **Plan storage:** Decide whether it will stand upright in a corner or slide under furniture, and choose a model that suits that plan.
- **Use a mat:** An exercise mat protects floors and helps reduce vibration for neighbors.
- **Check your height:** Rail length and listed height ranges matter. Tall users should look for a long rail; shorter users have more choice.
- **Start easy:** Row at low resistance for 10 to 15 minutes, focusing on legs, then core, then arms.

## Buying tips

- **Magnetic for quiet:** Magnetic rowers are usually the quietest choice for shared walls.
- **Hydraulic for light weight:** Hydraulic rowers tend to be lighter and smaller.
- **Look at weight limits:** Ranges in this guide run from 300 to 350 pounds where listed.
- **Free apps help motivation:** Both Sunny models connect to SunnyFit without a subscription, and Merach has its own app.
