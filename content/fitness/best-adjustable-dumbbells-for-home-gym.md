## How to choose adjustable dumbbells

- **Weight range:** think about your heaviest lift, often rows or goblet squats. If you are new, a set reaching 25 to 50 lb per hand leaves room to grow.
- **Increment size:** smaller jumps (2.5 lb) make it easier to progress on shoulder and arm exercises.
- **Adjustment style:** selector dumbbells (PowerBlock, NordicTrack) change weight in seconds. Plate-and-collar sets (FEIERDUN, Amazon Basics) are slower but flexible.
- **Footprint:** measure where the set will live. Trays or a case help keep the floor clear.
- **Future-proofing:** expansion kits like PowerBlock's let you add weight later instead of buying a new set.
- **Protect your floor:** put a rubber mat under your training area.

## A simple starter routine

Two or three times a week, try three rounds of goblet squats, dumbbell rows, floor presses, Romanian deadlifts and shoulder presses, 8 to 12 reps each. Pick a weight where the last two reps feel hard but your form stays clean, and add weight in small steps over time. If you have an injury or health condition, check with a professional before starting a new program.
