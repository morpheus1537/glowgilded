The Ally Peaks bar is made of 1.7mm thickened steel with a powder coating and holds up to 440 pounds. Foam-covered handles give multiple grip positions, and it can also be used on the floor for push-ups and dips.

## How to choose a fitness gift for him

- **Match his training style.** Home workouts call for a pull up bar or ab roller; gym-goers will appreciate recovery tools and accessories.
- **Recovery is always useful.** Almost everyone who trains gets sore. A massage gun works for lifters, runners and weekend warriors alike. For more ideas, see our guide to [muscle recovery tools at home](/fitness/muscle-recovery-tools-at-home/).
- **Check his space.** Measure doorways before buying a pull up bar and think about where equipment will be stored.
- **Don't guess on weights.** If he lifts at home, ask about his current equipment before buying something big. Our [best adjustable dumbbells for home gym](/fitness/best-adjustable-dumbbells-for-home-gym/) guide can help if you want to go big.
- **Bundle small gifts.** Shaker bottles and grip trainers make great stocking stuffers or add-ons to a main gift.

The Opove M3 Pro 2 massage gun is the best all-round fitness gift for men because nearly everyone who trains will use it.
