## Before you buy: measure your doorway

1. **Inside width:** Most leverage-style bars need a doorway roughly 24 to 32 or 36 inches wide. Measure between the frame sides.
2. **Wall depth:** Some bars list a door frame depth range. Thick walls can be a problem for hook-style bars.
3. **Trim height and thickness:** The bar rests on the trim on one side. Check the listing's acceptable trim height.
4. **Frame strength:** Use a solid wooden door frame in good condition, not a weak or decorative trim.
5. **Ceiling clearance:** Make sure you have room above the bar so your head does not hit the frame.

## Safety and training tips

- **Test before every session:** Tug down firmly on the grips before you hang your full weight.
- **Do not jump onto the bar:** Step up from a sturdy stool and lower yourself under control.
- **Start with hangs:** Dead hangs and slow negatives build grip and strength before full pull-ups.
- **Add resistance bands:** Bands looped over the bar can assist beginners.
- **Mind the trim:** Remove the bar if you notice cracks or movement in the door frame.
- **Recover well:** Pair your new routine with [muscle recovery tools at home](/fitness/muscle-recovery-tools-at-home/).
