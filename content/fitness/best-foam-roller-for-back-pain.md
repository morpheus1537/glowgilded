## How to choose a foam roller for your back

1. **Firmness:** Soft or medium is best to start; firm and bumpy rollers press deeper.
2. **Texture:** Smooth spreads pressure; ridges and knobs target knots.
3. **Length:** 36 inches covers the whole back and works for lengthwise stretches; 12 to 18 inches is compact.
4. **Shape:** Round rollers roll; half-round rollers stay put for gentle stretches and balance work.
5. **Core:** Solid or strong cores keep their shape longer than hollow, soft foam.

## Foam rolling tips for a tight back

- **Go slow:** Roll about an inch per second and pause on tight spots instead of rushing.
- **Support your head:** Clasp your hands behind your head when rolling your upper back.
- **Ease off the lower spine:** Roll the glutes, hips and sides instead of pressing hard on the low back.
- **Breathe:** Relax into the roller; tensing up works against you.
- **Fix the cause too:** A better desk setup helps; see our [ergonomic home office accessories for back pain](/office/ergonomic-home-office-accessories-for-back-pain/).
- **Stop if it hurts:** Discomfort is normal, sharp pain is not.
