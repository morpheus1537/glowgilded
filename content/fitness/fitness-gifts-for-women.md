We picked five fitness gifts for women that cover yoga, cardio, recovery, hydration and tracking. Each one is rated 4.3 stars or higher by thousands of shoppers, and we checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. ## Best for yoga: Gaiam Premium 6mm Print Yoga Mat

### Why we like it

Gaiam is one of the most popular names in yoga, and this mat has more than 22,000 ratings at 4.7 stars. It is 6mm thick for extra cushioning on joints but still lightweight. The textured, sticky non-slip surface gives grip, and the printed design looks great rolled up in the corner. The listing says it is PVC free of the seven phthalates DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP, DIDP, DnOP and DnHP, and it measures 68 x 24 inches. A free downloadable yoga workout is included.

### Who it's for

Yoga and Pilates fans, and anyone who does floor workouts at home.

### Pros

- 6mm cushioning
- Non-slip textured surface
- Stylish prints
- Free bonus yoga workout

### Cons

- Gaiam suggests airing it out for 2-3 days because of a harmless initial odor
- 68 inches may be short for very tall users

## Best for recovery: RENPHO Mini Thermacool Massage Gun

### Why we like it

This pocket-sized massage gun does more than most: it switches between heat to ease stiff muscles and cold to help with post-workout swelling, with 4 temperature settings. A brushless motor gives 5 speeds from 1,800 to 3,000 strokes per minute with a 7mm amplitude, and there are 4 interchangeable heads. The listing says it is whisper-quiet, fully recharges in about 2 hours, has a 10-minute auto-off and comes with a portable pouch. It has more than 30,000 ratings.

### Who it's for

Runners, lifters and anyone with sore muscles after a workout or a long day.

### Pros

- Heat and cold in one device
- 5 speeds and 4 heads
- Small enough for a gym bag
- Travel pouch included

## How to pick the right fitness gift

- **Match her workout.** Yoga lovers get the most from a mat; runners from a watch; gym-goers from a tumbler and massage gun.
- **Think recovery.** Many active people own plenty of workout gear but not recovery tools. Our [muscle recovery tools at home](/fitness/muscle-recovery-tools-at-home/) guide has more options.
- **Consider color.** Mats, tumblers and watches come in several colors; pick one that suits her style.
- **Bundle small gifts.** A jump rope and tumbler together make a great New Year starter kit.

## The bottom line

The **Gaiam Premium yoga mat** and the **Stanley IceFlow 2.0** are the safest bets for almost any active woman. Add the **RENPHO Mini Thermacool** for recovery, the **Loocio jump rope** for quick cardio, or go big with the **Garmin vivoactive 5** for the runner or data lover in your life.
