## How body composition scales work

1. **Bioelectrical impedance (BIA):** Electrodes send a small, painless current through your body to estimate fat, muscle and water.
2. **4 vs 8 electrodes:** Foot-only scales read your lower body; scales with a handle add your arms and torso for segmental readings.
3. **Dual frequency:** Some 8-electrode scales use two frequencies for what makers call more complete readings.
4. **Display vs app:** Basic scales show weight, BMI and body fat on the scale; others show 7 metrics or full segmental charts.
5. **Trends, not diagnoses:** Use the numbers to track direction over weeks, not as a medical test.

## Tips for better readings

- **Be consistent:** Same time, same conditions, bare feet, every day.
- **Hydration matters:** BIA readings shift with water levels, so avoid weighing right after a workout or a big drink.
- **Use a hard floor:** Carpet and uneven tiles can throw off weight.
- **Zero the scale:** Two listings suggest stepping on and off, waiting for 0.0, then stepping back on if a reading looks wrong.
- **Watch the weekly average:** Daily swings are normal; look at the trend.
