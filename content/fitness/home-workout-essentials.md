## A simple 20-minute routine with this gear

- **Warm up (3 minutes):** march in place, arm circles and gentle hip openers on the mat.
- **Strength circuit (12 minutes):** banded squats, glute bridges, lateral walks and push-ups. Do 40 seconds on, 20 seconds rest, three rounds.
- **Core (3 minutes):** planks, dead bugs and bicycle crunches on the mat.
- **Recovery (2 minutes plus):** roll calves, quads and upper back on the foam roller.

## Tips for sticking with home workouts

- **Keep gear visible:** a rolled mat by the couch is a daily reminder.
- **Schedule it:** treat a workout like a meeting on your calendar.
- **Progress slowly:** move to the next band level only when the current one feels easy for full sets.
- **Track trends, not days:** weigh in at the same time each morning and look at weekly averages.
