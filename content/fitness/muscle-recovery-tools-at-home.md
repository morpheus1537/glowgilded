## Tips for better recovery at home

- **Start gentle:** with massage guns and lacrosse balls, use the lowest speed or lightest pressure first and build up.
- **Keep it short:** a minute or two per muscle group is usually plenty for a massage gun. Do not stay on one spot for long.
- **Avoid sensitive areas:** skip the front of the neck, bones, joints and any injured, bruised or swollen area.
- **Add heat at the end of the day:** a heated massager or warm bath is a nice way to relax tight muscles before bed.
- **Hydrate and sleep:** no tool replaces good sleep, water and rest days.
- **Ask a pro when needed:** sharp, lasting or injury-related pain deserves a doctor or physical therapist.

## What to look for in recovery tools

- **Adjustability:** multiple speeds or intensity levels let you match the tool to how sore you are.
- **Noise:** if you will use it in front of the TV or at the office, quieter is better.
- **Power:** check whether a tool is cordless, USB-charged or plug-in before you buy.
- **Portability:** small tools like lacrosse balls are easy to bring on trips.
