## How to choose a grip strengthener

1. **Adjustable vs fixed:** Adjustable grippers change resistance with a dial or slider. Fixed grippers each have one strength, so you buy a set or step up over time.
2. **Resistance range:** Make sure the lowest setting is easy enough to start and the top setting gives room to grow.
3. **Handle comfort:** Silicone or rubber handles feel softer; knurled metal grips better but can dig into your palms.
4. **Build:** Steel springs and metal frames tend to last longer under heavy use.
5. **Extras:** Kits may include finger exercisers or other tools for a more complete hand workout.

## How to train grip safely

- **Start light:** Choose a level you can close fully for 8 to 15 controlled reps.
- **Control the release:** Let the handles open slowly instead of snapping back.
- **Train both hands:** Alternate hands so one side does not get far ahead.
- **Rest between sessions:** Grip muscles fatigue quickly; take rest days if you train heavy.
- **Stop if it hurts:** Discomfort in joints or tendons is a sign to back off and get advice.
- **Combine with carries and hangs:** Farmer carries, dead hangs and rows build grip that carries over to real life. Our [home workout essentials](/fitness/home-workout-essentials/) guide covers more gear.
