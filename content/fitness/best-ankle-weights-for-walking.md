## How to choose ankle weights for walking

1. **Weight range:** For walking, lighter weights are easier on your joints. Adjustable sets let you start around 1 pound per ankle and build up.
2. **Adjustable or fixed:** Removable weight packs let you progress without buying new weights. Fixed weights are slimmer and simpler.
3. **Strap and fit:** Look for long hook-and-loop straps and a secure closure. Sportneer lists a fit for ankles about 7.5 to 12.5 inches around.
4. **Comfort:** Soft, breathable, moisture-absorbing fabric helps on longer walks.
5. **Versatility:** Many ankle weights also work on wrists or arms for upper-body moves.
6. **Fill:** Most use iron sand or iron shot, which keeps the profile compact.

## Tips for walking with ankle weights

- **Start light:** Begin with 1 to 2 pounds per ankle and short walks, then build up slowly.
- **Keep your stride natural:** Don't let the weights change the way you walk. If your stride shortens or your knees feel strained, go lighter.
- **Strap snugly:** Fasten the strap just above the ankle bone so the weight does not bounce.
- **Mix in strength moves:** Leg lifts, glute kickbacks and Pilates moves are where ankle weights shine. Pair them with [resistance bands for beginners](/fitness/best-resistance-bands-for-beginners/) for variety.
- **Ask a professional:** If you have joint issues or are recovering from injury, ask a doctor or physical therapist before adding weight.
