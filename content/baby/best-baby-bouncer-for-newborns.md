## How to choose a baby bouncer

1. **Age and weight limits:** Most bouncers cover 0 to 6 months or up to 20 lbs. Convertible models last longer.
2. **Newborn support:** Look for an infant insert, head support or bolster for tiny babies.
3. **Soothing features:** Vibration, melodies and sounds can help calm a fussy baby. Check what batteries are needed.
4. **Harness:** Every pick has a 3-point or 5-point harness. Use it every time.
5. **Cleaning:** A machine-washable seat pad is a must for spit-up and diaper blowouts.
6. **Portability:** A bouncer that folds flat is handy for visits to grandparents.

## Bouncer safety tips

- **Awake time only:** Never use a bouncer for sleep. Move a sleeping baby to a crib or bassinet.
- **Floor only:** Keep the bouncer on the floor, away from stairs, tables and counters.
- **Always buckle:** Use the harness every time, even for a minute.
- **Stay close:** Keep baby within sight and reach.
- **Watch the limits:** Stop using it once your baby hits the weight limit or tries to sit up or climb out.
- **Do not carry baby in it:** Lift your baby out before moving the bouncer.

## How a bouncer fits into your day

A bouncer is a helper, not a babysitter. Short sessions while you make breakfast or take a quick shower work well, but newborns also need plenty of floor time, tummy time and being held.
