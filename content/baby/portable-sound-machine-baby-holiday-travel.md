Holiday travel with a baby is a lot: car seats, pack-and-plays, bottles and a new room every couple of nights. If your little one sleeps with white noise at home, the sound machine is one of the most important things to pack. A familiar sound helps cover unfamiliar noises like relatives talking downstairs, a hotel hallway or highway hum, and it signals that it is time to sleep wherever you are.

This guide covers four real Amazon US sound machines: two rechargeable portable models for naps on the go and two plug-in machines for longer stays at grandma's house. ## Sleep tips for holiday travel with a baby

- **Keep the routine:** Use the same sound, the same bedtime steps and, if possible, the same sleep sack.
- **Place it at a distance:** Put the machine away from the crib and keep the volume low and comfortable.
- **Charge every night:** Plug in portable machines overnight so they are ready for the next day's naps.
- **Darken the room:** A travel blackout shade or a towel over a window helps a baby nap in a bright guest room.
- **Bring a spare cable:** USB-C cables go missing on trips.

## Buying tips

- **Portable vs plug-in:** Rechargeable models are best for cars, strollers and hotels; plug-in models are better for longer stays.
- **Check the battery claim:** Compare listed battery life with how long your baby naps and sleeps.
- **Look for a child lock:** Curious toddlers love buttons.
- **Consider a night light:** Helpful for night feeds in an unfamiliar room.
- **Check the current listing:** Colors and bundles vary, and ratings can differ between versions.

## The bottom line
