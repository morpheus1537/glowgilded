High chairs are notorious space hogs. In a small apartment or a kitchen with a bistro table, a bulky chair with a giant tray can block the walkway for years. The good news is that there are smart alternatives: ultra-slim folding chairs that stand on their own, table chairs that clamp right onto your dining table and boosters that strap to a chair you already own.

## Space-saving high chair types

1. **Slim folding high chairs:** Full-height chairs that fold flat and stand up by themselves.
2. **Hook-on table chairs:** Clamp onto the edge of a sturdy table, with no legs on the floor.
3. **Strap-on boosters:** Sit on a dining chair and strap to it, bringing baby to table height.
4. **Multi-mode seats:** Floor seat, feeding seat and toddler booster in one.
5. **Check the fold:** Folded thickness and whether it stands alone matter most in tight kitchens.

## Buying tips for small kitchens

- **Measure the gap:** Check where the chair will live when folded, not just where it will sit.
- **Check your table:** Hook-on chairs need the right table thickness and no lip in the way.
- **Use the harness every time:** Even for a quick snack.
- **Protect the floor:** A splat mat makes cleanup faster in small spaces.
