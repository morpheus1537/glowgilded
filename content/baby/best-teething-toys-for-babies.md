## How to choose a teething toy

1. **Age range:** Check the listed age. Most teethers here are for 3 months and up.
2. **Material:** Silicone is soft and easy to clean; natural rubber has a classic feel.
3. **Texture:** Bumps, ridges and bristles massage gums in different ways.
4. **Cooling:** Some teethers are made to chill in the refrigerator.
5. **Grip:** Light, easy-to-hold shapes let babies self-soothe.
6. **Cleaning:** Follow the care instructions and inspect teethers often.

## Teether types explained

- **Cooling teethers:** Gel- or water-filled teethers, like the Nuby keys and Infantino rings, are chilled in the refrigerator for a cool feel on sore gums.
- **Silicone massagers:** Soft silicone teethers, like the NanaNubs and RaZberry, use bristles or bumps to massage gums and are easy to wash.
- **Natural rubber:** Sophie la girafe is made from natural rubber and doubles as a first toy.
- **Hands-free styles:** Pacifier-style teethers, like the RaZberry, are easier for young babies who cannot hold things well yet.
- **Rotate a few:** Many parents keep two or three different teethers, since babies often prefer different textures on different days.

A small set of teethers also makes a thoughtful baby shower or holiday gift. Pair one cooling teether with one silicone massager so parents have options.

## Teething safety tips

- **Supervise:** Always watch your baby while they use a teether.
- **No cords around the neck:** Never tie a teether around your baby's neck.
- **Inspect often:** Throw away any teether that is cracked, torn or leaking.
- **Follow chilling instructions:** Use the refrigerator or freezer only as the maker says.
- **Keep it clean:** Wash teethers often, especially after they fall on the floor.
- **Ask your pediatrician:** Check before using teething gels or medicine, and if your baby has a fever or seems very unwell.
