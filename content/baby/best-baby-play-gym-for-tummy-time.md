Tummy time helps babies build the neck, shoulder and core strength they will use to roll, sit and crawl, but many newborns are not fans at first. A good play gym makes it easier. A soft mat, toys to look at and bat, and music or a mirror give your baby something to focus on, so sessions feel like play instead of work. ## How to choose a baby play gym

1. **Stages of play:** Look for lay-and-play, tummy time and sit-and-play modes so it lasts longer.
2. **Toys:** Mirrors, rattles, crinkle toys and high-contrast items suit young babies.
3. **Music and lights:** Fun for many babies; quieter gyms suit calmer nurseries.
4. **Mat size and padding:** A thick, roomy mat is more comfortable on hard floors.
5. **Washability:** Machine-washable mats save time.
6. **Style:** Neutral and wooden gyms blend into living rooms.

## Tummy time tips

- **Start small:** Short, frequent sessions while baby is awake are easier than one long session.
- **Get down on the floor:** Your face is the best toy; lie down at eye level.
- **Try after a diaper change:** A fed, rested and comfortable baby is more willing.
- **Always supervise:** Never leave your baby alone on a play gym.
- **Rotate toys:** Swapping a few toys keeps things new.
- **Dress comfortably:** A soft onesie works better than a [swaddle](/baby/best-swaddle-for-newborns/) during play time.
