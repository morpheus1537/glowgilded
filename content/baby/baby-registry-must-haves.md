Ask parents a few months in what they actually reach for, though, and the answers get much shorter. They want help with sleep, a gentle wipe they trust at every change, a way to carry the baby and still have two free hands, and a mountain of burp cloths.

This guide covers those four everyday baby registry must-haves for first-time parents. ## How to build a baby registry you will actually use

- **Start with daily-use items:** sleep, diapering, feeding and carrying come first. Decor and novelty gadgets can wait.
- **Ask for multiples of the small stuff:** burp cloths, wipes and swaddles are used constantly, and group gifts of bulk packs are very practical.
- **Check weight and age ranges:** carriers, seats and sleep products all list limits. Make sure they fit your baby from day one.
- **Read the setup requirements:** app-connected products like a smart sound machine need Wi-Fi and a phone, so plan where they will live.
- **Favor long track records:** a high average across tens of thousands of ratings tells you more than a perfect score on a handful.

## Registry timing tips

Most parents create their registry in the second trimester and share it six to eight weeks before a baby shower. Add items across a range of budgets so friends and family can choose comfortably. Keep a few big-ticket items on the list too, since some guests like to go in together on a group gift. After the shower, many stores offer a completion discount on remaining items, which is a good time to pick up the bulk essentials yourself.

## Safety reminders

Always follow the manufacturer's instructions for carriers, including the correct positions for your baby's age and size, and keep your baby's face visible and airway clear. Place sound machines out of the crib and at a reasonable volume. When in doubt, ask your pediatrician what they recommend for your baby.
