A good bottle warmer takes the guesswork out of night feedings: no pot of water on the stove, no uneven microwave heating and no shaking drops onto your wrist over and over. Many models also keep milk warm, thaw frozen breast milk and shut off automatically, which matters when you are running on very little sleep.

## How to make night feedings easier

- **Set up a feeding station:** Keep the warmer, bottles, burp cloths and a dim light on one dresser or nightstand.
- **Pre-measure:** If you use formula, measure powder into a container before bed.
- **Use keep-warm wisely:** Follow each brand's guidance on how long milk can be kept warm.
- **Test every bottle:** Shake a few drops on your inner wrist; it should feel warm, not hot.
- **Clean daily:** Empty and dry the warmer to prevent mineral build-up, and descale according to the manual.
