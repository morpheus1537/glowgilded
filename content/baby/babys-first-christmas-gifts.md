## How to choose a first Christmas gift

- **Pick one keepsake:** an ornament or a book with an inscription becomes a family treasure.
- **Add something useful:** a toy or book that baby can play with right away.
- **Check the age range:** follow manufacturer age guidance for every toy.
- **Think small:** new parents often have limited space, so compact gifts are welcome.

## Buying tips

- **Order personalized gifts early:** customization can add shipping time.
- **Write in the book:** add a short note and the date inside the cover.
- **Skip small parts:** avoid toys with small pieces for babies and toddlers.
- **Check the current price on Amazon:** holiday prices change often.
