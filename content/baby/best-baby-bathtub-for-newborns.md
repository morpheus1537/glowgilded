Your baby's first baths can feel nerve-wracking. Newborns are slippery, they cannot hold their heads up, and an adult tub is far too big. A baby bathtub or sink bather gives your little one a supportive, reclined spot so you can keep one hand on your baby and the other free for washing.

This guide compares five well-rated baby bathtubs on Amazon US. We checked every listing on November 13, 2026, with delivery set to New York ZIP 10001. Each one was in stock with at least 4.5 stars and more than 2,000 ratings. We do not quote prices because they change often, so check the current price on Amazon. Our picks are based on listed features and Amazon ratings, not hands-on testing.

**Safety first:** Never leave a baby alone in or near water, even for a second, and keep one hand on your baby at all times. Supports and cushions are aids, not safety devices. Follow each tub's instructions and ask your pediatrician if you have questions about bathing.

## What to look for in a newborn bathtub

1. **Newborn support:** A sling, insert or cushion that cradles the head and keeps baby reclined.
2. **Grow-with-baby design:** Many tubs convert to a sitting or toddler tub, so you buy once.
3. **Where you bathe:** Sink bathers suit small homes; full tubs sit in an adult tub or on a counter.
4. **Storage:** Collapsible tubs fold flat, and many tubs have a hook for drying.
5. **Drainage:** A drain plug lets you empty the tub without lifting it full of water.

## Bath time tips for newborns

- **Gather everything first:** Towel, washcloth, baby wash, clean diaper and clothes should be within reach before you fill the tub.
- **Use only a few inches of water:** Newborns need very little water.
- **Check the temperature:** Test with your wrist or elbow, or use a thermometer.
- **Keep the room warm:** Babies lose heat quickly when wet, so warm the bathroom and wrap them in a hooded towel right away.
- **Keep it short:** A few minutes is plenty for a newborn.
