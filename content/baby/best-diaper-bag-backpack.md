A good diaper bag is one of the most-used items on any baby registry. You will carry it to every appointment, every family visit and every errand for the next couple of years, so it pays to choose one that is comfortable, organized and easy to open with one hand while holding a squirming baby. For most parents, a backpack style beats a tote because it keeps both hands free.

## How to choose a diaper bag backpack

1. **Capacity:** About 25 liters suits a full day out with one baby. Twins or toddlers plus a baby may need more.
2. **Pockets:** Insulated bottle pockets, a wipes pocket and a hidden valuables pocket make life easier.
3. **Opening:** A wide opening lets you see everything at a glance.
4. **Changing pad:** A wipe-clean pad is essential for public restrooms.
5. **Stroller straps:** Hang the bag on the stroller when your back needs a break.
6. **Comfort:** Padded straps and a padded back panel help on long days.
7. **Style:** A neutral look makes it easy to share between parents.

## Diaper bag packing tips

- **Pack a mini kit:** Diapers, wipes, a changing pad, a spare outfit, burp cloths and a plastic bag for soiled clothes.
- **Restock nightly:** Refill diapers and wipes before bed so you are always ready.
- **Use the insulated pockets:** Keep bottles at the right temperature on outings.
- **Add a parent pouch:** Keys, phone, wallet and hand sanitizer in one small pouch save digging.
