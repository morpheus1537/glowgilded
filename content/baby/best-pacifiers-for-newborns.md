## How to choose a pacifier for a newborn

1. **Age range:** Newborn sizes are usually labeled 0-3 or 0-6 months and are smaller and lighter.
2. **Nipple shape:** Round, symmetrical nipples work either way up. Orthodontic nipples are flatter and shaped for the palate.
3. **Material:** Most pacifiers are silicone, which is durable and easy to sterilize. Natural rubber is softer and more flexible but wears faster.
4. **One-piece design:** One-piece pacifiers have no joints for gunk to collect in.
5. **Shield:** Look for a curved shield with room for the nose and air holes to help reduce skin irritation.
6. **Night features:** Glow-in-the-dark designs make it easier to find a dropped pacifier at 3 a.m.

## Pacifier safety and buying tips

- **Expect some trial and error:** Many babies prefer one shape, so a couple of different packs can save frustration.
- **Inspect before every use:** Throw away any pacifier with cracks, tears or a sticky feel.
- **Never tie it around the neck:** Use a short, safety-tested pacifier clip instead of string or ribbon.
- **Clean often:** Sterilize before first use, then wash regularly following each maker's directions.
- **Keep spares everywhere:** Stash one in the diaper bag, car and nursery. A [swaddle for newborns](/baby/best-swaddle-for-newborns/) and a pacifier make a calming bedtime pair.
- **Ask your pediatrician:** If you have questions about breastfeeding and pacifiers, your pediatrician or lactation consultant can help.
