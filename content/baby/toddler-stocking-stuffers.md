Toddlers make stocking shopping fun, because they get just as excited about crayons and bath toys as they do about big presents. The trick is choosing small gifts that are safe for their age, keep them busy and do not make a huge mess on Christmas morning.

We picked five highly rated toddler stocking stuffers, each with thousands of Amazon ratings, and checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We do not list prices because they change often, so use the buttons to check the current price. Always check the age label on each item, since a few of these are made for ages 3 and up.

## Best overall: Crayola Mini Twistables Crayons (50 Count)

### Why we like it

Twistables solve the two biggest crayon problems with little kids: broken crayons and peeled wrappers. You twist the crayon up as it wears down, so there is no sharpening or peeling, and the mini size is easy for small hands to hold.

With 50 colors in the set, it is generous for a stocking stuffer, and more than 51,000 ratings back it up. It is labeled for ages 3 and up.

### Who it's for

Toddlers who love to color, and parents tired of broken crayons.

### Pros

- No sharpening or peeling
- 50 bright colors
- Small size for little hands
- Fits easily in a stocking

### Cons

- Labeled for ages 3 and up
- Crayons can still mark walls

## How to choose toddler stocking stuffers

- **Check age labels:** some toys are 18 months and up, while crayons and Play-Doh are 3 and up.
- **Avoid tiny parts:** skip anything small enough to be a choking hazard for younger toddlers.
- **Think about mess:** water-reveal pads and twistable crayons are easy on Christmas morning.
- **Mix it up:** combine one creative, one bath and one sensory gift for variety.
