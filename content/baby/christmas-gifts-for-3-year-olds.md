## How to choose gifts for a 3-year-old

- **Check the age label.** Many toys with small parts are labeled 3+. If there's a baby or toddler in the house, keep those pieces out of reach.
- **Pick open-ended toys.** Building sets and pretend play toys get played with far longer than single-purpose gadgets.
- **Mix active and quiet play.** Pair something energetic, like binoculars for outdoor adventures, with a calm activity like painting.
- **Think about batteries.** Stock up on AA batteries so electronic toys work on Christmas morning.
- **Fill the stocking.** For small extras, see our [toddler stocking stuffers](/baby/toddler-stocking-stuffers/).

## The bottom line

PicassoTiles magnetic tiles are the best Christmas gift for most 3-year-olds because they're easy to use now and still fun years from now.
