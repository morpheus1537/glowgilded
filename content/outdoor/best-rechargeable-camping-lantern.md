## How to choose a rechargeable camping lantern

1. **Brightness:** 1,000 lumens lights a campsite. Lower-lumen lanterns are enough inside a tent.
2. **Runtime:** Check how long the lantern lasts on a charge, and at what brightness.
3. **Light colors:** Warm light is cozier and less harsh at night. Daylight white is better for tasks.
4. **Water resistance:** IPX4 or IP44 handles splashes and rain. IP67 and IP68 survive submersion.
5. **Power bank:** Some lanterns can charge a phone in an emergency.
6. **Size and weight:** Backpackers need a light, packable lantern. Car campers can choose bigger, brighter ones.
7. **Hanging options:** Hooks or handles let you hang the lantern from a tent loop or branch.

## Camping lantern tips

- **Charge before every trip:** Top up the battery the night before you leave.
- **Use warm light at camp:** Warm modes are easier on the eyes and attract fewer bugs than bright white.
- **Hang it high:** Hanging the lantern from a tent loop or branch spreads light more evenly.
- **Dim to save battery:** Lower brightness can stretch runtime a lot.
- **Keep one in the house:** A charged lantern is a great backup for winter outages.
