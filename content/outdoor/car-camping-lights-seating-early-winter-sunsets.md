## Buying tips

### Plan for the campsite rather than an idealized photograph

Check the forecast, daylight and local rules before packing comfort accessories. A hammock needs appropriate anchors and permission; a chair needs suitable ground and cargo space. Charge lights and practice their controls before departure, and keep a backup accessible. Water resistance is not the same as unlimited weather protection. A summer-style hammock, camp chair or lantern does not supply cold-weather insulation, shelter or emergency communication. End the outing or move indoors when conditions exceed your equipment, experience or safety plan.

For a car camping setup that needs to be ready before an early sunset, make a short list of what must be true before a purchase is useful.
