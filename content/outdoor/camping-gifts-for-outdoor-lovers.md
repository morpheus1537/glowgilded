People who love camping are always looking for small upgrades that make nights outdoors easier and more comfortable. The best camping gifts are not complicated: better light, a comfortable place to sit or lie down, and a hot drink when the morning is cold.

We picked five highly rated camping gifts, each with tens of thousands of Amazon ratings, and checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We do not list prices because they change often, so use the buttons to check the current price. Each one also works for backyard nights, road trips and power outages.

## Best overall: Lepro 1000LM Rechargeable LED Camping Lantern

### Why we like it

A good lantern changes the whole campsite after dark, and the Lepro is one of the most popular on Amazon, with over 33,000 ratings. It puts out up to 1,000 lumens across a 360-degree beam and has four modes: white, warm, combined and flash, with dimming by long press.

The listing gives up to 12 hours on warm white, and the 4400mAh battery charges by USB-C and can double as a power bank for a phone. Hooks on the top and base let you hang it right side up or upside down inside a tent. It is IPX4 water resistant.

### Who it's for

Campers, families who want reliable light for power outages, and backyard entertainers.

### Pros

- Up to 1,000 lumens with dimming
- Warm and white light modes
- Rechargeable by USB-C, doubles as a power bank
- IPX4 water resistant

### Cons

- Not built to be submerged
- Does not support chargers above 5V

## Best for relaxing: Wise Owl Outfitters Camping Hammock

### Why we like it

A hammock is one of the most-loved pieces of camping gear, and the Wise Owl Outfitters single hammock packs down smaller than an eggplant and weighs about 16 oz. It measures 9 x 4.5 ft, so one person can stretch out comfortably.

It comes as a complete kit with 9-ft tree straps, carabiners and a built-in stuff sack, and the 5-loop straps let you adjust the height in minutes without tools. The durable nylon has triple-stitched seams.

### Who it's for

Backpackers, campers, beachgoers and anyone who wants to nap outside.

### Pros

- Lightweight and very packable
- Complete kit with straps and carabiners
- Sets up in minutes without tools
- Roomy 9 x 4.5 ft size

### Cons

## How to choose a camping gift

- **Think about their trips:** backpackers want light gear like the hammock and headlamps; car campers will love the rocking chair.
- **Lighting is always welcome:** a lantern and headlamps get used on every trip.
- **Comfort wins:** a good chair or hammock is something many campers never buy for themselves.
- **Bundle it:** pair the Stanley bottle with a bag of coffee for a cozy gift set.
