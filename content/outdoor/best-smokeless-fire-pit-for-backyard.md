Their double-wall airflow designs feed extra air into the top of the fire, burning off much of the smoke before it reaches your chair, so you get more flame and fewer teary eyes.

## How to choose a smokeless fire pit

1. **Size:** Around 19.5 inches suits small groups. 27 inches and up suits larger gatherings.
2. **Weight and portability:** Lighter pits travel to campsites and the beach. Heavier ones stay put.
3. **Stand:** A stand improves airflow and protects the surface below.
4. **Cleanup:** A removable ash pan makes emptying ash easy.
5. **Material:** 304 stainless steel resists rust and heat.
6. **Extras:** Covers, pokers, carry bags and grill grates add value.

## Smokeless fire pit tips

- **Burn dry, seasoned wood:** Wet or green wood smokes in any fire pit.
- **Do not overfill:** Keep wood below the top air holes so the airflow design can work.
- **Use a stand or fire-safe surface:** Protect decks and patios from heat.
- **Keep a safe distance:** Keep kids, pets and furniture well back from the flames.
- **Let it cool before cleaning:** Ash can stay hot for hours.
- **Cover it when cool:** A cover keeps rain out and reduces rust.
