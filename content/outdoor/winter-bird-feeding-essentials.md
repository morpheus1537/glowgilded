It is also when birds need the most help: natural food runs low, days are short, and water freezes solid. ## Best seed feeder: Brome Squirrel Buster Standard

### Why we like it

The Squirrel Buster is the feeder that ends the squirrel war. Openings in the shroud line up with the feeding ports, and when a squirrel climbs on, its weight forces the shroud down and closes the ports, according to Brome. Squirrels and some large birds are foiled but not harmed. A patented spring mechanism lets you adjust how much weight the shroud supports. The ports have perches for perching birds, and the wire shroud gives clinging birds a place to hold on. A seed ventilation system lets humid, hot air escape to keep seed fresher, and every part exposed to squirrels is chew proof. Brome backs it with its Lifetime Care support.

## Winter bird feeding tips

- **Feed consistently.** Birds learn where food is; keep feeders stocked through cold snaps.
- **Offer seed, suet and water.** This mix attracts the most species.
- **Clear snow.** Brush snow off feeder ports and the bath after storms.
- **Clean regularly.** Wash feeders and baths to keep birds healthy.
- **Give birds cover.** Place feeders near shrubs or trees so birds can escape predators.

## How to set up a winter bird station
