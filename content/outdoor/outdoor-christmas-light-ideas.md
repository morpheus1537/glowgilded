## Outdoor Christmas light ideas to try

1. **Light the path.** Line your walkway with C9 pathway lights for a classic, welcoming look.
2. **Project the house.** Aim a projector at the front of the house for full coverage without a ladder.
3. **Wrap the porch.** Wrap railings and columns at ground level with string lights.
4. **Glow up the garden.** Tuck solar stakes into garden beds and around trees.
5. **Highlight the door.** Hang a lit wreath on the front door and add lanterns on the steps.
6. **Gather around the fire.** If you spend evenings outside, our [fire pit accessories for backyard nights](/outdoor/fire-pit-accessories-for-backyard-nights/) guide adds warmth to the glow.

## Outdoor lighting safety tips

- Use only outdoor-rated lights, cords and power stakes outside.
- Keep plugs and connections off the ground and away from puddles and snow.
- Don't connect more strands than the manufacturer allows.
- Put everything on a timer so lights don't run all night.
- Inspect lights each year and toss any with frayed wires or cracked sockets.

## The bottom line

Start with the DEWENWILS power stake timer so your display turns itself on at dusk, then add the Brightown C9 pathway lights for a classic walkway.
