## How to choose a patio pressure washer

1. **Rated PSI and GPM:** Working pressure and water flow matter more than peak numbers.
2. **Nozzles:** A set of quick-connect tips (0°, 15°, 25°, 40° and soap) covers most jobs.
3. **Hose length:** A longer high-pressure hose means moving the machine less.
4. **Soap system:** Onboard tanks or a foam cannon make it easier to apply cleaners.
5. **Storage:** Compact units with wheels or handles are easier to tuck away.

## Patio pressure washing tips

- **Sweep first:** Clear leaves and loose debris before washing.
- **Start wide:** Use a 25° or 40° nozzle and only go narrower for stubborn spots.
- **Work in sections:** Keep the wand moving in overlapping passes to avoid stripes.
- **Protect plants:** Rinse nearby plants with plain water before and after using cleaners.
- **Winterize:** Drain hoses and the pump before cold weather; our [snow removal tools](/outdoor/snow-removal-tools/) guide covers the season ahead.
