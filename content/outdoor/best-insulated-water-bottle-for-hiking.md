## How to choose a hiking water bottle

1. **Capacity:** 24 to 26 ounces for most day hikes; 40 ounces or more for long or hot trips.
2. **Lid style:** Straw lids for sipping on the move, spouts for fast drinking, caps for leak protection.
3. **Hot and cold:** Check the listed hours for both if you want warm drinks in winter.
4. **Carrying:** A handle, carabiner or shoulder strap helps when the bottle rides outside your pack.
5. **Cleaning:** A wide mouth makes it easier to add ice and scrub.

## Tips for staying hydrated on the trail

- **Start hydrated:** Drink before you leave, not just on the trail.
- **Sip often:** Small sips every 15 to 20 minutes beat big gulps.
- **Pre-chill or pre-heat:** Fill with ice water or hot water for a few minutes before filling to boost insulation.
- **Carry extra in winter:** Cold air is dry, and you may drink less than you need.
- **Plan for early dark:** Pack a light and layers; our [late-fall camp comfort guide](/outdoor/late-fall-camp-comfort-short-weekend-trips/) has ideas.
- **Clean after every hike:** Wash lids and straws so nothing grows in the seals.
