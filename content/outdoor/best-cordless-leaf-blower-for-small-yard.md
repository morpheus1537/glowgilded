## CFM vs MPH in plain English

- **CFM (cubic feet per minute)** is how much air the blower moves. Higher CFM pushes bigger piles of dry leaves.
- **MPH (miles per hour)** is how fast the air leaves the nozzle. Higher MPH helps lift wet, matted leaves and debris from cracks.
- For a small yard, a balance of both matters more than the biggest number on the box.

## Tips for faster leaf cleanup

- **Blow when leaves are dry:** Dry leaves move with far less effort than wet ones.
- **Work with the wind:** Blow in the same direction the wind is going.
- **Make small piles:** Push leaves into several small piles, then bag or mulch them.
- **Use low speed near beds:** Lower speeds keep mulch and soil in place.
- **Wear protection:** Use safety glasses and hearing protection, and follow the manufacturer's safety directions.
- **Store batteries indoors in winter:** Lithium-ion batteries prefer moderate temperatures; follow each brand's storage guidance.
