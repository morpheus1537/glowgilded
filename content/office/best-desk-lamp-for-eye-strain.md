The right desk lamp gives you soft, even, flicker-free light where you need it, with brightness and color you can adjust as the day goes on.

## How to choose a desk lamp for eye strain

1. **Flicker-free light:** Look for lamps described as non-flickering.
2. **Diffused light:** A frosted shade or light panel reduces glare.
3. **Dimming:** Adjustable brightness lets you match the room.
4. **Color temperature:** Cooler light for focus, warmer light for evenings.
5. **Coverage:** A wide light bar lights more of the desk.
6. **Extras:** USB ports, timers and clamps add convenience.

## Tips for reducing eye strain at your desk

- **Light the room, not just the desk:** Avoid a bright screen in a dark room.
- **Place the lamp to the side:** This avoids glare on your screen.
- **Warm up the light in the evening:** Switch to a warmer color temperature after dark.
- **Follow the 20-20-20 rule:** Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
- **Position your monitor:** Keep it about an arm's length away and slightly below eye level. A [monitor arm](/office/best-monitor-arm-for-small-desk/) makes that easier.
- **See an eye doctor:** Persistent strain can mean you need glasses or a new prescription.
