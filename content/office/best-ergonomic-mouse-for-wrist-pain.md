A standard flat mouse keeps your palm facing down, which twists the forearm, and small, tense movements add up over a long workday. An ergonomic mouse, whether vertical, trackball or contoured, can put your hand in a more relaxed position.

## Types of ergonomic mice

1. **Vertical mice:** Hold your hand in a handshake position. Good for reducing forearm twist.
2. **Thumb trackballs:** Your hand stays still while your thumb moves the ball. Good for small desks.
3. **Contoured mice:** A sculpted shape with a natural tilt, but closer to a traditional mouse.
4. **Hand size:** Ergonomic mice are often sized; the wrong size can be less comfortable than a basic mouse.

## Tips for a more comfortable mouse setup

- **Keep the mouse close:** Place it right beside the keyboard so you do not reach.
- **Elbow at about 90 degrees:** Adjust chair or desk height so your forearm is level.
- **Tune cursor speed:** Higher DPI means less hand movement, but set it so you stay in control.
- **Take breaks:** Short stretch breaks help no matter what mouse you use.
- **Free up space:** A [monitor arm for a small desk](/office/best-monitor-arm-for-small-desk/) gives you more room for your mouse and keyboard.
