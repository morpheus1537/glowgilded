Working from home sounds quiet until you add a barking dog, a leaf blower, a partner on their own calls and a dishwasher running mid-meeting. Noise cancelling headphones are one of the simplest upgrades for a home office. They quiet steady background noise so you can focus, and with a good mic they make calls clearer on both ends.

## What matters for work-from-home headphones

1. **Noise cancelling:** Hybrid ANC with multiple mics usually handles a wider range of noise.
2. **Multipoint Bluetooth:** Stay connected to your laptop and phone at once.
3. **Microphones:** Beamforming or voice-targeting mics help your voice come through clearly.
4. **Comfort:** Weight and cushion softness matter more than anything over an 8-hour day.
5. **Battery life:** 24 hours or more covers several workdays between charges.
6. **Wired option:** A cable is a handy backup when the battery runs low.
7. **Transparency mode:** Hear the doorbell or a family member without taking them off.

## Best overall: soundcore by Anker Q20i

### Why we like it

The soundcore Q20i is one of the most-rated headphones on Amazon, with 4.5 stars from more than 79,000 ratings. It uses hybrid active noise cancelling with four built-in mics that filter out background noise, plus Normal and Transparency modes you can switch in the soundcore app.

For remote work, the key feature is multipoint: it stays connected to your laptop and phone at the same time and switches audio to whichever device needs you. An AUX cable is included for wired Hi-Res audio, though ANC is not available in AUX mode.

### Who it's for

Most remote workers who want ANC and multipoint without a premium price.

Sony calls the WH-CH720N its lightest wireless noise canceling headband ever, at just 192 grams. That light weight makes a real difference across back-to-back meetings. Dual noise sensor technology with Sony's Integrated Processor V1 handles noise cancelling.

## Tips for better calls and focus

- **Test your mic:** Record a short clip in your meeting app to hear how you sound.
- **Use multipoint wisely:** Pair your laptop and phone so calls switch automatically.
- **Take breaks:** Remove headphones for a few minutes every hour to rest your ears.
- **Keep volume moderate:** ANC lets you hear clearly at lower volumes.
- **Light your face:** Clear audio pairs well with good lighting; see our [video call lighting setup](/office/video-call-lighting-setup/).
