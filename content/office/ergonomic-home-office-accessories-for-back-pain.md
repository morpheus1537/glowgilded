If you work from home, you probably sit more than you did in an office. There is no walk to a meeting room and no commute, so many of us stay in the same chair for hours. That is when the aches start: a dull lower back by mid-afternoon, a sore tailbone, tight hips, and wrists that complain after a long day of typing. A new ergonomic chair can cost a lot. Four small accessories can fix most of the problem for much less.

This guide covers the four that matter most for a home office: lumbar support, a seat cushion, a footrest and a wrist rest. Each one is highly rated by thousands of buyers, and we checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We do not list prices, because they change often, so use the buttons to check the current price. One note before we start: these accessories can make sitting more comfortable, but they are not medical treatment. If your back pain is sharp, constant or getting worse, see a doctor or physical therapist.

## How to set up your chair and desk in five minutes

Accessories work best when the basics are right. Use this quick order:

- **Chair height first:** raise or lower the chair until your elbows are roughly level with the desk when your shoulders are relaxed.
- **Then your feet:** if they no longer rest flat on the floor, add the footrest.
- **Then your back:** strap the lumbar pillow at the small of your back, not your mid-back. Sit all the way back in the seat.
- **Then the screen:** the top of the monitor should be around eye level. A laptop stand helps here; see our [home office desk setup essentials](/office/home-office-desk-setup-essentials/).
- **Then your hands:** put the wrist rest in front of the keyboard and rest on it between bursts of typing.

## Buying tips

- **Fix one problem at a time:** if your lower back aches, start with lumbar support. If your tailbone aches, start with a seat cushion. Buying all four at once makes it hard to tell what helped.
- **Watch the height change:** a seat cushion raises you by an inch or more. Re-check desk and foot height afterward.
- **Choose washable covers:** anything you sit or rest on every day gets grimy. All four picks here have removable or wipe-clean covers.
- **Move, too:** no accessory replaces getting up. Stand and stretch every 30 to 60 minutes. Our guide to [muscle recovery tools at home](/fitness/muscle-recovery-tools-at-home/) has ideas for easing tight muscles after a long week at the desk.
