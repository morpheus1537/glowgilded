Messy cables are the one thing that makes even a beautiful home office look unfinished. A tangle of chargers, a power strip on the floor and a waterfall of cords behind the monitor add visual noise, collect dust and are easy to kick loose in the middle of a video call. The good news is that desk cable management does not take special skills, tools or a big budget. A few simple pieces turn the mess into one or two clean lines.

We picked five highly rated products that each solve a different part of the problem: where the power strip lives, how cords travel down to the wall, how extra length is bundled and how your everyday charging cables stay in reach. The listing says it can be assembled in about three minutes and installed facing inward or outward.

## How to manage desk cables in 30 minutes

1. **Unplug and sort.** Pull everything out, decide what stays on the desk, and remove cables you no longer use.
2. **Mount the tray.** Clamp the tray under the back of the desk and put the power strip, USB hub and power bricks in it.
3. **Plug in from above.** Plug each device into the strip in the tray, keeping cords short.
4. **Bundle the slack.** Fold extra length and strap it with reusable ties inside the tray.
5. **One line to the wall.** Run the remaining cords down a desk leg inside the split sleeve.
6. **Clip the daily cables.** Add a few adhesive clips at the back edge for phone and laptop chargers.

## Buying tips

- **Measure first.** Check your desk edge thickness for a clamp tray and your power strip size for a cable box.
- **Tray or box?** Use a tray if you can lift the strip off the floor; use a box if it must stay on the floor.
- **Plan for change.** Choose reusable ties over zip ties so adding a new device takes seconds.
- **Clean before you stick.** Wipe surfaces before using adhesive clips so they hold.
- **Leave some slack.** Standing desks need enough cord length to move up and down without pulling plugs.
