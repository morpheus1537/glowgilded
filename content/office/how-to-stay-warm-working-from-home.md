Working from home in winter has a hidden downside: the office is cold. You are sitting still for hours, your desk is often next to a drafty window, and heating the whole house all day just for one person gets expensive. The good news is that you do not need to crank the thermostat. You need to heat you and your workspace.

Here is how to stay warm working from home, with eight simple tips and four highly rated products that target the coldest spots: the room, your desk and your hands. ## 8 tips to stay warm in your home office

1. **Heat the room you are in.** A small space heater in a closed office uses far less energy than heating the whole house.
2. **Close the door.** Keep the heat where you are.
3. **Block drafts.** Close curtains on cold windows and put a draft stopper at the door.
4. **Move your desk.** If you can, sit away from exterior walls and windows.
5. **Warm your hands directly.** A heated desk pad or fingerless gloves fix cold fingers fast.
6. **Layer up.** A warm base layer, a sweater and a throw blanket over your lap go a long way.
7. **Use a rug.** Bare floors pull heat from your feet.
8. **Move every hour.** A short walk or stretch gets your circulation going again.

## Space heater safety basics

- Plug heaters directly into a wall outlet, not an extension cord or power strip.
- Keep at least three feet of clearance from papers, curtains and blankets.
- Turn heaters off when you leave the room.
- Choose models with tip-over and overheat protection, like the DREO and Amazon Basics heaters above.
