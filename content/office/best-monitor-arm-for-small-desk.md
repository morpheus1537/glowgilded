A small desk fills up fast. Between a monitor stand, a laptop, a keyboard, a coffee mug and a notebook, there is barely room to rest your arms. A monitor arm is one of the most effective upgrades for a compact workspace: it lifts the screen off the desk, clears away the bulky base and lets you set the monitor at a comfortable height and distance.

This guide compares five well-rated single monitor arms on Amazon US, with the listed screen size, weight capacity and clamp range for each. We checked every listing on November 11, 2026, with delivery set to New York ZIP 10001. Each was in stock with at least 4.3 stars and more than 9,000 ratings. ## Before you buy: measure these four things

1. **VESA pattern:** Look at the back of your monitor for four screw holes. Most arms here fit 75x75mm and 100x100mm.
2. **Monitor weight:** Check the spec sheet, without the stand.
3. **Desk thickness:** Measure where you will clamp the arm.
4. **Space behind the desk:** Arms need a little room behind the screen to swing and tilt.

## Small desk setup tips

- **Clamp at the back corner:** Mounting at a rear corner frees the most surface.
- **Set the top of the screen near eye level:** Then adjust distance to about an arm's length.
- **Route cables along the arm:** Use the built-in clips so cords do not drape over the desk.
- **Pair with a laptop stand:** Use the laptop as a second screen beside the monitor.
- **Tidy the rest:** Our [desk cable management ideas](/office/desk-cable-management-ideas/) can finish the job.
