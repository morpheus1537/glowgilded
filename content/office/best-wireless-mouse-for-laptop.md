## How to choose a wireless mouse for a laptop

1. **Connection:** USB receivers are plug-and-play; Bluetooth saves a port.
2. **Port type:** Receiver mice often need USB-A. USB-C-only laptops may need an adapter.
3. **Size:** Compact for travel and smaller hands; full size for long desk days.
4. **Battery:** Look for a long battery life or a rechargeable mouse.
5. **Extras:** Adjustable DPI, side buttons, quiet clicks and fast-scroll wheels.

## Tips for using a mouse with a laptop

- **Check your ports:** USB-C-only laptops may need a [USB-C hub](/tech/best-usb-c-hub-for-macbook-air/) for receiver mice.
- **Store the receiver in the mouse:** Several mice have a slot so it does not get lost in your bag.
- **Use a mouse pad:** It improves tracking and comfort, especially on glass or glossy desks.
- **Turn it off in your bag:** An on/off switch saves battery when traveling.
- **Raise your screen:** Pair a mouse with a laptop stand and keyboard for better posture.
- **Adjust DPI:** Lower DPI is more precise; higher DPI moves faster across big screens.
