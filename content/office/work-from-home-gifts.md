The best work from home gifts solve exactly these problems. They are not novelty items that end up in a drawer; they are things a remote worker reaches for every single day.

## Best overall: House Gem Coffee Mug Warmer for Desk

### Why we like it

The most common remote-work complaint we hear is cold coffee. You pour a cup, join a meeting, and an hour later it is lukewarm. This mug warmer from House Gem keeps a hot drink at the temperature you choose, with three settings: 130°F, 150°F or 176°F. A clear LED display and color-coded indicator lights (blue, purple and red) show which setting is on, so it is easy to read at a glance.

What makes it a good gift rather than a gamble is the safety design. It shuts off automatically after 4 hours by default, and you can set a timer from 2 to 12 hours if you want it to run through a long workday. The listing says it is certified by TÜV SÜD to UL 1026. It also remembers your last settings, so it is ready the next morning. The 5.2-inch glass plate fits most flat-bottom ceramic, glass and stainless steel mugs, and it wipes clean once it cools. The wood-look base looks at home on a desk instead of like a kitchen appliance.

### Who it's for

Coffee and tea drinkers who work from home, spend long stretches on calls, or simply forget their drink while they focus.

### Pros

- Three temperature settings with a clear display
- Auto shut-off after 4 hours, plus a 2 to 12 hour timer
- Remembers your preferred settings
- Large 5.2-inch plate fits most flat-bottom mugs

### Cons

- Works best with flat, thin-bottomed mugs; plastic cups are not recommended
- Keeps drinks warm rather than reheating a cold cup quickly

## How to choose a work from home gift

- **Think about their biggest annoyance:** cold coffee points to the mug warmer, a cold room to the wearable blanket, and lots of calls to the phone stand.
- **Consider where they work:** someone who moves between the desk and the couch will get more use from a lap desk than someone with a fixed office.
- **Keep coworker gifts simple:** for a remote team gift exchange, small, neutral and useful items like a phone stand or mug warmer are the safest choices.
- **Bundle for a bigger gift:** the mug warmer and wearable blanket make a cozy winter set, while the lap desk and phone stand make a "work anywhere" kit.

## Buying tips

- **Check their mug:** the mug warmer heats best with flat, thin-bottomed ceramic, glass or stainless steel mugs. Adding a favorite mug makes the gift complete.
- **Order early:** popular desk gifts can sell out or ship slowly in December, especially when shipping to a coworker's home.
