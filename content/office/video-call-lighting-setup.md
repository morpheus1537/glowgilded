## How to set up lighting for video calls

1. **Face the light.** Sit facing a window or put your main light in front of you. Never sit with a bright window behind you.
2. **Put the light near the camera.** Clip a ring light or LED panel at the top of your screen so light hits your face from roughly eye level.
3. **Raise the camera.** Put a webcam on top of your monitor, or raise your laptop on a stand, so the camera is at eye level.
4. **Add fill.** If one side of your face is darker, add a second light or turn a lamp's side bar toward you at lower brightness.
5. **Match color temperatures.** Set your lights to match your room. Warm 3000K-ish light feels cozy; cooler light feels more like daylight.
6. **Light the background softly.** A lamp behind you at low brightness adds depth so you don't look like you're floating in darkness.

## Common video call lighting mistakes

- **Overhead light only.** Ceiling lights cast shadows under your eyes. Add a light in front.
- **Too bright.** Turn brightness down until your face doesn't look shiny or washed out.
- **Mixed colors.** A blue-white ring light plus a yellow lamp can make you look odd. Match them.
- **Glare on glasses.** Raise the light higher and angle it slightly to one side.
