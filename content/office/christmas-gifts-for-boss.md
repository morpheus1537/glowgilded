Buying a Christmas gift for your boss is tricky. Go too personal and it's awkward. Go too generic and it feels like an afterthought. And in many workplaces, a big, expensive gift to a manager can look like you're trying too hard. The sweet spot is a gift that's useful, professional and well made, something they'll reach for at their desk every day.

We picked five Christmas gifts for your boss that fit that brief, from a YETI tumbler to a classic Moleskine notebook. Each one has 4.4 stars or more on Amazon with thousands of ratings, and we checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We don't list prices, so check the current price on Amazon. If your team is pooling money, any of these works as a group gift.

A YETI is one of the safest gifts you can give: it's useful, recognizable and suits nearly everyone. ## Gift etiquette for your boss

- **Check the policy:** some companies limit gifts to managers.
- **Go group:** a team gift avoids awkwardness and lets you buy something nicer.
- **Keep it professional:** skip clothing, perfume and joke gifts.
- **Add a short note:** a sincere thank-you card often means more than the gift.

## Buying tips

- **Pair for a set:** the pen and the Moleskine make a polished two-piece gift.
- **Know their devices:** buy the Anker charger only for Apple users.
- **Order early:** popular gifts can ship slowly in December.
- **Check the current price on Amazon:** prices change often during the holidays.
