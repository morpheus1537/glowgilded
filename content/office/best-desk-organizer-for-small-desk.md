## How to choose a desk organizer for a small desk

1. **Measure your desk:** Check the depth and width you can spare before ordering, as listings often suggest.
2. **Go vertical:** Tiered organizers and risers store more per square inch of desk.
3. **Think about what you store:** Paper-heavy desks need trays and file slots. Pen-heavy desks need cups and compartments.
4. **Use the drawer:** A drawer organizer keeps small items off the desktop entirely.
5. **Material:** Metal mesh and steel are sturdy. Look for non-slip pads to protect your desk.
6. **Room to grow:** Movable dividers and detachable parts let you change the layout later.

## Small desk organization tips

- **Clear everything first:** Take it all off the desk and only put back what you use daily.
- **Use the wall:** A pegboard or shelf above the desk frees up surface space.
- **Add a desk mat:** A [desk mat for your keyboard and mouse](/office/best-desk-mat-for-keyboard-and-mouse/) defines your work zone and protects the surface.
- **One in-tray:** Put all new mail and papers in one tray and sort it weekly.
- **Raise your laptop:** A riser or [laptop stand](/office/best-laptop-stand-for-desk/) frees space underneath and improves posture.
- **Reset daily:** Spend two minutes at the end of each day putting things back.
