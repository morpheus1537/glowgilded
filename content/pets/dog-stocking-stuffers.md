We picked four dog stocking stuffers that fit in a standard stocking and cover a treat, a calming activity and two durable chews. Every pick has 4.4 stars or more on Amazon with thousands of ratings, and we checked each rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We don't list prices, so check the current price on Amazon. Always supervise your dog with new treats and toys.

## Best for calming: LickiMat Classic Soother & Buddy, 2 Pack

### Why we like it

The holidays can be stressful for dogs, with guests, noise and a house full of new smells. LickiMat helps by encouraging licking, which the brand says helps calm and soothe your pet. Spread a little yogurt, peanut butter, purée or wet food on the textured surface, and your dog has to work slowly to lick it clean.

This pack includes two patterns: Soother (dots) for runny foods and Buddy (crosses) for chunky, spreadable treats. LickiMat says the licking action also generates saliva that helps protect teeth and gums. The mats contain no silicone, and the brand says they are not chew toys, so supervise your dog while they use them.

### Who it's for

Anxious dogs, dogs who eat too fast, and owners who need a quiet distraction during gift opening, dinner or fireworks.

### Pros

- Encourages calm, slow licking
- Two textures for different foods
- Easy way to give supplements or medication
- Flat 8 x 8 inch mats slip easily into a stocking
- 4.4 stars from nearly 10,000 ratings

### Cons

- Not a chew toy; heavy chewers need the LickiMat Tuff
- Needs supervision
- Must be washed after each use

## How to build a dog stocking

1. **Start with a treat:** a bag of holiday biscuits fills the top of the stocking.
2. **Add a chew:** pick a chew rated for your dog's size and chewing style.
3. **Add an activity:** a lick mat keeps them busy while you open gifts.
4. **Skip the danger zone:** keep tinsel, ribbon, chocolate and string away from dogs.

## Buying tips

- **Match the size:** check the weight range on every chew toy.
- **Know your chewer:** heavy chewers need tougher products than gentle chewers.
- **Introduce treats slowly:** new treats can upset some stomachs, so start small.
- **Supervise:** always watch your dog with new chews and toys.
- **Check the current price on Amazon:** seasonal treats can sell out in December.
