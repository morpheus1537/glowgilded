Bringing home a new puppy is one of the happiest moments of the year, and one of the most exhausting. The first few weeks are a blur of whining at 3 AM, chewed shoe laces, potty breaks and worrying about what the puppy is doing every time you leave the house. If someone you love just got a puppy, or is getting one for Christmas, the most useful gift is one that makes those first weeks easier.

That is the idea behind this guide to gifts for new puppy owners. Instead of another squeaky toy that lasts an afternoon, we picked three gifts that solve the three classic new-puppy problems: settling at night, teething, and being left alone. We also added a bonus pick for rainy or snowy days when a young puppy has more energy than you have patience.

Every pick is a long-running best seller with thousands of Amazon ratings. We checked each rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above for delivery to New York. Holiday prices change often, so check the current price on Amazon before you order.

## Best for the first nights: Snuggle Puppy Heartbeat Toy

### Why we like it

The first nights in a new home are hard on a puppy. They have just left their mother and littermates, and the quiet, dark crate feels lonely. The Snuggle Puppy is a soft plush puppy with a pouch in its belly that holds a battery-powered heart. Press the button and it gives off a steady pulsing beat; add the included disposable heat pack for warmth. The idea is simple: give the puppy something that feels like a littermate to curl up against.

The medium size is a 12 x 8 inch plush sized for dogs that grow to roughly 25 to 70 pounds, and SmartPetLove also makes a Junior version for small breeds and a Large for 70-pound-plus breeds. The listing says the heart runs for 8 hours with a single press, or continuously when you hold the button. Batteries are included, and the plush is machine washable once you remove the heart and heat pack.

It is one of the most reviewed puppy products on Amazon, with a 4.5-star rating across more than 67,000 ratings. It also happens to look adorable under the tree, with its floppy ears and little red heart tag.

### Who it's for

Anyone bringing home a young puppy, especially for crate training, plus rescue dogs that are nervous in a new home and dogs that get anxious during storms, fireworks or travel.

### Pros

- Pulsing heart and heat pack mimic the comfort of a littermate
- Batteries and one heat pack included, ready on day one
- Plush is machine washable (remove the heart first)
- Available in three sizes for small, medium and large breeds
- Huge track record with more than 67,000 ratings

### Cons

- Not a chew toy; remove the heart before unsupervised play
- The heat pack is single-use; replacement packs are sold separately
- It helps many puppies settle, but it is not a substitute for a consistent crate routine

## Best for peace of mind: Furbo 360° Dog Camera

### Why we like it

Every new puppy owner eventually has to leave the house, and the first few times are nerve-racking. Is the puppy crying? Chewing the baseboards? Fast asleep? The Furbo 360° Dog Camera answers that question from your phone. It streams 1080p live video with a motorized 360° rotating view, so you can pan around the room instead of staring at one fixed corner, and color night vision works in low light.

The feature that makes it a gift rather than just a security camera is treat tossing. Load the top with small dry treats, and you can toss one to the puppy from the Furbo app, with an adjustable treat size. Combined with two-way audio and real-time barking alerts, it lets you reward calm behavior while you are away, which is exactly what alone-time training needs. The Furbo comparison chart on the listing says it holds up to 100 treats.

We chose the Core Features version on purpose: it includes live view, rotation, barking alerts, audio, night vision and treat tossing with no subscription required. The optional Furbo Nanny plan adds AI alerts and video history, with a 14-day free trial. It holds a 4.4-star rating across more than 9,300 ratings, and the white body with a wood-look top looks more like a piece of decor than a gadget.

### Who it's for

New puppy owners who work outside the home, people training a puppy to be alone, families with separation-anxious dogs, and anyone who simply loves checking in on their dog.

## Best for teething: KONG Puppy Teething Chew Toy

### Why we like it

Puppies start teething within a few months, and they will chew whatever is in reach: chair legs, slippers, cords and hands. The best defense is a better option. The KONG Puppy is the puppy version of the famous KONG Classic, made from a softer natural rubber formulated for baby teeth and gums.

It does three jobs. It is a chew toy that is gentle on young teeth, a bouncy fetch toy thanks to its uneven snowman shape, and a stuffable treat toy. Fill it with kibble, a little paste or KONG's puppy treats and the puppy has to work to get it out, which is a great way to keep them busy in the crate or while you make dinner. KONG recommends the puppy rubber until about 9 months, then graduating to the KONG Classic.

It holds a 4.6-star rating across nearly 54,000 ratings, and KONG says it is made in the USA from globally sourced materials. We picked the Medium in pink, but it also comes in blue and in XS, Small and Large sizes.

### Who it's for

Any puppy under about 9 months, owners crate training a puppy, and anyone looking for a small, useful stocking stuffer to pair with a bigger gift.

### Pros

- Softer rubber made for puppy teeth and gums
- Stuffable with treats or kibble for longer enrichment
- Bounces unpredictably for fetch games
- Covered by KONG's Satisfaction Guarantee
- Several sizes to match the breed

### Cons

- Not for strong adult chewers; switch to the KONG Classic as adult teeth arrive
- Only two colors (pink or blue), and you need to pick the right size for the breed
- Like any toy, it should be supervised and replaced if damaged

## How to choose a gift for a new puppy owner

- **Ask about the puppy's size.** Both the Snuggle Puppy and the KONG Puppy come in different sizes. Buy for the adult size of the breed with the Snuggle Puppy, and for the current mouth size with the KONG.
- **Think about their schedule.** A pet camera is most valuable for someone who works outside the home or has a long commute. If they are home all day, a comfort toy or puzzle may be more useful.
- **Solve a real problem.** New puppy owners are usually short on sleep and time. Gifts that help with nights, teething or alone time get used every day.
- **Check the Wi-Fi.** The Furbo needs 2.4GHz Wi-Fi and a USB plug near where it will sit, ideally on a shelf or table the puppy cannot reach.
- **Coordinate with the family.** Puppy parents often get many of the same toys. A quick message to ask what they already have avoids duplicates.

## Gift pairing ideas

- **The first-night kit:** the Snuggle Puppy plus the KONG Puppy, stuffed with a few of the puppy's kibble for crate time.
- **The working parent bundle:** the Furbo 360 with a bag of small, round training treats for treat tossing.
- **The snow-day box:** the Dog Brick puzzle plus the KONG Puppy for a full afternoon of indoor enrichment.

## Buying tips

- Order early in December; holiday shipping slows down as Christmas gets closer.
- Remove the heart unit and heat pack from the Snuggle Puppy before washing or leaving it with a puppy that chews.
- Use small, round, dry treats about half an inch across in the Furbo so they toss cleanly.
- Supervise any new toy or puzzle the first few times and remove it if it gets damaged.
- Keep the gift receipt for sized items like the KONG Puppy in case the puppy grows faster than expected.
