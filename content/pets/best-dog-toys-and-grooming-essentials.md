A bored dog is usually a busy dog in the wrong way: chewed shoes, restless pacing or nonstop barking. The fix is often simple. Give them something rewarding to chew, a reason to run, and a few minutes of grooming that keeps their coat healthy and the couch less furry.

These four products are long-time favorites among dog owners, each with thousands of strong ratings. We checked every rating and rating count on Amazon on the date above. Prices change, so check the current price before buying, and always pick the size that fits your dog and supervise them with new toys.

## Best all-around toy: KONG Classic Stuffable Dog Toy

### Why we like it

The KONG Classic has been a staple for decades, and it still earns its place in nearly every dog-owning household. The natural rubber is tough and bouncy, so it works for chewing and for unpredictable fetch. The real magic is the hollow center: stuff it with kibble, treats or dog-safe peanut butter, and it becomes a puzzle that keeps dogs focused and calm.

Trainers often recommend it for crate training and separation anxiety, because it gives a dog something positive to do while you are away.

### Who it's for

Dogs that get bored easily, puppies in crate training, and owners who want a quiet, mentally engaging toy. The Large suits most medium-to-large dogs.

### Pros

- Durable natural rubber
- Stuffable for long-lasting enrichment
- Erratic bounce makes fetch fun
- Dishwasher safe on the top rack

### Cons

- Extreme chewers may need the tougher Extreme version
- Needs cleaning after sticky fillings

## How to pick the right dog toys

- **Size up, not down:** a toy that is too small can be a choking hazard. When in doubt, choose the larger size.
- **Match the chewer:** gentle chewers can enjoy most toys, while power chewers need durable rubber or nylon.
- **Rotate toys:** swapping toys every few days keeps them feeling new and interesting.
- **Inspect regularly:** replace any toy that cracks, splinters or becomes small enough to swallow.
- **Mix mental and physical play:** a stuffed KONG works the brain, while fetch works the body. Most dogs need both.

## The bottom line

If you only buy one thing, get the KONG Classic. It is durable, versatile and keeps dogs mentally busy in a way few toys can. Add the Benebone Wishbone for strong chewers, the Chuckit Ultra Ball for high-energy fetch, and the Hertzko slicker brush to keep shedding under control.
