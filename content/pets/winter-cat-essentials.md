When the temperature drops, cats get serious about warmth. They hunt down sunbeams, curl up on laptops and sleep pressed against radiators. Winter also means shorter days and less to watch out the window, so many indoor cats get bored and restless.

These five winter cat essentials tackle both problems: two ways to keep your cat warm, a sunny spot by the window and two ways to keep them moving. ## Winter care tips for indoor cats

- **Block drafts.** Move beds away from exterior doors and cold tile floors, or raise them off the ground.
- **Follow the sun.** Put a perch or mat where the sun lands in the afternoon.
- **Keep water fresh.** Indoor heating dries the air, so make sure water is always available.
- **Play every day.** Ten to fifteen minutes of active play helps prevent winter weight gain and boredom.
- **Brush more often.** Winter coats shed and mat, and grooming improves insulation.
- **Watch the space heater.** Keep cats away from space heaters and fireplaces, and never leave them unattended.
- **Check the car.** Outdoor and community cats sometimes sleep under car hoods for warmth; tap the hood before starting the engine.
