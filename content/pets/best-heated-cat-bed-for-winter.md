## How to choose a heated cat bed

1. **Indoor or outdoor:** Outdoor shelters need weather resistance and more heat.
2. **Heat control:** Pet-activated thermostats warm only when your cat is in the bed; adjustable pads let you pick the temperature.
3. **Shape:** High walls or floppy sides feel secure; flat pads fit crates and cat trees.
4. **Size:** Match the bed to your cat's weight and favorite sleeping position.
5. **Cleaning:** Removable, machine-washable covers make life easier.
6. **Cord safety:** Steel-wrapped or chew-resistant cords are worth it for curious cats.

## Tips for using a heated cat bed

- **Put it where your cat already naps:** Cats are more likely to use a bed in a favorite spot.
- **Give it time:** Some cats take a few days to trust a new bed; a familiar blanket or a few treats can help.
- **Test pet-activated beds properly:** Lay your hand flat on the bed for several minutes.
- **Check cords:** Route cords away from chewing and replace any damaged ones.
- **Outdoor cats need shelter:** A heated pad alone is not enough in wind and snow; use an insulated house.
