## Winter care tips for senior dogs

- **Block drafts:** Move beds away from doors, windows and cold tile floors. A rug under the bed helps.
- **Layer warmth:** Put a self-warming mat on top of or inside a supportive bed.
- **Shorten walks:** Two or three short walks are often easier than one long cold one.
- **Prevent slips:** Older dogs struggle on icy paths and slick floors. Runners and rugs indoors can help.
- **Watch for changes:** Stiffness, reluctance to climb or more time sleeping can be worth a check-up with your vet.

## Buying tips

- **Measure your dog:** Measure length lying down for beds, chest for sweaters and the height of the furniture for stairs.
- **Prioritize washability:** Senior dogs may have more accidents; washable covers make life easier.
- **Combine items:** A supportive bed plus a warming layer covers both comfort and warmth.
- **Check the current listing:** Sizes and colors vary; confirm the exact variant before ordering.
