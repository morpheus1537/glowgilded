Big dogs put a lot of weight on their hips, elbows and shoulders, and that pressure adds up as they age. A thin cushion or a flattened pillow bed leaves a large dog lying almost on the floor. An orthopedic bed with supportive foam spreads that weight out, which can help senior dogs, dogs with stiff joints and any big breed sleep more comfortably.

## How to choose an orthopedic dog bed for a large dog

1. **Foam type:** Memory foam contours to the body. Egg-crate foam spreads weight and adds airflow. Some beds combine both.
2. **Size:** Your dog should be able to stretch out fully. Check the listed dimensions and weight limit.
3. **Bolsters:** Dogs who curl up or lean love bolsters. Sprawlers may prefer a flat mattress.
4. **Low entry:** A low front helps dogs with stiff joints step in easily.
5. **Waterproofing:** A waterproof liner protects the foam from accidents and drool.
6. **Washable cover:** A removable, machine-washable cover is essential with big, shedding dogs.

## Tips for helping a large dog sleep comfortably

- **Place the bed away from drafts:** Cold floors and doors make stiff joints worse in winter.
- **Use the bed's full size:** If your dog hangs off the edge, size up.
- **Wash covers often:** Big dogs shed a lot. Our guide on [getting pet hair off the couch](/pets/how-to-get-pet-hair-off-couch-before-guests/) has tools that work on dog beds too.
- **Let foam expand fully:** Wait the recommended time before deciding a bed is too flat.
- **Watch how your dog sleeps:** Curlers like bolsters; sprawlers like flat mattresses.
- **Talk to your vet:** If your dog struggles to stand or lie down, ask about joint care.
