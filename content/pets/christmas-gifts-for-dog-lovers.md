### Why we like it

Few things make a dog lover tear up faster than their own dog's paw print. The Pearhead kit turns that print into a white clay heart ornament you can hang on the Christmas tree, a wall or a display stand, finished with a red polka dot ribbon that already looks festive. The process is simple: there is no mixing or baking, you just press your pet's paw into the soft clay to make an impression. That makes it a fun activity for Christmas Eve or a cozy afternoon, and it works for cats as well as dogs, so it suits multi-pet households too.

### Who it's for

Dog moms and dads who love sentimental keepsakes, families with a new puppy celebrating a first Christmas, and anyone who wants a meaningful gift that costs little. It is also a gentle, thoughtful sympathy gift for someone remembering a dog that has passed.

### Pros

- Turns a real paw print into a heart ornament for the tree

### Cons

## How to choose a gift for a dog lover

- **Think about their routine.** If they walk their dog twice a day, a hands-free leash will get far more use than another squeaky toy. If they are sentimental, the paw print ornament will mean more than anything practical.
- **Get the dog's size right.** Leashes, beds and harnesses are sized by weight. Ask casually, or check a recent photo, before you buy a sized item.
- **Mind the timing.** A DNA test makes a great Christmas gift, but results take a few weeks. Tuck a handwritten note in the box saying "results coming in January" so the wait feels like part of the fun.
- **Make the keepsake together.** Paw print kits are easier with two people: one to hold the dog calmly and one to press the paw. Offer to help, and it becomes a shared holiday memory.
- **Pair with something for the dog.** Dog lovers love gifts that include their dog. Add a toy or a few treats from our [dog stocking stuffers guide](/pets/dog-stocking-stuffers/) and you have a complete gift.
