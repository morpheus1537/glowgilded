## How to choose interactive toys for an indoor cat

1. **Play style:** Some cats chase, some pounce, some wrestle. Watch what your cat does and choose a toy that matches.
2. **With you or solo:** Wand toys need a person. Automatic toys and puzzles keep cats busy when you are working or away.
3. **Safety:** Avoid small parts that can be chewed off, and always supervise string toys.
4. **Power:** Automatic toys run on batteries or USB. Rechargeable or plug-in options save on batteries.
5. **Noise:** Timid cats may avoid loud motors, so look for quiet operation.
6. **Variety:** Rotating two or three types of toys keeps play interesting.

## Tips for better playtime

- **Move toys like prey:** Drag the wand away from your cat and let it hide behind furniture instead of dangling it in their face.
- **Let them win:** Finish each session with a catch, then offer a treat or a meal.
- **Rotate toys:** Put some away for a week and bring them back to keep them exciting.
- **Play before bed:** An evening play session can help night-active cats settle down.
- **Store strings safely:** Put wand toys away after play.
- **Check for wear:** Replace toys with loose parts or torn seams.
