A good litter mat sits at the box exit and catches much of that mess before it reaches the rest of your home.

## Types of cat litter mats

1. **Coiled mesh mats:** Thick vinyl coils and grooves grab litter. Soft underfoot, easy to vacuum or shake out.
2. **Honeycomb double-layer mats:** Litter falls through a top layer with large holes into a sealed bottom layer, then you pour it back into the box.
3. **PVC grooved mats:** A firmer surface with deep channels and borders to hold litter.
4. **Silicone mats:** Smooth and wipe-clean with raised edges. Less trapping power but very easy to clean.
5. **Size matters:** The bigger the mat, the more steps it covers, and the more litter it catches.

## Tips to reduce litter tracking

- **Make the mat big enough:** Your cat should take at least two steps on the mat when leaving the box.
- **Try a high-sided or top-entry box:** These help contain litter that gets kicked out.
- **Keep the box clean:** Cats dig and kick more in a dirty box, scattering more litter.
- **Trim long paw fur:** Long-haired cats carry more litter between their toes.
- **Clean the mat regularly:** A full mat stops trapping and starts spreading litter.
- **Upgrade other essentials:** A fresh water source helps too; see our [stainless steel cat water fountain picks](/pets/best-stainless-steel-cat-water-fountain/).
