There is something extra special about Christmas with a senior dog. They may not tear through wrapping paper like they did as puppies, but they still notice when the family gathers, and they still deserve a gift under the tree. The trick is choosing something that fits the dog they are now: a little stiffer in the morning, a bit more sensitive to the cold, and maybe gentler with their chew toys.

That is the idea behind this guide to Christmas gifts for senior dogs. Instead of novelty squeakers that get ignored, we picked three well-reviewed gifts that make an older dog's everyday life more comfortable: a warm place to rest, an easier way to eat and drink, and a toy designed for older teeth and gums.

We checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above for delivery to New York. Holiday prices change often, so check the current price on Amazon before you order.

## How to choose a gift for a senior dog

- **Notice what has changed.** If your dog seeks out sunny patches and heating vents, choose warmth. If they splash water or stoop awkwardly at the bowl, choose the raised feeder. If they chew more gently than they used to, choose the KONG Senior.
- **Measure before you buy.** Check your dog's shoulder height for the feeder, and compare your dog's sleeping size with the 24 by 19 inch heated pad.
- **Think about where they rest.** The K&H bed is designed for sheltered spaces like garages and covered porches, so it is ideal for dogs who spend time out there.
- **Keep chewers in mind.** Skip the heated bed for any dog that chews bedding or cords.
- **Ask your vet about health issues.** Comfort gifts are wonderful, but they do not replace a vet's advice for arthritis, dental pain or eating problems.

## Buying tips

- Order bulky gifts like the feeder early in December, since holiday shipping slows down closer to Christmas.
- Unwrap the heated bed before Christmas Day so your dog can try it; many dogs need a few days to adopt a new bed.
- Set up a new feeder next to the old bowls at first, so an older dog can get used to it at their own pace.
- Include a gift receipt if you are buying for someone else's dog, since sizing can be tricky.
