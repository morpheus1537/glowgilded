## Winter dog care tips

- **Wipe paws after every walk.** Salt and de-icers can irritate pads; wipe them off, then moisturize.
- **Trim paw fur.** Snow and ice balls form on long fur between the toes.
- **Shorten very cold walks.** Several short walks beat one long one in freezing weather.
- **Watch for signs of cold.** Shivering, lifting paws or slowing down means it is time to go inside.
- **Keep grooming up.** A clean, brushed coat insulates better; see our [best dog toys and grooming essentials](/pets/best-dog-toys-and-grooming-essentials/).

## How to measure your dog for a coat

Measure the widest part of the chest just behind the front legs, then the neck and back length from collar to tail. Use chest girth as the main guide, and size up if your dog falls between two sizes, as both coat makers in this guide recommend.
