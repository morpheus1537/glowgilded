## How to choose a gift for a cat

- **Start with needs:** every cat needs a sturdy scratching post, so it is never a wasted gift.
- **Think about the view:** cats that sit at the window will use a perch daily.
- **Match the play style:** wrestlers love kickers like the catnip banana; hunters love moving toys like the flapping bird.
- **Rotate toys:** put some toys away and swap them weekly to keep them interesting.
