The holidays often mean a short trip, and for cat owners that brings the same question every year: will my cat be OK while I am gone? Many healthy adult cats handle a weekend alone better than dogs would, but only if three things are covered: food on schedule, plenty of fresh water and a way for you to check in. The right gear makes a two- or three-night trip much less stressful for both of you.

This guide covers five real Amazon US products for a cat-alone weekend: three automatic dry-food feeders for different households, a filtered water fountain and a pan-tilt camera with two-way audio. Our picks are based on listed features and practical fit, not hands-on testing, and they are not a replacement for a sitter on longer trips or for cats with health needs.

## Before you leave: a cat-alone weekend checklist

1. **Run the feeder for a week:** Let your cat get used to it and confirm the portions.
2. **Fresh batteries:** Install backup batteries in the feeder.
3. **Two water sources:** Fill the fountain and leave a backup bowl in another room.
4. **Extra litter boxes:** Scoop right before you go and consider adding a second box.
5. **Cat-proof the house:** Close doors to risky rooms, secure cords and put away holiday decorations a cat might chew.
6. **Ask a backup person:** Give a neighbor or friend a key in case anything goes wrong.
7. **Know your limit:** For longer trips, kittens, seniors or cats on medication, book a sitter.

## Buying tips

- **Pick capacity for your trip:** Make sure the feeder holds more food than your cat will eat while you are gone.
- **Check kibble size:** Each feeder lists a maximum kibble size.
- **Look for battery backup:** Power outages happen, especially in winter storms.
- **Check your WiFi:** Cameras need a reliable 2.4 GHz network in the room where your cat spends time.
- **Confirm the variant:** Colors and bundles vary, so check the listing before ordering.
