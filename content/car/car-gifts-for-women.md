## How to choose a car gift for her

- **Match her driving.** Commuters love organizers; road trippers love mounts and coolers.
- **Pick her style.** Bling for the glam lover; neutral patterns for the minimalist.
- **Check fit.** Measure the steering wheel, headrest rods and dashboard surface.
- **Bundle small gifts.** Hooks plus a trash can make a great stocking stuffer combo.
- **Add a clean car.** Pair a gift with supplies from our [car interior cleaning essentials](/car/car-interior-cleaning-essentials/) guide.
