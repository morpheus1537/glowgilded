Winter driving brings more of everything that makes a dash cam worth having: dark commutes, wet roads, crowded parking lots at the mall and long holiday road trips. A front and rear dash cam quietly records the road ahead and behind, so if someone rear-ends you, sideswipes you or disputes what happened, you have video instead of your word against theirs.

This guide compares three well-rated front and rear dash cams on Amazon US, including a three-channel model with a cabin camera, plus a simple front-only camera for drivers who want something more discreet. We checked every listing on November 11, 2026, with delivery set to New York ZIP 10001. Each was in stock with at least 4.3 stars and more than 1,700 ratings. We do not quote prices because they change often, so check the current price on Amazon. Our picks are based on listed features, not road testing.

## Best overall: ROVE R2-4K Dual Dash Cam

### Why we like it

With more than 15,000 ratings, the ROVE R2-4K Dual is one of the most popular front and rear dash cams on Amazon, and it includes nearly everything you need. The front camera records at 2.5K (QHD) and upscales to 4K for playback; ROVE is upfront that this is upscaled 4K, not native 4K. The rear camera records Full HD 1080P. The front uses a Sony STARVIS 2 IMX675 sensor with an F1.5 aperture and HDR for better low-light footage.

Built-in GPS shows live speed and compass on the 3-inch screen and stamps route, location and speed data onto your video. Dual-band 5.8GHz and 2.4GHz Wi-Fi lets you view and download clips on your phone at up to 20 MB per second through the ROVE app. The box includes a 128GB microSD card, a dual USB car charger, cables, mounts and a trim tool.

### Who it's for

Most drivers who want a complete, easy-to-install front and rear system with a screen.

### Pros

- 128GB card and car charger included.
- STARVIS 2 front sensor with HDR.
- Built-in GPS and fast 5.8GHz Wi-Fi.
- Huge number of ratings.

### Cons

- Front 4K is upscaled from 2.5K, as the listing states.
- Parking mode needs a hardwire kit, sold separately.

## Best video quality front and rear: VIOFO A229 Plus

### Why we like it

Many dual dash cams pair a sharp front camera with a weaker rear one. The VIOFO A229 Plus uses two Sony STARVIS 2 IMX675 sensors and records 1440P on both the front and rear, with HDR on both channels. VIOFO says STARVIS 2 offers 2.5 times the dynamic range and light sensitivity of the original STARVIS, for less noise and motion blur at night. The front camera can also record at up to 60 frames per second, though the listing notes 60fps is not available while HDR is on.

You also get 12 hands-free voice commands, a quad-mode GPS module, 5GHz Wi-Fi for fast downloads and three 24-hour parking modes, including buffered event detection that saves footage from before and after an event.

### Who it's for

Drivers who want the clearest rear footage and plan to use advanced parking modes.

### Pros

- 1440P with HDR on both cameras.
- Buffered parking event recording.
- Voice control and quad-mode GPS.

### Cons

## Best front-only option: galphi 4K Mini Dash Cam

### Why we like it

Not everyone needs a rear camera wired through the car. The galphi is a small, screen-free front dash cam that records 4K with a 160° wide-angle lens. Without a screen it is less distracting and less visible, and you view and download footage through the free app over 5GHz Wi-Fi, which galphi says reaches up to 8 MB per second.

Loop recording overwrites the oldest files when the card is full, and a G-sensor locks footage when it detects a sudden jolt. The listing says a 32GB card is now included.

## How to choose a dash cam

- **Front and rear vs front only:** A rear camera records rear-end collisions and parking bumps; front-only is simpler to install.
- **Sensor matters as much as resolution:** Three of the four cameras here use Sony STARVIS 2 sensors, which are popular for low-light recording.
- **Plan for parking mode:** If you want recording while parked, budget for a hardwire kit or professional installation.
- **Get a good memory card:** Use a high-endurance microSD card designed for constant recording if yours is not included.
- **Mount it correctly:** Place the camera behind the rearview mirror so it does not block your view, and route cables along the headliner.

## The bottom line

For most drivers, the **ROVE R2-4K Dual** is the best front and rear dash cam, with GPS, a screen, fast Wi-Fi and a 128GB card in the box. Choose the **VIOFO A229 Plus** for the sharpest rear video and buffered parking mode, the **REDTIGER F17** if you want a cabin camera, or the **galphi** for a discreet front-only setup.
