## How to choose a trunk organizer for groceries

1. **Size:** Measure your cargo area. Adjustable dividers let you use part or all of the organizer.
2. **Sturdiness:** Thick base and side panels keep compartments upright when loaded or empty.
3. **Staying put:** Tie-down straps, non-slip bottoms or hook-and-loop strips stop sliding.
4. **Cooler bag:** Insulated sections help with frozen food and summer heat.
5. **Pockets:** Mesh and side pockets hold cleaning supplies, reusable bags and emergency gear.
6. **Folding:** Collapsible designs free up space when you need the whole trunk.

## Tips for packing groceries in your SUV

- **Heavy items first:** Put jugs, cans and bottles in the bottom of a compartment against the back seat.
- **Cold together:** Group frozen and chilled items in the cooler bag and unload them first.
- **Fragile on top:** Keep eggs and bread in a separate compartment.
- **Bring reusable bags:** Store them in the mesh pockets so you never forget them.
- **Leave room:** Fold half the organizer away on light shopping days.
- **Keep kit handy:** A trunk organizer is also a tidy home for jumper cables and an ice scraper; see our [road trip essentials for kids](/car/road-trip-essentials-for-kids/) for more ideas.

## Measuring your cargo area

Before ordering, measure the width of your cargo floor between the wheel wells and the depth from the back seat to the tailgate. Compare those numbers with each organizer's listed dimensions, and remember adjustable or collapsible designs can be shortened. If you often fold the rear seats, an organizer with tie-down straps is easier to move.
