A car fills up with clutter quickly. Groceries roll around the trunk, receipts and wrappers pile up in door pockets, the phone slides off the seat at every turn, and the floor collects crumbs and pet hair. A little organization makes every drive calmer, and it does not take much: four simple accessories cover nearly all of it.

These car organization essentials are all highly rated by thousands of drivers, and we checked every rating and rating count on Amazon on the date above. We do not list prices, so use the buttons to check the current price.

## Best phone mount: Lamicall Car Vent Phone Mount

### Why we like it

A phone sliding around the console is distracting and unsafe. The Lamicall vent mount uses a 4-lock design that grips the vent firmly, and Lamicall says it fits most vertical and horizontal air vents. The clamps are adjustable for phones in thick cases, and silicone pads protect both the phone and the vent.

The mount rotates 360 degrees, so you can switch between portrait and landscape for navigation. Lamicall lists a working temperature range from -4 to 194 degrees Fahrenheit, which helps in hot summers and cold winters.

### Who it's for

Drivers who use their phone for navigation or music, rideshare and delivery drivers, and anyone tired of a phone sliding across the dashboard.

## A simple car organization routine

- **Weekly:** take out trash, return stray items to the house, and do a quick hand vacuum of floors and seats.
- **Monthly:** wipe the dashboard and console, check the trunk organizer and restock tissues and wipes.
- **Seasonally:** swap seasonal gear such as ice scrapers or sunshades, and check your emergency kit.

## What to keep in the car

Keep a small set of essentials: phone charger cable, tissues, wet wipes, a reusable shopping bag, a basic first aid kit, a flashlight, and jumper cables or a jump starter. Store them in the trunk organizer so they are always in the same place.

## The bottom line

If you only buy one, start with the FORTEM trunk organizer. It makes the biggest difference to day-to-day clutter. Add the EPAuto trash can to keep the cabin tidy, the Lamicall mount for safer navigation, and the Bissell hand vacuum for quick, regular clean-ups.
