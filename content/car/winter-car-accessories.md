Winter is hard on cars and drivers. Snow piles on the windshield, ice glues the wipers down, the seats feel like ice, and every boot drags in slush and salt. A few simple winter car accessories make those cold mornings faster and a lot more comfortable.

We picked four that cover the most common winter headaches: a windshield cover, a de-icer spray, a heated blanket and all-weather floor mats. Each one has 4.3 stars or more on Amazon, and we checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We don't list prices, so check the current price on Amazon before you buy.

## Best for snow and frost: EcoNour Windshield Cover for Ice and Snow

### Why we like it

The easiest way to avoid scraping your windshield is to cover it the night before. EcoNour's cover sits over the glass so snow, frost and ice collect on it instead. In the morning you peel it off, shake it out and drive. EcoNour says it also helps against hail, rain and autumn leaves, so it's useful outside winter too.

It's made of tough, waterproof 600D Oxford fabric with a PVC insulated bottom layer. Dual mount straps and side flaps hold it in place even in strong wind. It measures 64 x 42 inches and fits compact cars, small sedans and small SUVs. With more than 11,000 ratings, it's one of the most popular windshield covers on Amazon.

### Who it's for

Anyone who parks outside overnight in snowy or frosty weather.

- Waterproof 600D Oxford fabric
- Straps and side flaps hold it in wind
- Useful year-round
- 4.5 stars from more than 11,000 ratings

### Cons

## Winter car checklist

- **Visibility:** a windshield cover, de-icer and a good scraper.
- **Warmth:** a heated blanket, gloves and a hat in the car.
- **Protection:** all-weather mats and a trunk liner for salt and slush.
- **Safety:** an emergency kit, jump starter, phone charger and flashlight.

## Buying tips

- **Measure first:** check your windshield size and floor dimensions before buying.
- **Store de-icer in the house or garage:** keep a bottle where you can reach it before you get to the car.
- **Don't drain the battery:** use 12V accessories only with the engine running.
- **Shop before the first storm:** winter car products sell out fast once snow is forecast.
- **Check the current price on Amazon:** prices change often in winter.
