## How to choose a car trash can

1. **Capacity:** Around 2 gallons suits most family cars. Small cup-holder bins handle wrappers and receipts.
2. **Lid style:** Flip lids, push-open lids and hinged covers keep trash hidden and help contain odors.
3. **Leakproof lining:** A waterproof liner handles sticky, wet trash without soaking through.
4. **Mounting:** Hanging straps work on headrests and consoles. Weighted floor bins stay put without straps.
5. **Shape retention:** Stiff panels keep a fabric bin from collapsing when you toss trash in.
6. **Extra storage:** Mesh pockets hold tissues, wipes and spare bags.

## Tips for keeping your car trash-free

- **Empty at every fill-up:** Make it a habit to empty the bin when you get gas.
- **Line it:** Even leakproof bins are easier to empty with a bag or liner.
- **Keep wipes nearby:** Store wipes in a mesh pocket for sticky spills.
- **Use two bins on road trips:** A hanging bin for the back seat plus a cup-holder bin up front keeps everyone tidy. See our [road trip essentials for kids](/car/road-trip-essentials-for-kids/).
- **Do a monthly clean-out:** Pair the bin with a quick vacuum to keep crumbs under control.
