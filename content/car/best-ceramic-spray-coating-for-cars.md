Keeping a car glossy through winter salt, rain and road grime is hard work. Ceramic spray coatings make it easier. You spray them on a clean car, wipe and buff, and they leave a slick, hydrophobic layer that makes water bead and roll off and helps dirt rinse away. They take minutes, not a full weekend, and do not require professional tools.

## How to choose a ceramic spray coating

1. **Stand-alone or topper:** Some sprays are the main protection. Others boost an existing wax or coating.
2. **Surfaces:** Check whether it is safe on glass, trim, wheels and plastic.
3. **Application:** Spray and wipe is easiest. Some products also work as a waterless wash.
4. **Coverage:** Bigger bottles cover more cars and more coats.
5. **Prep:** Every product works best on a clean, dry, decontaminated car.
6. **Microfiber towels:** Use clean, soft towels to avoid streaks and scratches.

## How to apply a ceramic spray coating

- **Wash thoroughly:** Remove dirt, salt and old residue first.
- **Decontaminate:** A clay bar removes bonded grime so the coating can bond.
- **Dry completely:** Unless the label says it works on wet paint, start with a dry car.
- **Work panel by panel:** Spray, spread, then buff before moving on.
- **Use two towels:** One to spread, one clean towel to buff.
- **Let it cure:** Keep the car dry for the time on the label.
- **Reapply when beading fades:** Water that sheets instead of beading means it is time.
