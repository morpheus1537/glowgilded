## How to choose a windshield cover

1. **Size:** Measure your windshield. Truck and SUV covers are larger than standard car covers.
2. **Material:** 600D Oxford fabric and multi-layer covers are thicker and more durable.
3. **How it attaches:** Door flaps, mirror straps, wheel hooks and magnets each help it stay put.
4. **Scratch protection:** Soft inner linings help protect glass and paint.
5. **Mirror covers:** Some include separate covers for side mirrors.
6. **Storage:** A storage pouch keeps a wet, snowy cover from soaking your trunk.

## Tips for using a windshield cover

- **Lift wipers or tuck them under:** Follow your cover's design so wipers do not freeze to the glass.
- **Install before the storm:** Put it on the night before snow or freezing rain.
- **Shake, then remove:** Brush off heavy snow before pulling the cover away.
- **Keep the hood clean:** Wipe grit away before laying a magnetic cover on paint.
- **Store it dry:** Let it dry before packing it away to avoid mildew.
- **Keep a scraper handy:** You will still need one for side windows; see our [winter car accessories](/car/winter-car-accessories/).
