## How to choose a digital tire pressure gauge

1. **Pressure range:** Cars need far less than 100 PSI; trucks and RVs may need 200 PSI or more.
2. **Accuracy:** Look for a listed standard such as ANSI B40.7 Grade 2A.
3. **Readability:** A backlit display and light help at night.
4. **Chuck and hose:** A swivel chuck or hose reaches awkward valve stems.
5. **Power:** Replaceable AAA batteries are easy to find.
6. **Extras:** Some gauges add an inflator for use with an air compressor, or a bleed valve.

## How to check tire pressure in cold weather

- **Find the right number:** Use the PSI on the sticker inside the driver's door or in the owner's manual.
- **Check cold tires:** Measure before driving or after the car sits for a few hours.
- **Press firmly:** Seat the gauge squarely on the valve to avoid hissing and false readings.
- **Check all four, plus the spare:** Spares are easy to forget.
- **Top up as needed:** A portable inflator makes adding air easy at home.
- **Recheck after a cold snap:** Big temperature drops can lower pressure overnight.
