## How to choose a backseat organizer for kids

1. **Pockets:** Count the pockets and look for a mix of large mesh pockets, bottle holders and a book pocket.
2. **Tablet holder:** A clear, touchable window lets kids watch or play without holding the tablet. Check the size it fits.
3. **Kick protection:** A full-coverage panel protects the seat back from shoes and spills.
4. **Tray:** A fold-down tray is great for snacks and drawing, but adds a little bulk.
5. **Fabric:** Thicker fabric, like 600D or 1680D Oxford, resists sagging and tearing.
6. **Cleaning:** Look for waterproof, wipe-clean or machine-washable fabric.
7. **Pack size:** A 2-pack covers both front seats.

## Backseat organization tips

- **Pack light:** Stick to soft items like books, wipes, snacks and plush toys.
- **Restock before trips:** Keep wipes, tissues and a spare change of clothes in the pockets.
- **Give each kid a side:** Fewer arguments when everyone has their own pockets.
- **Empty weekly:** Clear out wrappers and crumbs so the organizer stays fresh.
- **Pair with a trash can:** A car trash bin keeps wrappers out of the pockets.
