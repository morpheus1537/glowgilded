## Holiday road trip tips for dogs

- **Practice short drives:** Take a few short trips before the big drive so your dog gets used to the seat cover and belt.
- **Use a harness:** Attach seat belts to a well-fitted harness, never a collar.
- **Plan stops:** Stop every few hours for water, a bathroom break and a short walk.
- **Pack a dog bag:** Bring food, water, a bowl, waste bags, a towel for wet paws and a familiar blanket.
- **Never leave your dog alone in the car:** Cars get dangerously cold or hot quickly.
- **Keep ID current:** Make sure tags and microchip details are up to date before you travel.

## Buying tips

- **Measure your back seat:** Especially for hard-bottom covers.
- **Check seat type:** Bench seats fit most covers; captain's chairs may not.
- **Check buckle compatibility:** Seat belt tethers vary in tab size.
- **Choose waterproof:** Winter road trips bring snow and mud.
- **Check the current listing:** Colors and sizes vary, and ratings may cover several versions.
