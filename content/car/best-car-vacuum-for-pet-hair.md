## How to choose a car vacuum for pet hair

1. **Cordless or corded:** Cordless is quick and convenient; corded shop vacs have more power for deep cleans.
2. **Attachments:** A crevice tool for seams and a brush for loosening hair matter most.
3. **Runtime:** Look for listed minutes, and note that boost modes run shorter.
4. **Filter:** Washable filters save money; HEPA filters trap fine dust.
5. **Emptying:** A clear bin and one-touch emptying keep hair off your hands.
6. **Weight:** Lighter vacs are easier to hold while reaching under seats.

## Tips for removing pet hair from your car

- **Loosen first:** A rubber brush or the pet brush attachment pulls hair from fabric fibers.
- **Work top to bottom:** Seats first, then carpets, so hair does not fall onto clean areas.
- **Use the crevice tool on seams:** Hair collects where cushions meet.
- **Empty and rinse filters:** A clogged filter cuts suction fast.
- **Protect the seats:** A cover or hammock catches most hair; leather owners can also try a [seat protector for leather seats](/car/best-car-seat-protector-for-leather-seats/).
- **Keep a vac in the trunk:** Quick weekly cleanups beat one big monthly job.
