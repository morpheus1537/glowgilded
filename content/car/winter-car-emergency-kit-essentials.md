Winter is when cars let you down. Batteries that were fine in October struggle on a frozen morning, a surprise storm leaves the windshield under an inch of ice, and a minor slide into a ditch can mean an hour or more waiting for a tow in the cold. You cannot prevent all of that, but you can make sure your trunk holds the few things that turn a bad situation into a manageable one.

This winter car emergency kit covers the four essentials: a jump starter, a snow brush and ice scraper, a roadside safety kit and emergency blankets. Each one is highly rated by thousands of drivers, and we checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We do not list prices, so use the buttons to check the current price. Buy them before the first storm, when stock and shipping times are at their best.

The NOCO Boost GB40 delivers 1000 amps of peak power and, according to the listing, up to 20 starts per charge on engines up to 6.0L gas and 3.0L diesel. It is built to be foolproof. NOCO's UltraSafe design has spark-proof and reverse polarity protection, so connecting the clamps the wrong way will not cause sparks. It also doubles as a power bank for phones and tablets and has a 100-lumen LED flashlight with seven modes, including SOS and strobe. At 2.5 pounds it fits in a glove box, and the housing is IP65-rated against water and dust. With more than 130,000 ratings, it is one of the most reviewed car products on Amazon.

Every driver, but especially anyone with an older battery, anyone who drives alone, and anyone who parks outside in cold weather.

## What else to keep in your car this winter

The four picks above cover the essentials. Round out your kit with a few things you probably already own:

- **Warm layers:** a spare hat, gloves and a fleece or old coat.
- **Water and snacks:** a few bottles of water and calorie-dense snacks such as granola bars.
- **A phone charger:** a car charger, or use the NOCO's power bank.
- **Traction help:** a bag of cat litter or sand for tires stuck on ice, and a small folding shovel.
- **Organization:** a trunk organizer keeps it all in one place; see our [car organization essentials](/car/car-organization-essentials/).

## Buying tips

- **Check engine size:** jump starters are rated for engine size. The GB40 covers up to 6.0L gas and 3.0L diesel.
- **Charge before you need it:** put a reminder on your calendar to top up the jump starter every few months.
- **Keep the kit where you can reach it:** in a crash or a ditch, the trunk may be hard to open. Keep blankets and the jump starter in the cabin if you can.
- **Plan for road trips:** heading out for the holidays? Our [carry-on packing essentials](/travel/carry-on-packing-essentials/) help with the rest of the trip.

## The bottom line

If you buy only one thing, make it the NOCO Boost GB40, because a dead battery is the breakdown you are most likely to face in winter, and it lets you fix it yourself. Add the AstroAI snow brush for clear windows, the Everlit kit for a complete set of roadside tools, and the Mylar blanket 4-pack for warmth while you wait. Together they make a winter car emergency kit you will be glad to have the first time the weather turns.
