## How to choose a luggage scale

1. **Capacity:** Most digital scales here weigh up to 110 lbs (50 kg), well over typical checked bag limits.
2. **Power:** Battery, battery-free pump or manual.
3. **Display:** A large or backlit screen is easier to read in a dim hotel room.
4. **Hold or lock:** Auto weight lock keeps the reading after you lower the bag.
5. **Grip and hook:** A comfortable handle and a strong strap or hook make lifting easier.
6. **Size:** Smaller scales tuck into a pocket for the return trip.

## Tips to avoid overweight bag fees

- **Weigh at home and before flying home:** Souvenirs and gifts add up fast.
- **Leave a buffer:** Aim a pound or two under the limit in case scales differ.
- **Rebalance between bags:** Move heavy items like shoes and toiletries to the lighter bag.
- **Wear your heaviest layers:** Boots and coats on the plane save suitcase weight.
- **Use compression:** Packing cubes keep weight evenly spread and easy to shift.
