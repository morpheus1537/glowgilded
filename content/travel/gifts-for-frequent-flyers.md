Frequent flyers are tricky to shop for. They already own a good suitcase and probably a neck pillow, and they have strong opinions about what is worth carrying. The best gifts for people who fly a lot are small, packable things that fix one of the everyday annoyances of air travel: holding a phone for two hours, paying for airline earbuds, bright cabin lights, and a freezing cabin.

These four travel gifts each solve one of those problems, and each is highly rated by thousands of travelers. We checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We do not list prices, so use the buttons to check the current price. All four fit in a carry-on or a stocking.

## Best for in-flight movies: Perilogics Universal Airplane Phone Holder Mount

### Why we like it

Seatback screens are disappearing on many flights, so most people now watch downloaded shows on their phone. Holding it for a whole flight is tiring, and propping it against a cup never works. The Perilogics holder clamps to the edge of the tray table and holds the phone at eye level, in portrait or landscape, with two 360° rotating joints.

The listing says it fits phones from 2.17 to 3.82 inches wide and up to 0.43 inches thick with a case, and the clamp opens to 1.6 inches. Perilogics is upfront about the limits: it needs a firm, flat edge, extra-thick rugged cases may reduce grip, and severe turbulence can affect stability. It also works on a desk, a kitchen counter or a luggage handle, and folds flat for packing. With more than 22,000 ratings, it is the most popular airplane phone holder on Amazon.

### Who it's for

Anyone who watches downloaded shows on flights, and travelers who take video calls from hotel desks.

### Pros

- Clamps to tray tables, desks and luggage handles
- Dual 360° joints for portrait or landscape
- Fits most phones with cases
- Compact and foldable

### Cons

- Needs a firm, flat edge; rounded or padded surfaces will not work
- Very thick rugged or wallet cases may not grip securely

## Best for in-flight audio: Twelve South AirFly SE Bluetooth Audio Transmitter

### Why we like it

Here is a problem every frequent flyer with AirPods knows: the seatback screen has a headphone jack, and their earbuds do not. The Twelve South AirFly SE plugs into any 3.5mm audio jack and sends the sound to AirPods, wireless earbuds or Bluetooth headphones. No more airline earbuds or tangled cords.

The listing says the battery lasts over 20 hours on a single charge, enough for long-haul flights, and it can be used while charging with the included USB-C cable. It has built-in volume buttons. It also works with gym equipment and gaming devices that have a headphone jack, so it is useful on the ground too.

### Who it's for

Frequent flyers who use AirPods or wireless headphones, especially on airlines with seatback entertainment.

### Pros

- Connects wireless earbuds to any 3.5mm jack
- Over 20 hours of battery, usable while charging
- Small and light
- Works with gym machines and gaming devices too

### Cons

- Rated 4.3 stars, the lowest of our four picks
- Only useful where there is a headphone jack

## Best for sleeping on planes: MyHalos 3D Blackout Sleep Mask

### Why we like it

Cabin lights, window shades and the glow of a neighbor's screen make sleeping on a plane hard. A flat sleep mask presses on the eyes and lets light leak in around the nose. The MyHalos mask has a contoured 3D design with 16 mm deep eye cups that do not touch the eyelids or lashes, and an improved 23 mm nose height that the listing says blocks 100% of light.

It is designed with side sleepers in mind, with an angled shape the listing says reduces pressure on the temples, and an adjustable strap. The fabric is soft, breathable and hypoallergenic, it is fully washable, and it comes with a travel pouch. More than 26,000 ratings back it up.

### Who it's for

Red-eye travelers, people with lash extensions, light sleepers and anyone who naps in hotels or on trains.

### Pros

- Contoured cups that do not press on eyes or lashes
- Designed to block 100% of light
- Comfortable for side sleepers
- Washable, with a travel pouch

### Cons

- Bulkier than a flat silk mask
- Deep cups may not suit every face shape

## How to choose the right gift

- **Think about their routine:** someone who watches shows on every flight will love the phone holder; someone who sleeps through flights will get more from the mask.
- **Check their headphones:** the AirFly is only useful if they use wireless earbuds or headphones.
- **Small is better:** frequent flyers pack light. Everything here fits in a carry-on, and three of the four fit in a coat pocket.
- **Combine two:** the sleep mask and blanket make a nice "sleep on the plane" bundle, and the phone holder and AirFly make an "entertainment" bundle.
