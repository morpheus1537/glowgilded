Holiday season means holiday parties, office events and, for many people, a December wedding in another city. Folding a suit or cocktail dress into a roller bag almost guarantees creases you will be steaming in the hotel bathroom. A carry-on garment bag keeps your outfit hanging, protected and with you in the cabin, so it arrives ready to wear.

This guide compares five well-rated garment bags on Amazon US, from a structured business carry-on to a convertible duffel and a simple long cover for dresses and coats. We checked every listing on November 11, 2026, with delivery set to New York ZIP 10001. Each was in stock with at least 4.4 stars and thousands of ratings. We do not quote prices because they change often, so check the current price on Amazon. Our picks are based on listed features and dimensions, not hands-on testing. Airline size limits vary, so always check your carrier's rules before flying.

## How to pack a garment bag

1. **Use good hangers.** Sturdy, shaped hangers keep jacket shoulders in shape.
2. **Button and zip.** Button jackets and zip dresses before hanging.
3. **Layer smart.** Put the heaviest item at the back and lighter shirts in front.
4. **Use the straps.** Interior straps stop garments sliding to the bottom.
5. **Fill pockets with soft items.** Socks, ties and belts in side pockets keep the bag balanced.
6. **Hang it as soon as you arrive.** Light wrinkles often fall out within an hour.
