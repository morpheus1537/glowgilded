## How to choose an anti-theft travel bag

1. **Slash resistance:** Look for slash-resistant body panels and straps with steel cables or mesh.
2. **Locking zippers:** Zipper pulls that clip or lock make it much harder for someone to open your bag in a crowd.
3. **Lock-down strap:** A strap that clips around a chair or table leg stops grab-and-run thefts at cafes.
4. **RFID blocking:** RFID-blocking slots help protect contactless cards and passports from skimming.
5. **Size and weight:** Carry only what you need. A small, light bag is more comfortable on long sightseeing days.
6. **Style:** A bag that looks like an everyday purse or sling draws less attention than a bulky travel bag.

## Tips for staying safe from pickpockets

- **Wear it in front:** In crowds, slide your crossbody to the front where you can see it and keep a hand on it.
- **Lock your zippers:** Use the locking pulls every time, not just in busy places.
- **Clip it at cafes:** Wrap the lock-down strap around your chair leg instead of hanging the bag on the back.
- **Split your money:** Keep a backup card and some cash in a separate place, like a hotel safe.
