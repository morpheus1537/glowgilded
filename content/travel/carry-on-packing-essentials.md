## Carry-on packing checklist

- **Clothes:** roll clothes into packing cubes and plan outfits that mix and match.
- **Toiletries:** use travel-size bottles and keep liquids where you can reach them at security.
- **Tech:** pack the adapter, cables and a power bank in an easy-access pocket.
- **Documents:** passport, ID, boarding pass, travel insurance details and copies stored separately.
- **Comfort:** neck pillow, eye mask, earplugs, a light layer and a refillable water bottle.
- **Medication:** keep essential medicine in your carry-on, never in checked bags.

## Packing tips that save time

Check your airline's carry-on size and weight limits before you pack, since they vary. Put the items you need at security on top. Wear your bulkiest shoes and jacket on the plane. And leave a little space for anything you bring home.
