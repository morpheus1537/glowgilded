## How to choose a family passport holder

1. **Passport capacity:** Count your travelers and add one slot for spare documents.
2. **Pockets:** Boarding pass sleeves, card slots, cash pockets and a pen holder save time.
3. **Closure:** A zipper keeps everything inside if the holder is dropped or turned upside down.
4. **RFID blocking:** Helps prevent unauthorized scans of compatible cards.
5. **Material:** Waterproof or water-resistant fabric helps with spills and rain.
6. **Carry style:** Wristlet straps and slim profiles make it easier to keep in hand or in a bag.

## Family passport tips for travel days

- **Check expiration dates now:** Many countries require passports to be valid for several months beyond your trip, and children's passports often expire sooner. Check official government guidance.
- **Make copies:** Save photos of every passport page with ID details in a secure place, separate from the originals.
- **One handler:** Choose one adult to carry the family organizer at the airport to avoid mix-ups.
- **Pack a pen:** Some countries still use paper arrival forms.
- **Keep it on you:** Never pack passports in checked bags.
- **Bring the right adapter:** See our [best universal travel adapter for international travel](/travel/best-universal-travel-adapter-for-international-travel/).

## Family organizer vs individual holders

A family organizer is easiest with babies and young kids because one parent can present every passport and boarding pass at once. Individual holders spread the risk, so a lost bag does not mean losing every passport, and teens like having their own. Many families use both: an organizer for airport days and single holders for day trips once you arrive.
