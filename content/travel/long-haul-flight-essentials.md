Ten, twelve or fifteen hours in an economy seat leaves most people with a stiff neck, heavy legs and almost no real sleep, and you arrive already tired before the trip begins. The good news is that a few small items make a huge difference. You cannot add legroom, but you can support your neck, help your legs, block the cabin lights and keep your seat organized.

These four long haul flight essentials are the ones we would pack for any overnight flight. ## How to build a long haul flight kit

- **Prioritize sleep:** on overnight flights, the neck pillow and sleep mask matter most. Pack them where you can reach them before takeoff.
- **Put socks on before boarding:** compression socks are easiest to pull on at home or in the airport, not in a cramped seat.
- **Set up your seat early:** slip the tray cover on as soon as you sit down so everything has a place.
- **Dress in layers:** cabins get cold on long flights; a light sweater or a packable blanket helps you sleep.
- **Hydrate:** bring an empty water bottle to fill after security, and go easy on alcohol and caffeine.
