## What to look for in travel bottles

1. **Seal design:** Look for multi-layer lids, valves or locking caps. These matter more than the bottle itself.
2. **TSA-friendly size:** Containers of 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less, fitting in a single quart-size bag.
3. **Soft silicone:** Squeezable bottles make it easier to use thick products.
4. **Wide openings:** Easier to fill without a funnel and easier to clean.
5. **Extras:** Some kits add a toiletry bag, jars, spray bottles and labels.

## Packing tips for leak-free toiletries

- **Fill to about 80%:** Leave room for expansion during pressure changes.
- **Burp the bottle:** Squeeze out a little air before closing the cap.
- **Bag everything:** Keep bottles in a zippered bag, even if they are leak-proof.
- **Label clearly:** Shampoo and conditioner look identical in matching bottles.
- **Pack upright:** Stand bottles up in your bag's toiletry pocket where possible.
- **Choose solids where you can:** Solid shampoo and stick deodorant do not count toward your liquids.
