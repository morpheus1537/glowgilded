Holiday travel means hotel bathrooms with a sliver of counter space, shared guest bathrooms at the in-laws, and early flights where you need to find your toothbrush without unpacking everything. A hanging toiletry bag solves all three. It hooks onto a towel bar or door, unfolds to show every item at a glance, and keeps bottles upright and off a wet counter.

## What to look for

1. **A sturdy hook:** Metal hooks that swivel make hanging easier on odd towel bars and doors.
2. **Compartments:** Separate zipped and elastic pockets keep bottles upright and small items from getting lost.
3. **Size:** Match it to your bottles; full-size shampoo needs a bigger bag.
4. **Water resistance:** Wipe-clean linings or PVC pockets make spills less of a disaster.
5. **TSA liquids:** A removable clear bag speeds up airport security.

## Packing tips

- **Decant into small bottles:** Saves weight and keeps carry-on liquids under TSA limits.
- **Keep liquids together:** Pack them in one clear bag for quick screening.
- **Pack a spare toothbrush:** Easy to forget on early-morning departures.
- **Leave it packed:** Keep a stocked bag ready so holiday trips start faster.
