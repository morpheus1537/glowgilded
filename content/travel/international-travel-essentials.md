A few small, inexpensive items take most of those worries away, and all of them fit easily in a carry-on.

These five international travel essentials are the ones we would pack for any trip abroad. Drop an AirTag in each checked bag and you can see its location in the Find My app. According to the listing, the 2nd generation adds a 50% louder speaker, upgraded Ultra Wideband and Bluetooth chips to find items from farther away, and Precision Finding on both iPhone and Apple Watch.

The most useful travel feature is location sharing: the listing says you can share an AirTag's location temporarily and securely with trusted contacts or over 50 airline partners if you lose something. The battery lasts more than a year and is replaceable. A 4-pack covers checked bags, a carry-on and your keys.

iPhone users who check bags, families traveling with several suitcases, and anyone with tight connections.

## Best luggage locks: SURE LOCK TSA Approved Cable Luggage Locks

### Why we like it

If your trip passes through a US airport, TSA approved locks let security agents inspect your bag without cutting the lock. These SURE LOCK locks add an Open Alert indicator: a red button appears if TSA opened the lock, and stays visible until you unlock it with your code. You set your own 3-digit combination, and the flexible braided steel cable fits zipper pulls that rigid shackles can struggle with. The listing also highlights large, easy-to-read dials, which helps in a dim hotel room.

### Who it's for

Anyone checking a bag, especially on trips that connect through the United States.

- Resettable 3-digit combination

- Large, easy-to-read dials

## International travel checklist

- **Documents:** passport (check its expiration date), visas, printed and digital copies, travel insurance details.
- **Money:** two cards stored separately, some local cash, and a hidden neck wallet.
- **Tech:** phone, charger, a [portable charger](/tech/best-portable-charger-for-iphone/) and a travel adapter for your destination.
- **Bags:** luggage trackers, TSA locks and a luggage scale.
- **Comfort:** for overnight flights, see our [long haul flight essentials](/travel/long-haul-flight-essentials/).

## Buying tips

- **Check the plug type.** Outlets vary by country, so confirm your destination's plug before you go.
- **Match trackers to your phone.** AirTags need an iPhone; choose another tracker for Android.
- **Weigh before you leave and before you return.** Souvenirs add up fast.
- **Set lock codes at home.** Choose a combination you will remember after a long flight.
- **Test the door lock at home.** Practice once so it is quick in a hotel.
