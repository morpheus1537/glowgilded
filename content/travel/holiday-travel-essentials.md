We picked four packing helpers that solve the biggest holiday travel problems: overstuffed suitcases, no room for gifts, leaky toiletries and wrinkled outfits. ## Holiday packing checklist

- **Clothes:** layers, pajamas, one dressy outfit and comfy travel clothes.
- **Toiletries:** refillable bottles in a clear bag for the carry-on.
- **Tech:** chargers, a power bank and headphones.
- **Gifts:** unwrapped or in gift bags, plus a foldable duffel for the trip home.
- **Documents:** ID, boarding passes and any medications in your carry-on.

## Buying tips

- **Pack gifts unwrapped:** security may need to inspect them.
- **Leave room:** you'll come home with more than you packed.
- **Check airline rules:** baggage limits and fees vary, especially for extra bags.
- **Fly early:** the first flights of the day are less likely to be delayed during busy holiday weeks.
- **Check the current price on Amazon:** travel gear goes on sale often around the holidays.
