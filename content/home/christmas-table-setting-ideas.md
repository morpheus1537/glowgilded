It needs a few good layers: something rich running down the middle, a clean base at each seat, a festive detail on the napkins and soft light from candles. Get those four things right and even an everyday dinner set looks like a holiday spread.

## How to layer a Christmas table

Think of the table in layers, from the bottom up:

1. **Base:** a tablecloth if you have one, then the runner down the center.
2. **Place settings:** a placemat at each seat, then the dinner plate. A salad plate or bowl on top adds height.
3. **Cutlery and glasses:** forks on the left, knife and spoon on the right, glasses above the knife.
4. **Napkins:** fold the napkin, slip it through a napkin ring and set it on the plate or to the left of the forks.
5. **Centerpiece:** greenery, pinecones or ornaments down the runner, with candlesticks spaced evenly along it.

## Christmas table setting tips

- **Keep it low.** Centerpieces taller than about 12 inches block conversation; tapers in short holders are the exception because they are thin.
- **Repeat one metal.** Pick gold or silver and repeat it in napkin rings, candlesticks and cutlery.
- **Use real greenery sparingly.** A few cut branches from the tree lot or yard smell wonderful and cost little. Keep them away from real flames.
- **Set the table early.** Lay everything out the day before so you're not rushing while the turkey rests.
