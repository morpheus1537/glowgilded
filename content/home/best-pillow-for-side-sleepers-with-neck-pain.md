If you sleep on your side and wake up with a stiff, sore neck, your pillow is one of the first things to check. A pillow that is too flat lets your head drop toward the mattress, and one that is too tall pushes it up. Either way, your neck spends the night bent. The fix is usually a pillow with enough height and support to fill the space between your ear and your shoulder.

## How to choose a pillow for side sleeping and neck pain

1. **Loft (height):** Side sleepers need more height than back or stomach sleepers. Broad shoulders need even more.
2. **Adjustability:** Removable fill or foam layers let you fine-tune the height instead of guessing.
3. **Fill type:** Shredded memory foam molds and moves; solid contour foam holds a fixed shape.
4. **Shape:** Standard rectangles are versatile; contour and curved shapes cradle the neck and shoulder.
5. **Cover and care:** Look for breathable, removable, washable covers.
6. **Your mattress:** A soft mattress lets your shoulder sink, so you may need less loft than on a firm one.

## How to set up your pillow for side sleeping

- **Start high, then remove fill:** With adjustable pillows, start with all the fill, lie on your side and remove a handful at a time until your nose lines up with the center of your chest.
- **Check your neck line:** Ask someone to look at you from behind, or use a mirror. Your neck should look level, not tilted up or down.
- **Fluff daily:** Shredded fill packs down overnight; a quick shake keeps the loft even.
- **Give it time:** A new pillow height can feel strange for a week or two.
- **Mind the mattress:** If you switch to a softer mattress topper, re-adjust your pillow height.
- **Cover care:** Follow each brand's care instructions; removable covers make weekly washing easy.

## When to see a professional

A pillow can make sleep more comfortable, but it cannot diagnose or treat a condition. If neck pain comes with numbness, tingling, headaches or weakness, or if it lasts more than a few weeks, see a doctor or physical therapist. They can also suggest the best sleeping position for your situation.
