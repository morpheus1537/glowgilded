## Buying tips

### Compare mist type, tank access and placement

Start by checking whether the room actually needs added humidity. Condensation, damp surfaces or a musty smell are reasons to reassess operation rather than increase output. Keep the machine on a suitable stable surface and follow its manual for clearance, cleaning and water choice. A larger reservoir may mean fewer refills at a comparable setting, but it also means more water to handle. Do not compare advertised maximum runtimes as if they were measured under identical conditions.

### Prioritize the decision that matters in this setting

For a guest bedroom that needs an uncomplicated winter setup, make a short list of what must be true before a purchase is useful. Write down the relevant space, user preferences and any compatibility information before opening another listing. That small preparation step makes it easier to reject an attractive product that does not solve the actual problem.

### Compare total effort, not just the headline feature

Check what needs cleaning, charging, assembly, replacement or storage. Look at the selected variant and included components rather than assuming that every item visible in a listing photograph is supplied. A more elaborate option is only worthwhile when you want its extra function and can accommodate the routine that comes with it.
