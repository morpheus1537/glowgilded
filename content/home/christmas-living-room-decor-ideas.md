The living room is where Christmas actually happens: the tree, the gifts, the movie nights and the people who come by with cookies. But you do not need a truckload of decorations to make it feel festive. The best Christmas living room decor ideas change the room through a few things you see every day, like the sofa, the space under the tree and the glow on a side table.

This guide is built around three easy, top-rated pieces that work together: a pair of red corduroy pillow covers for the sofa, a white faux fur tree skirt, and a small pre-lit fir tree for a side table or console. Each one has thousands of ratings on Amazon US. ## Best for the sofa: MIULEE Soft Christmas Throw Pillow Covers, Christmas Red (Set of 2)

### Why we like it

Swapping pillow covers is the cheapest and fastest way to make a living room look like the holidays, and this MIULEE pair is one of the most-reviewed options on Amazon, with more than 14,000 ratings. The covers are a rich Christmas red in a ribbed corduroy texture with vertical stripes, which catches light and looks warmer than flat cotton. They fit standard 18 x 18 inch inserts and close with an invisible zipper, so the front looks clean and the inserts slide in and out easily.

Because they are a solid red rather than a print of trees or reindeer, they look festive in December but are not so themed that you must pack them away the day after New Year. Care is simple: MIULEE says to machine wash in cold water on gentle, separately, and tumble dry low, with no bleach.

### Who it's for

Anyone with a neutral sofa in cream, gray, beige or white who wants holiday color in two minutes. They also make an easy gift for a new homeowner or a friend who loves to decorate, since almost every couch already has 18 inch pillows waiting for a new look.

### Pros

- Rich red corduroy texture looks warm and expensive
- Hidden zipper for a clean front and easy insert changes
- Machine washable, per the listing
- Solid color works all winter, not just Christmas week

### Cons

- Covers only; you need your own 18 x 18 inch inserts
- Polyester corduroy can collect pet hair and lint
- Bright red may clash with a sofa that already has strong color

## How to style a cozy Christmas living room

### Start with the sofa

Most people look at the sofa first when they walk into a living room, so that is where holiday color pays off most. Put the red corduroy covers on your two corner pillows and keep any center pillows neutral, in cream, oatmeal or a soft knit. Add a throw over one arm in cream or plaid; our [heated throw blankets for winter](/home/heated-throw-blankets-for-winter/) guide has options that also keep you warm.

### Finish the base of the tree

A tree looks taller and fuller when the base is covered. Lay the faux fur skirt after the tree is up and the lights are on, fluff it with your hands, then arrange wrapped gifts in two or three loose groups instead of a ring. Gold and kraft paper wrap looks especially good on white fur.

### Repeat the glow

Lighting is what makes a room feel cozy at night. Put the mini tree on a side table or console across the room from the main tree so the light is spread out, and add [battery operated window candles with timers](/home/battery-operated-window-candles-with-timer/) on the windowsills. Turn off the overhead lights in the evening and let the trees and candles do the work.

### Keep one color story

Pick two main colors and one metal: for example red and white with gold, or green and cream with brass. The three picks here fit a classic red, white and gold scheme. For a softer, more natural look, see our [farmhouse Christmas decor ideas](/home/farmhouse-christmas-decor-ideas/) and [cozy Christmas decor ideas](/home/cozy-christmas-decor-ideas/).

## Buying tips

- **Measure your pillows first.** These covers are 18 x 18 inches. If your inserts are 20 or 22 inches, they will be overstuffed; if they are 16 inches, the covers will look floppy.
- **Match the skirt to the tree.** A 48 inch skirt suits most 6 to 7.5 foot trees. Choose 30 or 36 inches for slim trees and 60 inches for very wide ones.
- **Pick the right tree version.** Choose the plug-in mini tree if it sits near an outlet and the battery version for a table in the middle of the room.
- **Think about pets and kids.** Fur skirts and dangling cords are tempting for puppies and kittens, so tuck cords behind furniture.
- **Order early.** Popular colors and sizes can sell out in December, and holiday deals come and go quickly.
