## How to choose a quick-dry bath towel

1. **Fabric:** Microfiber dries fastest and packs small. Cotton feels more traditional but holds more water, so look for lighter or waffle-weave cotton.
2. **Weight (GSM):** Lower GSM towels are thinner and dry faster. Plush 600+ GSM towels feel luxurious but take longer to dry.
3. **Size:** Standard bath towels are around 27 x 54 inches. A 30 x 60 inch towel or a 35 x 70 inch bath sheet gives more coverage.
4. **Hanging:** A hanging loop lets you hang a towel on a hook, but spreading it fully on a bar dries it fastest.
5. **Care:** Check the washing instructions. Follow the label on fabric softener, and most towels last longer on low dryer heat.

## Tips to help towels dry faster

- **Spread them out:** A towel folded over a narrow hook stays damp in the middle. Hang it open on a bar or a ladder rack.
- **Run the fan:** Leave the bathroom exhaust fan on for 20 to 30 minutes after a shower, or crack the door.
- **Give each person their own bar:** Overlapping towels trap moisture.
- **Go easy on softener:** Heavy fabric softener can coat fibers. Tens Towels says to avoid it entirely, while JML suggests only a little for its microfiber, so follow each towel's care label.
- **Rotate two sets:** With two towels per person, one can fully dry while the other is in use.
- **Dry on low:** High dryer heat can increase lint and shorten towel life.

## Microfiber vs cotton: which should you buy?

Microfiber is the better choice if drying speed is your top priority. It holds water in fine fibers rather than thick loops, so it releases moisture to the air quickly. It also packs small for travel. The trade-off is texture: microfiber can feel slightly grippy, and you pat dry rather than rub.

Cotton feels familiar and soft, and it is easy to find in matching sets. To keep drying time down, choose a lighter weight or a waffle weave instead of a thick, plush towel.
