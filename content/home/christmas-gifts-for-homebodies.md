Every family has one: the person who would rather stay in on a Friday night than go anywhere. They have a favorite corner of the sofa, a stack of books on the nightstand and strong opinions about which Christmas movie to watch first. Shopping for a homebody is actually easy once you stop looking for something to take them out of the house and start looking for something that makes staying in even better.

We picked three cozy Christmas gifts for homebodies that cover the three classic nights in: lounging on the couch, reading or watching TV in bed, and a movie night with a big bowl of popcorn. Each one is a well-reviewed best seller on Amazon, and none of them requires knowing someone's decor style or clothing size. We checked every rating and rating count on Amazon on the date above, along with stock for delivery to New York. Holiday prices change often, so check the current price on Amazon before you order.

## Best for movie nights: Presto PopLite Hot Air Popcorn Popper

### Why we like it

No homebody gift guide is complete without movie-night snacks, and the Presto PopLite is one of the most popular popcorn makers on Amazon. It uses hot air instead of oil, and Presto says it pops up to 18 cups in about 2 1/2 minutes with virtually no unpopped kernels. That is two big bowls, enough for a family movie or a whole Christmas double feature.

It is also simple. Measure the kernels with the cup on top, switch it on, and the chute sends the popcorn straight into your bowl. That same cup doubles as a butter melter: drop in a tablespoon of butter while the corn pops, then pour it over the top. With no oil, the base and chute just wipe clean, and the built-in cord wrap makes it easy to store in a cabinet. It is a compact 9 by 6.5 by 14 inches, weighs about 2 pounds and comes with a 1-year limited warranty. It holds a 4.4-star rating across more than 55,000 ratings.

### Who it's for

Movie lovers, families with kids, anyone trying to snack a little lighter and people who are tired of microwave bags. It also makes a fun hostess or white elephant gift paired with a jar of gourmet kernels.

### Pros

- Pops up to 18 cups in about 2 1/2 minutes

- Measuring cup doubles as a butter melter

## How to choose a Christmas gift for a homebody

Start with how they actually spend their evenings. If they are on the couch under a blanket most nights, the **wearable blanket** will become part of their routine. If they read, work or watch shows in bed, the **reading pillow** solves a problem they deal with every night. If weekends are for movies, the **popcorn popper** turns any night into a small event.

Next, think about who you are buying for. One-size gifts like the wearable blanket and a reading pillow are safe for in-laws, coworkers and teens, because you do not need to know anyone's clothing size or decor style. A kitchen gadget like the popper works best for someone who will enjoy it rather than see it as one more thing on the counter, so it is a great fit for families and roommates who watch movies together.

## Gift pairing ideas

- **The ultimate night in:** the wearable blanket, the popcorn popper and a bag of gourmet kernels, wrapped together with a gift card for a streaming service.
- **The book lover's bed setup:** the reading pillow with a new hardcover and a clip-on book light.
- **The movie night basket:** the popcorn popper with paper popcorn boxes, a popcorn seasoning set and a couple of favorite holiday movies.

## Buying tips

- **Order the reading pillow early:** it arrives compressed and can take up to 48 hours to fully expand, so unpack it before you wrap it.
- **Pick a color they would wear:** the wearable blanket comes in several solids as well as plaid, and a neutral is the safer choice for someone with a minimalist home.
- **Add the extras:** a jar of popcorn kernels for the popper or a new book for the reading pillow makes the gift ready to use on Christmas Day.
- **Check the current price on Amazon:** holiday prices and stock change daily, so compare before you buy.
