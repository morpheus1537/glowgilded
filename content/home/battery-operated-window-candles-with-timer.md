## Timer vs. sensor: which should you choose?

- **Fixed timer:** Best if you want predictable hours, for example 5 PM to 11 PM every night. You simply switch the candles on at the right time once, and they repeat daily.
- **Dusk sensor with shut-off:** Best for hands-off decorating. The candles follow sunset, which moves earlier through December, and still switch off after a set time.
- **Dusk-to-dawn sensor:** Glows all night, which looks great for late arrivals but uses more battery power.
- **Remote timer:** Best for clusters of candles on a mantel or table where you want to change settings without picking each one up.

## Buying tips

- **Count your windows first:** Most sets come in 4 or 6. Count the windows that face the street before buying so every window matches.
- **Measure the sill:** Check depth for the base and the height from sill to the bottom of any blinds or valance.
- **Buy batteries in bulk:** None of these sets include batteries. Rechargeable AA or AAA cells can save money over a long season, but check the manufacturer's guidance on battery type.
- **Turn them on at the same time:** For timer models, switch all candles on at the time you want them to light each evening so they stay in sync.
- **Keep curtains clear:** Even without a flame, keep candles stable and away from curtains that could knock them off the sill.
