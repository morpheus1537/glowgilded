## How to stop a shower curtain from billowing

1. **Choose weight:** Heavier liners resist air movement better than thin, cheap ones. Look for terms like heavy duty, 8G or 10G.
2. **Add weight at the hem:** Weighted stones, a lead-wire hem or magnets keep the bottom edge down.
3. **Use every hook:** Hanging all 12 hooks gives even tension across the rod.
4. **Tuck the hem:** Tucking the bottom inch or two inside the tub rim keeps the liner from swinging out.
5. **Consider a curved rod:** A curved rod adds elbow room so the liner has space before it reaches you.

## Buying tips

- **Check your tub material:** Magnets only work on steel or cast iron tubs. For acrylic or fiberglass, choose a weighted hem or stones.
- **Size first:** 72 x 72 inches fits most standard tubs. Measure if you have a wide or extra-tall shower.
- **Clear vs opaque:** Clear liners let light into the shower; white fabric adds privacy.
- **Hooks:** Most liners do not list hooks as included, so you may need to reuse yours or buy a set.
- **Air it out:** Spread the liner fully closed after showering so it dries evenly instead of staying bunched and damp.
- **Ventilate:** Run the bathroom fan or crack a window to help the whole room dry, which also helps the liner stay cleaner.
