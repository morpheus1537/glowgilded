A small bedroom that never quite warms up makes winter nights miserable. Turning up the whole-house thermostat to fix one chilly room wastes energy, while a good space heater lets you warm just the space you sleep in. The best picks for a bedroom are quiet, hold a set temperature and switch themselves off.

## How to choose a space heater for a small bedroom

1. **Room size:** A 1500W heater suits most small bedrooms. Check the room size the brand lists.
2. **Thermostat:** A thermostat holds your chosen temperature, so the heater cycles instead of blasting all night.
3. **Timer:** An auto-off timer lets you fall asleep warm without running the heater until morning.
4. **Noise:** Fan heaters make some sound. Oil-filled radiators have no fan and are silent.
5. **Safety:** Look for tip-over protection, overheat protection and cool-touch housing.
6. **Footprint:** Slim towers fit beside a nightstand. Compact cubes are easy to move.

## Space heater safety tips for bedrooms

- **Keep 3 feet clear:** Keep bedding, curtains, clothes and furniture at least 3 feet from the heater.
- **Use the wall outlet:** Do not use extension cords or power strips with a 1500W heater.
- **Set a timer:** Warm the room before bed and let the timer switch it off after you fall asleep.
- **Place it on a hard, level floor:** Avoid thick rugs and never put a heater on a bed or chair.
- **Install a smoke alarm:** Test it monthly, especially when you use a heater regularly.
- **Keep pets and kids in mind:** Tip-over switches and cool-touch housing matter in busy homes.
