## How to set up a guest room in one afternoon

- **Make the bed in layers:** protector, fitted sheet, flat sheet, a warm blanket and a throw at the foot. Layers let guests adjust for a cold night or a warm radiator without asking.
- **Give each guest two pillows:** a pair per person is the easiest way to cover different sleeping styles.
- **Clear some space:** empty one drawer and leave a few hangers in the closet. Guests will unpack instead of living out of a bag.
- **Light for reading:** a lamp guests can switch off from bed beats walking across a dark room to the wall switch.
- **Power at the bedside:** a charger or power strip near the nightstand is one of the most appreciated small touches.
- **A welcome basket:** fresh towels, a bottle of water, a few snacks and the Wi-Fi password on a card. If you need inexpensive ideas, our [stocking stuffers under $20](/home/stocking-stuffers-under-20/) list has several that work as guest-room treats.
- **Sleep in it yourself:** spend one night in the room before guests arrive. You will find the drafty window, the squeaky floorboard or the street light that needs a curtain.

## What to look for when you buy

- **Washability:** everything that touches a guest should be easy to wash between visits. All three bedding and pillow picks above are machine washable.
- **Fit:** measure the mattress depth before buying a protector, and check that new pillows fit the pillowcases you already own.
- **Simplicity:** guests should not need an app or a manual. A single switch or button is ideal.
- **Storage:** anything that folds flat or tucks under the bed earns its place in a room that is only used a few weeks a year.
