Some of the best Christmas gifts are the ones that quietly upgrade a daily routine. Nobody gets excited about their bathroom in December, but almost everyone gets excited about a warm towel after a hot shower, a long bath with a book and a candle, or a thick, fluffy towel that feels like it came from a hotel. That is the idea behind this guide: spa bathroom gift ideas that turn an ordinary bathroom into a small home spa without a renovation.

These gifts also solve the classic problem of shopping for someone who "has everything." Most people do not buy themselves a towel warmer or a bath tray, even if they would love one. They are treats, which is exactly what makes them good gifts.

We picked three gifts that cover the three classic spa moments: stepping out of the shower, soaking in the tub and drying off. We also added one bonus pick for people who only shower. Each is a well-reviewed best seller on Amazon, and none needs a plumber or an electrician. We checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above for delivery to New York. Holiday prices change often, so check the current price on Amazon before you order.

## Best overall: Keenray Towel Warmer Bucket

### Why we like it

There is a reason towel warmers show up in every luxury spa: being wrapped in a warm towel on a cold morning feels like a small luxury every single day. The Keenray towel warmer brings that experience home in a simple bucket you plug into a regular outlet. Drop in a loosely folded towel before you shower, press the single button, and it is warm when you step out.

It is a bigger bucket than many, with a 21-liter capacity that the brand says fits up to two 40 x 70 inch oversized bath towels. That also means it works for bathrobes, a throw blanket or a pair of pajamas on a freezing night. Keenray says it starts heating within a minute and reaches high temperature in about six minutes, runs up to 60 minutes per cycle, and shuts off on its own.

The design is part of the appeal. It is a clean white cylinder with a natural wood-look base and a matching wood handle on the lid, so it looks more like a piece of spa decor than an appliance. It holds a 4.5-star rating across more than 6,300 ratings.

### Who it's for

Anyone with a cold bathroom, people who take long showers in winter, parents who want warm towels for kids after bath time, and older relatives who feel the cold. It is also a strong couple's gift, since the bucket fits two towels.

### Pros

- Big 21-liter bucket fits up to two oversized towels or a robe
- Simple single-button control, easy for anyone to use
- Heats quickly and runs up to 60 minutes per cycle
- Bucket and lid separate for easy cleaning
- White and wood design looks good in most bathrooms

### Cons

- Takes floor or counter space; it stands about 20 inches tall
- It warms towels; it will not fully dry a soaking-wet towel

## How to choose a spa bathroom gift

- **Think about how they wash.** Bath lovers get the most out of a bathtub tray. Shower-only people will use a towel warmer, towels or a new shower head every day.
- **Check the space.** A towel warmer bucket needs a spot near an outlet on the floor or a counter. Small bathrooms may be better suited to towels or a shower head.
- **Measure the tub.** For a bath tray, measure the outer width of the tub rim and confirm it falls between 29 1/2 and 43 inches.
- **Pick colors that fit the room.** Gray and white towels are the safest choice. When in doubt, choose neutral.

## Gift pairing ideas

- **The ultimate warm-up:** the Keenray towel warmer plus the Turkish towel set, so the first towel they warm is a brand-new one.
- **The Sunday bath night:** the bamboo bathtub tray with a favorite book, a candle and a small bottle of bubble bath.
- **The hotel bathroom makeover:** the SparkPod rain shower head plus the towel set for a full refresh that costs far less than a renovation.

## Buying tips

- Order bulky items like the towel warmer early in December; holiday shipping slows down as Christmas gets closer.
- Wash new towels before gifting or tell the recipient to wash them before first use; it helps absorbency.
- Load a towel warmer with dry, loosely folded towels so the heat can reach every layer.
- Keep the gift receipt with sized items like towels and trays in case the color or fit is wrong.
