Art Deco never really left. The fluted walls, curved velvet chairs and brass lamps you keep seeing in hotel lobbies and design magazines are the same ideas that made the 1920s feel glamorous, just simplified. Designers call this modern version Neo Deco, and the good news is that you do not need a renovation or a vintage dealer to get it. A few well-chosen pieces with the right textures and shapes do most of the work.

The Neo Deco look comes down to three things: **ribbed or fluted texture**, **warm metallic light** and **soft geometry** such as arches and domes. We picked one affordable, well-reviewed find for each: a ribbed glass vase, a cordless gold mushroom lamp and a thin gold arched mirror. Each has a long, strong rating history on Amazon, and we checked every listing on October 30, 2026. Prices change often, so check the current price before you order.

Together, these three pieces can carry a whole room. Use them in one space for a full Deco moment, or spread them through the house so the style feels consistent from the entryway to the bedroom.

## How to style Neo Deco in a modern home

The secret to Neo Deco is restraint. Original Art Deco interiors were packed with pattern, lacquer and gilding, which can feel like a theater set in a normal house. The modern version keeps the shapes and textures but gives them room to breathe.

- **Pick one metal and repeat it.** Gold or brass is the classic choice. Repeat it at least three times in a room, for example in a mirror frame, a lamp and a tray, so it looks intentional.
- **Layer texture, not clutter.** Ribbed glass, fluted wood, channel-tufted velvet and polished marble all add depth without adding more objects.
- **Use curves to soften straight lines.** Arches, domes and rounded furniture edges balance the strong verticals of fluted surfaces.
- **Choose one rich color.** Emerald green, deep navy, burgundy or warm walnut gives the gold something to glow against. Keep the rest of the room neutral.
- **Light low and warm.** Several small warm lights, like a cordless mushroom lamp and wall sconces, feel more Deco than a single bright ceiling light.

## Buying tips

- **Measure before you buy a mirror.** Leave a few inches of wall on each side of a vanity mirror, and hang it so the center sits roughly at eye level.
- **Check the finish in your light.** Gold finishes can look warmer or more yellow depending on your bulbs. Keep the listing photos in mind and compare with the metals you already own.
- **Charge cordless lamps fully first.** A full first charge helps the battery perform as listed, and keeps the lamp ready for dinner parties.
- **Think in pairs and trios.** A vase, a lamp and a mirror grouped on one console create a finished vignette. Odd-numbered groupings usually look most natural.
- **Mix in what you have.** Neo Deco works well with vintage finds, books and plants, so you do not need to replace everything to get the look.
