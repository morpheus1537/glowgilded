The living room gets the tree, the mantel and the stockings, but the bedroom is where you actually wind down on a cold December night. A few small touches can make it feel just as festive, and the best Christmas bedroom decor ideas are the ones that add a warm glow and some texture without turning your sleep space into a cluttered display.

We built this guide around three pieces that work together: fairy curtain lights for the window or headboard wall, a pair of lighted birch tree lamps for the nightstand or dresser, and a Stewart plaid chenille throw for the foot of the bed. Each one has well over a thousand ratings on Amazon US. We checked every star rating and rating count on Amazon on the date above, confirmed each item was in stock, and only describe features stated on the product page. Prices move a lot in December, so check the current price before you buy.

## How to decorate a bedroom for Christmas

### Layer three kinds of light

The coziest bedrooms mix a big soft glow, a few small points of light and darkness at bedtime. Curtain lights on the window give the background glow, the birch trees add small points of sparkle at eye level, and both have timers so the room goes dark when you sleep. Keep everything warm white; cool white and multicolor lights look busy in a small room.

### Pick one color story

Bedrooms feel calmer with fewer colors. A cream, red and green plaid throw works with natural wood and white bedding; if your room leans gray, add a white faux fur or knit throw instead and let the lights carry the holiday feeling. Two or three accents in the same palette, such as a pillow cover and a small wreath, are plenty.

### Keep the nightstand usable

A small tree should not crowd out your lamp, phone and water glass. If space is tight, put the birch trees on the dresser or a windowsill instead, and use their timer so you never need to reach over in the dark. For more small-space ideas, read [how to keep a cold bedroom warm](/home/how-to-keep-a-cold-bedroom-warm/).

### Think about guests

The same three pieces turn a spare room into a welcoming guest room for the holidays. Pair them with the basics from our [guest room essentials for holiday guests](/home/guest-room-essentials-for-holiday-guests/) list, and leave the curtain-light remote somewhere obvious so guests can dim it.

## Safety and setup tips

- Use the timers. Lights that switch off on their own are safer and easier on batteries.
- Never cover light strings with heavy fabric or bunch them under bedding.
- Hang curtain lights from a rod rather than tape on painted walls, which can peel paint when removed.
- Keep fringe throws and small trees out of reach of puppies, kittens and toddlers who like to chew.
