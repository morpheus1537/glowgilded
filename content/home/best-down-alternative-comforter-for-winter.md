## How to choose a winter comforter

1. **Fill weight:** Brands that list gsm (grams per square meter) make warmth easier to compare. More fill usually means more warmth and loft.
2. **Stitching:** Box stitching keeps the fill from shifting into clumps.
3. **Corner tabs:** Tabs or loops let you use the comforter as a duvet insert inside a washable cover.
4. **Size:** Check the exact dimensions, not just "queen." An oversized comforter drapes better over deep mattresses.
5. **Care:** Look for machine-washable fill so you can clean it at home.
6. **Look:** Reversible colors let you change the room's feel without a cover.

## Winter comforter tips

- **Layer instead of overbuying:** A medium comforter plus a throw is more flexible than one heavy comforter.
- **Give it time to fluff:** Shake it out and let it rest for a day before judging its loft.
- **Use a cover:** A washable duvet cover protects the insert and cuts down on full washes.
- **Wash on gentle:** Use a large-capacity machine, and dry on low until it is fully dry to avoid clumps.
- **Block drafts:** Pair your bedding with [thermal blackout curtains](/home/best-thermal-blackout-curtains-for-winter/) to keep heat in at night.
