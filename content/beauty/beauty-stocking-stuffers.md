The best stocking stuffers are small things she will actually use, and beauty is the easiest category to get right. A great lip balm, a soft scrunchie or a pampering mask feels like a treat, slips easily into a stocking and gets used long after the holidays.

We picked five beauty stocking stuffers that are popular, practical and easy to love. Every one is rated 4.5 stars or higher, most by tens of thousands of shoppers, and we checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. ## Best lip treat: LANEIGE Lip Glowy Balm

### Why we like it

LANEIGE is one of the biggest names in lip care, and the Lip Glowy Balm has nearly 35,000 ratings at 4.7 stars. It is a lightweight, moisture-coating balm made with murumuru and shea butters to hydrate and lock in moisture, according to the listing. It is pocket sized for easy, on-the-go application, and LANEIGE calls it the perfect daytime partner to its cult-favorite Lip Sleeping Mask. It is a stocking stuffer that feels like a little luxury.

### Who it's for

Anyone with dry winter lips, and fans of LANEIGE's Lip Sleeping Mask.

### Pros

- Murumuru and shea butters
- Lightweight, glossy finish
- Pocket size
- Nearly 35,000 ratings

### Cons

- Sheer; not a lip color
- Reapply through the day

## Best for hair: Kitsch Ultra Petite Satin Scrunchies, 6 Pack

### Why we like it

Kitsch scrunchies are a stocking classic, and this ultra-petite satin set has more than 54,000 ratings. Kitsch says the eco-friendly satin is designed to feel softer than silk and glides through strands to help reduce friction, pulling, breakage and creasing. The slim ruched design looks polished without bulk, and they can be worn at home, at work, while exercising or while sleeping, according to the listing. Six in a pack means you can split them across stockings.

### Who it's for

Anyone who wears her hair up, especially those with creases or breakage from regular hair ties.

### Pros

- Satin designed to feel softer than silk
- Helps reduce creases and friction
- Slim, polished look
- 6 per pack

### Cons

- Petite size may not hold very thick hair in one wrap
- Satin can slip in very fine hair

## Best pamper treat: BIODANCE Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask, 4 Pack

### Why we like it

The BIODANCE mask has become a viral favorite for its "glass skin" glow, with more than 51,000 ratings at 4.5 stars. Each hydrogel mask is solidified with 1.19 oz of essence containing ultra-low molecular collagen, oligo-hyaluronic acid, galactomyces ferment filtrate and niacinamide, according to the listing. The mask turns transparent as the ingredients absorb. BIODANCE recommends wearing it overnight or for at least 3-4 hours. A four-pack is perfect for a self-care night or two.

### Who it's for

Skincare lovers who enjoy a K-beauty pampering ritual.

### Pros

- Collagen and hyaluronic acid formula
- Hydrogel turns transparent as it absorbs
- Overnight wear
- Over 51,000 ratings

### Cons

- Needs at least 3-4 hours of wear
- Patch test if she has sensitive skin

## How to build a beauty stocking

- **Start with one hero item.** A LANEIGE balm or a set of BIODANCE masks feels special.
- **Add everyday basics.** Scrunchies and claw clips get used daily.
- **Split multi-packs.** Hand creams and hair accessories fill several stockings.
- **Think winter.** Lips, hands and skin need extra moisture this time of year; see our [winter skincare for dry skin](/beauty/winter-skincare-for-dry-skin/) guide.
- **Wrap small items together.** A ribbon around clips and scrunchies looks like a mini gift set.
