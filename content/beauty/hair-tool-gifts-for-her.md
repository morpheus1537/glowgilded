A great hair day is one of the small luxuries that makes a woman feel put together, and a professional hair tool is a gift she will use several times a week for years. That makes hair tools one of the best-value beauty gifts there is: a single splurge that pays off every morning, unlike a candle or a palette that gets used up. The trick is choosing a tool that matches how she actually styles her hair.

We picked four premium, highly rated hair tool gifts for her, from an all-in-one multi-styler to a salon-style dryer, a professional flat iron and a cult-favorite blow dryer brush. Each one has thousands of Amazon ratings, and we checked every rating, rating count and stock status on Amazon on the date above. We never quote prices, so use the buttons to check the current price. ## Best overall: Shark FlexStyle Air Multi-Styler & Drying System

### Why we like it

The Shark FlexStyle is the ultimate "she can do everything" gift. With a simple twist, it changes from a powerful hair dryer into a multi-styler, so she can use it as a blow dryer, a hot air brush or a curling tool. The listing describes interchangeable attachments: 1.25-inch auto-wrap curlers that use Coanda airflow to wrap and set curls (two of them, to curl in either direction), an oval brush for smoothing and volume, a paddle brush for straightening, a concentrator, and a curl-defining diffuser with retractable prongs.

It is designed for every hair type, from straight and wavy to curly and coily. Shark says the FlexStyle uses intelligent heat control to reduce heat exposure, which makes it more comfortable to use every day than many hot tools. The compact, lightweight design also makes it easier to pack than a full set of separate tools.

### Who it's for

The woman who likes to change her look, from smooth blowouts to bouncy curls, and anyone who wants fewer tools on the vanity.

### Pros

- Dryer and multi-styler in one
- Auto-wrap curlers, brushes and a diffuser per the listing
- Intelligent heat control
- Designed for all hair types

### Cons

- Fewer ratings than the other picks, though still in the thousands
- Takes a little practice to master the curlers

## Best hair dryer: BaBylissPRO Nano Titanium Portofino Hair Dryer

### Why we like it

Some women want a classic dryer that simply does the job well, the way a salon dryer does. The BaBylissPRO Portofino is a professional 2000-watt dryer with Nano titanium technology that the listing says generates gentle, even heat for shiny hair. A true ionic generator helps fight static and close the cuticle.

It has a long-life Italian AC motor, six heat and speed settings with a cold shot to set styles, and an ergonomic handle. The stainless steel filter removes for easy cleaning, and it comes with a diffuser and three concentrators, so it handles everything from sleek blowouts to defined curls. ## Best flat iron: CHI G2 Titanium-Infused Ceramic Flat Iron (1.25")

### Why we like it

CHI is one of the most recognized names in professional flat irons, and the G2 is a favorite for sleek, frizz-free styles. Its titanium-infused ceramic floating plates are designed to spread heat evenly and glide smoothly, which helps avoid snagging and repeated passes. The 1.25-inch plates style more hair per pass, so it is quicker on longer hair.

A color-coded digital display with preset modes for fine, medium and coarse hair makes it easy to pick the right heat, up to 425°F. The listing says it reaches styling temperature in about 40 seconds, and it is dual voltage, so it is ready for holiday travel abroad. A long swivel cord, a thermal mat and auto shut-off add convenience and peace of mind. With more than 10,000 ratings at 4.6 stars, it is the highest-rated pick in this guide.

### Who it's for

Women who wear their hair straight or sleek, and frequent travelers who need a dual-voltage tool.

### Pros

- Titanium-infused ceramic floating plates
- Digital display with hair-type presets up to 425°F
- Heats in about 40 seconds
- Dual voltage, thermal mat and auto shut-off

## How to choose the right hair tool gift

- **Match her routine:** if she wears her hair sleek, choose the CHI G2; if she loves volume, the Hot Tools brush; if she changes styles often, the Shark FlexStyle.
- **Consider her hair type:** thick or coarse hair benefits from the power of the BaBylissPRO Portofino, while the FlexStyle and its diffuser suit curly and coily hair.
- **Think about travel:** the CHI G2 is dual voltage, which matters if she travels internationally.
- **Add a finishing touch:** a heat protectant spray, silk scrunchies or a satin pillowcase make any hair tool feel like a complete gift.

## Buying tips

- **Check what is included:** attachments vary between versions, so read the listing before you buy, especially for the FlexStyle.
- **Order early for Christmas:** premium hair tools are popular December gifts and can sell out.
