Large, visible pores are one of the most common skin complaints, especially on the nose, cheeks and forehead of oily and combination skin. Niacinamide (vitamin B3) is a favorite for them because it helps balance oil and smooth texture, which makes pores look smaller, and it is gentle enough for most skin types.

## How to choose a niacinamide serum for pores

1. **Strength:** 10% is a common starting point; 12-20% suits experienced users.
2. **Supporting ingredients:** Zinc helps with oil; tranexamic acid and arbutin target dark spots.
3. **Texture:** Watery, lightweight serums layer best under moisturizer and sunscreen.
4. **Fragrance:** Fragrance-free formulas are gentler on sensitive skin.
5. **Size:** Jumbo bottles save on reorders if a serum works for you.
6. **Skin type:** Very oily skin may like a matte finish; dry skin needs hydration too.

## How to use niacinamide for pores

- **Patch test first:** Try a little on your jaw or behind the ear for a few days.
- **Apply to clean skin:** Use after cleansing and toner, before moisturizer.
- **Less is more:** A few drops covers the whole face.
- **Moisturize:** Even oily skin needs hydration. Our [hyaluronic acid serum guide](/beauty/best-hyaluronic-acid-serum-for-dry-skin/) has lightweight options.
- **Wear sunscreen:** Sun damage stretches pores over time.
- **Add retinol slowly:** Retinol also helps texture; see our [retinol for beginners](/beauty/best-retinol-serum-for-beginners/) guide.
- **Be patient:** Give a serum 4-8 weeks before judging results.

## Other habits that help pores

Cleanse twice a day, avoid squeezing, use non-comedogenic products and remove makeup every night. A weekly exfoliating step can help clear congestion, but do not overdo it.
