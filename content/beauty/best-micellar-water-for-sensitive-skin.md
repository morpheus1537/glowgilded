If your skin stings, flushes or tightens after washing, your cleanser may be part of the problem. Micellar water is a gentle, no-rinse option: tiny oil molecules called micelles attract makeup, dirt and oil so you can wipe them away with a cotton pad. It is quick at night, handy when traveling, and kind to skin that is already stressed by cold, dry winter air.

## How to choose micellar water for sensitive skin

1. **Skin focus:** Look for formulas made for sensitive skin or for your specific concern, like dehydration.
2. **Fragrance:** Fragrance-free is usually the safest bet for reactive skin.
3. **pH:** Formulas at skin or physiological pH aim to respect the skin's natural balance.
4. **Eye safety:** Check for eye-area testing if you remove eye makeup.
5. **Bottle size:** Bigger bottles last longer if you use it twice a day.

## Tips for using micellar water on sensitive skin

- **Soak, then hold:** Press a soaked pad on the skin for a few seconds before wiping, especially over eyes.
- **Do not scrub:** Gentle sweeps cause less redness than rubbing.
- **Use fresh pads:** Switch pads until they come away clean.
- **Moisturize after:** Follow with a gentle moisturizer; our [winter skincare for dry skin](/beauty/winter-skincare-for-dry-skin/) guide has ideas.
- **Try reusable pads:** Soft washable pads are kinder to skin and waste less.
- **Patch test:** Try a new formula on your jaw for a few days before using it all over.
