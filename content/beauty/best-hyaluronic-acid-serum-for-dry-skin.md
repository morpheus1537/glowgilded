Winter is hard on dry skin. Cold air outside and heated air inside pull moisture away, leaving skin tight, flaky and dull. A hyaluronic acid serum is one of the simplest fixes. Hyaluronic acid attracts water, so a few drops under your moisturizer can leave skin feeling plumper and more comfortable.

## How to choose a hyaluronic acid serum for dry skin

1. **Supporting ingredients:** Ceramides, vitamin B5 and glycerin help hold moisture in.
2. **Multiple molecular weights:** Serums with several sizes of hyaluronic acid target hydration at different depths.
3. **Fragrance-free:** Dry, sensitive skin usually does better without fragrance.
4. **Texture:** Lightweight water-based serums layer easily; richer serums feel more cushioning.
5. **Extras:** Some add vitamin C or anti-aging claims.
6. **Size:** Check the bottle size if you use serum twice a day.

## How to use hyaluronic acid serum for dry skin

- **Cleanse gently:** Use a non-stripping cleanser. Our [cleansing balm guide](/beauty/best-cleansing-balm-for-makeup-removal/) has gentle options.
- **Apply to damp skin:** Pat on serum while skin is still slightly wet.
- **Seal with moisturizer:** This locks in the hydration the serum draws in.
- **Use morning and night:** Most hyaluronic serums are gentle enough for twice daily use.
- **Add SPF in the morning:** Sunscreen is still essential in winter.
- **Add a humidifier:** It helps keep moisture in the air, and your skin, overnight.
- **Patch test new products:** Especially if your skin is sensitive.

## Pairing hyaluronic acid with other actives

Hyaluronic acid layers well under most treatments. If you also use a [vitamin C serum for dark spots](/beauty/best-vitamin-c-serum-for-dark-spots/) or a [retinol serum](/beauty/best-retinol-serum-for-beginners/), add hyaluronic acid to help offset dryness.
