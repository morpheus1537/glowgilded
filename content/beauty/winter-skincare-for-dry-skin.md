Winter is hard on skin. Cold wind outside, dry heated air inside and long hot showers all strip moisture away, and by December many of us are dealing with tight cheeks, flaky patches, chapped lips and cracked knuckles. The fix is not a 10-step routine or the most expensive cream on the counter. Dry winter skin needs a stronger moisture barrier, and a few proven, fragrance-free formulas do that job well.

These five winter skincare picks for dry skin cover every need, from an everyday face and body cream to a balm for wind-chapped cheeks and an ointment for cracked hands. ## A simple winter routine for dry skin

1. **Cleanse gently.** Use lukewarm water and a non-stripping cleanser.
2. **Moisturize on damp skin.** Apply CeraVe or Kiehl's within a minute of washing.
3. **SPF in the morning.** Winter sun still counts, especially on snow.
4. **Treat problem spots.** Cicaplast on chapped cheeks, FAB on itchy patches.
5. **Seal at night.** A thin layer of Aquaphor on hands, heels and lips before bed.

## Buying tips

- **Look for ceramides.** They help support the skin barrier, which is what winter weakens.
- **Go fragrance-free.** Irritated winter skin is more reactive to fragrance.
- **Add humidity.** A bedroom humidifier helps your skin overnight; see our [best humidifiers for the bedroom](/home/best-humidifiers-for-bedroom/).
- **Shorter, cooler showers.** Hot water strips natural oils.
- **Patch test.** Try a new product on a small area first, and see a dermatologist for persistent eczema or cracking.
