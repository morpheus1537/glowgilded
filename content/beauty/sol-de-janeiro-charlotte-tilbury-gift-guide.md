## How to layer body cream and perfume mist

Scent layering sounds complicated, but it is simple, and it is the reason these gifts work so well together.

- **Start with body cream on clean skin.** Apply it after a shower while skin is still slightly damp. The cream gives the fragrance something to cling to.
- **Mist over the top.** Spritz the perfume mist on pulse points, then a little through the air and into your hair from a distance.
- **Match or contrast.** Pairing a cream and mist with similar notes, such as the vanilla in Cheirosa 59 and Cheirosa 48, gives a cohesive scent. Mixing a warm cream with a fruity mist like Cheirosa 68 creates something more personal.
- **Reapply the mist, not the cream.** Keep the travel-size mist in the pouch for a mid-afternoon refresh.

## How to pick a nude lipstick as a gift

- **Look at what she already wears.** If she reaches for soft pinks and rosy browns, a Pillow Talk shade is likely a good match.
- **Think about depth.** Medium is a step deeper than the original Pillow Talk, a good option if she wants a slightly bolder nude.
- **Consider her lips.** If she has very dry lips, mention that a quick balm under a matte formula feels more comfortable. Pairing the lipstick with a lip treatment like the one in our [luxury skincare gift guide](/beauty/luxury-skincare-gift-guide/) makes a thoughtful duo.
- **Include a gift receipt.** Shade is the one thing you cannot guarantee.

- **Order early.** Gift sets and Amazon exclusives can sell out close to the holidays.

- **Check the shade name.** Pillow Talk comes in several versions; make sure the listing says Medium if that is the one you want.
