## What causes dark circles?

1. **Pigmentation:** Brownish circles from extra melanin; brightening ingredients help most.
2. **Thin skin:** Bluish or purple tones from visible blood vessels; hydration and retinol can help over time.
3. **Puffiness:** Swelling casts shadows; caffeine and cool temperatures help.
4. **Dryness:** Dehydrated skin looks dull and crepey; hyaluronic acid and ceramides help.
5. **Lifestyle:** Sleep, allergies and sun all play a part.

## Tips for brighter under-eyes

- **Pat, do not rub:** The skin around the eyes is thin and delicate.
- **Wear sunscreen:** Sun makes pigmentation worse; use a gentle SPF around the eyes.
- **Cool down puffiness:** Store your eye cream in the fridge for a cooling effect.
- **Sleep on your back:** Elevating your head can reduce morning puffiness.
