## A simple winter hair care routine for dry hair

1. **Wash less often.** Two or three washes a week help preserve natural oils.
2. **Use lukewarm water.** Hot water strips oils from hair and scalp.
3. **Mask once a week.** Apply the Arvazallia mask after shampoo and leave it on for a few minutes.
4. **Leave in moisture.** Spray Redken One United on damp hair to detangle and protect from heat.
5. **Seal with oil.** Smooth a few drops of Moroccanoil over mid-lengths and ends.
6. **Treat overnight.** Use the Kerastase 8H Magic Night Serum a few nights a week.
7. **Sleep on silk.** Swap to the ZIMASILK pillowcase to reduce friction and frizz.

## More winter hair tips

- **Limit heat styling.** Air dry when you can and always use heat protection.
- **Cover up.** Wear a hat in cold wind, ideally lined with silk or satin.
- **Run a humidifier.** Adding moisture to indoor air helps hair and skin.
- **Get regular trims.** Removing split ends keeps hair looking healthier.
