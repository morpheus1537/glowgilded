## How to choose pimple patches

1. **Day or night:** Overnight patches prioritize strong adhesion. Daytime patches are thinner and more invisible.
2. **Sizes:** Packs with several sizes let you match the patch to the spot. Apply one slightly larger than the blemish.
3. **Pure or infused:** Pure hydrocolloid is simplest for sensitive skin. Some patches add ingredients like tea tree oil.
4. **Finish:** Matte, translucent patches blend in better, especially under makeup.
5. **Count:** Bulk packs are good value if you get frequent breakouts.
6. **Spot type:** Patches are made for surface spots like whiteheads, not deep, painful cysts.

## Tips for using pimple patches

- **Start clean and dry:** Apply to freshly cleansed, dry skin with no serum or moisturizer underneath.
- **Size up:** Choose a patch slightly larger than the spot so the edges seal.
- **Don't pop first:** Let the patch do the work instead of squeezing.
- **Replace when white:** When the patch turns white or opaque, swap in a fresh one.
- **Remove makeup gently:** At night, take off makeup before patching. A [cleansing balm](/beauty/best-cleansing-balm-for-makeup-removal/) melts it away without scrubbing.
- **See a dermatologist:** For deep, painful or frequent breakouts, patches are not a substitute for professional care.
