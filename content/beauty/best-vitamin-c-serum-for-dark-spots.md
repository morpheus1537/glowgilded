Dark spots and uneven tone are some of the most common skincare frustrations, especially after a summer in the sun. A vitamin C serum is a popular first step for brightening, because vitamin C is an antioxidant and many formulas are designed to help even out skin tone over time. The challenge is choosing a formula: vitamin C comes in different forms and strengths, and the strongest is not always the best for your skin.

## How to choose a vitamin C serum

1. **Form of vitamin C:** L-ascorbic acid is the classic, pure form. Derivatives like SAP are often gentler.
2. **Strength:** Around 10% is a common starting point. 20% formulas are more intense and better for experienced users.
3. **Supporting ingredients:** Hyaluronic acid adds hydration, ceramides support the barrier, and vitamin E and ferulic acid are classic antioxidant partners.
4. **Packaging:** Vitamin C can oxidize. Opaque or protective packaging helps.
5. **Sunscreen:** Any brightening routine works best with daily SPF.

## Tips for getting results from vitamin C

- **Wear sunscreen every day:** Sun exposure darkens spots, so SPF is the most important step in any brightening routine.
- **Start slowly:** Use your serum every other morning for the first week, then daily if your skin is happy.
- **Apply to clean, dry skin:** Follow with moisturizer, then sunscreen.
- **Store it well:** Keep the cap closed and store away from heat and sunlight.
- **Be patient:** Expect to use a serum consistently for several weeks before judging it.
