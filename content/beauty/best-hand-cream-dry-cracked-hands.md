## How to repair dry, cracked hands in winter

1. **Wash with lukewarm water:** Hot water strips more oil from skin.
2. **Pat dry and apply right away:** Put on hand cream while hands are still slightly damp.
3. **Reapply after every wash:** Keep a tube by every sink.
4. **Treat overnight:** Apply a thick layer before bed, and wear cotton gloves if you like.
5. **Wear gloves outdoors:** Cold, windy air dries skin quickly.
6. **Use gloves for chores:** Protect hands from dish soap and cleaning products.
7. **Run a humidifier:** Indoor heating dries the air, and a humidifier can help.

## Buying tips

- **Match the formula to your skin:** Very cracked hands need a thick, protective cream; frequent washers may prefer a fast-absorbing formula.
- **Choose fragrance-free for sensitive skin:** Fragrance can irritate already damaged skin.
- **Look for barrier ingredients:** Ceramides, glycerin, dimethicone and shea butter all appear in these picks.
- **Buy more than one tube:** Keep one at home, one at work and one in your bag.
- **Check the current listing:** Sizes and pack counts vary.
