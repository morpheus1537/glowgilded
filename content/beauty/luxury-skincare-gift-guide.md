## How to choose a skincare gift

- **Pick a texture, not a promise.** Most people know whether they like rich balms or light gels. If unsure, a cleanser or lip treatment is safest.
- **Avoid strong actives for surprise gifts.** Retinoids and acids are personal; save them for someone who asked for them.
- **Think about fragrance.** Several premium products are lightly scented. For sensitive skin, choose the silk pillowcase or ask first.

## Build a set and wrap it like a ritual

Cleansing Balm + Marine Cream + Lip Sleeping Mask covers a full routine for someone who enjoys skincare. Add the silk pillowcase for a true splurge.

Tuck the products into a soft towel or small basket, add a handwritten card with the order to use them (cleanse, moisturize, lip mask last), and finish with a ribbon. It turns a few jars into an experience.

## Gift ideas by recipient

- **Your mom:** the Night Cream, paired with the silk pillowcase.
- **Your sister or best friend:** the Cleansing Balm and Lip Sleeping Mask as a duo.
- **Your partner:** the Slip pillowcase, the gift that feels most personal.
- **A coworker or Secret Santa:** the Lip Sleeping Mask on its own.
- **The skincare enthusiast:** the Marine Cream, which she may already love and will happily restock.

## Buying tips

- **Check the size.** Premium skincare is often sold in several sizes on one listing.
- **Look for official listings** and items shipped and sold by Amazon.
- **Order early** so you have time for exchanges.
- **Include a gift receipt** in case she prefers a different product.
