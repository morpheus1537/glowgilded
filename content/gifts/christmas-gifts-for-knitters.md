## How to choose a gift for a knitter

1. **Tools over yarn:** Yarn choice is personal. Tools are easier to get right unless you know their favorite fiber and weight.
2. **Skill level:** Beginners appreciate counters and markers; experienced knitters love needle sets and winders.
3. **Where they knit:** Commuters and travelers need a project bag. Couch knitters may love a yarn bowl or neck light.
4. **Duplicates:** If they already own a set of needles, a different size range or a winder may be more welcome.
5. **Budget:** Pair a small item like a row counter with a bigger gift for a complete package.

## Gift tips for knitters

- **Ask casually:** Ask what they are making next or which needle sizes they use most.
- **Build a bundle:** A project bag with a counter and stitch markers makes a thoughtful set.
- **Add yarn wisely:** If you add yarn, choose a soft neutral in a common weight and include a gift receipt.
- **Think comfort:** Knitters often settle in for hours, so a cozy blanket from our [gifts for people who are always cold](/gifts/gifts-for-people-who-are-always-cold/) guide pairs nicely.
- **Wrap it in yarn:** Tie the gift with a length of nice yarn they can actually use.
