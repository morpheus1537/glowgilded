Half-finished bottles, wine that tastes tight right after opening, and drips down the bottle are everyday small frustrations. ## How to choose a gift for a wine lover

1. **Think about their habits:** Do they often leave wine in the bottle? A saver or stopper helps. Do they drink mostly reds? An aerator is a natural fit.
2. **Keep it practical:** Tools that work with any bottle get used more than novelty gadgets.
3. **Check cleaning:** Dishwasher-safe pieces are easier to live with.
4. **Size the gift:** Stoppers and pourers are great stocking stuffers; aerators and pumps work as a main gift.
5. **Bundle it:** Pair a tool with a bottle they love for a complete present.

### Why we like it

The Vacu Vin wine saver has the most ratings in this guide, 4.7 stars from more than 31,000 ratings. It is a manual vacuum pump that removes air from opened red or white wine bottles to slow oxidation, and Vacu Vin says it keeps wine fresh for up to 10 days.

It needs no batteries. You insert a stopper and pump until a click tells you the vacuum seal is reached. The set includes 2 reusable BPA-free stoppers that fit most standard bottles and are dishwasher safe. It is designed and made in the Netherlands and stores compactly.

Wine lovers who enjoy a glass or two at a time and hate pouring leftovers away.

## Best for red wine: Vinturi V1010 Essential Red Wine Aerator

### Why we like it

The Vinturi V1010 has 4.7 stars from more than 5,800 ratings and is made specifically for red wine. You hold it over a glass and pour through it. It is made from a specially formulated acrylic with an easy-to-grip silicone body, and a filter screen catches cork bits and sediment. It comes with a base, and Vinturi notes the distinctive sound it makes as you pour.
