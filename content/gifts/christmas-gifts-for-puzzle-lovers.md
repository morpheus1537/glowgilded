## How to choose a gift for a puzzle lover

1. **Table space:** Boards and mats let puzzlers work without taking over the dining table.
2. **Piece count:** Match boards and mats to the size they usually do, often 1,000 or 1,500 pieces.
3. **Sorting style:** Trays and drawers keep edges, colors and sections separate.
4. **What happens after:** Glue or adhesive sheets turn a finished puzzle into wall art.
5. **Bundle it:** Pair an accessory with a new puzzle for a gift they can start on Christmas Day.
