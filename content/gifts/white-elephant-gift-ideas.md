## How to win at white elephant

- **Pick broad appeal.** A gift most people would like beats one perfect gift for one person.
- **Mix fun and useful.** The most stolen gifts are funny and practical, like a s'mores maker or Saucemoto.
- **Wrap it well.** A great wrap gets your gift picked first.
- **Stick to the limit.** Check each gift's current price against your group's budget.
- **Add a little extra.** Tuck s'mores ingredients in with the maker or a small candle in with the warmer.

## White elephant rules in one minute

1. Everyone brings one wrapped gift to a shared pile.
2. Players draw numbers to set the order.
3. On your turn, unwrap a new gift or steal an opened one.
4. Anyone whose gift is stolen picks again.
5. Most groups limit each gift to two or three steals.
