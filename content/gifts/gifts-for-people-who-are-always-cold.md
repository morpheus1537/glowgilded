## How to build a warm gift bundle

- **Pair big and small:** Combine a wearable blanket with a hot water bottle for a full cozy-night kit.
- **Add a warm drink:** Tuck in a favorite tea or cocoa. Our [Christmas gifts for tea lovers](/gifts/christmas-gifts-for-tea-lovers/) has ideas.
- **Think about where they get cold:** Office, car, bed or outdoors? Pick the gift that fixes that moment.
- **Include care notes:** For the heated vest, mention to fully charge the battery before first use.

## Buying tips

- **Know their size:** Apparel and slippers need the right fit. Include a gift receipt when unsure.
- **Check the battery details:** For heated clothing, confirm whether the battery and charger are included in the exact listing you choose.
- **Choose washable:** Cozy gifts get used a lot; machine-washable picks are easier to live with.
- **Check the current listing:** Colors and sizes change ratings and availability, so confirm before ordering.
