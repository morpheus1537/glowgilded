## How to choose a gift for an artist

1. **Match the medium:** Pencil, ink, color or paint. Look at what they post or what is on their desk.
2. **Skill level:** Complete kits suit beginners; single-medium sets suit experienced artists.
3. **Consumables win:** Pens, pencils and sketchbooks always get used.
4. **Portability:** A case or travel set helps urban sketchers and students.
5. **Quality cues:** Archival ink, labeled pencil grades and color names are good signs.

## Gift tips for artists

- **Peek at their supplies:** Note brands and pencil grades already in use, then add to the collection.
- **Add a sketchbook:** A fresh sketchbook pairs well with pens or pencils.
- **Think about storage:** A pencil case or desk organizer helps artists who work on the go.
- **Wrap it creatively:** Kraft paper and twine invite a doodle on the package.
- **Bundle small items:** Micron pens make great stocking stuffers alongside our [white elephant gift ideas](/gifts/white-elephant-gift-ideas/).
- **Keep receipts:** Artists are picky; a gift receipt makes swaps easy.
