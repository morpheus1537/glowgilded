## Best overall: Hario V60 Pour Over Coffee Starter Set

### Why we like it

The Hario V60 is the pour-over dripper many coffee shops use, and this starter set includes everything needed except coffee and water: the V60 02 dripper, a glass server and a 40-pack of paper filters. The single large hole in the dripper lets you control the flow, pouring fast for a lighter cup or slower for a richer one.

The set makes 1 to 4 cups (700 mL) and is made in Japan, with a heatproof glass server and BPA-free handle and lid. With a 4.8-star rating from over 11,000 ratings, it is the highest-rated pick here.

### Who it's for

Coffee lovers who are new to pour-over, or anyone who still uses a basic drip machine.

### Pros

- Complete kit with dripper, server and filters
- Single-hole design gives full control over flavor
- Makes 1 to 4 cups
- Made in Japan

### Cons

- Takes a few minutes of hands-on brewing
- A gooseneck kettle helps but is sold separately

## Best for entertaining: Chemex Classic 8-Cup Pour-Over

### Why we like it

The Chemex is a design classic that looks beautiful on any counter. It is made of non-porous borosilicate glass that does not absorb odors or residues, and its thick bonded filters make an exceptionally clean cup.

The 8-cup size (Chemex counts 5 oz as a cup) brews enough for a few people at once, so it is ideal for weekend brunches. The design also lets you cover and refrigerate brewed coffee for reheating without losing flavor.

### Who it's for

Coffee lovers who host, design lovers, and anyone who wants a clean, smooth cup.

### Pros

- Timeless, elegant glass design
- Brews enough for several people
- Non-porous glass does not hold odors
- Very clean-tasting coffee

### Cons

- Filters are not included

## Best upgrade on a budget: Hamilton Beach Fresh Grind Coffee Grinder

### Why we like it

Grinding beans just before brewing is the cheapest way to improve coffee, and the Hamilton Beach Fresh Grind is one of the most popular grinders on Amazon, with more than 64,000 ratings. It grinds enough for up to 12 cups with one press of a button.

The stainless steel grinding bowl is removable and dishwasher safe, the cord hides under the base, and the listing says it is quieter than a leading competitor. It also grinds spices, herbs and seeds.

### Who it's for

Anyone still buying pre-ground coffee.

### Pros

- Simple one-button grinding
- Removable, dishwasher-safe bowl
- Hidden cord storage
- Also grinds spices and seeds

### Cons

- Blade grinder, so grounds are less uniform than a burr grinder
- You control the grind by time, which takes practice

## How to choose a coffee gift

- **Know how they brew:** if they use a drip machine, a pour-over set or grinder is a real upgrade.
- **Check what they drink:** latte lovers will use a frother daily; black coffee drinkers prefer brewing gear.
- **Think about their routine:** commuters get the most from a travel mug.
- **Bundle it:** pair a brewer with filters and a bag of local beans for a complete gift.
