## How to choose a gift for a griller

1. **Think about their style:** Smokers and low-and-slow fans love thermometers, claws and brisket knives. Quick weeknight grillers may prefer tools and a grill basket.
2. **Upgrade what they use most:** A better thermometer or knife gets used every cook.
3. **Consumables are safe bets:** Spice rubs get used up, so a sampler never goes to waste.
4. **Check materials:** Stainless steel tools and baskets last longer and clean up more easily.
5. **Consider easy cleanup:** Dishwasher-safe tools are a bonus after a long cookout.
6. **Year-round use:** Thermometers, knives and claws also work in the kitchen during winter.
