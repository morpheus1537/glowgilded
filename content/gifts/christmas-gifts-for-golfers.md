Golfers do not stop thinking about golf just because the course is frozen. Winter is when many of them quietly lose their touch, so a gift that keeps them practicing at home is one they will actually use. The good news: you do not need to know their handicap, club brand or ball preference to get it right.

1. **For the living room:** A compact putting green they can leave out all winter.

## Best for beginners: Callaway Swing-Easy Golf Swing Trainer

### Why we like it

The Callaway Swing-Easy is one of the most popular training aids around. It promotes a one-piece takeaway and keeps the arms and body in sync throughout the swing. Callaway says it also helps prevent a common fault: a "flying" back elbow that drifts away from the body, leading to a poor swing plane.

It is especially useful for beginners learning fundamentals, but works for any level. It fits right- and left-handed golfers of any size and is small enough to keep in a golf bag. It has 4.5 stars from more than 1,500 ratings.

### Who it's for

New golfers, returning golfers and anyone working on swing consistency.

### Pros

- Teaches a connected, one-piece takeaway.
- Fits right- and left-handed golfers.
- Portable for the range or backyard.
- Great beginner gift.

### Cons

- Focuses on one fundamental only.

## Gift-giving tips

- **Check the space:** Putting greens need about 9 feet of clear floor; full swings need a net and ceiling height.
- **Bundle it:** A putting mirror plus a sleeve of their favorite balls makes a great combo.
- **Think about the 19th hole:** Golfers who like a dram after a round may enjoy our [Christmas gifts for whiskey lovers](/gifts/christmas-gifts-for-whiskey-lovers/).

- **Order early:** Bulky items like mats can take longer to ship in December.
