Heated bird bath or de-icer?

  • Heated bird bath: A complete basin with a built-in heater. Easiest if you do not already own a bath, and you can mount it on a deck railing or stand.
  • De-icer: A small heater that sits in the water of a bath you already own. Easiest if you have a favorite bath or a concrete fountain.

How to choose

  1. Wattage: Lower wattage uses less electricity; higher wattage suits larger or colder setups.
  2. Thermostat: All four here are thermostatically controlled to run only when needed.
  3. Mounting: Deck mount, clamp or ground stand options make placement easier.
  4. Bath material: De-icers should be safe for your bath's material.
  5. Depth and perching: Shallow basins and grippable rims are friendlier to small birds.
  6. Power: Plan for a GFCI-protected outdoor outlet and an outdoor-rated cord.

Winter bird bath tips

  • Keep it clean: Scrub the basin regularly and refill with fresh water.
  • Keep it shallow: One to two inches of water is plenty for most songbirds.
  • Watch the water level: De-icers must stay covered, and water evaporates faster than you think.
  • Use safe power: Plug into a GFCI-protected outlet and use an outdoor-rated cord.
  • Skip antifreeze: Never add chemicals to keep water open.
  • Place near cover: Shrubs and trees nearby help birds escape predators.
  • Track the cold: A wireless indoor-outdoor thermometer shows when temperatures dip below freezing.

Frequently asked questions

Do birds need water in winter?

Yes. Birds need water for drinking and for cleaning their feathers, which helps them stay insulated. When ponds and puddles freeze, open water can be harder to find than food, so a heated bath can attract birds that skip your feeders.

Is a heated bird bath safe for birds?

The heaters in this guide are thermostatically controlled and designed to keep water from freezing rather than making it warm. GESAIL says its de-icer will not heat up the water, and its heating element is wrapped in cast aluminum to protect animals from scalding.

Do heated bird baths use a lot of electricity?

They range from 50 to 150 watts, and thermostats switch them on only when needed. The GESAIL de-icer uses 50 watts, the GESAIL bath uses 75 watts and each Farm Innovators de-icer uses 150 watts.

Can I put a de-icer in any bird bath?

GESAIL says its de-icer works in metal, plastic, stone or glass bird baths and will not melt plastic. Farm Innovators designs its de-icers for bird baths and concrete fountains. Always keep the heater fully covered by water.

Where should I put a heated bird bath?

Place it within reach of a GFCI-protected outdoor outlet, near shrubs or trees where birds can quickly take cover, and where you can see it from a window. Use an outdoor-rated extension cord if needed.
