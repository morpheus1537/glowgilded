How to choose a squirrel-proof bird feeder

  1. Mechanism: Weight-activated shrouds close the ports when a squirrel climbs on. They are the most proven design.
  2. Capacity: Bigger feeders mean fewer refills but more weight to hang. Small feeders suit balconies and small yards.
  3. Ports: More ports mean more birds at once. Perches suit perching birds, and wire shrouds give clinging birds like woodpeckers a grip.
  4. Cleaning: Feeders that come apart without tools are much easier to keep clean.
  5. Chew-proof materials: Exposed parts should be metal or chew-proof so squirrels cannot gnaw their way in.
  6. Mounting: Decide whether you will hang the feeder or mount it on a pole, and check whether a pole adapter is included.

Tips for keeping squirrels away

  • Mind the jump: Hang feeders well away from branches, fences, roofs and decks that squirrels can leap from.
  • Use a baffle on poles: A cone or cylinder baffle stops squirrels from climbing a feeder pole.
  • Clean up spills: Seed on the ground attracts squirrels to the area, so rake up hulls or use a tray.
  • Pick the right seed: Safflower is less popular with squirrels than many mixes.
  • Adjust the spring: On adjustable models, set the shroud to close for squirrels but stay open for your birds.
  • Feed in winter: Birds rely on feeders more when natural food is scarce. Check the temperature with a wireless indoor outdoor thermometer and top up before cold snaps.

Cleaning your bird feeder

Dirty feeders can spread disease among birds, so clean yours every couple of weeks and after wet weather. Empty old seed, take the feeder apart, scrub the tube and ports with hot soapy water or a diluted vinegar solution, rinse well and let every part dry completely before refilling. Feeders that come apart by hand, like the Squirrel Buster and Squirrel Solution models, make this much faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do squirrel-proof bird feeders work?

Most use weight. On Squirrel Buster and Squirrel Solution feeders, the shroud's openings line up with the ports, and when a squirrel climbs on, its weight pushes the shroud down and closes the ports. Squirrels are foiled but not harmed.

Are squirrel-proof bird feeders really squirrel proof?

Weight-activated designs like Squirrel Buster are among the most effective. Placement still matters, so hang the feeder away from branches, roofs and fences that squirrels can jump from.

What seed is best for a squirrel-proof feeder?

Brome recommends sunflower seeds, safflower seeds and dried mealworms for its Squirrel Buster Mini and Squirrel Solution feeders. Black oil sunflower attracts the widest range of backyard birds.

How often should I clean a bird feeder?

Clean it every couple of weeks, and more often in wet weather. Squirrel Buster and Squirrel Solution feeders come apart by hand without tools, which makes cleaning easier.

Will a squirrel-proof feeder keep big birds away?

It can. Brome says squirrels and some large birds are foiled by the weight-activated shroud, and its weight-adjustable spring on the Plus and Standard models lets you choose how much weight closes the ports.
