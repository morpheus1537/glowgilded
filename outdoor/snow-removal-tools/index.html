How to clear snow safely

  1. Start early: clear snow before it gets packed down by cars and footsteps.
  2. Push, don't lift, when you can: pushing is easier on your back than lifting.
  3. Lift with your legs: bend your knees and keep the load small.
  4. Treat ice last: apply ice melt after you've removed the loose snow.
  5. Take breaks: shoveling is hard exercise, especially in the cold.

Buying tips

  • Buy before the storm: shovels and ice melt sell out quickly once snow is forecast.
  • Match tools to your property: a long driveway may need a wider pusher, while steps need ice melt and a scraper.
  • Think about pets and plants: a salt-free melt is gentler on paws and lawns.
  • Store tools near the door: keep them in the garage or by the entryway so they're easy to grab.

Frequently asked questions

What tools do I need for snow removal?

Most homeowners need a good shovel or pusher for snow, an ice scraper or chopper for packed ice, and an ice melt for steps and walkways. If you get heavy snow, a roof rake helps you clear the roof edge from the ground.

Is a snow pusher better than a snow shovel?

A pusher is faster for light, fluffy snow on flat surfaces, while a shovel is better for lifting and throwing heavy snow. The True Temper in this guide has a combination blade that does both.

Is Safe Paw ice melt really safe for pets?

Safe Paw is salt-free and chloride-free and says it won't burn paw pads or irritate skin the way rock salt can. It's still a good idea to wipe your dog's paws after walks and keep pets from eating any de-icer.

How low a temperature does Safe Paw work at?

Safe Paw says it melts ice down to -2°F and covers about 100 square feet per pound.

Do I need a roof rake?

If your area gets heavy snow, a roof rake helps remove snow from the edge of the roof, which can reduce strain on the roof and help prevent ice dams. Always work from the ground and stay clear of power lines.

If your area gets heavy snow, the Snow Joe roof rake helps protect your roof, and the Bully Tools steel ice scraper handles the packed ice that a plastic shovel can't.
