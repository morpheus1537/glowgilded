Outdoor Christmas light ideas to try

  1. Light the path. Line your walkway with C9 pathway lights for a classic, welcoming look.
  2. Project the house. Aim a projector at the front of the house for full coverage without a ladder.
  3. Wrap the porch. Wrap railings and columns at ground level with string lights.
  4. Glow up the garden. Tuck solar stakes into garden beds and around trees.
  5. Highlight the door. Hang a lit wreath on the front door and add lanterns on the steps.
  6. Gather around the fire. If you spend evenings outside, our fire pit accessories for backyard nights guide adds warmth to the glow.

Outdoor lighting safety tips

  • Use only outdoor-rated lights, cords and power stakes outside.
  • Keep plugs and connections off the ground and away from puddles and snow.
  • Don't connect more strands than the manufacturer allows.
  • Put everything on a timer so lights don't run all night.
  • Inspect lights each year and toss any with frayed wires or cracked sockets.

Frequently asked questions

How do you decorate the outside of a house for Christmas without a ladder?

Use light projectors aimed at the house, line walkways with stake pathway lights, wrap porch railings and shrubs at ground level, and add solar stake lights in garden beds. All of these can be installed from the ground.

Are Christmas light projectors worth it?

For a big effect with little effort, yes. A projector can cover the front of a house with patterns in minutes, with no roof work. They work best on light-colored walls and in dark settings.

How do I put outdoor Christmas lights on a timer?

Plug your lights into an outdoor timer or power stake. The DEWENWILS power stake in this guide can turn lights on at dusk and off after 2, 4, 6 or 8 hours, and it can be controlled with a remote from up to 100 feet away in line of sight.

Do solar pathway lights work in winter?

They can, but shorter days and weaker sun mean less charging, so run times may be shorter. Place solar lights where they get the most direct sun and keep the panels clear of snow.

Can you connect multiple sets of pathway lights?

The Brightown C9 pathway lights in this guide allow up to 3 strands to be connected. Check the label on any light set before connecting more strands than the manufacturer allows.

The bottom line

Start with the DEWENWILS power stake timer so your display turns itself on at dusk, then add the Brightown C9 pathway lights for a classic walkway. A laser projector or the double-head snowflake projector covers the house in minutes without a ladder, and the solar pathway lights brighten far corners with no wiring at all.
