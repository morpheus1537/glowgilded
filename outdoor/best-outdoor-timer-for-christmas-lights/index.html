How to choose an outdoor timer for holiday lights

  1. Timer type: Dusk-to-dawn sensors adjust to the season automatically; digital timers set exact times; mechanical pins are simple and reliable.
  2. Countdown options: On at dusk and off after 2 to 8 hours is ideal for holiday lights.
  3. Outlets: Two outlets suit a porch or a couple of strands; power stakes with 6 outlets handle full yard displays.
  4. Cord length: A longer cord gives you more placement options.
  5. Weatherproofing: Look for outlet covers and outdoor-rated cords, and mount the timer off the ground.
  6. Load rating: Check that your lights stay within the timer's rated load.

Safety tips for outdoor Christmas light timers

  • Use outdoor-rated gear only: Timers, cords and lights should all be rated for outdoor use.
  • Mount it high: Hang timers vertically at least 2 feet above the ground, as the listings advise, to keep out rain and snow.
  • Plug into a GFCI outlet: Outdoor outlets should be GFCI protected; test the button each season.
  • Do not overload: Add up your lights' wattage and stay within the timer's rated load.
  • Keep connections dry: Use outlet covers and keep plugs off the ground and out of puddles.
  • Place sensors well: Point photocells toward the sky, away from porch lights that could fool them.
  • Prefer an app? Our best smart plug for Christmas lights guide covers Wi-Fi options.

Which timer type is best for you?

Dusk-to-dawn timers are the easiest for most homes because they follow sunset automatically all season; add a 4- or 6-hour countdown to switch lights off at bedtime. Digital timers like the BN-LINK 7-day are best if you want different schedules on weekends or a vacation mode. Mechanical timers suit anyone who likes a visible, hands-on schedule. For big yards, a multi-outlet stake cuts down on extra extension cords.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of timer do I need for outdoor Christmas lights?

You need a timer rated for outdoor use. Dusk-to-dawn timers with a light sensor are the easiest, digital timers give exact times, and mechanical pin timers are simple and repeat daily.

How do dusk-to-dawn timers work?

A built-in photocell sensor turns lights on when it gets dark and off at dawn. Many, like the BN-LINK and DEWENWILS picks here, can also switch off after 2, 4, 6 or 8 hours.

How long should Christmas lights stay on each night?

Many households run outdoor lights for about 4 to 6 hours each evening, from dusk to bedtime. A countdown timer makes that automatic and avoids leaving them on all night.

Can an outdoor timer get wet?

Outdoor timers are built to be weatherproof, but most listings say to mount them vertically at least 2 feet above the ground to help keep water out of the outlets.

Will a digital timer lose its settings in a power outage?

The BN-LINK 7-day timer has a built-in backup battery that helps preserve its settings after a power interruption or when unplugged.
