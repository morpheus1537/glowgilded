How to choose a camping headlamp

  1. Brightness and modes: A bright mode for trails, a low mode for camp chores and a red mode for night vision.
  2. Battery and charging: USB-C is the most convenient. Check listed runtimes.
  3. Water rating: IPX4 for rain, IPX5 for heavier water, IP67 for submersion.
  4. Weight and fit: Lighter headlamps with soft, adjustable bands are more comfortable for long wear.
  5. Tilt: An adjustable lamp head points light where you need it.
  6. Extras: Motion sensors, lock modes and battery indicators can be handy.

Headlamp tips for camping

  • Charge before you go: Top up every headlamp the night before a trip.
  • Use red light at camp: It keeps your night vision and is kinder to neighbors.
  • Lock it in your pack: A lock mode, like on the Black Diamond, stops it from switching on by accident.
  • Pack a power bank: USB charging makes it easy to recharge on multi-day trips.
  • Keep it warm in winter: Cold drains batteries faster, so keep the headlamp in a pocket or sleeping bag overnight. Pair it with rechargeable hand warmers for cold nights.
  • Point it down: Tilt the lamp head down when talking to others so you do not blind them.

Frequently asked questions

How many lumens do I need for camping?

Around 200 to 400 lumens covers most campsite tasks and trail walking. The Lepro lists 300 lumen LEDs and the Black Diamond Spot 400-R lists up to 400 lumens. Brighter modes drain the battery faster.

What does IPX4, IPX5 or IP67 mean on a headlamp?

These are water ratings. IPX4 handles splashes and rain, IPX5 handles stronger water jets, and IP67 means the light can be submerged. Black Diamond says the Spot 400-R handles full submersion for up to 30 minutes.

Why do headlamps have a red light?

Red light helps preserve your night vision and is less disruptive to others at a shared campsite. All five picks here include a red mode.

Is a motion sensor headlamp useful?

It can be. With the Blukar and LHKNL, you wave your hand to turn the light on or off, which helps when your gloves or hands are dirty.

Can I use a headlamp while it charges?

Some models allow it. LHKNL says its headlamp can charge and stay on at the same time. Check each listing before relying on that feature.
