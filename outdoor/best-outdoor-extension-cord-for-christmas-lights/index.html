How to choose an outdoor extension cord for Christmas lights

  1. Outdoor rating: Look for an outdoor or SJTW-rated jacket that resists moisture, abrasion and sunlight.
  2. Gauge and rating: 16-gauge cords rated 13 amps and 1625 watts handle most LED displays.
  3. Outlets: Multiple or evenly spaced outlets mean fewer cords and splitters.
  4. Color: Green blends into grass and shrubs; black hides along dark trim and mulch.
  5. Cold flexibility: A cord that stays soft in winter is easier to route.
  6. Safety listing: Choose ETL-listed or similarly certified cords.

Outdoor Christmas light safety tips

  • Use a GFCI outlet: Plug outdoor displays into a GFCI-protected outdoor outlet.
  • Do the math: Add up the wattage of everything on a cord and stay under its rating.
  • Keep connections up: Lift plugs off the ground, away from snow and puddles.
  • Do not daisy-chain: Use one longer cord rather than many short ones linked together.
  • Inspect each year: Replace cords with cracked jackets or bent prongs.
  • Prefer smart control? Our best smart plug for Christmas lights guide covers app and voice options.

Cord color: green, black or orange?

Green is best for lawns, shrubs and garland. Black blends into dark trim, railings and mulch. Bright orange is the most visible, which is safer around work areas but stands out in a holiday display. For most Christmas lights, choose green or black.

Frequently asked questions

What gauge extension cord do I need for Christmas lights?

For most LED Christmas light displays, a 16-gauge (16/3) outdoor cord is plenty. All four cords here are rated 13 amps and 1625 watts at 125V; add up the wattage of everything you plug in and stay under the cord's rating.

Can I use an indoor extension cord outside for Christmas lights?

No. Use cords rated for outdoor use, which have weather-resistant jackets. All four cords in this guide are listed for outdoor use, and DEWENWILS is SJTW-rated with outlet covers for wet locations.

How do I keep extension cord connections dry outside?

Use cords with outlet safety covers, keep connections off the ground and away from puddles, and plug into a GFCI-protected outdoor outlet. A cord connection cover adds extra protection.

How long of an extension cord do I need for outdoor lights?

Measure from the outlet to your display along the path the cord will take, then add a little slack. 25 feet covers most front yards; avoid chaining many cords together.

How do I turn Christmas lights on and off automatically?

An outdoor power stake timer like the BN-LINK has a dusk-to-dawn light sensor and countdown timers, plus a remote, so your display turns on at sunset without a trip outside.
