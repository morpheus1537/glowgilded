What makes a good winter cover

  1. Waterproof fabric: Look for heavy 420D or 600D fabric with a waterproof coating or backing.
  2. Taped seams: Taped interior seams stop water from leaking through the stitching.
  3. Vents: Air vents reduce condensation and help stop wind from ballooning the cover.
  4. Secure fit: Straps, buckles and a drawcord hem keep the cover on in storms.
  5. Right size: A cover that is too small will not reach the ground; too big lets wind in.

Winterizing tips

  • Clean and dry first: Wash off dirt and let furniture dry fully before covering.
  • Store cushions inside: Even under a cover, cushions last longer stored indoors.
  • Lift the cover's center: A ball or bucket on the table helps water and snow run off.
  • Clear heavy snow: Brush it off after storms. See our snow removal tools.
  • Check straps monthly: Tighten anything that has loosened in the wind.

Frequently asked questions

Should I cover my patio furniture in winter?

Covering furniture you leave outside helps protect it from snow, ice, moisture and debris, and keeps it cleaner for spring. If you have space, storing cushions indoors and covering the frames gives the best protection.

How do I measure for a patio furniture cover?

Measure the full outside width, depth and height of the furniture. For a dining set, push the chairs in and measure the full footprint, including how far the chair backs stick out. Vailge suggests a cover 1 to 2 inches larger than your furniture.

Do waterproof patio covers cause mold?

Moisture can get trapped under any cover. Built-in vents, like those on the ULTCOVER and Vailge covers, help airflow and reduce condensation. Make sure furniture and cushions are dry before covering, and check under the cover after big storms.

How do I keep patio covers from blowing away?

Tighten every strap and the hem drawcord. All five covers here use straps, buckles or clips plus an elastic hem cord. Kipiea says its reinforced straps can support over 1,200N of force.

Should I remove snow from patio furniture covers?

Yes, brush off heavy snow before it builds up. Pooling water and heavy snow loads can stretch a cover or bend lightweight frames. A soft broom works well; avoid sharp tools that can tear the fabric.
