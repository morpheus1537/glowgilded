How to plan your patio lighting

  • Layer your light: combine overhead ambient light (string lights), task or security light (motion lights) and accent light (path lights). Each layer does a different job.
  • Map your power: note where outdoor outlets are. Use plug-in lights near the house and solar lights where cords would be awkward.
  • Measure before you buy: sketch the run for string lights and add slack. Zigzags across a seating area need roughly twice the length of the space.
  • Mind the color: warm white feels cozy for seating areas, while cool white is better for security and paths.
  • Check weather ratings: look for IP65 or sealed, waterproof sockets for anything that stays outside year-round.

Installation tips

For string lights, run a guide wire between posts or the house and a fence, then clip the strand to it. That takes the strain off the cord and keeps the line straight. Place solar panels where they get the most direct afternoon sun, away from trees and eaves. Test motion lights at night before you mount them permanently, so the sensor catches the path people actually walk.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to light a patio without electricity?

Solar string lights and solar pathway lights. They charge in daylight and turn on at dusk, so there is no wiring. The Brightech solar string lights in this guide have a detachable panel you can stake in the ground or clip to a railing.

How many feet of string lights do I need for a patio?

Measure the path you want the lights to follow, such as the perimeter, a zigzag or straight runs between posts, and add a few extra feet for slack and reaching the power source. A zigzag over a seating area usually needs about twice the length of the space.

Are solar pathway lights bright enough?

They are made for marking a path and adding accent light rather than lighting up a whole yard. For security and brighter coverage, pair them with motion sensor lights such as the HMCITY lights.

Can I dim LED globe string lights?

The 100ft G40 globe lights in this guide are listed as dimmer compatible, but a dimmer is not included. Buy an outdoor-rated plug-in dimmer separately.

How long do solar pathway lights stay on?

DenicMic says its solar path lights charge for 6 to 8 hours and can light up for 10 to 12 hours in summer. Run time is shorter in winter or on cloudy days.
