Tips for buying gifts for coworkers

  • Know the rules. Check whether your workplace has a gift policy, budget or exchange.
  • Keep it equal. If you're giving to several people, give similar gifts so no one feels left out.
  • Stay universal. Skincare, cozy items and entertaining gifts suit most people; skip sizes, strong scents and anything personal.
  • Be careful with alcohol. Only give wine-related gifts to people you know drink.
  • Add a handwritten note. A short thank-you card often means more than the gift.
  • Gifts for the boss or parents? See our Christmas gifts for parents for more grown-up ideas.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for a coworker?

Choose something useful and universal rather than personal. Skincare gift sets, a cozy wearable blanket, a rechargeable candle lighter or an entertaining gift like a charcuterie board work for most people.

Should you buy Christmas gifts for coworkers?

It isn't required, and many workplaces set a budget or do a gift exchange. If you do give gifts, keep them similar in value across the team and avoid anything too personal.

What gifts should you avoid giving coworkers?

Avoid clothing with sizes, strong perfumes, anything with a romantic feel, and alcohol unless you know the person drinks. Gag gifts can also land badly in a professional setting.

Is a wine opener a good gift if I don't know whether my coworker drinks?

Only give wine-related gifts to people you know enjoy wine. For everyone else, a skincare tin, candle lighter or wearable blanket is a safer choice.

What is a good gift for a whole team?

A charcuterie board set can be shared at the next team gathering, or you can buy several Burt's Bees gift sets so everyone receives the same thing.
