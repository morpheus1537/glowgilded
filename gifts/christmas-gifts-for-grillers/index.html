How to choose a gift for a griller

  1. Think about their style: Smokers and low-and-slow fans love thermometers, claws and brisket knives. Quick weeknight grillers may prefer tools and a grill basket.
  2. Upgrade what they use most: A better thermometer or knife gets used every cook.
  3. Consumables are safe bets: Spice rubs get used up, so a sampler never goes to waste.
  4. Check materials: Stainless steel tools and baskets last longer and clean up more easily.
  5. Consider easy cleanup: Dishwasher-safe tools are a bonus after a long cookout.
  6. Year-round use: Thermometers, knives and claws also work in the kitchen during winter.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get a griller who has everything?

Go for upgrades and consumables. A good meat thermometer, a set of new rubs or a quality brisket knife are things even experienced grillers use constantly, and spice sets get used up, so they are always welcome.

What is a good stocking stuffer for someone who loves to grill?

Meat shredder claws, like the Alpha Grillers set, and a BBQ spice sampler are small, practical and fun. Both are highly rated and easy to wrap.

Is a meat thermometer a good gift for a griller?

Yes. A probe thermometer with an alarm, like the TempPro TempTrack, takes the guesswork out of doneness for steaks, chicken and smoked meats, and it is useful year round in the oven too.

What grilling tools does a beginner need?

A beginner needs long tongs, a spatula, a fork, a basting brush and a grill brush. A complete set like the Kaluns 21-piece kit includes all of these, plus skewers, corn holders, an apron and a storage case.

Can you use grilling gifts in winter?

Many can. A meat thermometer, carving knives, meat claws and spice rubs all work indoors in the oven, slow cooker or smoker, so they are useful long before grilling season starts again.
