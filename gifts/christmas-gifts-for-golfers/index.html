Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for a golfer who has everything?

Practice gear is a safe bet because golfers rarely buy it for themselves. A putting green, a putting mirror or a swing trainer helps them keep their stroke sharp through the off-season, and none of it depends on knowing their club preferences.

Can you practice golf indoors in winter?

Yes. Putting is the easiest skill to practice inside on a mat like the PUTT-A-BOUT or SKLZ green. For full swings, a hitting mat like the GoSports Elite works in a garage or basement, but you also need a net and enough ceiling height.

What golf gift is good for a beginner?

The Callaway Swing-Easy is aimed at the fundamentals beginners struggle with. Callaway says it promotes a one-piece takeaway and keeps the arms and body in sync, and it fits both right- and left-handed golfers.

What is a good golf stocking stuffer?

The KINGTOP putting mirror is small at 12 by 6 inches, comes with five tees and a carry bag, and fits in a golf bag. Golf balls, ball markers and tees are other easy stocking stuffers.

Do I need to know their handedness for these gifts?

Not for most. Putting greens and mirrors work for everyone, the GoSports mat has tee holes for right- and left-handed golfers, and the Callaway Swing-Easy fits both.
