How to choose a gift for a nurse

  1. Think about the shift: Long hours on hard floors mean feet and legs need the most help.
  2. Go practical: Nurses appreciate gifts they can use at work, not more clutter.
  3. Check workplace rules: Some units have dress code limits on colors or accessories.
  4. Get sizes right: For socks and insoles, check the size chart or ask a friend.
  5. Splurge or stack: A premium stethoscope is a big gift; socks, insoles and hand cream make a great bundle.

Gift-giving tips for nurses

  • Bundle a shift kit: Socks, insoles and hand cream together feel generous and personal.
  • Add a note: A handwritten thank-you means a lot to someone working the holidays.
  • Think about timing: Night-shift nurses may sleep during the day, so leave gifts at the door.
  • Skip strong scents: Many units discourage fragrance.
  • Include the team: For a unit gift, a snack basket in the break room goes a long way. See our white elephant gift ideas for group exchanges.
