How to choose a gift for grandparents

  • Think personal: gifts that capture memories or connect them with family mean the most.
  • Think comfort: cozy, practical gifts make everyday life a little easier.
  • Consider tech comfort: if they aren't tech-savvy, plan to help with setup or pick a simpler gift.
  • Add a personal touch: a handwritten note or framed family photo makes any gift feel special.

Gift pairing ideas

  • The memory keeper: the life story journal with a nice pen and a framed family photo.
  • The relaxer: the word search pack with reading glasses and a tin of tea.
  • The organizer: the Skylight Calendar preloaded with everyone's birthdays.
  • The spa night: the foot massager with fuzzy socks and a cozy blanket.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get grandparents who have everything?

Give them something personal or something that makes daily life more comfortable. A guided life story journal, a puzzle book set or a heated foot massager are all thoughtful choices that they're unlikely to already own.

What is a good sentimental gift for a grandmother?

A guided memory journal like Tell Me Your Life Story, Grandma lets her record her stories with more than 200 prompts, and the finished book becomes a family keepsake.

Does the Skylight Calendar need a subscription?

No. The calendar syncs with Google, iCloud, Outlook, Cozi and Yahoo and works on its own. Skylight offers an optional Plus Plan that adds extras such as Magic Import and a photo screensaver. It needs Wi-Fi to set up and sync.

Are large print word search books good for seniors?

Yes, for many seniors they are. Large print is easier on the eyes, and word searches are relaxing and low-pressure. The KAPPA pack includes six full-size books with varied themes and solutions in the back.

Is a foot massager a good gift for older parents?

It can be, especially for grandparents who spend a lot of time on their feet or just love being pampered. The Cloud Massage model has adjustable intensity, two heat levels and a remote. Anyone with circulation problems, diabetes or other health concerns should check with a doctor first.

For a gift Grandma will treasure, choose the Tell Me Your Life Story, Grandma journal; it becomes a family heirloom. For puzzle lovers, the KAPPA large print word search pack is a crowd-pleaser. Busy grandparents will love the Skylight Calendar, and the Cloud Massage shiatsu foot massager is a cozy, pampering gift they'll use all winter.
