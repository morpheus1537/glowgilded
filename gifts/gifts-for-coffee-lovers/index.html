Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for someone who loves coffee?

A brewing upgrade they would not buy themselves, like the Hario V60 pour-over starter set, paired with a bag of their favorite beans. For a smaller budget, a handheld milk frother or a leak-proof travel mug gets used every day.

Is pour-over coffee better than drip?

Pour-over gives you more control over how fast water flows through the grounds, so many people find it brighter and more flavorful. It takes a few minutes of hands-on time, which coffee lovers usually enjoy.

Does the Chemex come with filters?

No. The Chemex listing says filters are not included, so add a pack of Chemex bonded filters (FP-1, FC-100, FS-100 or FSU-100) to make the gift ready to use.

Is a blade grinder good enough for pour-over?

A blade grinder like the Hamilton Beach Fresh Grind makes a big flavor upgrade over pre-ground coffee and is easy to use. Serious enthusiasts may later want a burr grinder for more consistent grounds.

How long does the Contigo West Loop keep coffee hot?

According to the listing, up to 11 hours hot or 24 hours cold, and its Autoseal lid locks between sips so it is leak- and spill-proof.
