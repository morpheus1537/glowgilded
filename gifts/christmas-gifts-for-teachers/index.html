How to pick a teacher gift

  1. Think daily use: Pens, bottles and small self-care items get used constantly.
  2. Keep it neutral: Avoid strong scents and personal items unless you know the teacher well.
  3. Check school rules: Some schools have gift limits or policies, especially for group gifts.
  4. Add a note: A few sentences from the student about a favorite moment goes a long way.
  5. Consider the class: If several families want to give, combine into one larger gift.

Teacher gift tips

  • Build a mini basket: Combine pens, lip balm and a handwritten card for a thoughtful set.
  • Go in with the class: A group gift lets families give something bigger, like the Owala bottle.
  • Avoid "apple" overload: Many teachers already have plenty of themed mugs and ornaments.
  • Include support staff: Aides, librarians, bus drivers and front office staff appreciate small gifts too.

Frequently asked questions

What do teachers really want for Christmas?

Many teachers appreciate practical things they use every day, like good pens, a reliable water bottle and small comforts, plus a short handwritten note from the student. Classroom supplies are always useful because many teachers buy their own.

How much should you spend on a teacher gift?

There is no rule, and a thoughtful note matters more than the price. Many families keep individual gifts small, and some classes pool money for one larger group gift. Check whether your school or district has a gift policy.

Are pens a good gift for a teacher?

Yes, especially popular brands like Paper Mate Flair, which the brand itself calls a teacher favorite. Teachers go through pens quickly when grading and planning.

Is a water bottle a good teacher gift?

A good insulated bottle is a practical choice for people who talk all day. The Owala FreeSip keeps drinks cold for up to 24 hours, though Owala notes it is wider than standard cupholders.

What is a good teacher gift from the whole class?

A group gift can combine several smaller items, such as a bottle, a set of pens and lip balm, in one basket with a card signed by the students.
