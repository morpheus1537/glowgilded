How to choose a gift for a cyclist

  1. How they ride: Commuters value lights and phone bags. Road riders value pumps and compact tools. Winter riders need warmth.
  2. Fit and compatibility: Choose items that work with most bikes, such as strap-on lights and universal valve pumps.
  3. Size and weight: Cyclists care about grams and pocket space, so compact gear goes over well.
  4. Rechargeable: USB or USB-C charging beats buying batteries.
  5. Weather resistance: Rain and road spray are part of riding, so look for water-resistant ratings.
