How to choose a gift for a fisherman

  1. Freshwater or saltwater: Lures and tackle are often made for a specific type of fishing, such as freshwater bass.
  2. Gear they already have: If they own a lot of tackle, organizers and tools are safer than another rod.
  3. How they fish: Bank, boat and kayak anglers have different needs. A sling bag suits anglers who hike or paddle to their spot.
  4. Garage or storage space: A wall rod rack is a great fit for anglers with rods leaning in a corner.
  5. Timing: An advent calendar needs to arrive before December 1 to use as intended.
  6. Fun factor: Mystery boxes and calendars add surprise, which is great for the off-season.
