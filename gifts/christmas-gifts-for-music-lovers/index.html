How to choose a gift for a music lover

  1. Listener or player: Listeners love turntables and decor; players appreciate gear like tuners and new instruments.
  2. Their format: Vinyl collectors, streamers and concertgoers all enjoy music differently.
  3. Skill level: A beginner-friendly instrument works for everyone; serious musicians may already have pro gear.
  4. Space: A turntable needs a shelf; earplugs and tuners fit in a stocking.
  5. Giftability: Products with gift boxes or carry cases are easy to wrap.
