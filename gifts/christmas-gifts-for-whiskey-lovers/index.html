How to choose a gift for a whiskey lover

  1. Neat or on the rocks: Neat drinkers love tasting glasses like the Glencairn. On-the-rocks drinkers appreciate tumblers, stones or ice ball molds.
  2. Home bar style: Crystal glassware suits a classic bar cart, while a smoker kit suits someone who loves experimenting with cocktails.
  3. Presentation: Most picks here come in a gift box or carton, which saves you wrapping time.
  4. Everyday use: Dishwasher-safe glassware is more likely to be used often.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who loves whiskey?

Barware they will use every time they pour is a safe bet. A set of Glencairn tasting glasses, heavy crystal rocks glasses, whiskey stones or ice ball molds all suit whiskey drinkers without you having to guess their favorite bottle.

Are Glencairn glasses good for bourbon?

Yes. Glencairn designed its glass for single malts and aged blends, but the tapered mouth and wide bowl work for any whiskey you want to nose and taste neat, including bourbon.

Do whiskey stones work better than ice?

Whiskey stones chill a drink without melting, so they do not dilute it. Kollea says 2 to 3 of its stainless steel cubes bring 250 ml of whiskey to about 64°F in 45 seconds. Ice chills colder and adds a little water, which some drinkers prefer.

How does an electric cocktail smoker work?

The Smokpub kit sits on top of a glass and burns a small amount of wood chips with a built-in heating system instead of a butane torch. Smoke fills the glass and adds a smoky aroma to the drink.

Is it okay to give barware if I do not know their favorite whiskey?

Yes, and it is often easier than buying a bottle. Glasses, stones and ice molds work with any whiskey, and they last long after the holidays.
