How to choose a gift for a puzzle lover

  1. Table space: Boards and mats let puzzlers work without taking over the dining table.
  2. Piece count: Match boards and mats to the size they usually do, often 1,000 or 1,500 pieces.
  3. Sorting style: Trays and drawers keep edges, colors and sections separate.
  4. What happens after: Glue or adhesive sheets turn a finished puzzle into wall art.
  5. Bundle it: Pair an accessory with a new puzzle for a gift they can start on Christmas Day.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get someone who loves puzzles?

Accessories make the best gifts because puzzle lovers usually already have plenty of puzzles. A rotating puzzle board, roll-up mat, sorting trays or puzzle glue all solve real frustrations like lost pieces, crowded tables and finished puzzles with nowhere to go.

Is a puzzle board or a puzzle mat better?

A board like the ALL4JIG gives a rigid, rotating surface with drawers and a cover, so it suits dedicated puzzlers. A roll-up mat like the Becko is easier to store and lets you clear the table in minutes, but the puzzle needs careful rolling.

What size puzzle board do I need for a 1,000-piece puzzle?

Check the finished size of the puzzle against the board. The ALL4JIG board in this guide is rated for 1,500 pieces at about 24.9 by 33.6 inches, and the winter scene puzzle here finishes at 27.6 by 19.7 inches.

How do you preserve a finished jigsaw puzzle?

You can brush puzzle glue like Mod Podge Puzzle Saver over the front, which dries clear and glossy, or stick adhesive sheets like the Buffalo Games Puzzle Saver to the back. Adhesive sheets keep the original finish and avoid glue cleanup.

Are puzzles a good gift for grandparents?

Yes, puzzles and accessories are a popular, screen-free gift for all ages. A puzzle with lettered backs, like the winter scene puzzle here, makes sorting easier for anyone.
