How to choose a gift for a gamer

  1. Know the platform: PC, PlayStation, Xbox or Switch matters for headsets and controllers.
  2. Upgrade the setup: Stands, lighting and cleaning tools work with any system.
  3. Think comfort: Long sessions are easier with comfortable audio and eye-friendly habits.
  4. Add nostalgia: Retro gaming merch is a hit with adults who grew up with classic consoles.
  5. Mix big and small: Pair one main gift with a stocking stuffer.

Gift tips for gamers

  • Peek at their setup: Note the console, headset and controllers before you buy.
  • Avoid buying games blind: Many players own games digitally. A gift card is safer if you want to give a game.
  • Bundle small gifts: Coasters, a cleaning kit and glasses make a fun themed bundle.
  • Think about the room: Lighting and organization upgrades are welcome in any game room.
  • Keep receipts: Headsets are personal, so a gift receipt helps.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for a gamer who has everything?

Accessories that upgrade their setup are a safe bet, such as a headset stand with charging ports, a keyboard cleaning kit or fun collectibles like the Paladone NES cartridge coasters. They work no matter which games they play.

Will the Logitech G733 work with PS5 and Nintendo Switch?

Logitech says the G733 is built for gamers on PC, PS5, PS4 and Nintendo Switch, and it plays in stereo on PS4. Check the listing for the exact connection details for your console.

Are gaming gifts OK for adults as well as teens?

Yes. Many adult gamers love practical upgrades and nostalgic gifts. The NES coasters, for example, feature classic games like The Legend of Zelda and Donkey Kong that many grown-ups grew up with.

What is a good stocking stuffer for a gamer?

A keyboard and electronics cleaning kit or a pair of blue light glasses fits easily in a stocking. Both are useful for anyone who spends hours at a desk.

How do I choose a gaming gift if I do not know their console?

Pick something that works with any setup, such as a headset stand, coasters or a cleaning kit. If you choose a headset, check which platforms it supports first.
