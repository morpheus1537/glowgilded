How to choose a gift for a wine lover

  1. Think about their habits: Do they often leave wine in the bottle? A saver or stopper helps. Do they drink mostly reds? An aerator is a natural fit.
  2. Keep it practical: Tools that work with any bottle get used more than novelty gadgets.
  3. Check cleaning: Dishwasher-safe pieces are easier to live with.
  4. Size the gift: Stoppers and pourers are great stocking stuffers; aerators and pumps work as a main gift.
  5. Bundle it: Pair a tool with a bottle they love for a complete present.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who loves wine?

Useful accessories make great gifts because they get used with every bottle. A wine saver pump like the Vacu Vin, an aerator like the Rabbit or Vinturi, or a set of airtight stoppers are popular, well-rated choices.

Do wine aerators really work?

Aerators mix air into wine as it pours, which is meant to open up aroma and flavor without waiting for it to breathe in a decanter. How much difference you notice depends on the wine and your taste.

How long does wine last after opening with a vacuum stopper?

Vacu Vin says its pump and stoppers keep wine fresh for up to 10 days by removing air to slow oxidation. Results vary by wine, and storing bottles upright and cool helps.

Can wine stoppers be used on other bottles?

Many can. Rabbit says its stoppers fit standard 750 mL wine bottles plus soda, sparkling water, craft beer, olive oil and vinegar bottles. Haley's Corker also lists liquors, syrups and oils.

Red wine aerator or universal aerator: which should I buy?

The Vinturi V1010 is made specifically for red wine. The Rabbit aerator pourer is listed for both red and white, so it is the more flexible gift if you are unsure.
