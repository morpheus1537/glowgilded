Frequently asked questions

What is a good cozy gift for a woman who has everything?

Choose something that upgrades a daily ritual: a heated throw for evenings on the couch, a set of oversized mugs for morning coffee, or a smart speaker for music while she cooks.

Is a heated throw blanket safe to use?

Look for certified models with auto shut-off. The VENTIMI throw has an automatic off timer, and like any heated blanket it should be used according to the instructions and never folded while on high heat.

Do pillow covers come with inserts?

Most pillow covers, including the Booque Valley set, are covers only. Check the listing for the size and pair it with inserts of the same size or slightly larger for a full look.

When should I order holiday gifts?

Order early in December, or sooner, so you have time for delivery and any exchanges. Popular sizes and colors can sell out close to the holidays.
