Frequently asked questions

What do you get someone who is always cold?

Choose gifts that warm the spots they complain about most. A wearable blanket covers couch time, a hot water bottle warms hands, feet or back, a heated vest keeps the core warm outdoors, and fleece-lined slippers fix cold feet on hard floors.

Is a heated vest a good gift?

It can be a great gift for someone who commutes, walks a dog or spends time outside in winter. The ORORO vest includes a rechargeable battery and lists up to 10 hours of warmth on the low setting. Check the size chart, because fit affects how well the heat reaches the body.

Are hot water bottles still worth buying?

Yes. They need no batteries or outlets, they are inexpensive, and they are easy to use in bed or on the sofa. Follow the directions, avoid boiling water and use the knitted cover so the bottle is not directly against skin.

What size wearable blanket should I buy?

The Tirrinia hooded blanket measures 70 by 52 inches, long enough to wrap around the legs and feet for most adults. If the person is very tall, check the length against their height before ordering.

What is a good last-minute gift for someone who is always cold?

A hot water bottle or a pair of fleece-lined slippers are easy picks because they do not need batteries or exact clothing sizes beyond shoe size. Pair either one with a warm drink for a simple, thoughtful bundle.
