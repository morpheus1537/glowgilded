How to win at white elephant

  • Pick broad appeal. A gift most people would like beats one perfect gift for one person.
  • Mix fun and useful. The most stolen gifts are funny and practical, like a s'mores maker or Saucemoto.
  • Wrap it well. A great wrap gets your gift picked first.
  • Stick to the limit. Check each gift's current price against your group's budget.
  • Add a little extra. Tuck s'mores ingredients in with the maker or a small candle in with the warmer.

White elephant rules in one minute

  1. Everyone brings one wrapped gift to a shared pile.
  2. Players draw numbers to set the order.
  3. On your turn, unwrap a new gift or steal an opened one.
  4. Anyone whose gift is stolen picks again.
  5. Most groups limit each gift to two or three steals.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good white elephant gift?

The best white elephant gifts are either genuinely useful or genuinely funny, and ideally both. An indoor s'mores maker, a card game, a gag gift like The Screaming Goat, a clever car gadget or a candle warmer lamp all work for mixed groups.

What is the difference between white elephant and Secret Santa?

In Secret Santa, each person buys a gift for one assigned person. In a white elephant exchange, everyone brings one gift for a shared pile, and players take turns picking a new gift or stealing one that has already been opened.

What should you not bring to a white elephant exchange?

Skip anything too personal, like clothing in a specific size, and anything that would embarrass people in a work or family setting. Keep to the group's spending limit, and choose something most people would be happy to take home.

Is The Screaming Goat a good white elephant gift?

It is a classic gag gift. The 3-inch goat sits on a tree stump base and lets out a goat scream when pressed, and it comes with a mini illustrated book of goat facts. Its publisher describes it as a perfect white elephant and gift exchange gift.
