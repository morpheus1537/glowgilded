How to choose an automatic cat feeder

  1. Capacity: A larger hopper means fewer refills, but food stays freshest when you fill only what you need.
  2. Portion size: Small portions (about 7 to 10 grams) give you fine control over each meal.
  3. Meals per day: Most feeders allow up to 6 meals; WiFi models may allow more.
  4. Backup power: Batteries keep meals coming during outages.
  5. Freshness: Look for a sealed lid and desiccant to keep kibble crisp.
  6. Cleaning: Removable, dishwasher-safe bowls and hoppers save time.
  7. Kibble size: Check the maximum kibble size so food does not jam.

Tips for using an automatic feeder

  • Test before you travel: Run the feeder for a few days while you are home to confirm portions and timing.
  • Use fresh batteries: Add backup batteries before any trip.
  • Fill only what you need: Smaller fills keep kibble fresher.
  • Keep water flowing: Pair the feeder with a fountain. See our best stainless steel cat water fountains.
  • Clean weekly: Wash bowls and wipe the hopper to remove oily residue.
  • Arrange check-ins: Feeders do not replace a sitter for litter, water and health checks.
  • Watch weight: Portions on a feeder are easy to adjust; ask your vet how much your cat should eat.

Frequently asked questions

Are automatic cat feeders good for dry food?

Yes, most timed feeders are designed specifically for dry kibble. Check the listed kibble size. oneisall allows kibble under 0.5 inch and Petory under 0.7 inch in diameter.

How long can an automatic feeder feed my cat?

It depends on the hopper size and your cat's portions. oneisall says its 5-liter tank feeds two adult cats for 10 days, and Petory says its 16.9-cup hopper can feed cats and small dogs for 20 days. Your cat still needs fresh water and someone checking in.

What happens if the power goes out?

Feeders with dual power keep working on batteries. Petory, IMIPAW and ANDOLL HOME all accept backup batteries, which are not always included, so add them before you travel.

Can an automatic feeder work for two cats?

Yes. The oneisall feeder has two stainless steel bowls placed 16 inches apart so each cat gets the same amount without crowding. You can also use two separate feeders.

Do I need a WiFi cat feeder?

Not necessarily. Timer feeders work without WiFi. A WiFi model like the Frienhund adds a camera, two-way audio and remote schedule changes, which helps if you travel often.
