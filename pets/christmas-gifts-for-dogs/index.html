How to choose a Christmas gift for a dog

  • Match the gift to the dog's personality. Cuddlers and nervous dogs love a bolstered bed; busy, curious dogs need a puzzle; outdoorsy dogs benefit most from a paw cleaner.
  • Get the size right. Bed sizes go by weight and sleeping style, toy sizes by your dog's mouth, and the MudBuster by paw width. When in doubt between two bed sizes, size up.
  • Know the chewing level. Plush toys and faux fur beds are for gentle dogs. For power chewers, pair the gift with a durable rubber chew from our dog toys and grooming essentials guide.
  • Plan for washing. Choose machine-washable bedding, especially for puppies and senior dogs.
  • Skip unsafe extras. Keep chocolate, grapes, raisins, xylitol, tinsel and ribbon away from dogs, and never wrap gifts with food inside if your dog might open them unsupervised.
  • Supervise new toys. Watch the first few play sessions and remove any toy that starts to come apart.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for a dog?

The best Christmas gifts for dogs are things they use every day: a cozy bed, an enrichment toy that makes them think, or a practical care item like a paw cleaner. A calming donut bed and a hide-and-seek puzzle toy are two of the most popular, highly rated choices.

Are calming donut beds really calming for dogs?

Many dogs relax more easily in a donut bed because the raised rim lets them curl up and rest their head, which can feel secure. It is not a treatment for serious anxiety, so talk to your vet if your dog's stress is severe.

Is the Hide-A-Squirrel safe for aggressive chewers?

No. It is a soft plush toy made for searching, carrying and gentle play, and the brand itself says no toy is indestructible. Supervise play and remove any squirrel that gets torn; heavy chewers do better with a rubber toy like the KONG Classic.

What size MudBuster do I need?

Measure the width of your dog's front paw. The Small fits paws up to 2 inches, the Medium fits 1.5 to 2.5 inches and the Large fits 2.5 to 3.5 inches, according to the listing.

What can I put in a dog's Christmas stocking?

Good stocking fillers for dogs are a few safe chew treats, a small puzzle or squeaky toy and a care item such as paw wipes. Avoid chocolate, grapes, raisins, xylitol and cooked bones, which are dangerous for dogs.
