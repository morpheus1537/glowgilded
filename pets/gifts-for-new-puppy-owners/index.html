Why we like it

The first nights in a new home are hard on a puppy. They have just left their mother and littermates, and the quiet, dark crate feels lonely. The Snuggle Puppy is a soft plush puppy with a pouch in its belly that holds a battery-powered heart. Press the button and it gives off a steady pulsing beat; add the included disposable heat pack for warmth. The idea is simple: give the puppy something that feels like a littermate to curl up against.

The medium size is a 12 x 8 inch plush sized for dogs that grow to roughly 25 to 70 pounds, and SmartPetLove also makes a Junior version for small breeds and a Large for 70-pound-plus breeds. The listing says the heart runs for 8 hours with a single press, or continuously when you hold the button. Batteries are included, and the plush is machine washable once you remove the heart and heat pack.

It is one of the most reviewed puppy products on Amazon, with a 4.5-star rating across more than 67,000 ratings. It also happens to look adorable under the tree, with its floppy ears and little red heart tag.

Who it's for

Anyone bringing home a young puppy, especially for crate training, plus rescue dogs that are nervous in a new home and dogs that get anxious during storms, fireworks or travel.

Pros

How to choose a gift for a new puppy owner

  • Ask about the puppy's size. Both the Snuggle Puppy and the KONG Puppy come in different sizes. Buy for the adult size of the breed with the Snuggle Puppy, and for the current mouth size with the KONG.
  • Think about their schedule. A pet camera is most valuable for someone who works outside the home or has a long commute. If they are home all day, a comfort toy or puzzle may be more useful.
  • Solve a real problem. New puppy owners are usually short on sleep and time. Gifts that help with nights, teething or alone time get used every day.
  • Check the Wi-Fi. The Furbo needs 2.4GHz Wi-Fi and a USB plug near where it will sit, ideally on a shelf or table the puppy cannot reach.
  • Coordinate with the family. Puppy parents often get many of the same toys. A quick message to ask what they already have avoids duplicates.

Gift pairing ideas

  • The first-night kit: the Snuggle Puppy plus the KONG Puppy, stuffed with a few of the puppy's kibble for crate time.
  • The working parent bundle: the Furbo 360 with a bag of small, round training treats for treat tossing.
  • The snow-day box: the Dog Brick puzzle plus the KONG Puppy for a full afternoon of indoor enrichment.

Buying tips

  • Order early in December; holiday shipping slows down as Christmas gets closer.
  • Remove the heart unit and heat pack from the Snuggle Puppy before washing or leaving it with a puppy that chews.
  • Use small, round, dry treats about half an inch across in the Furbo so they toss cleanly.
  • Supervise any new toy or puzzle the first few times and remove it if it gets damaged.
  • Keep the gift receipt for sized items like the KONG Puppy in case the puppy grows faster than expected.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for a new puppy owner?

The best gifts for new puppy owners solve the problems of the first few weeks: sleepless nights, teething and leaving the puppy alone. A heartbeat comfort toy like the Snuggle Puppy, a soft rubber teething toy like the KONG Puppy and a treat-tossing pet camera like the Furbo 360 cover all three.

Does the Snuggle Puppy really help puppies sleep?

Many owners say it helps. The Snuggle Puppy has a battery-powered pulsing heart and a disposable heat pack meant to feel like a littermate, and it holds a 4.5-star rating across more than 67,000 ratings on Amazon. Results vary by puppy, and it works best alongside a consistent crate routine.

Do you need a subscription for the Furbo 360 dog camera?

Not for the version we recommend. The Furbo 360° Dog Camera Core Features model includes 1080p live view, 360° rotation, barking alerts, two-way audio, color night vision and treat tossing with no subscription. AI alerts and video history require the optional Furbo Nanny plan, which comes with a 14-day free trial.

What size KONG Puppy should I buy?

Choose the size for the puppy's current mouth and expected adult size. The Medium suits most medium-breed puppies; KONG also makes XS, Small and Large versions. The toy should be big enough that the puppy cannot fit the whole thing in its mouth.

When should a puppy switch from a KONG Puppy to a KONG Classic?

KONG recommends the softer KONG Puppy rubber until about 9 months of age, when adult teeth are in. After that, most dogs can graduate to the firmer KONG Classic.

Is a pet camera worth it for a new puppy?

It is for anyone who has to leave a puppy home for part of the day. A camera lets you see whether the puppy is settled or stressed, talk to them, and get an alert when they bark, which makes the early weeks of alone-time training much less of a guessing game.

The bottom line

The best gift for a new puppy owner is one that makes the first weeks calmer for both the puppy and the person. For most puppies, that starts with the Snuggle Puppy Heartbeat Toy, which helps with the hardest part: the first nights in a new home. If the owner works or travels, the Furbo 360° Dog Camera gives real peace of mind and makes alone-time training easier, with no subscription needed for the core features. And every puppy needs something to chew, which makes the KONG Puppy the easy, inexpensive add-on. For snowy days, add the Dog Brick puzzle. Together they turn the chaos of a new puppy into something closer to a cozy Christmas.
