How to choose a winter coat for a small dog

  1. Fit: Measure chest, neck and back length. Chest girth matters most.
  2. Weather protection: A waterproof or water-resistant shell helps in sleet and wet snow.
  3. Warmth: Fleece linings, fiberfill and sherpa add insulation.
  4. Leash access: A leash hole or D-ring lets you clip on without removing the coat.
  5. Fastenings: Hook-and-loop, snaps and zippers each have trade-offs for wiggly dogs.
  6. Visibility: Reflective strips help on dark winter evenings.

Winter walk tips for small dogs

  • Check the fit: You should fit two fingers under the coat at the chest and neck.
  • Protect paws too: Salt and ice can hurt pads, so wipe paws after walks.
  • Keep walks shorter in extreme cold: Several short outings can be easier than one long one.
  • Use a harness: A front-clip harness, like our no-pull dog harness picks, works well under coats with leash holes.
  • Dry the coat: Hang coats to dry between walks so your dog is not put in a damp jacket.
  • Take it off indoors: Avoid overheating once you are home.

Frequently asked questions

Do small dogs need a winter coat?

Many do. Small, short-haired, very young and senior dogs lose heat faster than large, thick-coated breeds, so a coat helps on cold or windy walks. Watch for shivering or lifting paws as signs your dog is cold.

How do I measure my dog for a coat?

Measure the widest part of the chest, the neck and the back from collar to tail base. Brands like Vecomfy and JoyDaog say to prioritize chest girth and size up if your dog is between sizes.

Should a dog wear a coat with a harness?

Look for a leash hole so you can clip to a harness underneath. FUAMEY has a built-in harness for calm small dogs but recommends a separate harness for dogs that pull or lunge.

Can my dog wear a coat indoors?

It is best to take it off indoors so your dog does not overheat. Coats are for time outside in the cold.

Snaps, zipper or hook-and-loop: which is easiest?

Hook-and-loop straps, like on Kuoser, adjust quickly. Metal snaps, like on JoyDaog, do not catch fur the way zippers can. FUAMEY uses an anti-jam zipper designed not to snag hair.
