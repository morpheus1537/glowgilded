How to pick the right dog toys

  • Size up, not down: a toy that is too small can be a choking hazard. When in doubt, choose the larger size.
  • Match the chewer: gentle chewers can enjoy most toys, while power chewers need durable rubber or nylon.
  • Rotate toys: swapping toys every few days keeps them feeling new and interesting.
  • Inspect regularly: replace any toy that cracks, splinters or becomes small enough to swallow.
  • Mix mental and physical play: a stuffed KONG works the brain, while fetch works the body. Most dogs need both.

Frequently asked questions

What size KONG should I buy?

Choose a size your dog cannot fit entirely in its mouth. The Large suits most dogs of roughly 30 to 65 pounds; check KONG's size chart on the listing for your dog's weight.

Is the Benebone safe for puppies?

Benebone recommends its regular chews for adult dogs and offers puppy versions for young teeth. Always supervise chewing and replace the chew when it becomes small or worn.

How often should I brush my dog?

Short-coated dogs do well with weekly brushing, while double-coated breeds like retrievers and huskies benefit from brushing several times a week, especially during shedding season.

How do I make a KONG last longer?

Stuff it with a mix of kibble and a soft food or dog-safe peanut butter, then freeze it overnight. A frozen KONG can keep many dogs busy for 20 minutes or more.
