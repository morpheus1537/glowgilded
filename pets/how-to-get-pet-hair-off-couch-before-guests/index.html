The 10-minute couch cleanup

  1. Remove cushions and throws. Shake throws outside and toss them in the wash if you have time.
  2. Roll the big surfaces. Use a reusable roller with short back-and-forth strokes on each cushion and the backrest.
  3. Rake the seams. Use a rubber detailer along seams, piping and corners where hair collects.
  4. Vacuum. Use an upholstery attachment to pick up the hair the detailer loosened.
  5. Cover the pet's spot. Lay a washable throw where your pet usually sleeps so the couch stays clean during the party.

Tips to keep the couch clean through the holidays

  • Brush your pet ahead of time: A good brushing a day or two before guests arrive removes loose coat before it lands on the furniture.
  • Wash throws and pet beds: Fresh blankets mean less hair transferring back onto the couch.
  • Keep a roller by the door: Guests can do a quick pass on their coats on the way out.
  • Use a cover: A washable throw on your pet's favorite spot catches most of the shedding.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to get pet hair off a couch?

Use a reusable pet hair roller like the ChomChom with short back-and-forth strokes over each cushion, empty the chamber, then vacuum. Rollers lift loose hair quickly, and a rubber detailer helps with hair that is woven deep into the fabric.

Do reusable pet hair rollers really work?

They have huge followings on Amazon. The ChomChom has more than 200,000 ratings and the ACE2ACE more than 100,000. They work best on taut fabric and may take a few more passes on loose throws or very soft materials.

How do I get cat hair off a microfiber couch?

Roll a reusable pet hair roller across the cushions, then go over seams and corners with a rubber pet hair detailer. A damp rubber glove is a free trick that also helps on microfiber. Test any tool on a hidden area first.

What removes pet hair that the vacuum misses?

Rubber detailers like the Lilly Brush Mini are designed for deeply embedded and vacuum-resistant hair. Use short, rake-like strokes to pull hair to the surface, then vacuum it away.

How can I reduce pet hair before guests come over?

Brush your pet outside or in one room a day or two before, wash throw blankets and pet beds, put a washable throw over your pet's favorite seat, and do a final roller pass right before guests arrive.
