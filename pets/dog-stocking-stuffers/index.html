How to build a dog stocking

  1. Start with a treat: a bag of holiday biscuits fills the top of the stocking.
  2. Add a chew: pick a chew rated for your dog's size and chewing style.
  3. Add an activity: a lick mat keeps them busy while you open gifts.
  4. Skip the danger zone: keep tinsel, ribbon, chocolate and string away from dogs.

Buying tips

  • Match the size: check the weight range on every chew toy.
  • Know your chewer: heavy chewers need tougher products than gentle chewers.
  • Introduce treats slowly: new treats can upset some stomachs, so start small.
  • Supervise: always watch your dog with new chews and toys.
  • Check the current price on Amazon: seasonal treats can sell out in December.

Frequently asked questions

What do you put in a dog's Christmas stocking?

A good dog stocking has a treat, something to chew and something to keep them busy. Holiday biscuits, a durable chew toy and a lick mat you can fill with a spread cover all three.

Are Blue Buffalo Santa Snacks healthy for dogs?

Blue Buffalo says Santa Snacks are made with oatmeal and cinnamon, contain no chicken or poultry by-product meals, and are free from corn, wheat, soy, artificial preservatives and artificial colors. Treats should still be a small part of your dog's daily diet.

What can I put on a LickiMat for my dog?

LickiMat lists yogurt, peanut butter, purées, spreads and wet or raw food. Use a small amount and choose dog-safe foods; avoid anything with xylitol.

Is a LickiMat a chew toy?

No. LickiMat says the Classic mats are not chew toys and you should supervise your pet. For puppies or known chewers, the brand recommends its tougher LickiMat Tuff.

Which chew toy is right for my dog's size?

Hartz says the large Tuff Bone is for dogs 50 to 75 pounds and suits mild to moderate chewers. SPOT says the 6-inch Bam-Bones T-bone is for dogs 30 to 60 pounds and moderate to heavy chewers.

The bottom line

For a stocking every dog will love, combine Blue Buffalo Santa Snacks with the LickiMat 2-pack for a calm holiday morning. Then add a chew that matches your dog: the Hartz Tuff Bone for larger, gentler chewers, or the SPOT Bam-Bones T-bone for medium dogs who chew harder. It's a simple stocking that keeps your dog busy while everyone else unwraps gifts.
