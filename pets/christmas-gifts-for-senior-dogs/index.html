How to choose a gift for a senior dog

  • Notice what has changed. If your dog seeks out sunny patches and heating vents, choose warmth. If they splash water or stoop awkwardly at the bowl, choose the raised feeder. If they chew more gently than they used to, choose the KONG Senior.
  • Measure before you buy. Check your dog's shoulder height for the feeder, and compare your dog's sleeping size with the 24 by 19 inch heated pad.
  • Think about where they rest. The K&H bed is designed for sheltered spaces like garages and covered porches, so it is ideal for dogs who spend time out there.
  • Keep chewers in mind. Skip the heated bed for any dog that chews bedding or cords.
  • Ask your vet about health issues. Comfort gifts are wonderful, but they do not replace a vet's advice for arthritis, dental pain or eating problems.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for an old dog?

The best Christmas gift for an old dog is one that adds comfort to their daily routine: a warm place to rest, an easier way to eat and drink, or a gentle toy that suits older teeth. Our picks are the K&H Lectro-Soft heated pet bed, the Neater Feeder Deluxe raised bowls and the KONG Senior toy, all highly rated on Amazon.

Are heated beds good for senior dogs?

Many older dogs feel the cold more and enjoy extra warmth. The K&H Lectro-Soft is thermostatically controlled and, according to K&H, warms the surface to your pet's normal body temperature only when your pet is lying on it. It is not recommended for dogs that chew, so skip it for a dog that destroys beds, and ask your vet if your dog has health conditions.

Do raised dog bowls help older dogs?

Raised bowls can give a more comfortable feeding position for tall dogs, and Neater Pet Brands says its elevated feeder helps reduce neck and joint strain. The large Neater Feeder Deluxe has an 8-inch feeding height and is recommended for dogs over 40 pounds with a shoulder height over 14 inches. Ask your vet before switching if your dog has a medical condition that affects eating.

What is the difference between a KONG Senior and a regular KONG?

The KONG Senior uses a softer, distinct natural rubber formula that KONG says is designed to be kind to older teeth and gums. It has the same stuffable snowman shape as the Classic, so you can fill it with kibble or a little peanut butter.

How do I choose a gift for a senior dog?

Start with what has changed for your dog: if they seek out warm spots, choose warmth; if they struggle to bend to the bowl or splash water everywhere, choose a raised feeder; if their teeth are sensitive, choose a softer toy. Check sizing carefully, since older dogs are often less tolerant of a bed or bowl that does not fit.
