Buying tips

Choose by weight, teeth and supervision needs

Weight ranges on the two chews are different, and the mat is not a chew at all. Read each label rather than treating a high rating as permission to ignore size or behavior. Keep treats within an appropriate feeding plan, check for dietary restrictions and introduce new items while you can watch. Remove damaged toys and wash food-contact surfaces. Decorations, human snacks, gift ribbon and packaging should stay out of the activity area. Consult a veterinarian about dental issues or unusual reactions.
