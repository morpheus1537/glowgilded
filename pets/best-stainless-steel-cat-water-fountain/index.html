What to look for in a cat fountain

  1. Material: 304 stainless steel is durable and easy to scrub.
  2. Capacity: Larger tanks mean fewer refills, especially with more than one cat.
  3. Noise: Look for a listed decibel rating around 25 to 28 dB for bedrooms.
  4. Filtration: Multi-stage filters catch hair and debris; budget for replacements.
  5. Water window: A level window lets you check water without opening the lid.

Cat fountain care tips

  • Introduce it slowly: Leave the old bowl nearby until your cat is comfortable with the fountain.
  • Keep the water level up: Pumps get louder when water runs low.
  • Clean the pump: Hair and mineral buildup slow the pump; rinse it during weekly cleanings.
  • Use filtered water if you have hard water: It can reduce scale on steel and pump parts.
  • Winter comfort: Cats drink more consistently in warm, draft-free spots; see our winter cat essentials.

Frequently asked questions

Are stainless steel cat fountains better than plastic?

Many owners prefer stainless steel because it is durable and easy to clean. The fountains in this guide use 304 stainless steel, and Veken lists its water-contact parts as BPA-free and top-rack dishwasher safe.

How often should I clean a cat water fountain?

Follow the manufacturer's instructions, but many owners rinse and refill every few days and do a full clean of the basin, pump and filter about once a week. Replace filters on the schedule the brand recommends.

Are cat water fountains noisy?

Good ones are quiet. Veken and ATMZIQXR list about 25 dB for their pumps, and Neareal lists 28 dB or less, which is quiet enough for most bedrooms and living rooms.

How long does a cat fountain last between refills?

It depends on capacity and how many pets you have. Neareal says its 2.2-liter fountain can last up to 7 days, and ATMZIQXR says its 2.6-liter model lasts 7 to 9 days.

Do cats drink more from a fountain?

Many cats prefer moving water, which is why fountain makers design flowing streams. Results vary by cat, so leave the old water bowl out for a while when you introduce a fountain.
