How to choose an orthopedic dog bed for a large dog

  1. Foam type: Memory foam contours to the body. Egg-crate foam spreads weight and adds airflow. Some beds combine both.
  2. Size: Your dog should be able to stretch out fully. Check the listed dimensions and weight limit.
  3. Bolsters: Dogs who curl up or lean love bolsters. Sprawlers may prefer a flat mattress.
  4. Low entry: A low front helps dogs with stiff joints step in easily.
  5. Waterproofing: A waterproof liner protects the foam from accidents and drool.
  6. Washable cover: A removable, machine-washable cover is essential with big, shedding dogs.

Tips for helping a large dog sleep comfortably

  • Place the bed away from drafts: Cold floors and doors make stiff joints worse in winter.
  • Use the bed's full size: If your dog hangs off the edge, size up.
  • Wash covers often: Big dogs shed a lot. Our guide on getting pet hair off the couch has tools that work on dog beds too.
  • Let foam expand fully: Wait the recommended time before deciding a bed is too flat.
  • Watch how your dog sleeps: Curlers like bolsters; sprawlers like flat mattresses.
  • Talk to your vet: If your dog struggles to stand or lie down, ask about joint care.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a dog bed orthopedic?

An orthopedic dog bed uses supportive foam, such as memory foam or egg-crate foam, instead of loose stuffing. The foam spreads your dog's weight evenly and is designed to relieve pressure on joints.

What size dog bed does a large dog need?

Measure your dog from nose to base of tail while lying down and add several inches. WNPETHOME's 42 x 30-inch bed is listed for dogs under 90 lbs, and EHEYCIGA offers an XL 44-inch bed.

Is memory foam or egg-crate foam better for an older dog?

Both support joints. Memory foam contours closely to the body, while egg-crate foam spreads weight and adds airflow. EHEYCIGA combines memory foam with egg-crate foam for a balance of softness and support.

Can you wash an orthopedic dog bed?

Usually you wash the cover, not the foam. All four beds here have removable, machine-washable covers, and the brands say the foam insert should not be washed.

Why does a new dog bed look flat when it arrives?

Foam beds ship compressed. EHEYCIGA recommends letting the bed expand for 24-48 hours, and Sunheir suggests waiting 48 hours after opening.
