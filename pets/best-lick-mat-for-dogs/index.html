How to choose a lick mat for dogs

  1. Where you will use it: Suction cups are essential for bath and grooming time. Flat mats work well on the floor or in a crate.
  2. Material: Most mats are food-grade silicone. LickiMat uses a non-silicone material and offers a tougher Tuff line for chewers.
  3. Pattern: Shallow grooves suit runny foods, deeper ridges suit thick spreads and kibble. Variety keeps dogs interested.
  4. Size: Larger mats suit big dogs and hold more food. Smaller mats suit puppies, small breeds and cats.
  5. Cleaning: Dishwasher-safe mats save scrubbing peanut butter out of grooves.
  6. Chewing: None of these are chew toys. Supervise and remove the mat if your dog starts chewing.

Lick mat tips

  • Choose dog-safe spreads: Avoid peanut butter containing xylitol, and skip chocolate, grapes and onion-based foods.
  • Go thin: A thin layer pressed into the grooves lasts longer than a big blob on top.
  • Freeze for longer sessions: Prep a few mats ahead and freeze them for nail trims or busy evenings.
  • Count the calories: Treats on a mat still count toward your dog's daily food.
  • Supervise and store: Pick the mat up when your dog is done so it does not become a chew toy.
  • Pair with a gentle shampoo: For bath time, see our best dog shampoo for itchy skin guide.

Lick mat filling ideas

Mix plain yogurt with a little pumpkin puree for an easy everyday spread, or mash a few pieces of your dog's regular wet food into the grooves. Soaked kibble pressed into a deeper pattern makes a slower, lower-calorie option. For special occasions, a thin layer of xylitol-free peanut butter topped with a few blueberries keeps most dogs busy. Check with your vet before adding new foods if your dog has allergies or a sensitive stomach.

Frequently asked questions

Are lick mats good for dogs?

For many dogs, yes. Lick mats slow down treats and give dogs a calm, focused activity, and brands like LickiMat say licking helps soothe pets during stressful moments like baths, nail trims, vet visits and fireworks.

What can I put on a dog lick mat?

Spreadable dog-safe foods work best, such as plain yogurt, xylitol-free peanut butter, pumpkin puree, wet food or soaked kibble. Use small amounts and count them as part of your dog's daily food.

Can you freeze a lick mat?

Yes, if the listing says it is freezer safe. LUKITO and MateeyLife say their mats are freezer safe, and freezing makes treats last longer.

Are lick mats safe to leave with a dog?

No. LickiMat and MateeyLife both say their mats are not chew toys and that you should supervise your pet. Remove the mat when your dog finishes or starts chewing it.

How do I clean a lick mat?

Rinse it soon after use, then wash it in warm soapy water or the dishwasher if the listing allows. LUKITO, MateeyLife, Honomastry and Petbank list their silicone mats as dishwasher safe.
