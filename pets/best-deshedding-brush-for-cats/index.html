How to choose a deshedding brush for your cat

  1. Coat length: Deshedding blades work best on short coats. Long-haired cats usually need a rake or slicker that will not pull tangles.
  2. Cat size: FURminator sizes its tools by weight, so choose large for cats over 10 pounds.
  3. Gentleness: Look for a skin guard, rounded or rubber-tipped pins and a light touch.
  4. Cleaning: A release button pushes fur off the brush in seconds.
  5. Your cat's temperament: Nervous cats often accept a soft slicker before a stronger deshedding tool.

How to deshed a cat without stress

  • Start small: Brush for a minute or two at first, then end on a good note with a treat.
  • Go with the grain: Brush in the direction the hair grows, using light pressure.
  • Avoid sensitive spots: Skip the belly, legs and tail unless your cat is relaxed.
  • Do not force mats: Brushing over a tight mat can pull skin. Ask a groomer or vet for help with severe mats.
  • Pick a calm time: Many cats are most relaxed after a meal or nap.
  • Clean up after: Brush on a towel or in an easy-to-vacuum room, then use a lint roller on yourself.
  • Give them a place to scratch: A sturdy post like those in our best cat scratching post for indoor cats guide helps keep claws healthy too.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best brush for a cat that sheds a lot?

A deshedding tool like the FURminator removes loose undercoat that regular brushes miss. For short-haired cats it is our top pick; for long or thick coats, pair a rake with a gentle slicker brush.

How often should I deshed my cat?

Many short-haired cats do well with deshedding about once a week, with extra sessions during seasonal shedding. Keep sessions short and gentle, and brush long-haired cats more often to prevent mats.

Do cats like being brushed?

Many cats enjoy it once they get used to it. Start with short sessions, brush in the direction of hair growth, offer treats, and stop if your cat seems stressed.

Can a deshedding tool hurt my cat?

Used gently, these tools are designed not to cut skin. FURminator adds a curved skin guard and Aumuca uses rubber-tipped pins. Avoid pressing hard, do not brush over mats or irritated skin, and ask your vet about sore spots.

Does brushing reduce hairballs?

Brushing removes loose hair before your cat swallows it while grooming, which many owners find helps. Ask your vet if your cat has frequent hairballs, since they can have other causes.
