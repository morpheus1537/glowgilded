Before you leave: a cat-alone weekend checklist

  1. Run the feeder for a week: Let your cat get used to it and confirm the portions.
  2. Fresh batteries: Install backup batteries in the feeder.
  3. Two water sources: Fill the fountain and leave a backup bowl in another room.
  4. Extra litter boxes: Scoop right before you go and consider adding a second box.
  5. Cat-proof the house: Close doors to risky rooms, secure cords and put away holiday decorations a cat might chew.
  6. Ask a backup person: Give a neighbor or friend a key in case anything goes wrong.
  7. Know your limit: For longer trips, kittens, seniors or cats on medication, book a sitter.

Frequently asked questions

Can I leave my cat alone for a weekend?

Many healthy adult cats can manage a short weekend with reliable food, fresh water and clean litter, but it depends on your cat's age, health and habits. For longer trips, or for kittens, seniors and cats on medication, arrange a sitter or a friend to visit.

How long can a cat stay home with an automatic feeder?

It depends on the feeder's capacity and your cat's portions. The oneisall listing says a full 5 L tank feeds two adult cats for about 10 days, and the Petory listing says its 4 L tank can feed cats and small dogs for about 20 days. A feeder handles food only, so water and litter still matter.

Do automatic cat feeders work during a power outage?

The three feeders here list battery backup. VOLUAS and Petory both use three D-cell batteries as a backup, and Petory says meal settings are remembered when power returns. Install fresh batteries before you leave.

Is a cat water fountain better than a bowl when I am away?

A fountain holds more water and keeps it moving and filtered. The Veken C1 holds 95 oz. Many owners also leave a regular bowl as a backup in case the pump stops.

Should I test an automatic feeder before a trip?

Yes. Set it up at least a week before you go so your cat learns where meals come from, and confirm the portions and timing are right while you are still home to fix them.
