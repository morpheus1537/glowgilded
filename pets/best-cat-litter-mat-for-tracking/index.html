Types of cat litter mats

  1. Coiled mesh mats: Thick vinyl coils and grooves grab litter. Soft underfoot, easy to vacuum or shake out.
  2. Honeycomb double-layer mats: Litter falls through a top layer with large holes into a sealed bottom layer, then you pour it back into the box.
  3. PVC grooved mats: A firmer surface with deep channels and borders to hold litter.
  4. Silicone mats: Smooth and wipe-clean with raised edges. Less trapping power but very easy to clean.
  5. Size matters: The bigger the mat, the more steps it covers, and the more litter it catches.

Tips to reduce litter tracking

  • Make the mat big enough: Your cat should take at least two steps on the mat when leaving the box.
  • Try a high-sided or top-entry box: These help contain litter that gets kicked out.
  • Keep the box clean: Cats dig and kick more in a dirty box, scattering more litter.
  • Trim long paw fur: Long-haired cats carry more litter between their toes.
  • Clean the mat regularly: A full mat stops trapping and starts spreading litter.
  • Upgrade other essentials: A fresh water source helps too; see our stainless steel cat water fountain picks.

Frequently asked questions

Do cat litter mats really stop tracking?

A good mat catches much of the litter that sticks to your cat's paws as it steps out of the box, but no mat catches every granule. Combining a mat with a low-tracking litter and a large enough mat to cover several steps works best.

What size litter mat do I need?

Choose a mat that extends at least one or two cat steps beyond the box exit. Small mats around 24 by 15 inches suit a single box in a tight space, while 35 by 24 inches or larger suits big boxes or multiple cats.

Which is better, a honeycomb or a mesh litter mat?

Honeycomb mats like the Pieviev let litter fall through to a bottom layer that you empty back into the box. Coiled mesh mats like the Gorilla Grip grab litter in thick coils and grooves and can be vacuumed or shaken out.

Are litter mats waterproof?

Most are designed to keep liquids from passing through. Pieviev calls its bottom layer waterproof and urine proof, Amazon Basics uses a waterproof PVC backing, and the Garoopion silicone mat has high raised edges.

How often should I clean a cat litter mat?

Empty or vacuum loose litter every day or two, then wash the mat with mild soap and water about once a week or whenever it gets soiled. Let it air dry fully before putting it back.
