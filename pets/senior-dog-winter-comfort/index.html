Winter care tips for senior dogs

  • Block drafts: Move beds away from doors, windows and cold tile floors. A rug under the bed helps.
  • Layer warmth: Put a self-warming mat on top of or inside a supportive bed.
  • Shorten walks: Two or three short walks are often easier than one long cold one.
  • Prevent slips: Older dogs struggle on icy paths and slick floors. Runners and rugs indoors can help.
  • Watch for changes: Stiffness, reluctance to climb or more time sleeping can be worth a check-up with your vet.

Buying tips

  • Measure your dog: Measure length lying down for beds, chest for sweaters and the height of the furniture for stairs.
  • Prioritize washability: Senior dogs may have more accidents; washable covers make life easier.
  • Combine items: A supportive bed plus a warming layer covers both comfort and warmth.

Frequently asked questions

How do I keep my senior dog warm in winter?

Give them a supportive bed away from drafts, add a self-warming layer for extra insulation, and use a sweater for short-coated or small dogs on walks. Shorter, more frequent walks are often easier for older dogs in cold weather.

Is a self-warming pet mat safe for older dogs?

Self-warming mats like the FurHaven ThermaNAP have no electrical parts; the listing says they reflect the pet's own body heat. That makes them a simple option, but always watch for signs your dog is too warm and give them space to move off.

Are dog stairs better than a ramp for old dogs?

It depends on the dog. Stairs take less floor space and work for many small and medium dogs, while some dogs with significant mobility issues do better on a gentle ramp. Ask your vet if your dog has joint problems.

What kind of bed is best for an older dog with stiff joints?

Look for supportive foam, a low entry and bolsters for head support. The Bedsure orthopedic bed uses egg-crate foam with a lowered entrance, which the listing says protects knees and improves access.

Do older dogs need sweaters?

Small, thin-coated and senior dogs often feel cold sooner. A well-fitting sweater can help on walks; measure chest size carefully, because most sizing issues come from the chest measurement.
