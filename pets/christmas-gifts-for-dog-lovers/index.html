How to choose a gift for a dog lover

  • Think about their routine. If they walk their dog twice a day, a hands-free leash will get far more use than another squeaky toy. If they are sentimental, the paw print ornament will mean more than anything practical.
  • Get the dog's size right. Leashes, beds and harnesses are sized by weight. Ask casually, or check a recent photo, before you buy a sized item.
  • Mind the timing. A DNA test makes a great Christmas gift, but results take a few weeks. Tuck a handwritten note in the box saying "results coming in January" so the wait feels like part of the fun.
  • Make the keepsake together. Paw print kits are easier with two people: one to hold the dog calmly and one to press the paw. Offer to help, and it becomes a shared holiday memory.
  • Pair with something for the dog. Dog lovers love gifts that include their dog. Add a toy or a few treats from our dog stocking stuffers guide and you have a complete gift.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get a dog lover for Christmas?

The best Christmas gifts for dog lovers make life with their dog easier or more meaningful: a hands-free leash for daily walks, a paw print keepsake ornament, or a dog DNA test that reveals breed mix and genetic health risks. Practical gifts that get used every day are the safest choice.

Is a dog DNA test a good gift?

Yes, especially for owners of mixed-breed or rescue dogs who often wonder about their dog's background. The Embark kit is a cheek swab done at home and mailed back with a prepaid label, and the listing says results generally arrive in 2 to 4 weeks.

Paw print ornament tips: how do you get a clear print?

Knead and roll the clay smooth, then press your dog's paw down gently and evenly, with a helper holding the dog calm. The Pearhead clay can be reshaped and pressed again if the first try smudges, so you can retry until you like the impression.

What size hands-free dog leash should I buy?

Choose by your dog's weight. The iYoShop leash we picked is the Large size for dogs from 25 to 120 pounds, and the listing offers a Medium for dogs from 8 to 25 pounds. The waist belt adjusts from 27 to 48 inches.

Are hands-free leashes safe for dogs that pull?

They work best for dogs that already walk reasonably well on a leash. A strong puller can throw you off balance when the leash is at your waist, so use the traffic handle for close control and keep working on loose-leash training.
