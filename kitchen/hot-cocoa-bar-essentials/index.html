How to set up a hot cocoa bar

  1. Pick a spot. Use a corner of the counter or a sideboard near an outlet for a kettle or milk steamer.
  2. Set up the base. Cocoa envelopes, hot water or hot milk, and mugs.
  3. Add toppings. Mini marshmallows, crushed candy canes, chocolate chips, whipped cream and cinnamon in small jars.
  4. Add the tools. A frother, spoons and stirrers, napkins, and a dish for used spoons.
  5. Label it. A small sign or chalkboard tells guests how to build their cup.

Buying tips

  • Plan quantities: count one or two cups per guest for an evening party.
  • Offer one non-dairy option: keep a carton of oat or almond milk on hand for guests who skip dairy.
  • Keep it warm: a kettle or milk steamer near the bar keeps drinks hot all night.
  • Watch the kids: keep hot water and steamers out of small hands and let an adult pour.
  • Check the current price on Amazon: cocoa and holiday treats often go on sale and sell out in December.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need for a hot cocoa bar?

At minimum you need a good cocoa mix, a way to heat water or milk, mugs, and a few toppings like mini marshmallows, whipped cream, crushed candy canes or chocolate chips. An organizer for packets and stirrers keeps the station tidy when guests serve themselves.

How do you make hot cocoa creamier?

Use hot milk instead of water and whisk it well. A frother or milk steamer blends the cocoa evenly and adds a light foam. SIMPLETASTE suggests milk with more than 3% fat for the best froth.

Can you make Ghirardelli hot cocoa with water?

Yes. Ghirardelli says you simply add hot water to the premium hot cocoa envelopes. Many people use hot milk for a richer cup.

How many marshmallows do you need for a hot cocoa bar?

One 10 oz bag of mini marshmallows goes a long way for a family or a small party. For a big crowd or a party that runs all evening, buy two bags.

Can a handheld milk frother mix cocoa powder?

Yes. Zulay lists hot chocolate, matcha and powdered drinks among the uses for its handheld frother, which helps break up clumps right in the mug.

How do you keep a hot cocoa bar organized?

Use a compartment caddy for packets, stirrers and cups, put toppings in small jars or bowls with spoons, and set out a mug of clean spoons and a small dish for used ones.
