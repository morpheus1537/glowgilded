How to choose a baking gift

  • Match the gift to what they bake most. Cake people will love the Bundt pan or turntable, while pie and cookie bakers will use a French rolling pin every week in December.
  • Upgrade a tool they already use. Most bakers own a rolling pin and some kind of cake pan; a better version of a daily tool is more welcome than a gadget for a recipe they never make.
  • Think about skill level. The Bundt pan makes a beautiful cake with no decorating skills. The turntable kit suits someone who wants to learn, and the French pin rewards a little practice.
  • Check storage space. A rolling pin fits in a drawer, but a 12-inch Bundt pan and an 11-inch turntable need cabinet space.
  • Read the care instructions. Wood should be wiped clean, not soaked. For nonstick bakeware, gentle washing with a soft sponge helps protect the coating.

Holiday baking tips for using these gifts

  • Grease Bundt pans carefully. Brush softened butter or shortening into every flute and dust with flour, or use a baking spray with flour, then let the cake cool in the pan for about 10 to 15 minutes before turning it out.
  • Chill dough before rolling. Cold pie and cookie dough sticks less and holds its shape. Roll from the center outward and give the dough a quarter turn every few strokes.
  • Crumb coat layer cakes. Spread a thin first layer of frosting on the turntable, chill the cake for 15 to 20 minutes, then add the final coat and smooth it with the straight scraper as you spin.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who loves to bake?

A well-made tool they will reach for every holiday season is the safest choice. A cast aluminum Bundt pan, a tapered French rolling pin and a cake turntable with frosting spatulas are all highly rated, useful for many recipes and unlikely to duplicate a basic baking set.

Is a Nordic Ware Bundt pan worth it?

For regular bakers, yes. The Nordic Ware Anniversary Bundt pan is cast aluminum for crisp detail and even browning, has a PFAS- and PTFE-free silicone nonstick coating, is oven safe to 400°F and holds 15 cups. It has a 4.8-star average from more than 8,700 ratings.

Is a French rolling pin better than a rolling pin with handles?

Many bakers prefer it for pie and cookie dough. A tapered French pin has no handles, so your palms rest on the wood and you can feel the dough and adjust pressure. Pins with handles can be easier for people with wrist or grip issues.

Cake turntable: do beginners really need one?

It is the tool that makes the biggest difference to smooth frosting. Spinning the cake while you hold a spatula or scraper still is much easier than walking around the cake. The Kootek kit adds two spatulas and three scrapers, so a beginner has the basics in one box.

What size Bundt pan is standard?

Most Bundt recipes are written for a 10- to 12-cup pan. The Nordic Ware Anniversary pan holds 15 cups, so it fits standard recipes with room to spare; Nordic Ware says to fill it no more than three-quarters full to avoid overflow.

How do you care for a wooden rolling pin?

Wipe it with a damp cloth, dry it right away and never soak it or put it in the dishwasher. The Karryoung listing suggests wiping it with mineral oil now and then to bring back the wood finish.
