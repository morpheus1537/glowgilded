Soup making tips for winter

  • Build flavor first. Brown onions, carrots and celery in the pot before adding liquid. Even in a slow cooker, a quick sauté makes a big difference.
  • Season at the end. Broth reduces as it cooks, so add most of the salt once the soup is done.
  • Blend carefully. Keep the immersion blender's blade fully submerged to avoid splatter and start on a low speed.
  • Cool before freezing. Let soup cool, then fill containers leaving about an inch of headspace so they don't crack or pop their lids.
  • Label everything. Write the soup name and date on the lid with a marker.
  • Prep faster. A good knife speeds up all that chopping; see our best chef knife for home cooks.

Slow cooker or stock pot?

Use the slow cooker for soups that improve with long, gentle cooking, like beef stew, bean soups and chili, or when you'll be out of the house. Use the stock pot for faster soups, homemade broth you'll skim as it cooks, and anything you want to brown and simmer in one vessel on the stove. Many cooks use both: stock in the pot on the weekend, soup in the slow cooker on a weekday.

Frequently asked questions

What equipment do you need to make soup?

At minimum you need a large pot, a ladle and containers for leftovers. A slow cooker makes long-simmered soups hands-off, and an immersion blender lets you purée creamy soups in the pot instead of transferring hot liquid to a blender.

What size stock pot is best for soup?

An 8-quart stock pot is a good all-round size for a family, with room for a whole chicken for stock or a double batch of soup to freeze. Smaller 4- to 6-quart pots suit couples.

Is an immersion blender better than a regular blender for soup?

For most home cooks, yes. An immersion blender purées soup directly in the pot, so there is less mess and no need to move hot soup in batches. A countertop blender can make a slightly smoother texture.

How long does homemade soup last in the freezer?

Most broth-based soups keep well in the freezer for about 2 to 3 months. Cool soup before freezing, leave headspace in the container for expansion and label it with the date.

Can you put deli containers in the freezer?

The deli containers in this guide are listed as freezer, microwave and dishwasher safe. Let hot soup cool before filling them and leave room at the top for expansion.
