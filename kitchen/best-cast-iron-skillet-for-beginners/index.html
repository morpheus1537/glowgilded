How to choose your first cast iron skillet

  1. Size: 10 to 12 inches covers most cooking. Bigger pans are heavier.
  2. Pre-seasoning: Choose a pan seasoned at the factory so you can cook right away.
  3. Handles: A helper handle or dual handles make a heavy pan easier to lift.
  4. Lid: A lid lets you braise, simmer and steam.
  5. Weight: Cast iron is heavy. If wrist strength is a concern, start smaller.
  6. Care: Every pan here needs hand washing, drying and a light coat of oil.

Cast iron care tips for beginners

  • Cook fatty foods first: Bacon, burgers and shallow-frying help build seasoning.
  • Clean while warm: Rinse, scrub with a brush or sponge, and use a little soap if needed.
  • Dry completely: Water causes rust. Dry with a towel, then warm the pan on the stove for a minute.
  • Oil lightly: Wipe a thin layer of oil over the surface while the pan is warm.
  • Skip the dishwasher and soaking: Both strip seasoning and cause rust.
  • Use a handle holder or mitt: Cast iron handles get hot in the oven and on the stove.
  • Go easy on long acidic simmers at first: Tomato sauces can wear new seasoning.

What to cook first

Start with something forgiving: fried eggs with plenty of butter, smash burgers, roasted potatoes or skillet cornbread. For bigger projects, a Dutch oven for bread baking is the natural next piece of cast iron, and a sharp chef knife makes prep faster.

Frequently asked questions

What size cast iron skillet should a beginner buy?

A 10 to 12-inch skillet is the most useful first pan. A 12-inch pan like the Lodge fits family meals and roasting, while a 10.25-inch pan is lighter and easier to handle for one or two people.

Do I need to season a new pre-seasoned cast iron skillet?

No. Pre-seasoned pans like Lodge, Utopia Kitchen and Cuisinel are ready to cook out of the box. The seasoning improves with use; after washing, dry the pan completely and wipe it with a thin coat of oil.

Can you use soap on a cast iron skillet?

Yes, a little mild dish soap is fine on a seasoned pan. Utopia Kitchen recommends hot soapy water and a sponge once the pan has cooled. Avoid soaking it and never put it in the dishwasher.

Why does my new cast iron pan have a rust-colored spot?

Lodge says a spot that looks like rust on a new pan is usually oil that has not fully carbonized and will disappear with use. If you do see real rust, scour it with steel wool, wash, dry completely and rub with vegetable oil.

Does cast iron work on induction and glass cooktops?

Yes. Cast iron works on induction, gas, electric and glass tops, plus the oven, grill and campfire. Lift the pan rather than sliding it on glass to avoid scratches.
