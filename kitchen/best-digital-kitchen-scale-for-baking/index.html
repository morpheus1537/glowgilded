How to choose a kitchen scale for baking

  1. Capacity: An 11 lb (5 kg) scale covers most recipes. Bread bakers and big-batch cooks may want 22 or 33 lb.
  2. Precision: Look for 1 gram increments, which every scale here offers.
  3. Units: Grams are the baker's standard. Ounces, pounds and ml or fluid ounce modes are handy for recipes written in other units.
  4. Tare function: Essential for weighing ingredients into a bowl. All five have it.
  5. Readable display: A backlit screen, or a display that sits beyond the platform, stays visible under a big mixing bowl.
  6. Power: Most use AAA batteries. A rechargeable model saves buying batteries.
  7. Easy cleaning: A stainless steel top or sealed controls wipe clean after a flour spill.

Baking scale tips

  • Weigh in grams: Grams are more precise than ounces and easiest for scaling recipes up or down.
  • Tare between ingredients: Add flour, tare, add sugar, tare, and you can mix in one bowl.
  • Use a flat, hard surface: Scales can read inaccurately on a towel or an uneven counter.
  • Place the bowl first: Put your bowl on the scale before turning it on or pressing tare.
  • Replace weak batteries: Odd readings are often a sign the batteries are low.
  • Pair with good tools: A scale works best with sturdy bowls and pans, and our Dutch oven picks for bread baking are a natural match for the 22 lb Greater Goods.

Frequently asked questions

Do you really need a kitchen scale for baking?

A scale is the easiest way to bake consistently, because a cup of flour can vary a lot depending on how you scoop it. Weighing in grams gives you the same amount every time and means fewer measuring cups to wash.

What precision do you need in a baking scale?

For most home baking, 1 gram increments are enough. Every scale in this guide reads in 1 gram steps, which covers flour, sugar, butter and even yeast in bread-sized batches.

What does the tare button do on a kitchen scale?

Tare resets the display to zero with a bowl or container on the scale, so you weigh only the ingredient. You can tare again between ingredients to add everything to one bowl.

What capacity kitchen scale should I buy?

An 11 lb (5 kg) scale handles most cookie and cake recipes. Choose a 22 lb or 33 lb scale like the Greater Goods or CROWNFUL if you bake large batches of bread, use a heavy Dutch oven, or weigh big holiday roasts.

Can a kitchen scale measure milk and water?

Several can. The Amazon Basics, Ultrean, CROWNFUL and Greater Goods scales list ml or fluid ounce modes, and the Ultrean and CROWNFUL add a milk ml setting.
