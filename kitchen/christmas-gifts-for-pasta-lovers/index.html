How to choose a gift for a pasta lover

  • Start with what they already own. If they have no pasta tools, the pasta machine is the gift that changes how they cook. If they already have a machine, the ravioli mold and drying rack fill the gaps.
  • Think about the shapes they love. Fans of long noodles will use the drying rack every time. Anyone who orders ravioli or tortellini at restaurants will get the most from the ravioli mold.
  • Check their counter. A clamp-on pasta machine needs a sturdy counter or table edge with a lip the clamp can grip. Check that their counter has one, or plan to use a sturdy wooden table.
  • Bundle for a bigger moment. All three tools together make a complete "pasta night" kit. Add a bag of 00 flour or semolina, a wedge of parmesan and a handwritten recipe card for a gift that feels personal.

Tips for the first homemade pasta night

  • Rest the dough. Wrap egg pasta dough and let it rest at room temperature for about 30 minutes before rolling. It rolls out smoother and tears less.
  • Roll gradually. Start at the widest setting, fold the sheet and run it through a few times, then move one setting thinner on each pass instead of jumping ahead.
  • Use semolina for dusting. A little semolina or flour on sheets, cut noodles and the ravioli frame keeps everything from sticking.
  • Cook fresh pasta briefly. Fresh noodles often cook in just 2 to 4 minutes in well-salted boiling water, so taste early.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who loves pasta?

A tool that lets them make fresh pasta at home is the most memorable choice. A hand-crank pasta machine is the centerpiece gift, while a ravioli mold or a pasta drying rack is a smaller, useful add-on that most home cooks do not already own.

Is the Marcato Atlas 150 worth it?

For anyone who will make pasta more than a few times a year, yes. It is made and assembled in Italy from chrome-plated steel, rolls sheets up to 150 mm (about 6 inches) wide, cuts fettuccine and tagliolini, and has a 4.6-star average from more than 27,000 Amazon ratings.

Can you put a pasta machine in the dishwasher?

No. Marcato says the Atlas 150 is not dishwasher safe. Let leftover dough dry for about 30 minutes, then brush it off with a dry brush. Do not wash the rollers with water.

How do you keep ravioli from sticking to a ravioli mold?

Dust the metal frame well with flour or semolina, or give it a light coat of nonstick spray, before you lay down the first pasta sheet. Many reviewers of the Bellemain mold say this is the step that makes the finished ravioli drop out cleanly.

Do you need a pasta drying rack for fresh pasta?

You do not strictly need one, but it helps. Hanging long noodles keeps them from clumping while you roll and cut the rest of the batch, and lets you dry pasta partly before cooking or storing it.

Can I use a ravioli mold with store-bought pasta sheets or dumpling wrappers?

Yes, as long as the sheets are thin and pliable enough to stretch into the cups. Fresh sheets you roll yourself usually seal best; dry them for a minute or two less if they feel sticky.
