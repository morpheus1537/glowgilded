How to choose glass containers for leftovers

  1. Seal: Locking lids with silicone gaskets are best for gravy, soup and sauces.
  2. Sizes: A mix of small and large containers handles both a scoop of cranberry sauce and a whole tray of stuffing.
  3. Oven and microwave use: Check which parts are oven-safe and how lids should be used in the microwave.
  4. Stacking: Uniform or modular shapes save fridge space.
  5. Glass type: Borosilicate and tempered glass are both common; follow the maker's temperature guidance.
  6. Lid care: Some lids are dishwasher-safe, others are hand-wash only.

Tips for storing holiday leftovers

  • Cool quickly: Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Shallow containers help food cool faster.
  • Portion before storing: Split big dishes into meal-sized containers so you only reheat what you will eat.
  • Label and date: A strip of masking tape and a marker helps you track the 3-4 day fridge window.
  • Freeze extras early: Turkey, gravy and soup freeze well; leave room for liquids to expand.
  • Avoid thermal shock: Do not move glass straight from the freezer to a hot oven, and do not add cold liquid to hot glass.
  • Turn leftovers into soup: Turkey stock and vegetable scraps make a great next-day meal; see our winter soup-making essentials.

Glass container care

Wash lids gently and dry seals fully before storing to avoid trapped moisture. Remove silicone gaskets now and then to clean underneath. Inspect glass for chips or cracks and replace any damaged containers.

Frequently asked questions

Are glass containers better than plastic for leftovers?

Glass resists stains and odors, does not hold onto tomato sauce or curry smells, and can usually go from fridge to microwave. Plastic is lighter and cheaper, so many people use glass at home and plastic for packed lunches.

Can glass food storage containers go in the oven?

Many glass bases can, but the lids usually cannot. Rubbermaid lists its Brilliance glass bases as oven-safe up to 450°F; always remove plastic lids before the oven and check each set's instructions.

Can you freeze food in glass containers?

Yes, if the set is listed as freezer-safe, which all five here are. Leave headspace for liquids to expand, cool food before freezing and avoid moving a container straight from the freezer into a hot oven.

How long do Thanksgiving leftovers last in the fridge?

The USDA advises eating refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days, or freezing them for longer storage. Refrigerate food within 2 hours of cooking.

Can you microwave glass containers with the lid on?

Usually only with the lid vented or unlatched. Rubbermaid's Brilliance lids have vents for microwaving with the latches open, while M MCIRCO says its plastic lids should not go in the microwave.
