What to look for in a hand mixer for cookies

  1. Power: Thick cookie dough needs more muscle. Look for a listed wattage or a boost button.
  2. Low starting speed: A gentle first speed keeps flour and sugar in the bowl.
  3. Attachments: Beaters handle most jobs. Dough hooks help with stiff dough, and a whisk is for whipped cream and meringue.
  4. Storage: A snap-on case or bag keeps beaters from getting lost.
  5. Easy release: A one-touch eject button keeps your hands clean.
  6. Cleanup: Dishwasher-safe attachments save time during big baking days.

Hand mixer tips for better cookies

  • Soften the butter first: Cold butter strains the motor and mixes unevenly. Room-temperature butter creams faster.
  • Start low: Begin on the lowest speed when adding flour, then increase.
  • Use the boost wisely: QuickBurst or a high speed helps for a few seconds when dough gets thick, but avoid overmixing.
  • Fold in chips by hand or on low: This keeps chips whole and the dough tender.
  • Switch to dough hooks for stiff dough: If the beaters struggle, hooks share the load.
  • Eject over the sink: Releasing beaters over the sink keeps your counter clean.

Hand mixer vs. stand mixer

A hand mixer is cheaper, lighter and easy to store, and it is enough for most cookie, cake and frosting recipes. A stand mixer frees your hands and handles large batches and frequent bread dough better. If you bake a few dozen cookies at a time, a hand mixer is usually all you need. Frequently asked questions

Can a hand mixer handle cookie dough?

Yes. A hand mixer creams butter and sugar well and can stir in chips on low speed. For very stiff dough, a model with more power or dough hooks, like the Hamilton Beach 62636J or KitchenAid KHM926, helps.

How many speeds does a hand mixer need?

Five or six speeds cover most baking. Extra low speeds, like the three SmoothStart speeds on the Cuisinart HM-90S, help keep flour from flying when you start mixing.

Hand mixer or stand mixer for holiday baking?

A hand mixer is cheaper, smaller and easy to store, and it is enough for most cookie, cake and frosting recipes. A stand mixer is better for big batches and frequent bread kneading.

Are hand mixer beaters dishwasher safe?

For these picks, yes. Hamilton Beach and KitchenAid list their attachments as dishwasher safe. Always unplug the mixer and wipe the body by hand.

What does QuickBurst mean on a Hamilton Beach mixer?

It is a button that gives an extra burst of power at any speed, which helps when dough gets thick, for example after adding chocolate chips or oats.
