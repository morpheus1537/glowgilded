How to choose a chef's knife

  • Weight and balance: forged knives like the Wüsthof and Henckels feel heavier and more solid. Stamped-style knives like the Victorinox feel light and nimble. Neither is better; pick the feel you like.
  • Handle grip: if your hands are often wet or you cook a lot of meat, a textured or rubbery handle (Victorinox, Mercer) is easier to control.
  • Care: check the listing. Victorinox and Henckels say dishwasher safe; Mercer and Amazon Basics say hand wash only. Hand washing and drying right away is best for every knife.
  • Bolster: a full bolster adds weight and protects your fingers but can make sharpening the heel harder. A semi bolster or none keeps the whole edge usable.
  • Plan for sharpening: even the best knife dulls. A honing steel used regularly and a sharpener or professional sharpening every few months will keep any of these cutting well.

How to keep your knife sharp longer

Always cut on wood or plastic boards, never glass, stone or plates. Store the knife on a magnetic strip, in a block or with a blade guard, not loose in a drawer. Wash it by hand when you can, dry it right away, and hone it often so the edge stays straight between sharpenings.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best chef knife for a home cook?

For most home cooks, the Victorinox Fibrox Pro 8-inch is the safest pick: it has a huge number of top ratings, a non-slip handle and is easy to care for. If you want a heavier forged knife, look at the Henckels Classic or Wüsthof Classic.

Is an 8-inch chef knife the right size?

Yes, 8 inches is the most common and versatile chef knife length for home kitchens. It is long enough to slice a big onion or a roast and short enough to control on a standard cutting board.

Are chef knives dishwasher safe?

Some listings say yes, including the Victorinox Fibrox Pro and Henckels Classic, while Mercer and Amazon Basics say hand wash only. Even when allowed, hand washing and drying right away is gentler on any blade.

What is the difference between a forged and a stamped knife?

A forged knife is shaped from a single block of heated steel and usually has a bolster and more weight, like the Wüsthof Classic. A stamped knife is cut from a sheet of steel and is usually lighter and cheaper. Both can be excellent; it comes down to how you like a knife to feel.

How often should I sharpen a chef knife?

Hone the edge often with a honing steel to keep it straight, and sharpen it whenever it starts to slip on tomato skins or onions. For many home cooks that means a few times a year.
