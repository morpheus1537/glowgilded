How to choose a power bank for your iPhone

  • Capacity: 10,000mAh is about two iPhone charges and fits a pocket. 20,000mAh is roughly double, but heavier.
  • Charging speed: higher USB-C output (watts) refills your phone faster. Look for a listed figure, like 30W or 45W, rather than vague "fast charging" claims.
  • Cable type: iPhone 15 and newer use USB-C. Older iPhones need a USB-C to Lightning cable.
  • Built-in vs. detachable cable: built-in cables are impossible to forget; detachable ones are easy to replace if they fray.
  • Flying: keep power banks in your carry-on and check your airline's rules.
  • Laptop support: if you also want to charge a laptop, look for 60W or more from a single port.

Power bank care tips

Do not leave a power bank in a hot car or in direct sun, and avoid charging it under a pillow or blanket. Recharge it every few months even if you are not using it, and stop using any power bank that swells, gets unusually hot or is physically damaged.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best portable charger for an iPhone?

For most iPhone owners, a compact 10,000mAh charger with fast USB-C output, like the INIU 10000mAh 45W, is the best balance of size and power. If you travel a lot, a 20,000mAh model like the Anker Zolo gives you several full charges.

How many times can a 10,000mAh power bank charge an iPhone?

Usually about two times. Anker says its PowerCore Slim 10000 provides 2 charges for an iPhone 15, and INIU says its 10,000mAh charger gives nearly 2 full charges for a smartphone. Exact results depend on your model and battery health.

Do these power banks work with older Lightning iPhones?

Yes, but you need the right cable. The chargers here use USB-C, so an iPhone 15 or newer can plug straight in, while an iPhone 14 or older needs a USB-C to Lightning cable.

Can I bring a power bank on a plane?

Power banks generally have to go in your carry-on, not checked luggage. INIU describes its 10,000mAh model as TSA-compliant, and Anker says its 20,000mAh 87W bank is approved for airline travel. Check your airline's rules before you fly.

Is a magnetic MagSafe power bank better than a cable power bank?

Magnetic packs are convenient because they snap on, but cable power banks usually charge faster and hold more for their size. If speed and capacity matter most, a USB-C power bank like the ones in this guide is the better value.
