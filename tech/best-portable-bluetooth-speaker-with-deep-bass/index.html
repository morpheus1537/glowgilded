What makes a speaker sound bassy

  1. Driver size and count: Bigger woofers and dedicated subwoofers move more air for deeper bass.
  2. Bass tuning: Features like JBL AI Sound Boost, Soundcore BassUp and Sony Extra Bass shape the sound for fuller lows.
  3. Power: Higher wattage helps a speaker stay full at volume outdoors, though bigger speakers are heavier.
  4. Placement: Setting a speaker near a wall or on a table naturally reinforces bass; open grass weakens it.
  5. Battery: Bass-heavy music at high volume drains batteries faster, so longer listed playtime helps.

Buying tips

  • Match size to space: A compact speaker suits a picnic blanket; a backyard party needs a larger speaker with more power.
  • Check the water rating: IP67 and IPX7 handle submersion for a short time; IP68 adds stronger protection, per JBL.
  • Think about charging: A built-in power bank is useful on camping trips and beach days.
  • Pair for bigger sound: If you may add a second speaker, stick with one brand's pairing system.
  • Use the EQ: App EQ settings can add or tame bass to suit your music.
  • Respect volume: Keep volume reasonable around neighbors and wildlife, especially at campsites.

Frequently asked questions

Which portable Bluetooth speaker has the best bass?

For sheer output, the Soundcore Boom 2 Plus lists dual woofers and up to 140W max with BassUp 2.0. For an all-rounder with rich bass and a longer track record of drop protection, the JBL Charge 6 is our top pick.

Does a bigger speaker mean more bass?

Usually. Larger speakers have room for bigger drivers, passive radiators or a dedicated subwoofer, which move more air at low frequencies. Small speakers like the Sony XB23 use tuning such as Extra Bass to sound fuller for their size.

Are these Bluetooth speakers waterproof?

Yes. The JBL Charge 6 is rated IP68, the Sony XB23 and Bose SoundLink Flex are IP67, and both Soundcore Boom models are IPX7 and floatable, per their listings.

Can I connect two speakers for stereo sound?

Most of these can. JBL and Soundcore Boom 2 Plus use Auracast, Bose offers Stereo and Party Mode with compatible Bose speakers, and Sony's Party Connect syncs up to 100 compatible speakers.

Can a Bluetooth speaker charge my phone?

The JBL Charge 6, Soundcore Boom 2 and Boom 2 Plus all have a built-in power bank for charging a phone. The JBL cannot charge laptops, per the listing.
