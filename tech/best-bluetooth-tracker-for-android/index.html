First, pick the right network

A tracker is only as useful as the network of phones that can spot it when it is far away. For Android users there are three main options:

  • Samsung SmartThings Find: Works with Samsung Galaxy phones and tablets. Best if your whole household uses Galaxy devices.
  • Google Find Hub: Built into Android, so it works across many brands of Android phones. Third-party tags like UGREEN's support it.
  • Tile: Uses its own app and network, and works on both iPhone and Android, which helps in mixed households.

Buying tips

  • Match the network to your phone: Galaxy owners should look at SmartTag2. Other Android phones should look for Google Find Hub support. Mixed households can use Tile.
  • Check the battery type: Coin-cell tags need a new battery about once a year; rechargeable tags need a USB-C charge.
  • Think about shape: Round fobs suit keys and bags. Card shapes suit wallets.
  • Use them for travel: Place a tag inside checked luggage before you fly home for the holidays.
  • Respect privacy: Use trackers only for your own belongings. Major networks include features that warn people about unknown trackers traveling with them.

Frequently asked questions

Do AirTags work with Android?

Apple AirTags are designed for the Apple Find My network and iPhone. Android users should choose a tracker built for Google Find Hub, Samsung SmartThings Find or the Tile app instead, like the picks in this guide.

What is the best tracker for Samsung phones?

The Samsung Galaxy SmartTag2 is built for Galaxy phones and uses the SmartThings Find app. The listing for this 4-pack says it is compatible with Galaxy devices only, and it supports ultra-wideband (UWB) for precise finding on compatible Galaxy phones.

Does Google Find Hub work with third-party trackers?

Yes. Google's Find Hub works with compatible tags from several brands. The UGREEN Finder Duo in this guide lists Find Hub support on Android and Apple Find My on iPhone, but it can only be connected to one platform at a time.

Do Tile trackers need a subscription?

No. The Tile app lets you ring your Tile nearby and see its last known location for free. Tile offers optional Premium plans that add features such as smart alerts and item reimbursement.

Can I use a Bluetooth tracker for luggage?

Yes, they are popular for checked bags during holiday travel. Put the tag inside the suitcase, not on the outside, and remember that location updates depend on nearby phones on the same network.

The bottom line

If you have a Samsung Galaxy phone, the Galaxy SmartTag2 4-pack is the best Bluetooth tracker for Android, with UWB and strong water resistance. For any other Android phone, the rechargeable UGREEN Finder Duo works with Google Find Hub. Choose Tile Mate for mixed iPhone and Android homes, and Tile Slim for your wallet or passport.
