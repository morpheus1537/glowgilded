How to choose a MagSafe charger stand

  1. How many devices: Single stands charge a phone; 2-in-1 adds earbuds; 3-in-1 adds an Apple Watch.
  2. Charging standard: Qi2 stands like the two Anker models list 15W charging with magnetic alignment.
  3. Angles and orientation: Rotation and tilt let you use landscape StandBy mode or prop the phone for calls.
  4. Lights: If it lives on a nightstand, look for dim, switchable or sleep-friendly indicators.
  5. Power adapter: Check whether one is included and what wattage the stand needs.
  6. Case compatibility: Thick non-magnetic cases weaken the magnetic hold and charging.

Tips for a better charging setup

  • Check your adapter: Many stands need a 20W to 30W or higher USB-C adapter for full speed.
  • Go caseless or MagSafe: Thick, non-magnetic cases weaken alignment and slow charging.
  • Try StandBy mode: Turn your iPhone sideways on a stand to use it as a bedside clock.
  • Mind the lights: Choose a stand with dimmable or switchable lights for the bedroom.

Frequently asked questions

Do MagSafe-compatible stands work with any iPhone?

Magnetic stands like these snap to iPhone 12 and newer. The Hevanto listing says iPhone 8 through 11 and the 16e need a MagSafe case for magnetic alignment, and most listings recommend charging without a case or with a MagSafe case.

What is Qi2 and does it matter?

Qi2 is a wireless charging standard with built-in magnetic alignment, similar to MagSafe. Both Anker MagGo stands here list Qi2 15W charging for compatible iPhones.

Can I use StandBy mode with a charging stand?

Yes. StandBy mode needs the iPhone charging and turned on its side, so a stand that supports landscape orientation works well. The Anker MagGo 360 rotates a full 360 degrees and the Ouotoo stand supports both portrait and landscape.

Do I need a special power adapter?

Often, yes. Anker says a 30W or higher adapter is required for the best experience with its MagGo 2-in-1 stand, which ships with a cable but no adapter. The Hevanto stand includes an adapter.

Will a charging stand light keep me awake?

Several stands include gentle or switchable lights. The Ouotoo night light can be turned off or dimmed with a tap, and the Voice-tank and Hevanto stands list sleep-friendly indicators.
