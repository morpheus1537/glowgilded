How to choose a USB-C charger for a laptop and phone

  1. Wattage: 65W covers many thin-and-light laptops. Choose 100W for larger laptops, and 30W if you only charge phones, tablets and earbuds.
  2. Number of ports: A single port is smallest. Three ports let you charge a laptop, phone and earbuds together, but power is split between ports when several are in use.
  3. Port types: Two USB-C ports plus one USB-A port covers newer devices and older cables.
  4. Size and plug: GaN chargers are much smaller than old laptop bricks, and a foldable plug makes them easier to pack.
  5. Cable: Some chargers include a USB-C cable and some do not. For laptops, use a cable rated for the charger's wattage.
  6. Safety features: Look for temperature monitoring and protection against over-voltage, over-current and short circuits.

Tips for charging a laptop and phone from one charger

  • Check your laptop's charger rating: Match or exceed the wattage of the charger that came with your laptop for full-speed charging.
  • Use the right cable: A cable rated for 60W or 100W is needed to get full laptop charging speed.
  • Know how power is shared: On multi-port chargers, the laptop gets less power when phones and earbuds are plugged in too.
  • Pack one charger, not three: A single multi-port charger and a universal travel adapter cover most trips abroad.
  • Carry a backup: For days away from outlets, pair your charger with a portable charger for iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Can one USB-C charger charge both my laptop and phone?

Yes, if the laptop charges over USB-C Power Delivery. A 65W charger like the UGREEN Nexode 65W or Anker 735 can charge many USB-C laptops and phones, and multi-port models charge them at the same time, sharing the power between ports.

How many watts do I need to charge a laptop over USB-C?

Many ultrabooks like the MacBook Air run well on 65W, while bigger laptops like a 16-inch MacBook Pro benefit from 100W. Phones, tablets and earbuds need much less, so a 30W charger is plenty for them.

What is a GaN charger?

GaN (gallium nitride) chargers use a newer type of semiconductor that lets the charger be smaller and run efficiently. UGREEN and Anker use GaN in several of the chargers in this guide, which is why they are much smaller than older laptop bricks.

Is it safe to charge a phone with a 65W or 100W laptop charger?

Yes. USB-C Power Delivery chargers negotiate with the device and only send the power it asks for, so a phone will draw its normal charging speed from a higher-watt charger.

Do these chargers come with a cable?

The Anker 317 100W includes a 6-foot USB-C to USB-C cable. The Anker Nano II 65W listing says the cable is not included, so check each listing and plan to use a cable rated for your laptop's wattage.
