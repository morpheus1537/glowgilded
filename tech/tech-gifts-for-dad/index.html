How to choose a tech gift for dad

  • Start with his routine: what does he already do every day? Read, walk, drive, work in the garage? Pick the gadget that improves that.
  • Keep setup simple: a gift he can use within five minutes of unwrapping is more likely to be used for years.
  • Check compatibility: know his phone's charging port before buying a power bank or cable.
  • Think durability: if he is outdoors a lot, waterproof ratings like IP67 matter more than extra features.
  • Bundle it: a Kindle plus a gift card, or a speaker plus a power bank, makes a bigger present without a big jump in price.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good tech gift for a dad who has everything?

Pick something that upgrades a habit he already has. A reader gets a Kindle, a dad who is always playing music in the garage gets a rugged speaker, and the one whose phone always dies gets a compact power bank. Useful beats flashy.

Is a Kindle a good gift for an older dad?

Often, yes. The Kindle Paperwhite has a glare-free, high-contrast display, an adjustable white-to-amber light for reading in any light, and no social media or notifications to manage, so it is simple to pick up and read.

Do I need to know what phone he has before buying these?

For the earbuds and speaker, no, as they connect over Bluetooth. For the power bank, check his charging port. The Anker model here has a built-in USB-C cable, which suits recent iPhones and most Android phones. Older iPhones with a Lightning port need their own cable.

Which of these is best for a dad who is outdoors a lot?

The JBL Flip 6 is the obvious pick. The listing says it is IP67 waterproof and dustproof and plays for up to 12 hours on a charge. Pair it with the Anker power bank if he camps or fishes away from outlets.

What is a good budget tech gift for dad?

The Soundcore P20i earbuds and the Anker power bank are both well-reviewed, everyday tools that usually sit at the lower end of the price range for this list. Check current prices on Amazon, since they change often during the holidays.
