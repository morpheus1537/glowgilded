How to choose a phone tripod

  1. Height: 60 inches or more lets the camera reach eye level for group shots.
  2. Phone fit: Check the clamp width against your phone in its case.
  3. Remote range: Listed ranges here run from 30 to 100 feet.
  4. Stability: Aluminum legs and a wider stance resist tipping, especially outdoors.
  5. Portability: Selfie-stick tripods fold small; full camera tripods are steadier but bulkier.
  6. Extras: A cold shoe adds a light or mic; a 1/4-inch screw fits cameras.

Tips for better holiday photos with a tripod

  • Set the camera at eye level: It is more flattering than shooting up from a table.
  • Use the timer and the remote: Burst mode plus a remote gives you a few shots to choose from.
  • Mind the light: Face windows or warm lamps; our video call lighting setup guide covers simple fixes.
  • Spread the legs fully: Wider legs mean less wobble, especially on carpet.
  • Keep the phone charged: Video drains batteries fast; a portable charger for iPhone helps on long shoots.
  • Pair the remote first: Connect it in Bluetooth settings before everyone gathers.

Frequently asked questions

Do phone tripod remotes work with iPhone and Android?

Yes, the Bluetooth remotes in this guide are listed for both. Atumtek lists iOS 7.0 and Android 5.0 or newer. Pair the remote in your phone's Bluetooth settings, then open the camera app and press the button.

How tall should a phone tripod be for group photos?

Taller is better for groups, so the camera sits at or slightly above eye level. Models here extend to 62 inches (EUCOS), 64 inches (Liphisy), 72 inches (UBeesize) and 75 inches (XXZU).

Can I use a phone tripod for video calls?

Yes. A tripod keeps the phone steady at eye level for calls, live streams and recipe videos. UBeesize and EUCOS also have a cold shoe for adding a small light or microphone.

Will a big phone fit in a tripod holder?

Check the clamp width. EUCOS fits phones 2.2 to 3.8 inches wide, Atumtek up to 3.54 inches, and UBeesize says its clamp holds phones up to 5.7 inches wide. Measure your phone with its case on.

Can a phone tripod hold a camera?

Many can with the standard 1/4-inch screw mount. The XXZU tripod lists DSLR compatibility and a max load of 17.64 pounds vertical, while UBeesize lists digital cameras, action cameras and webcams.
