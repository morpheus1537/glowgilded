How to choose a smart bulb for Alexa

  1. Alexa support: All five work with Alexa. If others in your home use Google Assistant or Apple Home, check which bulbs support those too.
  2. Hub or no hub: Every bulb here works over Wi-Fi without a hub. Philips Hue can add a Hue Bridge for more features.
  3. 2.4GHz Wi-Fi: Most smart bulbs need a 2.4GHz network, not 5GHz.
  4. Color and white range: Look for millions of colors plus tunable whites, from warm amber to cool daylight.
  5. Brightness and base: These are A19 bulbs with a standard E26 base and roughly 800 lumens, similar to a 60W bulb.
  6. Extras: Music sync, schedules, sunrise and sunset modes, and energy monitoring vary by brand.
  7. Pack size: Packs range from single bulbs to 4-packs, so plan how many rooms you want to light.

Smart bulb tips

  • Use 2.4GHz Wi-Fi: Join your phone to the 2.4GHz network during setup if your router splits bands.
  • Leave the switch on: A smart bulb cannot respond to Alexa if the wall switch cuts its power.
  • Name rooms clearly: Group bulbs by room so "Alexa, turn off the bedroom" works.
  • Set a wake-up routine: A slow sunrise schedule helps on dark winter mornings, and our sunrise alarm clock picks go even further.
  • Schedule away mode: A routine that turns lights on and off while you travel helps the house look lived in.
  • Check fixtures: These are standard A19 E26 bulbs, so check enclosed fixtures and lamp sizes before you buy.

Frequently asked questions

Do smart light bulbs need a hub to work with Alexa?

No, not the bulbs in this guide. Amazon Basics, Kasa and Linkind state that no hub is required, Govee connects through its app, and Philips Hue Essential works through the Hue app, with an optional Hue Bridge for extra features.

Do smart bulbs work with 5GHz Wi-Fi?

Most do not. Amazon Basics, Kasa and Linkind list 2.4GHz Wi-Fi only, and Govee notes it does not support 5G. Check that your router has a 2.4GHz network before buying.

Can smart bulbs be used with a regular dimmer or smart switch?

Generally, no. Keep the wall switch on and dim through the app or by voice. Amazon Basics says its bulbs dim only via the Alexa app or voice, and Govee notes its bulbs cannot be used with smart switches.

Which smart bulb is easiest to set up with Alexa?

The Amazon Basics Smart A19 bulb is the simplest, because you screw it in and set it up directly in the Alexa app with no third-party app or skill. It works exclusively with Alexa, though.

Do smart bulbs use power when they are off?

Smart bulbs draw a small amount of standby power to stay connected to Wi-Fi. When on, these bulbs are 60W-equivalent LEDs, and Kasa lists an actual draw of 9 W.
