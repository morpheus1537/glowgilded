How to pick a tech gift for a teen

  • Check what they already own: ask a parent or sibling about their phone and gaming setup first.
  • Think social: gifts they can use with friends, like an instant camera or a multiplayer console, tend to get the most use.
  • Personalize their space: room lighting is a hit with teens who are redecorating.
  • Bundle for impact: pair earbuds with a phone case, or the Instax with extra film, for a bigger gift.

Frequently asked questions

What tech do teens want for Christmas?

Teens tend to love tech that's social or helps them personalize their space: instant cameras, handheld gaming, wireless earbuds, and colorful room lighting like LED strips and sunset lamps are all popular. Ask what devices they already have so the gift works with their phone.

Is the Instax Mini 12 good for teens?

Yes. It's an easy, point-and-shoot instant camera, and this bundle includes a camera case, 60 instant film prints and a photo album, so they can start shooting and saving photos right away.

Is the Nintendo Switch Lite a good gift for a teenager?

It's a great choice for handheld gaming. Nintendo describes it as compact, lightweight and designed exclusively for handheld play, with online and local wireless multiplayer. It does not connect to a TV, so pick a standard Switch if they mainly want to play on the big screen.

Do Govee LED strip lights work with Alexa?

The 16.4 ft Govee strip in this guide is controlled with the Govee Home app and the listing notes it does not support Alexa. Govee sells other versions that work with Alexa and Google Assistant if voice control matters.

Do JLab Go Air Pop+ earbuds work with iPhone and Android?

They connect over Bluetooth 5.3, so they pair with most phones. JLab also lists Google Fast Pair for easy pairing with Android devices and the JLab app for custom EQ settings.
