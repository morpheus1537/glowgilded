What to look for in a shower speaker

  1. Waterproof rating: Look for IPX7 or IP67, which means protection against temporary immersion, not just splashes.
  2. A way to hang it: A strap, loop or carabiner lets you hang the speaker from a caddy or hook, away from the spray.
  3. Battery life: A long battery means you charge it less often, which is helpful because you should only charge it when it is completely dry.
  4. Size and volume: You need enough volume to hear over the water, but a huge speaker is awkward in a small bathroom.
  5. Stereo pairing: Some speakers can pair with a second unit, which is nice if you also use them outdoors.

Shower speaker care tips

  • Keep it out of the direct spray: Waterproof does not mean it needs a constant blast of hot water.
  • Close the port cover: If your speaker has a charging-port flap, make sure it is sealed before each shower.
  • Charge only when dry: Wipe it down and let the port dry fully before plugging in.
  • Rinse after the beach: Salt and sand are harder on speakers than shower water.
  • Mind your soap: Avoid getting shampoo or body wash on the grille, which can muffle sound over time.
  • Keep a charger handy: A small portable charger can top up your speaker and phone on trips.

Frequently asked questions

Is IPX7 or IP67 better for a shower speaker?

Both ratings mean the speaker is protected against temporary immersion in water. The 6 in IP67 adds dust protection, which matters more at the beach than in the shower, so either rating is fine for bathroom use.

Can a Bluetooth speaker stay in the shower all the time?

A waterproof speaker can handle splashes and steam, but it is best to keep it out of the direct spray, dry it off afterward and let it air out with the charging port cover closed. Always charge it fully dry and outside the shower.

Do shower speakers float?

Some do. Anker says the Soundcore Select 4 Go floats, and Ultimate Ears lists the WONDERBOOM 4 as floatable. Check the listing if you plan to use one in a bath or pool.

How do I hang a Bluetooth speaker in the shower?

Use the built-in strap, loop or carabiner to hang it from a shower caddy, towel hook or curtain rod. The JBL Clip 5 has an integrated carabiner with a wide opening, and the Soundcore Select 4 Go comes with a strap.

Can I take calls on a shower speaker?

Only if the listing mentions a built-in microphone. The listings in this guide focus on music playback, so check the product details if hands-free calling matters to you.
