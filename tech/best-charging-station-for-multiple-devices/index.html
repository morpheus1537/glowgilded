Wireless stand or multi-port charger?

  • Wireless 3-in-1 stands charge a phone, watch and earbuds without plugging anything in. They are tidy and great at the bedside, but slower than a cable.
  • Multi-port desktop chargers power several devices through USB-C and USB-A cables. They are faster and can charge laptops, but you still need a cable for each device.
  • Mix and match: Many homes use a wireless stand on the nightstand and a multi-port charger at the desk.

Tips for choosing a charging station

  • Count your devices: List everything you charge daily, then pick a station with enough spots.
  • Check your phone: Magnetic stands work best with iPhone 12 or newer or a magnetic case.
  • Look at wattage: Laptops need high-wattage USB-C ports; earbuds need very little.
  • Check what is in the box: Some stations include the adapter and cable; others do not.
  • Plan the cables: Pair a desktop charger with short cables and clips. See our desk cable management ideas.
  • Mind the bedroom: If indicator lights bother you, choose a stand where you can turn the light off.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best charging station for an iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods?

A 3-in-1 magnetic stand is the simplest choice. The Anker MagGo 3-in-1 lists 15W Qi2 charging, certified fast charging for Apple Watch and an adjustable stand, and includes the 40W adapter and cable.

Is wireless charging slower than a cable?

Usually, yes. Wireless pads in this guide top out at 15W for phones, while a USB-C port on the Anker desktop chargers can deliver up to 30W or 100W depending on the model. Wireless wins on convenience; cables win on speed.

Can I use a charging station with a phone case?

Usually, if the case is thin. GETPALS supports cases up to 4 mm and asks you to avoid metal rings, pockets or magnets. Magnetic stands like the Ouotoo need a magnetic case on iPhone 12 or newer.

Can one charging station charge a laptop and a phone?

Yes, a multi-port USB-C desktop charger can. The Anker 112W charger has 3 USB-C and 3 USB-A ports with up to 30W on one USB-C port, and the Anker Prime 200W can deliver up to 100W on each of two USB-C ports at once.

Do multi-device chargers include cables?

It depends. The Anker MagGo 3-in-1 includes a USB-C cable and 40W adapter, and the Anker Prime includes an AC cord, but the Anker 112W desktop charger does not include USB-C charging cables.
