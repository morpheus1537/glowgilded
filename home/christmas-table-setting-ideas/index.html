How to layer a Christmas table

Think of the table in layers, from the bottom up:

  1. Base: a tablecloth if you have one, then the runner down the center.
  2. Place settings: a placemat at each seat, then the dinner plate. A salad plate or bowl on top adds height.
  3. Cutlery and glasses: forks on the left, knife and spoon on the right, glasses above the knife.
  4. Napkins: fold the napkin, slip it through a napkin ring and set it on the plate or to the left of the forks.
  5. Centerpiece: greenery, pinecones or ornaments down the runner, with candlesticks spaced evenly along it.

Christmas table setting tips

  • Keep it low. Centerpieces taller than about 12 inches block conversation; tapers in short holders are the exception because they are thin.
  • Repeat one metal. Pick gold or silver and repeat it in napkin rings, candlesticks and cutlery.
  • Use real greenery sparingly. A few cut branches from the tree lot or yard smell wonderful and cost little. Keep them away from real flames.
  • Set the table early. Lay everything out the day before so you're not rushing while the turkey rests.
  • Plan for guests staying over. If you have overnight visitors, our guest room essentials for holiday guests guide covers the rest of the house.

Frequently asked questions

How do you set a Christmas table?

Start with a base layer such as a table runner, add a placemat at each seat, then the dinner plate, cutlery and glasses. Finish with a folded napkin in a napkin ring and a centerpiece of greenery and candles down the middle of the runner.

What colors are best for a Christmas table?

Deep green, red, white and gold are the classic combination. A green velvet runner and green placemats with red napkins and gold accents look festive without feeling busy.

How long should a table runner be?

A common rule is to choose a runner about 12 inches longer than your table so 6 inches hang over each end, or longer if you want a dramatic drape. The Green Velvet Table Runner in this guide is 20 by 120 inches.

Are flameless taper candles safe for a dinner table?

Flameless LED tapers have no open flame, so they avoid dripping wax and the risk of napkins or greenery catching fire. The set in this guide runs on 2 AAA batteries per candle (not included) and has a remote with a timer.

Can you wash cloth placemats?

The Softalker cloth placemats in this guide are listed as machine washable, which makes them practical for holiday meals with gravy and red wine.
