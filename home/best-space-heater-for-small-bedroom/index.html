How to choose a space heater for a small bedroom

  1. Room size: A 1500W heater suits most small bedrooms. Check the room size the brand lists.
  2. Thermostat: A thermostat holds your chosen temperature, so the heater cycles instead of blasting all night.
  3. Timer: An auto-off timer lets you fall asleep warm without running the heater until morning.
  4. Noise: Fan heaters make some sound. Oil-filled radiators have no fan and are silent.
  5. Safety: Look for tip-over protection, overheat protection and cool-touch housing.
  6. Footprint: Slim towers fit beside a nightstand. Compact cubes are easy to move.

Space heater safety tips for bedrooms

  • Keep 3 feet clear: Keep bedding, curtains, clothes and furniture at least 3 feet from the heater.
  • Use the wall outlet: Do not use extension cords or power strips with a 1500W heater.
  • Set a timer: Warm the room before bed and let the timer switch it off after you fall asleep.
  • Place it on a hard, level floor: Avoid thick rugs and never put a heater on a bed or chair.
  • Install a smoke alarm: Test it monthly, especially when you use a heater regularly.
  • Keep pets and kids in mind: Tip-over switches and cool-touch housing matter in busy homes.

Pair your heater with warmer bedding

A heater warms the air, but warm bedding keeps the heat with you. A heated mattress pad or thermal curtains can let you set the heater lower. If you work from home in a chilly room, our tips on how to stay warm working from home help too.

Frequently asked questions

What size space heater do I need for a small bedroom?

Most small bedrooms are well served by a 1500W heater with a lower setting. Lasko rates its digital tower for rooms up to 150 sq. ft., and GiveBest says its ceramic heater is rated to heat up to 200 square feet.

Is it safe to leave a space heater on overnight?

Many people run a heater at bedtime, but choose one with tip-over and overheat protection and use an auto-off timer so it does not run all night. Keep it at least 3 feet from bedding and curtains, plug it straight into a wall outlet and follow the manual.

What is the quietest type of space heater for sleeping?

Oil-filled radiators like the De'Longhi have no fan, so they are the quietest option. Among fan heaters, DREO says the Atom One uses a brushless DC motor engineered to be quieter, and Lasko describes its two heat settings as quiet.

Do space heaters dry out the air in a bedroom?

Any heater lowers relative humidity as it warms the air. De'Longhi says its oil-filled radiator delivers gentle heat that minimizes dry air with no fan to stir up dust; a humidifier also helps.

Should I plug a space heater into an extension cord or power strip?

No. Plug heaters directly into a wall outlet. Lasko's listing says to plug its heater directly into a wall outlet, and the same rule applies to most 1500W heaters.
