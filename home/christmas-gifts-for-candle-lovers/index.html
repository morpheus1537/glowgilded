How to choose a gift for a candle lover

Start by noticing how they enjoy their candles. If they light a candle and then worry about leaving it burning, or they have curious kids or pets, the candle warmer lamp is the most useful gift. If they love the ritual of lighting a wick and watching the flame, the wick trimmer set makes that ritual cleaner and prettier. And if you are not sure what they need, a seasonal scent in a big jar is always welcome in December.

Think about scent preferences too. Some candle lovers adore woodsy, outdoorsy notes like balsam and cedar, while others stick to vanilla, citrus or bakery scents. Peek at the jars already on their shelves to find out which camp they are in.

Buying tips

  • Measure before you buy a warmer lamp. The lamp needs enough space between the shade and the top of the jar, so compare its adjustable height with the candles they use most.
  • Keep a spare bulb. Warmer lamps rely on their bulb for heat, so a spare GU10 bulb makes the gift last longer.
  • Trim wicks before every burn. Shorter wicks burn cleaner, so the trimmer set pays off quickly, especially with large jars.
  • Follow the first-burn rule. Let a new jar candle melt to a full pool on its first burn to help prevent tunneling.
  • Never leave a flame unattended. Keep lit candles away from trees, curtains, pets and children, and blow them out (or snuff them) before you leave the room.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get someone who loves candles for Christmas?

Get them something that makes their candles better, not just another candle. A candle warmer lamp lets them enjoy their jars without a flame, a wick trimmer and snuffer set keeps every burn clean, and one large seasonal candle gives them a festive scent to use all December. Our picks are the Innqoo candle warmer lamp, the calary 3-in-1 candle accessory set and the Village Candle Christmas Tree jar.

Is a candle warmer lamp a good gift?

Yes, for most candle lovers a candle warmer lamp is a thoughtful gift because it melts the top of a jar candle with light and heat instead of a flame, so there is no smoke or soot. The Innqoo lamp has four brightness settings, a timer and an adjustable height, so it works with many jar sizes. Check the height of their favorite jars before you buy.

Why do candle lovers need a wick trimmer?

A wick trimmer cuts the wick short and straight before each burn, which helps reduce flickering, tall flames and black soot on the jar. The angled blades reach down into deep jars where regular scissors cannot. A snuffer and wick dipper let you put the flame out without blowing smoke and wax around the room.

How long does a large jar candle last?

Village Candle says its large jar candles burn for up to 140 hours, depending on size. For the best results, the brand recommends burning for at least 2 hours and no more than 4 hours at a time, or until the wax melts to a full pool.

Are candle warmers safer than burning candles?

A candle warmer removes the open flame, which is why many people with children or pets prefer one. It still gets hot, so place it on a stable surface, keep it out of reach of little hands and paws, and use the timer so it switches off when you leave the room.
