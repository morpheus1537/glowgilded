How to warm a cold bedroom, step by step

  1. Find the drafts. On a windy evening, hold your hand near the window frame, outlets on exterior walls and the bottom of the door. Wherever you feel moving air, that is where to start.
  2. Seal the door gap. A door sweep costs little and stops cold hallway air from flowing across the floor.
  3. Cover the windows at night. Close heavy curtains at dusk, and open them on sunny days to let natural heat in.
  4. Change the bedding. Flannel sheets and a heavier comforter make a big difference without any electricity.
  5. Preheat the bed. Turn on a heated mattress pad 15 to 30 minutes before bed, then lower the setting or let the timer switch it off.
  6. Add a rug. Bare floors feel cold underfoot. A rug beside the bed helps you avoid that first icy step.

Buying tips

Frequently asked questions

How can I make my bedroom warmer without turning up the heat?

Stop drafts first, then warm the bed itself. Thermal curtains over windows, a draft stopper under the door, flannel sheets and a heated mattress pad together can make a cold bedroom feel much warmer while the thermostat stays where it is.

Is a heated mattress pad better than an electric blanket?

Many people prefer a heated mattress pad because the heat comes from underneath and the pad stays in place under the fitted sheet. An electric blanket sits on top and can be moved to the couch. Both work; choose based on whether you want to warm the bed or the person.

Do thermal curtains really keep a room warmer?

Heavy lined curtains add a layer between the room and a cold window pane. The NICETOWN listing describes its blackout panels as thermal insulated and energy-saving. Results depend on your windows, so close them at dusk and make sure they cover the full window frame.

Are flannel sheets warmer than cotton percale?

Yes, for most people flannel feels warmer because the brushed cotton fibers trap more air than a smooth percale weave. The Bare Home set is 100% cotton flannel, so it stays breathable while feeling cozier on cold nights.

Is it safe to sleep with a heated mattress pad on all night?

Follow the manufacturer's directions. Both pads in this guide include auto shut-off timers; the Bedsure listing says it defaults to an 8-hour shut-off, and the Sunbeam has a selectable shut-off of up to 12 hours plus overheat protection.
