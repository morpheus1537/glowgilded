How to choose a heated throw blanket

Heat settings. Three settings are enough for most people, but if you run hot or cold, more levels give you finer control. The Bedsure has 6 and the Sunbeam and GOTCOZY have 4.

Auto shut-off. Every pick here turns off by itself. Short timers (2 hours) are reassuring if you tend to doze off; longer or adjustable timers suit long movie nights.

Fabric. Sherpa feels thick and fluffy, flannel and fleece are smoother, and microplush is light and silky. Reversible throws give you both.

Size. A standard throw is about 50 x 60 inches, perfect for one person on a couch. For a whole bed, look for a heated blanket in your bed size instead.

Washability. Choose a throw with a detachable controller so you can machine wash it. Every pick in this guide qualifies.

Tips for using a heated throw safely

  • Lay it flat or loosely draped; do not fold or bunch it while it is on.
  • Keep the controller out from under cushions and pets.
  • Unplug it when you are not using it, even with auto shut-off.
  • Do not use it with an infant or anyone who cannot operate the controls.
  • Inspect the cord before each winter and replace a damaged throw.

Frequently asked questions

Are heated throw blankets safe to use?

Heated throws from established brands are designed with safety features such as automatic shut-off and overheat protection, and the picks here list safety certifications such as ETL or UL. Always follow the manual, do not fold or bunch a throw while it is on, and unplug it when you are done.

How long do heated throws stay on before they shut off?

It depends on the model. In this guide, the GENIANI and Beautyrest throws shut off after 2 hours, the GOTCOZY after 3 hours, the Sunbeam after 4 hours, and the Bedsure has timer settings from 1 to 10 hours with a default 8-hour auto shut-off.

Can you wash a heated throw blanket?

Yes, all five throws in this guide are listed as machine washable once you detach the controller. Check the care label for the drying instructions, which differ between brands.

Is a heated throw cheaper to run than a space heater?

Usually, because a throw warms you rather than the whole room. Bedsure lists its throw at 100W and says it uses about one-thirtieth the energy of a traditional heater, while a typical space heater draws far more.

What size is a heated throw blanket?

Most heated throws are about 50 x 60 inches, which covers one adult on a couch. The Sunbeam and GOTCOZY are 50 x 60 inches and the Beautyrest is 50 x 64 inches, according to their listings.
