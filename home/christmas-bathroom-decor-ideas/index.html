How to decorate a bathroom for Christmas in four steps

  1. Start with the biggest surface. Swap the shower curtain first; it sets the theme for everything else. A full set, like the Yddsky fireplace set, handles the rugs and toilet lid cover at the same time.
  2. Choose one color story. Red and cream, red and green, or a winter white palette all work. If your curtain is a busy print, keep the towels and mat simple.
  3. Add soft textiles, not counter clutter. Holiday hand towels and a festive bath mat change the room without taking up space guests need.
  4. Finish with one natural touch. A sprig of pine in a small vase, a mini tree on a shelf or a few battery fairy lights around the mirror add warmth. Keep anything with an open flame away from towels and curtains.

Buying tips for Christmas bathroom decor

  • Measure your shower first. Standard curtains are about 70 to 72 inches square. For a tall or extra-wide shower, look for a long or wide size instead.
  • Use a liner with a fabric curtain. A separate liner protects a printed curtain from soap scum and helps it last more seasons. Our guide to shower curtain liners that don't billow can help.
  • Check the care label. Printed rugs and chenille mats usually prefer cold water and low or air drying. Wash before the guests arrive, not the night before.
  • Think beyond the season. Winter-themed pieces, like cardinal towels or a solid red mat, can stay out longer than Santa prints, which makes them better value.
  • Keep practical towels nearby. Decorative towels are lovely, but busy households should keep a stack of absorbent everyday towels close by. Our quick-dry bath towels guide covers good options.

Frequently asked questions

How do I decorate a bathroom for Christmas?

Start with the largest surface, which is usually the shower curtain, then add holiday hand towels and a festive bath mat. A small pine sprig, a candle or a mini tree on the counter finishes the look without cluttering a small room.

How do I make a small bathroom look festive without clutter?

Swap textiles you already use instead of adding objects. A themed shower curtain, hand towels and a bath mat change the whole room while keeping the counter clear for guests.

Are Christmas hand towels actually usable, or just for show?

Many are both. The SKL Home cardinal towels in this guide are cotton terry, so they can be used, but embroidery can feel less absorbent than plain terry, so some hosts keep a plain towel nearby for heavy use.

Can I wash a Christmas shower curtain set?

The set in this guide is listed as washable by hand or machine in cold water without bleach. Check each listing's care label before washing, especially printed rugs with rubber or flannel backing.

When should I put up Christmas bathroom decor?

Most people decorate the bathroom along with the rest of the house, usually the weekend after Thanksgiving. If you host early December parties, put it up then so guests see it.
