How to choose flannel sheets

  1. Fabric: True flannel is brushed cotton. Microfleece "flannel" is polyester and feels fuzzier but breathes less.
  2. Weight: Heavier GSM usually feels thicker and warmer. Many cotton flannel sets list about 160 GSM.
  3. Pocket depth: Measure your mattress, including any topper. Pockets here range from 14 to 18 inches.
  4. Prints vs. solids: Plaids and holiday prints feel seasonal; solids work all winter.
  5. Care: Most flannel prefers cold water, a gentle cycle, low heat and no fabric softener.

Buying tips for flannel sheets

  • Measure the mattress first: Include toppers. A pocket that is too shallow is the most common complaint with any sheets.
  • Wash before first use: It softens the fabric and removes loose lint from brushing.
  • Go cold and low: Cold water, a gentle cycle and low dryer heat help prevent shrinking and pilling.
  • Skip fabric softener: Several brands here say it can affect brushed fibers.
  • Layer smartly: Flannel plus a heated throw blanket or a good comforter is warmer than adding more heat to the room. Our guide on how to keep a cold bedroom warm has more ideas.
  • Buy two sets: Rotating sets means one is always clean during the cold months.

Frequently asked questions

Are flannel sheets warmer than regular cotton sheets?

Yes, generally. Flannel is brushed to raise soft fibers that trap body heat, so it feels warmer to the touch than smooth percale or sateen. That makes it a popular swap for cold bedrooms in winter.

What does GSM mean for flannel sheets?

GSM is grams per square meter, a measure of fabric weight. RUVANTI and Sleep Philosophy list 160 GSM cotton flannel, while BEDELITE lists 200 to 220 GSM microfleece.

Why do new flannel sheets shed lint?

Brushing the fabric loosens some fibers, so a little lint at first is normal. Sleep Philosophy suggests a cold first wash with half a cup of vinegar and says shedding eases after a few washes.

How do you keep flannel sheets from pilling?

Wash cold on a gentle cycle, avoid overloading the dryer, tumble dry low and skip fabric softener. Bare Home notes some light pilling over time is normal for brushed cotton flannel.

Can you use flannel sheets all year?

Some people do. Several brands here describe their flannel as breathable for year-round use, but most people find flannel best from late fall to early spring and switch to lighter sheets in summer.
