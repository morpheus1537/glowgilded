How to choose for your new homeowner

Think about what stage they are at:

  • Just moved in: go practical. The lazy Susan and the throw get used immediately.
  • Settled and hosting: the charcuterie board set is the crowd-pleaser.
  • Still making it feel like theirs: scent and light, so the reed diffuser or flameless candles.

Pairing one practical gift with one cozy gift (for example, the lazy Susan plus the reed diffuser) feels generous without overspending.

What to skip

  • Wall art and decor with strong style. People have their own taste, and big decorative pieces are hard to return.
  • Specialty kitchen gadgets. Single-use tools often end up in a drawer.
  • Anything that needs to be installed. New homeowners already have a long to-do list.

Whatever you choose, include a gift receipt so they can swap a color or size.

Housewarming gift etiquette

You do not need to spend a lot. A thoughtful, useful gift is always better than an expensive one that does not fit their home. If you are attending a housewarming party, bring something they can use that night or keep for later, and avoid anything perishable that needs to be refrigerated immediately. Include a short card: new homeowners often keep them as mementos of their first months in the house.

Frequently asked questions

How much should you spend on a housewarming gift?

There is no rule. For a friend or coworker, a single useful item like a reed diffuser is plenty. For close family, pair two items from this list.

What is a good housewarming gift for someone who has everything?

Choose consumable or cozy items: a reed diffuser, a quality throw, or a hosting board they will use when friends visit.

Is a charcuterie board a good housewarming gift?

Yes, especially for people who like to host. Look for a set that includes bowls and utensils so it is ready to use, and remind them wooden boards should be hand-washed.

When should you give a housewarming gift?

Any time in the first few months is fine. Bring it to the housewarming party, or send it a week or two after moving day when the boxes are unpacked.
