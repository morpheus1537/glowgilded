How to choose the right organizer for a small space

Before you buy anything, walk through your apartment and look for the three kinds of wasted space these picks solve.

  • Deep spaces: cabinets and counters where things get pushed to the back. A lazy Susan brings everything to the front with one spin.
  • Tall spaces: closet shelves and cupboards with a lot of empty air above your stuff. Stackable drawers split that height into usable levels.
  • Narrow spaces: the 10 to 16 inch gaps beside the fridge, the sofa or the bed. A slim rolling cart turns that strip of floor into three shelves.

Most small apartments have all three, which is why these organizers work well together.

Buying tips

  • Measure twice. Note the width, depth and height of the shelf or gap, and leave about an inch of clearance so drawers slide and turntables spin freely.
  • Organize by zone, not by room. Group items by how you use them (coffee things together, cleaning things together) and give each group one container.
  • Match your finishes. Pick one or two materials, such as warm wood and clear acrylic, so organized spaces look calm instead of busy.
  • Put heavy items low. On carts and stacked drawers, keep the heaviest things on the bottom for stability.
  • Declutter first. Spend ten minutes clearing out expired products and duplicates before you organize, so you do not buy containers for things you do not need.
  • Think about hosting. In a small home, pieces that switch roles, like a serving turntable or a rolling drinks cart, save you from buying separate party gear.

Frequently asked questions

How do I organize a small apartment with no storage?

Start by using vertical space and the gaps you already have. Stackable drawers turn one tall shelf into several usable levels, a slim rolling cart fits in the gap beside a counter or sofa, and a lazy Susan makes deep corners easy to reach.

Are clear storage drawers worth it?

For most small spaces, yes. Because you can see what is inside, you stop buying duplicates and you put things back where they belong. They are less helpful if you want to hide clutter, so pair them with a lidded basket for anything you would rather not see.

What size lazy Susan should I get for a kitchen counter?

A 9 to 10 inch turntable suits most counters and upper cabinets. Measure the depth of the cabinet or the free corner of your counter and leave about an inch of clearance so it spins without catching.

Is a rolling cart sturdy enough for kitchen use?

A metal cart with a reinforced frame handles pantry goods, jars and small appliances well. Keep the heaviest items on the bottom shelf, and check the listed weight limit before loading it up.
