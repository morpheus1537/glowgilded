How to style a Christmas mantel in 4 steps

  1. Lay the base. Drape the garland along the full length of the mantel and let the ends fall a few inches down each side.
  2. Add height. Group three flameless candles at one end, or place pairs at each end for a symmetrical look.
  3. Hang the stockings. Space the weighted holders evenly and check that each one sits flat.
  4. Add one focal point. A mirror, wreath or piece of art in the center keeps the eye from wandering.

Buying tips

  • Measure first: check the length and depth of your mantel shelf before you buy a garland or stocking holders.
  • Plan your power: battery garlands and candles avoid cords running down the fireplace; plug-in pieces need an outlet nearby.
  • Keep it away from heat: don't place garland or stockings where they touch a fireplace that's in use.
  • Pick one color story: warm white light with greenery reads classic; mixing too many colors can look busy.
  • Check the current price on Amazon: holiday decor prices change often in November and December.

Frequently asked questions

How do you decorate a Christmas mantel?

Start with a garland as the base layer, add height with candles or a few taller pieces at one or both ends, then hang stockings below. Keep the center open or use one focal piece, like a mirror or wreath, so the mantel does not look cluttered.

How long should a garland be for a mantel?

A 9-foot garland works on most standard mantels because it lets you add gentle drape or let the ends hang down the sides. Measure your mantel shelf first; if it is short, you can coil or tuck the extra length at the ends.

Are flameless candles safe on a mantel with garland?

Battery-operated LED candles are the safer choice next to faux greenery because there is no open flame. The candles in this guide are real wax on the outside with an LED flame inside and run on batteries with a remote and timer.

How do you hang stockings without damaging the mantel?

Use weighted stocking holders that sit on top of the mantel. The Iconikal snowflake holders have felt bottoms to protect the surface, hold up to 2 lbs each and need a mantel at least 2.75 inches deep.

Do I need an outlet near the fireplace for these?

Check each listing. The MorTime garland runs on 3 AA batteries (not included) and the flameless candles are battery operated, so neither needs an outlet. Check the pre-lit cedar garland's listing for its power details before you plan placement.
