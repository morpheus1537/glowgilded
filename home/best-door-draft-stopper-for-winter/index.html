How to choose a door draft stopper

  1. Stick-on sweep or weighted snake: Adhesive silicone sweeps attach to the door and move with it. Weighted fabric blockers sit on the floor and need no tools, but you have to nudge them back after opening the door.
  2. Gap size: Measure the gap under your closed door. Thin silicone sweeps suit small to moderate gaps, while a plump weighted blocker can cover a bigger gap on an interior door.
  3. Door width: Most doors are 30 to 36 inches wide. Silicone sweeps can be trimmed to fit, and fabric blockers come in fixed lengths like 32, 36 or 38 inches.
  4. Interior or exterior: For exterior doors, a sealed sweep that stays put is the stronger choice. Fabric snakes are better for interior doors.
  5. Cleaning: A removable, washable cover keeps a floor-level blocker fresh through a muddy winter.
  6. Looks: Decorative door snakes can add a little personality to a room, while clear or neutral sweeps disappear.

Tips for stopping drafts under doors

  • Find the leak first: On a windy day, feel along the bottom of the door or watch a candle flame for flicker.
  • Measure before you buy: Measure both the door width and the gap height so the stopper covers the full opening.
  • Prep the surface: For stick-on sweeps, clean and dry the bottom of the door before applying the adhesive, ideally in a warm room.
  • Use both types: A stick-on sweep on the outside door plus a weighted blocker on a bedroom door is a strong combination for cold homes.
  • Don't forget the sides: If you still feel air, check the door frame and windows too. Heavy curtains help on windows.
  • Warm the room efficiently: Once drafts are blocked, a space heater for a small bedroom works less hard to keep the room cozy.

Frequently asked questions

Do door draft stoppers really work?

Yes, for gaps under a door they are one of the easiest fixes. A stick-on silicone sweep or a weighted fabric blocker closes the gap where cold air, dust and noise slip in, which can make a room feel noticeably less drafty.

What is better, a door sweep or a door snake?

A stick-on door sweep stays attached and moves with the door, so it suits doors you open often and exterior doors. A weighted door snake needs no installation and is easy to move, which suits renters and interior doors, but you have to reposition it after opening the door.

Will a stick-on door sweep damage my door?

Vellure says its adhesive can be removed without damaging the door if needed. Always clean the surface first and test on a hidden spot if your door has a delicate finish.

How do I know if my door needs a draft stopper?

Hold your hand or a lit candle near the bottom of a closed door on a cold, windy day. If you feel cold air or the flame flickers, or you can see daylight under the door, a draft stopper will help.

Can you wash a door draft stopper?

Some, yes. The Sunolga blocker has a removable cover you can unzip and machine wash. The MARWOOD dog draft stopper is listed as not machine washable, so spot clean it instead.
