How to set up a guest room in one afternoon

  • Make the bed in layers: protector, fitted sheet, flat sheet, a warm blanket and a throw at the foot. Layers let guests adjust for a cold night or a warm radiator without asking.
  • Give each guest two pillows: a pair per person is the easiest way to cover different sleeping styles.
  • Clear some space: empty one drawer and leave a few hangers in the closet. Guests will unpack instead of living out of a bag.
  • Light for reading: a lamp guests can switch off from bed beats walking across a dark room to the wall switch.
  • Power at the bedside: a charger or power strip near the nightstand is one of the most appreciated small touches.
  • A welcome basket: fresh towels, a bottle of water, a few snacks and the Wi-Fi password on a card. If you need inexpensive ideas, our stocking stuffers under $20 list has several that work as guest-room treats.
  • Sleep in it yourself: spend one night in the room before guests arrive. You will find the drafty window, the squeaky floorboard or the street light that needs a curtain.

What to look for when you buy

  • Washability: everything that touches a guest should be easy to wash between visits. All three bedding and pillow picks above are machine washable.
  • Fit: measure the mattress depth before buying a protector, and check that new pillows fit the pillowcases you already own.
  • Simplicity: guests should not need an app or a manual. A single switch or button is ideal.
  • Storage: anything that folds flat or tucks under the bed earns its place in a room that is only used a few weeks a year.

Frequently asked questions

What should I put in a guest room for holiday visitors?

Start with comfortable pillows, clean protected bedding, a way to block noise and a place for luggage. After that, add fresh towels, a phone charger, a lamp guests can reach from bed and a few snacks or a bottle of water.

How many pillows should a guest bed have?

Two sleeping pillows per guest is a good rule, so a couple sharing a queen bed gets four. That lets each person stack them or swap to a firmer or softer one without having to ask.

Do I really need a mattress protector on a guest bed?

Yes, if you want the mattress to last. A guest mattress gets used by many different people over the years, and a washable waterproof protector keeps spills, sweat and accidents from soaking in and staining it.

Does a white noise machine help guests sleep in a new house?

For many people, yes. A steady sound masks unfamiliar noises like a fridge, a furnace, early risers or a dog, which is exactly what tends to wake guests in a house they do not know.

Where should a luggage rack go in a guest room?

Put it near the closet or along a wall at the foot of the bed, where guests can open a suitcase fully without blocking the door. If space is tight, choose a rack that folds flat so you can store it under the bed between visits.
