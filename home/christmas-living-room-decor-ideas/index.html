How to style a cozy Christmas living room

Start with the sofa

Most people look at the sofa first when they walk into a living room, so that is where holiday color pays off most. Put the red corduroy covers on your two corner pillows and keep any center pillows neutral, in cream, oatmeal or a soft knit. Add a throw over one arm in cream or plaid; our heated throw blankets for winter guide has options that also keep you warm.

Finish the base of the tree

A tree looks taller and fuller when the base is covered. Lay the faux fur skirt after the tree is up and the lights are on, fluff it with your hands, then arrange wrapped gifts in two or three loose groups instead of a ring. Gold and kraft paper wrap looks especially good on white fur.

Repeat the glow

Lighting is what makes a room feel cozy at night. Put the mini tree on a side table or console across the room from the main tree so the light is spread out, and add battery operated window candles with timers on the windowsills. Turn off the overhead lights in the evening and let the trees and candles do the work.

Keep one color story

Pick two main colors and one metal: for example red and white with gold, or green and cream with brass. The three picks here fit a classic red, white and gold scheme. For a softer, more natural look, see our farmhouse Christmas decor ideas and cozy Christmas decor ideas.

Buying tips

  • Measure your pillows first. These covers are 18 x 18 inches. If your inserts are 20 or 22 inches, they will be overstuffed; if they are 16 inches, the covers will look floppy.
  • Match the skirt to the tree. A 48 inch skirt suits most 6 to 7.5 foot trees. Choose 30 or 36 inches for slim trees and 60 inches for very wide ones.
  • Pick the right tree version. Choose the plug-in mini tree if it sits near an outlet and the battery version for a table in the middle of the room.
  • Think about pets and kids. Fur skirts and dangling cords are tempting for puppies and kittens, so tuck cords behind furniture.
  • Order early. Popular colors and sizes can sell out in December, and holiday deals come and go quickly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make my living room look cozy for Christmas?

Combine warm white light, soft texture and one clear color story. Swap your everyday pillow covers for a festive red pair, put a plush skirt under the tree, and add a small lighted tree or candles on a side table so the room glows even when the big tree is off.

What colors make a Christmas living room feel cozy?

Warm reds, deep greens, cream and natural wood feel the coziest. A rich red on the sofa with a white faux fur skirt under the tree and natural greenery keeps the room festive without looking busy.

What size Christmas tree skirt do I need?

A 48 inch skirt suits most 6 to 7.5 foot trees; go smaller for slim or tabletop trees and larger for very full trees. The AOGU skirt in this guide is 48 inches across and also comes in 30, 36 and 60 inch sizes on the same listing.

Do Christmas pillow covers come with inserts?

Usually not. The MIULEE listing is for a set of two 18 x 18 inch covers with hidden zippers, so you reuse 18 inch inserts you already have or buy them separately.

Is the National Tree Company mini tree battery operated?

It depends on the version you pick. The listing has plug-in versions with a red or gold bag and a battery-operated version with a red bag; the plug-in red bag version we checked has 35 clear lights and 71 branch tips.
