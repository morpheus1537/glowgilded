How to choose a gift for a clean freak

  • Find the chore they hate. Listen for complaints. "The grout is disgusting" means the spin scrubber. "The dog threw up on the rug again" means the Little Green Mini. "My mop is gross" means the O-Cedar.
  • Check the floors. Spot cleaners are for carpets, rugs and fabric. Spin mops are for hard floors only.
  • Think about storage. Apartment dwellers may prefer the compact spot cleaner or the spin scrubber over a mop bucket.
  • Consider their body. For someone with bad knees or a sore back, the long-handled scrubber and a hands-free wringer are genuinely helpful.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get someone who loves to clean?

Get them a tool that makes their least favorite job faster rather than another bottle of cleaner. Our top picks are the BISSELL Little Green Mini for carpet and couch stains, a cordless electric spin scrubber for tubs and tile, and the O-Cedar EasyWring spin mop for hard floors. All three are well reviewed on Amazon and easy to wrap.

Is a cleaning gadget a rude Christmas gift?

Not for the right person. Someone who genuinely enjoys a tidy home, or who has mentioned a cleaning chore they hate, will usually appreciate a tool that saves time. If you are unsure, frame it as a time-saver, add a small treat like a candle or a coffee gift card, and include a gift receipt.

Does the BISSELL Little Green Mini work on couches?

Yes. BISSELL says it sprays, scrubs and suctions dirt and stains from upholstery, car interiors and pet beds as well as carpet and rugs. It comes with a 4-inch Tough Stain Tool and a HydroRinse self-cleaning hose tool. Always test a hidden spot first and check the fabric care tag.

Can an electric spin scrubber be used in water?

The Leebein spin scrubber is rated IPX7 waterproof and can be rinsed under a faucet, but the brand says it should not be used under water. Use it on wet surfaces, then rinse the brush heads and let them dry.

How often should you replace a spin mop head?

O-Cedar recommends replacing the EasyWring microfiber mop head about every 3 months for the best results. The head is machine washable between replacements; skip fabric softener so the microfiber keeps picking up dirt.

Is a spin mop safe for hardwood floors?

O-Cedar says the EasyWring is safe for all hard floors, including finished hardwood, laminate, tile and vinyl. The key on wood is to spin the head well so it is damp rather than wet, which the foot-pedal wringer makes easy.
