How to choose a spa bathroom gift

  • Think about how they wash. Bath lovers get the most out of a bathtub tray. Shower-only people will use a towel warmer, towels or a new shower head every day.
  • Check the space. A towel warmer bucket needs a spot near an outlet on the floor or a counter. Small bathrooms may be better suited to towels or a shower head.
  • Measure the tub. For a bath tray, measure the outer width of the tub rim and confirm it falls between 29 1/2 and 43 inches.
  • Pick colors that fit the room. Gray and white towels are the safest choice. When in doubt, choose neutral.

Gift pairing ideas

  • The ultimate warm-up: the Keenray towel warmer plus the Turkish towel set, so the first towel they warm is a brand-new one.
  • The Sunday bath night: the bamboo bathtub tray with a favorite book, a candle and a small bottle of bubble bath.
  • The hotel bathroom makeover: the SparkPod rain shower head plus the towel set for a full refresh that costs far less than a renovation.

Buying tips

  • Order bulky items like the towel warmer early in December; holiday shipping slows down as Christmas gets closer.
  • Wash new towels before gifting or tell the recipient to wash them before first use; it helps absorbency.
  • Load a towel warmer with dry, loosely folded towels so the heat can reach every layer.
  • Keep the gift receipt with sized items like towels and trays in case the color or fit is wrong.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good spa gift for the bathroom?

A good spa gift for the bathroom is something that makes an ordinary bath or shower feel special every day. Our top choices are a towel warmer bucket for warm towels and robes, a bamboo bathtub tray that holds a book, candle and drink, and a set of thick Turkish cotton towels. All three are well reviewed on Amazon and need no installation.

Is a towel warmer a good Christmas gift?

Yes, especially for someone who lives somewhere cold or has a chilly bathroom. A bucket-style towel warmer like the Keenray plugs into a regular outlet, needs no installation and also warms robes, blankets and pajamas. The main thing to check is counter or floor space, since the Keenray stands about 20 inches tall.

How big a towel can a towel warmer bucket hold?

The Keenray towel warmer has a 21-liter bucket that the brand says fits up to two 40 x 70 inch oversized bath towels. For best results, fold towels loosely rather than stuffing them in, so warm air can reach every layer.

Will a bathtub tray fit my tub?

The ROYAL CRAFT WOOD bathtub tray expands from 29 1/2 inches to 43 inches, which covers most standard and many wider tubs. Measure the outside width of the tub rim before you order; the tray needs to rest on both sides, with the silicone grips on the rim.

What does 600 GSM mean for towels?

GSM means grams per square meter and measures how dense a towel is. Around 600 GSM is a plush, mid-to-heavy weight that feels thick and absorbent like a hotel towel, while still drying faster than very heavy 800+ GSM towels.

Is a rain shower head hard to install?

Usually not. The SparkPod 6 inch rain shower head hand-tightens onto any standard 1/2 inch shower arm with no tools, and the box includes plumber tape and a spare washer. Most people can swap it in about five minutes.
