How to choose a Christmas gift for a homebody

Start with how they actually spend their evenings. If they are on the couch under a blanket most nights, the wearable blanket will become part of their routine. If they read, work or watch shows in bed, the reading pillow solves a problem they deal with every night. If weekends are for movies, the popcorn popper turns any night into a small event.

Next, think about who you are buying for. One-size gifts like the wearable blanket and a reading pillow are safe for in-laws, coworkers and teens, because you do not need to know anyone's clothing size or decor style. A kitchen gadget like the popper works best for someone who will enjoy it rather than see it as one more thing on the counter, so it is a great fit for families and roommates who watch movies together.

Gift pairing ideas

  • The ultimate night in: the wearable blanket, the popcorn popper and a bag of gourmet kernels, wrapped together with a gift card for a streaming service.
  • The book lover's bed setup: the reading pillow with a new hardcover and a clip-on book light.
  • The movie night basket: the popcorn popper with paper popcorn boxes, a popcorn seasoning set and a couple of favorite holiday movies.

Frequently asked questions

What do you buy a homebody for Christmas?

Buy a homebody something that makes their favorite night in more comfortable. Good choices are a wearable blanket for the couch, a backrest reading pillow for reading or watching TV in bed, and a popcorn popper for movie nights. They get used every week, not just on Christmas morning.

Is a wearable blanket a good Christmas gift?

Yes, especially for someone who runs cold. THE COMFY Original is one size fits most adults, so you only have to pick a color, and it is machine washable. With a 4.8-star rating across more than 94,000 ratings, it is one of the most reliable cozy gifts on Amazon.

Can you wash THE COMFY wearable blanket?

Yes. The Amazon listing says to machine wash it cold and tumble dry low, and not to bleach, iron or dry clean it. Washing it inside out on a gentle cycle helps keep the sherpa lining soft.

Is a reading pillow with arms worth it?

It is worth it if you read, work or watch TV sitting up in bed. A backrest pillow with arms holds you upright and supports your elbows, so you are not stacking and re-stacking regular pillows. The Linenspa pillow is filled with shredded foam you can shape, but note that the listing says spot clean only.

Why does my Linenspa reading pillow look flat when it arrives?

It ships compressed and rolled in a box. Linenspa says to unroll it, shake and fluff it, and allow up to 48 hours for it to fully expand, so give it a day or two before Christmas if you can.

Is a hot air popcorn popper better than microwave popcorn?

For most people, yes. A hot air popper like the Presto PopLite uses no oil, pops up to 18 cups in about 2 1/2 minutes and lets you choose your own kernels, butter and seasonings. You also skip the bags and the burnt-popcorn smell.

The bottom line
