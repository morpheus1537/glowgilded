What to look for in a heated mattress pad

  1. Dual controls: On queen and king pads, two controllers let each sleeper choose their own heat. Essential for couples who disagree about temperature.
  2. Heat settings: More settings give finer control. Pads here range from 9 to 12 settings.
  3. Auto shut-off: A timer turns the pad off after a set time. Look for one that runs up to 10 or 12 hours if you want warmth all night.
  4. Preheat: A boost mode that warms the bed faster before you climb in.
  5. Pocket depth: Deep skirts keep the pad from sliding off thick or pillow-top mattresses.

Buying tips

  • Measure your mattress depth: Check the pocket depth before you buy, especially with a topper on top.
  • Choose dual controls for couples: A single control usually leads to one partner too warm and one too cold.
  • Use the timer: Set it to turn off after you fall asleep or a few hours before your alarm.
  • Keep the controller uncovered: Several listings warn against covering the controller while in use.
  • Layer smart: A sherpa or flannel layer on top holds the warmth. Our heated throw blankets guide covers the couch, too.
  • Hot sleeper partner? Pair a single-side pad with a cooling blanket on their side; see our cooling weighted blanket picks.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to sleep on a heated mattress pad all night?

Many people do, but use the auto shut-off timer as a safety net. Every pad in this guide has one: up to 12 hours on the Reaks, VALUXE, Serta and Sealy, and 2 to 10 hours on the GOTCOZY. Never use a pad with a damaged cord, and do not cover the controller.

Is a heated mattress pad better than an electric blanket?

A heated mattress pad sits under your fitted sheet and warms you from below, so it stays put and does not slide off in the night. An electric blanket goes on top. Cold sleepers often prefer a pad because heat rises toward the body and the bed is warm the moment you get in.

Can you wash a heated mattress pad?

Yes. All five pads here are listed as machine washable once the controller is unplugged. Follow each care label; most say no bleach, no ironing and no dry cleaning.

Do you feel the wires in a heated mattress pad?

Sometimes. Thin pads can let you feel the wires slightly; Reaks says so in its own listing. Thicker quilted pads like VALUXE say the padding is plush enough that you do not feel them, and a mattress topper on top adds even more cushion.

What size heated mattress pad should I buy?

Match the pad to your mattress size and check the pocket depth. Serta and Sealy fit mattresses up to about 17 inches thick, while VALUXE lists 8 to 21 inch pockets. A mini twin like the GOTCOZY warms one sleeper's side of any bed.
