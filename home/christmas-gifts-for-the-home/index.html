How to choose a Christmas gift for the home

Start with the room the person spends their evenings in. If they curl up on the couch with a candle burning, the candle warmer lamp upgrades a habit they already have. If they love to decorate and host, the flameless tapers will be on their mantel or table every December for years. If they prefer a clean, light scent, or live somewhere candles are not allowed, the diffuser is the easiest fit.

Then think about safety. All three picks avoid an open flame, which makes them a better fit than real candles for homes with children, pets, a dry Christmas tree or a lot of paper and ribbon around during the holidays.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Christmas gift for the home?

The best Christmas gifts for the home are things people use every evening, like lighting and scent. A candle warmer lamp, a set of flameless taper candles or an essential oil diffuser all make a room feel cozier, suit almost any style and do not require knowing someone's exact taste in decor.

Is a candle warmer lamp better than burning a candle?

It is safer and cleaner, because there is no open flame, smoke or soot. The lamp melts the wax from the top down with a halogen bulb, so the candle releases its scent without burning the wick. Many people also find their candles last longer this way.

Does the GODONLIF candle warmer lamp come with bulbs?

Yes. The Amazon listing title says it comes with 2 bulbs, and the spec table lists a 50-watt halogen MR16 bulb with a GU10 base, so you have a spare when the first one burns out.

Do flameless taper candles look real?

Good ones come close. The GenSwin tapers have a real wax exterior and a 3D wick with a flickering flame, so they read as real candles from across the room. Up close the flame is clearly LED, which is the trade-off for no dripping wax and no fire risk.

Do the GenSwin flameless candles come with batteries?

No. Each taper takes 2 AAA batteries, which are not included, so a set of six needs 12 AAA batteries. Candle holders are not included either.

Does the InnoGear diffuser come with essential oils?

No. The box includes the 100 ml diffuser, an AC/DC adapter and a user manual. Add a small bottle of a holiday scent like pine, orange or cinnamon if you want it to be ready to use on Christmas morning.
