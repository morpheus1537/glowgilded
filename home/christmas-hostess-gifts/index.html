How to choose a Christmas hostess gift

Start with how the host lives. Someone who hosts often will get the most from a serving set they can use at every party. A host with a small kitchen or a minimalist style will appreciate something consumable, like a candle, that does not need storage space. A wine lover will enjoy a tool that upgrades a habit they already have.

Then think about timing. Hostess gifts are usually handed over at the door, so pick something that does not need to be opened, served or refrigerated during the party. All three picks here can be set aside and enjoyed later.

  • The party starter: the SMIRLY board with a wedge of good cheese and a box of crackers tucked inside the gift box.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good hostess gift for a Christmas party?

Pick something the host can use after the party instead of something they have to deal with during it. A charcuterie board set for future gatherings, a long-burning holiday candle, or a wine opener and preserver set are all practical, giftable choices that do not need to be opened on the spot.

How much should you spend on a hostess gift?

There is no fixed rule. Many guests spend about what they would on a nice bottle of wine. A candle is an easy lower-cost choice, while a charcuterie set or a wine gift set works well as a thank-you for a big holiday dinner or an overnight stay. Check current prices on Amazon, since they change often during the holidays.

Is a candle a good hostess gift?

Yes, as long as the scent is crowd-pleasing. A fir or pine scent like the Thymes Frasier Fir candle suits the season, and the host can save it for later. Thymes lists a burn time of about 45 hours for the 6.5 oz jar.

Should the host open a hostess gift at the party?

Usually not. Hand it over at the door with a short note and let the host open it later. That is why gifts that do not need to be served right away, like a candle or a serving set, work better than food that needs a plate.

Does the Ivation electric wine opener need batteries?

No. It is rechargeable and charges on the included base, which has a blue LED light. Ivation says the opener removes up to 30 corks on a full charge.

Is the SMIRLY charcuterie board easy to clean?

It is bamboo, so treat it like a wooden cutting board: hand wash it, dry it right away and do not put it in the dishwasher or leave it soaking.
