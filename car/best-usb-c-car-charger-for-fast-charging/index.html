How to choose a USB-C car charger

  1. Wattage per port: Total wattage is shared. Check the maximum on each USB-C port, not just the headline number.
  2. Number of ports: Two or three ports let passengers charge too.
  3. Phone standard: iPhones fast charge over USB-C Power Delivery. Samsung's fastest mode needs PPS.
  4. Laptops: Most USB-C laptops need roughly 45W to 100W from a single USB-C port.
  5. Size: Flush, compact chargers look cleaner and are less likely to get bumped.
  6. Cable: Some chargers include a cable, some have one built in, and some include none.

Tips for faster, safer charging in the car

  • Use a good cable: A worn or low-rated cable can limit charging speed.
  • Plug fully into the socket: Loose chargers can cut out on bumps.
  • Cool the phone: Phones slow charging when hot, so avoid leaving them on a sunny dash.
  • Charge before navigation-heavy trips: GPS and screen brightness use a lot of power.
  • Unplug when parked for days: In cars with always-on sockets, unplug to avoid draining the battery.
  • Keep cables tidy: Route cables away from the gear shift and pedals.

Frequently asked questions

How many watts do I need in a car charger for fast charging?

For most phones, a USB-C port with 20W to 30W is enough for fast charging. Samsung's fastest charging uses 45W PPS, and most USB-C laptops need roughly 45W to 100W from a single port, depending on the model.

What is PPS on a car charger?

PPS (Programmable Power Supply) is a USB-C fast-charging standard used by Samsung Super Fast Charging. UGREEN lists 45W PPS on its second USB-C port for Galaxy Ultra phones.

Can a car charger charge a laptop?

Yes, if it has a high-watt USB-C port and your laptop charges over USB-C. Anker's 167.5W charger has a 100W USB-C port, and Anker says it charges a 16-inch MacBook Pro from 0 to 50% in 47 minutes.

Does a car charger drain the car battery?

In many cars the 12V socket turns off with the ignition, so the charger stops drawing power. In cars where the socket stays live, unplug the charger or your device if the car will sit for days.

Do I need a special cable for fast charging in the car?

Yes, the cable matters. Use a USB-C to USB-C cable rated for the wattage you need. Anker's 167.5W charger includes a 3.3 ft USB-C cable and the ANDHOT has a built-in cable, while UGREEN's does not include a cable.
