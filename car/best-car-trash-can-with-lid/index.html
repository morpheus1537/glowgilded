How to choose a car trash can

  1. Capacity: Around 2 gallons suits most family cars. Small cup-holder bins handle wrappers and receipts.
  2. Lid style: Flip lids, push-open lids and hinged covers keep trash hidden and help contain odors.
  3. Leakproof lining: A waterproof liner handles sticky, wet trash without soaking through.
  4. Mounting: Hanging straps work on headrests and consoles. Weighted floor bins stay put without straps.
  5. Shape retention: Stiff panels keep a fabric bin from collapsing when you toss trash in.
  6. Extra storage: Mesh pockets hold tissues, wipes and spare bags.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the best place to put a trash can in a car?

The back of a front seat, the center console or the front passenger floor are the most common spots. Hanging bins like Pritent and Farasla strap to a headrest or console, while the High Road TrashStand sits on the floor and uses a bottom weight to stay upright.

Are car trash cans leakproof?

Many are. Pritent uses a seamless hot-pressed PEVA lining, High Road and Farasla include removable leakproof liners, and HOTOR's ABS bins are listed as waterproof and leakproof, which helps with wet trash like juice boxes.

Why get a car trash can with a lid?

A lid keeps trash out of sight, helps contain odors and stops wrappers from spilling while you drive. HOTOR's push-open lid closes automatically, and High Road uses a hinged cover with a hook-and-loop closure.

What size car trash can do I need?

About 2 gallons suits most cars and family road trips; Pritent, High Road and SIBOFUEGO are all 2-gallon bins. For just receipts and wrappers, a small cup-holder bin like HOTOR is enough.

Do car trash cans need special bags?
