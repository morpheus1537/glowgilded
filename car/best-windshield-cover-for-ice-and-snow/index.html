How to choose a windshield cover

  1. Size: Measure your windshield. Truck and SUV covers are larger than standard car covers.
  2. Material: 600D Oxford fabric and multi-layer covers are thicker and more durable.
  3. How it attaches: Door flaps, mirror straps, wheel hooks and magnets each help it stay put.
  4. Scratch protection: Soft inner linings help protect glass and paint.
  5. Mirror covers: Some include separate covers for side mirrors.
  6. Storage: A storage pouch keeps a wet, snowy cover from soaking your trunk.

Tips for using a windshield cover

  • Lift wipers or tuck them under: Follow your cover's design so wipers do not freeze to the glass.
  • Install before the storm: Put it on the night before snow or freezing rain.
  • Shake, then remove: Brush off heavy snow before pulling the cover away.
  • Keep the hood clean: Wipe grit away before laying a magnetic cover on paint.
  • Store it dry: Let it dry before packing it away to avoid mildew.
  • Keep a scraper handy: You will still need one for side windows; see our winter car accessories.

Frequently asked questions

Do windshield covers really work for ice and snow?

Yes. A cover sits between the glass and falling snow, sleet or frost, so you peel it off in the morning instead of scraping. EcoNour says its rear cover saves time by eliminating the need to scrape snow.

How do windshield covers stay on in the wind?

Most use a mix of side flaps that tuck inside the closed doors, straps that hook to mirrors or wheels, and embedded magnets. Tevlaphee lists five magnets, QcoQce lists three, and AstroAI uses four elastic straps with hooks.

Will a magnetic windshield cover scratch my car?

Covers with soft inner linings are designed to avoid scratches. QcoQce says its magnets will not scratch your car surface, and Tevlaphee and EcoNour list soft inner linings. Clean grit off the hood and cover before placing it, and note that magnets will not stick to aluminum hoods.

How do I choose the right windshield cover size?

Measure your windshield and compare it to the listed cover size. The EcoNour truck cover measures 74 by 43 inches for full-size SUVs and trucks, while the QcoQce lists 145 by 118 cm for small and midsize cars.

Can I use a windshield cover in summer?

Several are all-season. EcoNour and Tevlaphee say their covers also block sun and reduce interior heat in summer, and keep leaves and debris off in fall.
