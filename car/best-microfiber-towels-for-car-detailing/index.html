Types of microfiber towels for car detailing

  1. Drying towels: Large waffle weave or twisted loop towels soak up a lot of water quickly.
  2. Plush towels: High-pile towels are soft and grab residue, great for wax, polish and quick detailer.
  3. Dual-sided towels: One plush side for cleaning and one smoother side for buffing.
  4. General-purpose packs: Thinner, cheaper towels for wheels, door jambs, interiors and engine bays.
  5. Size: 16 x 16 inches is a handy all-round size; drying towels run around 24 x 36 inches.

Detailing tips for scratch-free results

  • Rinse first: Remove loose grit with water before touching the paint.
  • Pat or drag gently: Lay a drying towel flat and pull it slowly instead of scrubbing.
  • Color-code: Keep separate colors for paint, glass, interior and wheels.
  • Fold into quarters: Folding gives you eight clean sides to rotate through.
  • Retire dropped towels: A towel that hits the ground can hold grit; move it to wheel duty.
  • Wash right: Wash microfiber separately, skip fabric softener and air-dry or tumble on low heat.
  • Finish the interior: Pair towels with a vacuum from our best car vacuum for pet hair guide.

How to build a simple microfiber kit

You do not need dozens of specialty towels to get great results. A practical starter kit looks like this:

  1. One large drying towel, such as the Chemical Guys Gray Matter or Blackline, for the body panels after washing.
  2. Four to six all-purpose towels, like the MR.SIGA pack, for quick detailer, door jambs and a final wipe.
  3. Two or three plush towels, like the Chemical Guys Workhorse, reserved for removing wax, sealant or ceramic spray.
  4. A bulk pack of cheaper cloths, like the Amazon Basics 144-pack, for wheels, tires, interior plastics and anything greasy.

Store clean towels in a sealed bin or bag so they do not collect dust and grit in the garage, and keep used towels in a separate bucket until wash day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best microfiber towel for drying a car?

A large drying towel works fastest. The Blackline 24 x 36 inch twisted loop towel says it absorbs more than 6 times its weight, and the Chemical Guys Gray Matter is a 25 x 36 inch waffle weave towel designed for streak-free drying.

How many microfiber towels do I need to detail a car?

Plan on one or two large drying towels plus several smaller towels for wax removal, glass, interior and wheels. Keep separate towels for paint and for dirty jobs like wheels to avoid dragging grit onto paint.

What is the difference between plush and waffle weave microfiber?

Plush or high-pile microfiber is soft and grabs dust, wax residue and detail spray, which suits buffing. Waffle weave has a grid texture that soaks up water and leaves fewer streaks, which suits drying and glass.

How do you wash microfiber towels?

Wash them separately from cotton items so they do not collect lint. MR.SIGA recommends warm water under 60°C and Amazon Basics says to wash with like colors. Skip fabric softener, which can clog the fibers.

Can microfiber towels scratch car paint?

Clean, soft microfiber is designed to be non-abrasive, but any towel can scratch if it picks up grit. Rinse the car well first, use clean towels on paint, and retire towels that have been dropped on the ground.
