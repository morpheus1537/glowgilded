Frequently asked questions

What is a good car gift for a woman?

Practical gifts that also look nice tend to win: a bling steering wheel cover, a patterned car trash can, headrest hooks for her purse, a front-seat organizer or a sturdy phone mount. Think about how she uses her car, whether it is commuting, school runs or road trips.

Will a steering wheel cover fit my car?

The ChuLian bling cover fits steering wheels 14.5-15 inches (37-38 cm) in diameter, which the listing calls universal fit. Measure the wheel before you buy.

How do car headrest hooks work?

Hooks like the Amooca 4-pack clip onto the metal headrest posts without tools and fit rods 0.32-0.55 inches in diameter. Each hook holds up to 55 lbs and rotates so it can face front or back.

Does the VANMASS mount work on any dashboard?

It can mount on a dashboard, windshield or air vent. VANMASS notes that the suction cup needs a flat surface and fails on leather dashboards, and the vent hook is not compatible with round, cross or diagonal vents.

Can the High Road CarHop be used as a cooler?

Yes. High Road says the insulated main compartment keeps food and drinks chilled for up to 8 hours with cold packs. It recommends cold packs rather than ice.
