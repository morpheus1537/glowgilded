How to choose a snow brush and ice scraper

  1. Reach: A 27-inch brush suits sedans; SUVs and trucks benefit from 39 inches or more.
  2. Head design: Pivoting heads reach corners; a squeegee clears slush and water.
  3. Bristles: Soft, flexible bristles that stay soft in the cold are kinder to paint.
  4. Scraper: A wide, sturdy scraper and ice-crushing teeth make quick work of thick ice.
  5. Grip: Foam grips stay warmer and more secure in gloves.
  6. Storage: Detachable designs and storage bags fit easily in the trunk.

How to clear snow and ice from your car safely

  • Start the car first: Turn on the defroster (with the exhaust pipe clear of snow) while you brush.
  • Top down: Clear the roof first, then windows, lights, mirrors and the hood.
  • Push, do not scrub: Push snow off in long strokes to avoid grinding grit into paint.
  • Score thick ice: Use the scraper's teeth or knuckles first, then the flat blade.
  • Clear lights and sensors: Headlights, taillights and cameras need to be visible.
  • Prevent tomorrow's ice: A windshield cover helps; see our winter car accessories.

Snow brush vs. snow shovel

A car snow brush is built for glass and paint, with soft bristles and plastic scrapers. A driveway shovel or steel ice scraper is for pavement and can scratch a car. For the driveway, see our snow removal tools guide.

Frequently asked questions

What size snow brush do I need for an SUV?

For SUVs, trucks and minivans, an extendable brush that reaches 39 inches or more makes it easier to clear the middle of the roof and windshield. The AstroAI 46-inch, Snow MOOver 39-inch and JOYTUTUS 47-inch all extend.

Will a snow brush scratch my car paint?

A clean, soft-bristle brush is designed not to scratch. AstroAI describes its bristles as paint-safe; keep brushes free of grit and push snow rather than scrubbing.

Is it OK to use hot water to melt ice on a windshield?

It is best not to. Hot water on cold glass can risk cracking it. Run your defroster and use an ice scraper instead.

Should I clear snow off my car roof?

Yes. Snow left on the roof can slide onto your windshield when you brake or blow onto cars behind you, and some states require it. A long-reach brush makes roof clearing easier.

How do I store a snow brush in my car?

Choose a detachable model and keep it in its bag in the trunk or behind a seat. The AstroAI 27-inch and 46-inch both include storage bags.
