How to choose a jump starter for winter

  1. Engine size: Check the listed gas and diesel engine limits for your vehicle.
  2. Safety features: Spark-proof and reverse polarity protection help prevent mistakes in the dark and cold.
  3. Extras: Built-in air compressors, power banks and flashlights add winter usefulness.
  4. Size and weight: Smaller packs fit a glovebox; bigger units belong in the trunk.
  5. Charging: Pick one you will actually keep charged, ideally with USB-C.

Winter jump-starting tips

  • Keep it charged: Top up your jump starter every month or two, and after every use.
  • Store it smart: Check the listed temperature range and bring it inside on the coldest nights if needed.
  • Clamp in the right order: Follow your manual, usually red to positive first, then black to a ground point or negative.
  • Replace weak batteries: A battery that needs repeated jumps should be tested or replaced.

Frequently asked questions

Why do car batteries die more often in cold weather?

Cold temperatures slow the chemical reaction inside a battery, so it delivers less power, while cold, thick engine oil makes the engine harder to turn over. That combination is why weak batteries often fail on the first freezing mornings.

How many amps do I need in a jump starter?

Match the jump starter to your engine size, not just peak amps. NOCO lists the GB70 for gas engines up to 8.0 liters and diesel up to 6.0 liters, and AstroAI lists the S8 Air for gas up to 9.0 liters and diesel up to 6.5 liters.

Can I leave a jump starter in my car during winter?

Many people do, but extreme cold can reduce a pack's output and charge. Check your model's operating and storage temperature range, keep it charged, and consider bringing it inside on the coldest nights.

Is a lithium jump starter better than a lead-acid one?

Lithium packs like the NOCO GB70 are smaller and lighter, while lead-acid units like the Clore JNC660 are heavier but use a large battery and thick cables. Both types work; choose based on size, weight and the vehicles you drive.

Can a jump starter charge my phone?

Yes, for most models here. The NOCO GB70 has an integrated power bank, the AstroAI S8 Air lists a high-capacity power bank, and the HALO Bolt Air has a 58.8Wh USB power bank.
