How to choose a ceramic spray coating

  1. Stand-alone or topper: Some sprays are the main protection. Others boost an existing wax or coating.
  2. Surfaces: Check whether it is safe on glass, trim, wheels and plastic.
  3. Application: Spray and wipe is easiest. Some products also work as a waterless wash.
  4. Coverage: Bigger bottles cover more cars and more coats.
  5. Prep: Every product works best on a clean, dry, decontaminated car.
  6. Microfiber towels: Use clean, soft towels to avoid streaks and scratches.

How to apply a ceramic spray coating

  • Wash thoroughly: Remove dirt, salt and old residue first.
  • Decontaminate: A clay bar removes bonded grime so the coating can bond.
  • Dry completely: Unless the label says it works on wet paint, start with a dry car.
  • Work panel by panel: Spray, spread, then buff before moving on.
  • Use two towels: One to spread, one clean towel to buff.
  • Let it cure: Keep the car dry for the time on the label.
  • Reapply when beading fades: Water that sheets instead of beading means it is time.

Frequently asked questions

Is ceramic spray as good as a professional ceramic coating?

No. Spray-on ceramic products are easier and cheaper to apply but are thinner and do not last as long as a professionally installed coating. They are a good middle ground between traditional wax and a pro coating.

How long does a ceramic spray coating last?

It depends on the product, the weather and how often you wash the car. SHINE ARMOR says Fortify Quick Coat gives months of protection; reapply when water stops beading.

Can you apply ceramic spray in the sun or in winter?

Turtle Wax says its Hybrid Solutions Ceramic Spray Wax can be applied in direct sunlight. For any product, start with a clean, dry car and follow the label; in cold weather, work in a garage if you can.

Do I need to clay bar my car before using a ceramic spray?

It helps. Turtle Wax recommends removing contaminants with a clay bar or polish before application for best results. At minimum, wash and dry the car thoroughly.

Can ceramic spray be used on glass and wheels?

Many can. Turtle Wax lists glass, headlights, trim and wheels, and Nexgen lists paint, glass, plastic, rubber, vinyl, chrome and wheels. Check each label before use.
