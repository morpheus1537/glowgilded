Road trip packing checklist for kids

  • Entertainment: tablet with downloaded shows, headphones, crayons, sticker books, small new toys.
  • Food and drinks: a cooler of water and milk, easy snacks, a few treats for emergencies.
  • Comfort: small pillow, a blanket, a favorite stuffed animal and layers for temperature changes.
  • Cleanup: wipes, paper towels, trash bags, a change of clothes for younger kids.
  • Health: any medicines, a small first-aid kit and motion sickness bags.
  • Car safety: in winter, pack a kit for the unexpected; see our winter car emergency kit essentials.

Tips for a smoother road trip with kids

  1. Leave at nap time or early morning. Kids who sleep for the first hours make the drive feel shorter.
  2. Stop every two to three hours. Let everyone stretch, run around and use the bathroom.
  3. Ration the surprises. Wrap a few small toys and hand one out every hour or so.
  4. Mix screen and screen-free time. Alternate shows with drawing, games and audiobooks.
  5. Keep the car tidy. A quick tidy at each stop prevents the back seat from turning into chaos. Our car interior cleaning essentials help after the trip.

Frequently asked questions

What do kids need for a long road trip?

Plan for entertainment, snacks and drinks, comfort and cleanup. A backseat organizer keeps things within reach, a lap tray gives kids a surface for drawing and eating, and a small cooler keeps drinks cold. Pack wipes, a change of clothes and a few new toys or activities too.

How do you keep kids entertained in the car without screens?

Give them a lap tray with crayons and drawing supplies, sticker books, travel games like I Spy, audiobooks and music. The kids travel tray in this guide has an erasable drawing surface and comes with 30 printable road trip activity sheets.

Are backseat organizers safe?

Keep heavy or hard items out of the pockets, since loose objects can move in a sudden stop. Use organizers for light items like tablets, crayons, wipes and soft toys, and make sure straps are tight.

How long does a car seat cooler keep drinks cold?

High Road says its SnackStash cooler keeps drinks and snacks cool for up to 10 hours and hot foods warm for up to 6 hours. Chill time is shorter above 70°F, and the brand recommends cooler packs over ice.

Will a vent phone mount work in my car?

The Miracase mount fits horizontal and vertical vents with blades under 1.4 inches wide and holds phones from 4.0 to 7.2 inches. It isn't suitable for round vents.
