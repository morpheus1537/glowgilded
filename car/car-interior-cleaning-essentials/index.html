How to clean your car interior in one hour

  1. Declutter. Remove trash, mats and loose items.
  2. Dust from the top. Brush vents, seams and controls so dust falls to the floor.
  3. Wipe hard surfaces. Spray interior cleaner onto a towel, not the screen, and wipe the dash, doors, console and wheel.
  4. Treat stains. Spray stain remover on fabric spots, agitate and wipe.
  5. Vacuum last. Vacuum seats, carpet and mats once all the dust has fallen.
  6. Glass. Finish windows with a clean, dry microfiber towel.

Buying tips

  • Check your surfaces. Make sure each cleaner is listed as safe for your seat material.
  • Test first. Try any new cleaner on a hidden area before a whole seat.
  • Spray the towel, not the screen. It avoids overspray on electronics and glass.
  • Keep towels separate. Use one set for glass and another for dirtier jobs.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to clean my car interior myself?

Five things cover almost every job: an all-purpose interior cleaner, a stain remover for fabric and carpet, soft detailing brushes, a stack of microfiber towels and a cordless handheld vacuum. Work from the top down and vacuum last.

What is the best way to clean a car dashboard?

Vacuum or brush the dust off first, then spray an interior cleaner onto a microfiber towel and wipe. Chemical Guys Total Interior Cleaner is a spray-and-wipe formula for leather, vinyl, plastic, rubber and glass that the listing says needs no rinsing or buffing.

How do you get stains out of car seats?

Spray a fabric-safe interior stain remover, agitate lightly with a soft brush or microfiber towel, and wipe it away. CAR GUYS Super Cleaner is a no-rinse formula made for leather, upholstery, carpet and fabric; test any cleaner on a hidden spot first.

How do you clean car air vents?

Use a small soft detailing brush to sweep dust out of each vent slat, ideally while holding a vacuum nozzle nearby. The HMPLL set includes dedicated air vent brushes for this.

How often should you clean the inside of your car?

A quick vacuum and wipe-down every couple of weeks keeps most cars in good shape, with a deeper clean including stains and vents every few months or before a long trip.
