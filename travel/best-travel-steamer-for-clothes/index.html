How to choose a travel steamer

  1. Voltage: Dual-voltage (100-240V) steamers work abroad with just a plug adapter. 120V-only steamers are for the US, Canada and similar countries.
  2. Power and heat-up: Higher wattage and fast heat-up times mean quicker touch-ups.
  3. 2-in-1 ironing: A heated dry-iron panel creates crisp collars, cuffs and creases.
  4. Tank size: Bigger tanks steam more garments per fill.
  5. Weight and size: Compact steamers around 1.6 to 1.9 pounds fit in a carry-on.
  6. Leak protection: Leak-proof designs matter when you steam at odd angles.

Tips for steaming on the road

  • Empty before packing: Always pour out the water before the steamer goes back in your bag.
  • Hang and pull taut: Hang garments and gently pull fabric straight while steaming.
  • Steam from the inside: For delicate fabrics, steam the inside of the garment.
  • Check voltage first: Never plug a 120V-only steamer into a 220V outlet, even with an adapter.
  • Use the right adapter: Pick a plug adapter rated for the steamer's wattage.
  • Pack smarter: Rolling clothes and using a garment bag reduces wrinkles; see our carry-on packing essentials.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take a travel steamer on a plane?

In the US, TSA generally allows clothing steamers in carry-on and checked bags, but empty the water tank before you pack it. Rules can change and vary by country, so check your airline and the TSA website before you fly.

Do I need a dual-voltage steamer for international travel?

Yes, if you are traveling to a country with 220 to 240-volt power. Jack & Rose, OGHom's 110-240V model and Reewit are dual voltage and only need a plug adapter. The 120V-only OGHom and BEAUTURAL steamers can be damaged if plugged into 220V, even with an adapter.

Is a steamer better than a hotel iron?

A steamer is faster for hanging garments and gentler on delicate fabrics like silk and chiffon, and you do not need an ironing board. The 2-in-1 models here also have a dry ironing panel for crisp collars and cuffs.

How long does a travel steamer take to heat up?

Listed heat-up times range from 15 seconds for the Jack & Rose to about 20 to 30 seconds for the Reewit, OGHom and BEAUTURAL models.

Can you put perfume or essential oils in a steamer?

No. OGHom says scented substances like perfume and essential oils are strictly prohibited in its steamers. Use plain water, and check each listing for water recommendations.
