How to choose an anti-theft travel bag

  1. Slash resistance: Look for slash-resistant body panels and straps with steel cables or mesh.
  2. Locking zippers: Zipper pulls that clip or lock make it much harder for someone to open your bag in a crowd.
  3. Lock-down strap: A strap that clips around a chair or table leg stops grab-and-run thefts at cafes.
  4. RFID blocking: RFID-blocking slots help protect contactless cards and passports from skimming.
  5. Size and weight: Carry only what you need. A small, light bag is more comfortable on long sightseeing days.
  6. Style: A bag that looks like an everyday purse or sling draws less attention than a bulky travel bag.

Tips for staying safe from pickpockets

  • Wear it in front: In crowds, slide your crossbody to the front where you can see it and keep a hand on it.
  • Lock your zippers: Use the locking pulls every time, not just in busy places.
  • Clip it at cafes: Wrap the lock-down strap around your chair leg instead of hanging the bag on the back.
  • Split your money: Keep a backup card and some cash in a separate place, like a hotel safe.

Frequently asked questions

Do anti-theft bags really work?

They make a thief's job harder. Features like slash-resistant panels and straps, locking zippers and straps that clip to a chair help stop the quick slash, grab or pickpocket attempts that happen in crowded tourist areas.

What is a 5-point anti-theft system?

Travelon uses the term for its combined security features. On the Heritage bag, Travelon lists slash-resistant body panels, a slash-resistant strap with dual steel cables, locking zipper pulls, a lock-down strap and RFID-blocking pockets.

Is RFID blocking necessary when traveling?

It is an inexpensive extra layer of protection. RFID-blocking pockets are designed to stop electronic skimming of contactless cards and passports, and every bag in this guide includes at least one RFID-blocking pocket or slot.

Is a crossbody or a sling bag better for travel?

A crossbody keeps the bag at your side or in front of you where you can see it. A sling like the Pacsafe Vibe 150 can also be worn as a waist pack. Both are safer than a backpack in crowds because you can keep a hand on them.

Can you lock an anti-theft bag to a chair?

Yes. Travelon's lock-down straps can be attached to a stationary object like a chair, and Pacsafe says the Vibe 150's detachable, wire-reinforced strap can be locked to a table or chair, with an optional padlock.
