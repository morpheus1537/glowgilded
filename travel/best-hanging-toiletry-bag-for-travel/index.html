What to look for

  1. A sturdy hook: Metal hooks that swivel make hanging easier on odd towel bars and doors.
  2. Compartments: Separate zipped and elastic pockets keep bottles upright and small items from getting lost.
  3. Size: Match it to your bottles; full-size shampoo needs a bigger bag.
  4. Water resistance: Wipe-clean linings or PVC pockets make spills less of a disaster.
  5. TSA liquids: A removable clear bag speeds up airport security.

Packing tips

  • Decant into small bottles: Saves weight and keeps carry-on liquids under TSA limits.
  • Keep liquids together: Pack them in one clear bag for quick screening.

Frequently asked questions

Why use a hanging toiletry bag?

Hotel and guest bathrooms often have little counter space. A hanging bag hooks onto a towel bar, door hook or shower rod, keeping everything visible, upright and off a wet counter.

Is a hanging toiletry bag TSA approved?

The bag itself does not need to be. For carry-on liquids, TSA's 3-1-1 rule limits you to containers of 3.4 ounces or less in one quart-size bag. The BAGSMART organizer includes a separate clear TSA-approved bag for this.

What size toiletry bag do I need?

For a weekend with travel-size bottles, a compact bag like the NISHEL at about 10.4 by 4.5 by 8 inches is enough. For longer trips or full-size bottles, choose a larger organizer like the BAGSMART or HOTOR, which unfold to hold more.

How do I stop toiletries from leaking in my bag?

Use leak-proof bottles, leave a little air at the top, and pack liquids upright in elastic loops. Bags with waterproof PVC or water-resistant linings, like the HOTOR and BAGSMART, are easier to wipe clean if something does leak.

Can men use these toiletry bags?

Absolutely. The HOTOR and Vorspack are marketed for both men and women, and the BAGSMART dopp kit is designed as a men's shaving and toiletry bag.
