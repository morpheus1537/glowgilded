International travel checklist

  • Documents: passport (check its expiration date), visas, printed and digital copies, travel insurance details.
  • Money: two cards stored separately, some local cash, and a hidden neck wallet.
  • Tech: phone, charger, a portable charger and a travel adapter for your destination.
  • Bags: luggage trackers, TSA locks and a luggage scale.
  • Comfort: for overnight flights, see our long haul flight essentials.

Buying tips

  • Check the plug type. Outlets vary by country, so confirm your destination's plug before you go.
  • Match trackers to your phone. AirTags need an iPhone; choose another tracker for Android.
  • Weigh before you leave and before you return. Souvenirs add up fast.
  • Set lock codes at home. Choose a combination you will remember after a long flight.
  • Test the door lock at home. Practice once so it is quick in a hotel.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring when traveling internationally?

Beyond your passport, the essentials that prevent the most stress are a secure way to carry documents, a luggage tracker, TSA approved locks, a luggage scale and a travel adapter for your destination. Add copies of your passport and insurance details stored separately.

Is a neck wallet or money belt better for international travel?

Both keep valuables hidden under clothing. A neck wallet like the HERO is easier to reach and fits a passport plus a large phone, while a money belt sits flatter at the waist; choose the one you will actually wear every day.

Can you put an AirTag in checked luggage?

Yes, many travelers do, so they can see their bag's location in the Find My app. The 2nd generation AirTag also lets you share an item's location with over 50 airline partners if your bag is lost, according to the listing; it requires an iPhone.

Do I need TSA approved locks for international flights?

If you fly through the United States, TSA approved locks let security open and relock your bag without cutting the lock. The SURE LOCK models add a red indicator that shows if TSA opened the lock.

Do portable door locks work in every hotel?

No. The Addalock is designed for most inward-swinging hinged doors, which covers many hotel rooms and rentals, but it will not work on doors that open outward. Check the door before relying on it.
