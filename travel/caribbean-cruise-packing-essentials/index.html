Caribbean cruise packing checklist

  • Documents: Passport or required ID, cruise documents and printed luggage tags.
  • Sun care: Reef-friendly sunscreen if your ports require it, sunglasses and a hat.
  • Layers: A light jacket or sweater for windy decks and cool dining rooms.
  • Day bag: A small waterproof bag for beach days and excursions.
  • Medications: Pack them in your carry-on, along with anything you need on the first afternoon.
  • Cabin helpers: Magnetic hooks, a fan and a non-surge power strip.

Buying tips

  • Check your line's rules first: Each cruise line publishes prohibited items; power strips and fans are common questions.
  • Pack hooks and tags in your carry-on: Checked bags can arrive at your cabin hours after you board.
  • Charge before you sail: Fully charge the fan and power banks the night before.
  • Count your devices: Make sure your power strip has enough ports for everyone.
  • Check the current listing: Pack sizes and colors vary.

Frequently asked questions

What should I pack for a Caribbean cruise in winter?

Pack for warm weather in port and cooler evenings on deck, plus a few cabin helpers. Magnetic hooks add storage, a small fan helps with airflow, luggage tag holders protect printed tags, and a cruise-friendly power strip solves the limited outlet problem.

Are magnetic hooks allowed on cruise ships?

Many cruisers use magnetic hooks because cabin walls are often metal, and they leave no marks. Rules can vary by cruise line, so check your line's guidance before you sail.

Can I bring a power strip on a cruise?

Cruise lines generally prohibit surge-protected power strips. The power strip in this guide is listed without surge protection specifically for cruise use, but always confirm your cruise line's current electrical rules before packing.

Do I need luggage tag holders for a cruise?

Most cruise lines ask you to print paper luggage tags. A plastic holder keeps them from tearing or getting wet when porters handle your bags. The SEAVILIA holders are listed as waterproof with stainless steel hardware.

Are fans allowed on cruise ships?

Policies vary. The magnetic fan in this guide is listed as complying with major cruise lines' policies, including a Norwegian Cruise Line policy update cited in the listing, but check your own line's rules before you go.

The bottom line

For most first-time cruisers, magnetic hooks are the single best cabin upgrade, adding instant storage to metal walls. Add SEAVILIA luggage tag holders to protect your printed tags on embarkation day, a non-surge travel power strip to charge everything at once and the magnetic cruise fan if you sleep better with a breeze.
