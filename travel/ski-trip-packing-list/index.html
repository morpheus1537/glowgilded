The ski trip packing checklist

On the mountain

  • Waterproof ski jacket and pants
  • Moisture-wicking base layers (top and bottom)
  • Fleece or light down mid layer
  • Ski socks (one pair per day, ideally)
  • Waterproof gloves or mittens, plus liners
  • Helmet and goggles
  • Neck gaiter and a thin beanie for under the helmet
  • Sunscreen and SPF lip balm
  • Hand warmers
  • Small backpack, water bottle and snacks
  • Lift pass, ID and a phone with a charged battery

Off the mountain

  • Warm, waterproof après-ski boots
  • Casual layers and a warm coat
  • Swimsuit for the hot tub
  • Moisturizer for dry mountain air
  • Chargers and a portable battery
  • Boot dryer

If you are renting skis, snowboards or boots, book ahead and bring your own socks and goggles.

Ski trip packing tips

  • Wear your boots on the plane if you own them; they are the hardest thing to replace.
  • Pack layers, not bulk. Base, mid and shell layers adapt to any weather.
  • Put goggles in a pouch so the lens does not get scratched.
  • Pack a spare pair of socks in your backpack for the afternoon.
  • Bring a small first-aid kit with blister care.

Frequently asked questions

What should I pack for a ski trip?

Pack a waterproof jacket and pants, base layers, a mid layer, ski socks, gloves or mittens, a helmet, goggles, a neck gaiter, a beanie, sunscreen and lip balm, plus après-ski clothes and boots. Hand warmers and a boot dryer make the trip more comfortable.

How many pairs of ski socks should I bring?

Plan on one pair per ski day if you can, or at least two pairs so one can dry while you wear the other. Wear one thin-to-medium pair of ski socks, not several layers, which can cut circulation.

Can you wear glasses under ski goggles?

Yes, with OTG (over-the-glasses) goggles. The OutdoorMaster OTG goggles are designed to fit over glasses and have a dual-layer anti-fog lens.

Are rechargeable hand warmers allowed on planes?

Rechargeable hand warmers contain lithium batteries, which airlines generally require in carry-on bags rather than checked luggage. Check your airline's rules before you fly.

Do I need a boot dryer for a ski trip?

It is not required, but damp boots are cold boots. The PEET Original dryer uses silent convection heat with no fan to dry boots and is safe on all materials, according to PEET.

The bottom line

Do not hit the slopes without good merino ski socks and OTG goggles if you wear glasses. Add a KGC neck gaiter for wind, UNIHAND rechargeable hand warmers for cold lifts and the PEET boot dryer so every morning starts with warm, dry boots.
