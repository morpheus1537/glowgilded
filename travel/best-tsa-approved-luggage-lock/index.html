How to choose a TSA luggage lock

  1. TSA or Travel Sentry approval: This lets screeners open and relock your bag without damage.
  2. Cable or shackle: Cable locks thread through small zipper pulls easily. Shackle padlocks feel more solid.
  3. Combination or key: Combinations mean no key to lose. Keyed locks are faster to open.
  4. 3 or 4 digits: A 4-digit lock has 10,000 possible codes, compared with 1,000 for 3 digits.
  5. Open alert: An indicator tells you if your bag was opened for inspection.
  6. Pack size: Buy enough for every checked bag and carry-on you want to secure.
  7. Warranty: Several brands offer lifetime coverage.

Luggage lock tips

  • Set your code before you leave: Practice opening the lock at home.
  • Write down the code: Store it somewhere safe, but not on the bag.
  • Keep keys in your carry-on: Never pack the only key in the locked suitcase.
  • Lock zipper pulls together: Thread the cable through both zipper pulls on the main compartment.
  • Carry valuables on board: Keep electronics, medication and documents in your carry-on.
  • International trips: TSA rules apply to US airports, so check other countries' rules too. Our international travel essentials list covers more prep.

Frequently asked questions

What does TSA approved mean on a luggage lock?

A TSA approved or Travel Sentry lock can be opened by airport security screeners with a master tool, so they can inspect your checked bag and relock it without cutting the lock. Non-approved locks may be cut off if your bag is selected for inspection.

Should I lock my checked luggage?

Locking a checked bag deters casual tampering and keeps zippers from opening in transit. Use a TSA approved lock on flights from or within the US, and keep valuables, medications and electronics in your carry-on instead.

Is a combination or keyed luggage lock better?

Combination locks mean no small key to lose, and you can reset the code. Keyed locks like the Master Lock 4689Q are quick to open and keyed alike, but you need to keep the key safe, ideally in your carry-on.

What is the open alert on a TSA lock?

An open alert is a small indicator, often a red button or dot, that pops up when the lock has been opened with a TSA master key. The BV TSA008 and SURE LOCK listings include this feature, and Forge's cable locks are designed so the agent has to relock them.

Do TSA locks work on hard-shell suitcases?

Cable locks are the most flexible choice. Forge says its cable fits through standard zippers and hard case lock holes. Many hard-shell suitcases also have a built-in TSA lock, so check yours before buying.
