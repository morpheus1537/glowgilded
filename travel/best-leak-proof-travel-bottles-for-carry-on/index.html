What to look for in travel bottles

  1. Seal design: Look for multi-layer lids, valves or locking caps. These matter more than the bottle itself.
  2. TSA-friendly size: Containers of 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less, fitting in a single quart-size bag.
  3. Soft silicone: Squeezable bottles make it easier to use thick products.
  4. Wide openings: Easier to fill without a funnel and easier to clean.
  5. Extras: Some kits add a toiletry bag, jars, spray bottles and labels.

Packing tips for leak-free toiletries

  • Fill to about 80%: Leave room for expansion during pressure changes.
  • Burp the bottle: Squeeze out a little air before closing the cap.
  • Bag everything: Keep bottles in a zippered bag, even if they are leak-proof.
  • Label clearly: Shampoo and conditioner look identical in matching bottles.
  • Pack upright: Stand bottles up in your bag's toiletry pocket where possible.
  • Choose solids where you can: Solid shampoo and stick deodorant do not count toward your liquids.
  • Pick the right bag: Our best carry-on backpack for weekend trips guide covers bags with easy-access liquids pockets.

Frequently asked questions

What size travel bottles are allowed in carry-on luggage?

In the US, TSA allows liquids in containers of 3.4 ounces (100 ml) or less, all fitting in one quart-size bag per passenger. Every bottle in this guide is 3.4 ounces or smaller.

How do I keep travel bottles from leaking on a plane?

Do not overfill them, squeeze out a little air before closing, make sure caps are fully shut or locked, and pack them upright in a zippered bag. Pressure changes during flights can push liquid out of loose caps.

Are silicone travel bottles better than plastic?

Silicone bottles are soft and squeezable, so it is easier to get thick products like conditioner out. Hard plastic bottles suit thin liquids and sprays. Most kits here mix silicone bottles with plastic jars or sprays.

Can I put oil-based products in silicone travel bottles?

Check the listing. Quatish says oil-based products are not recommended in its bottles, so many travelers keep oils and serums in small glass or hard plastic containers instead.

How do I clean silicone travel bottles?

Rinse them with warm soapy water and use a small brush. Quatish, Mrsdry and Gemice all highlight wide openings that fit a brush. Let them dry fully with caps open before storing.
