How to build a long haul flight kit

  • Prioritize sleep: on overnight flights, the neck pillow and sleep mask matter most. Pack them where you can reach them before takeoff.
  • Put socks on before boarding: compression socks are easiest to pull on at home or in the airport, not in a cramped seat.
  • Set up your seat early: slip the tray cover on as soon as you sit down so everything has a place.
  • Dress in layers: cabins get cold on long flights; a light sweater or a packable blanket helps you sleep.
  • Hydrate: bring an empty water bottle to fill after security, and go easy on alcohol and caffeine.

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring on a long haul flight for comfort?

The four things that make the biggest difference are a supportive neck pillow, compression socks, a light-blocking sleep mask and a way to keep your essentials within reach. Add layers, water and snacks and you are set for a 10 to 15 hour flight.

Should you wear compression socks on a long flight?

Many travelers do, to help their legs feel less heavy after hours of sitting. Physix Gear socks use 20-30 mmHg graduated compression, strongest at the ankle, and the listing recommends putting them on before a long flight; ask your doctor first if you have a circulation condition.

How do you sleep on a long haul flight in economy?

Choose a window seat, support your head with a neck pillow that cradles your chin and neck, block light with a contoured sleep mask, and keep warm. Avoiding screens before you try to sleep also helps.

Do tray table covers fit all airplane seats?

Is the MZOO sleep mask good for side sleepers?
