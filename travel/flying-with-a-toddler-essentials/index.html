Tips for flying with a toddler

  1. Book smart. Nonstop flights and times that line up with naps are easier.
  2. Board strategically. Some parents prefer to board last to cut time sitting on the plane; others want early boarding to set up. Pick what suits your child.
  3. Help with ear pressure. Offer a drink or snack during takeoff and landing to encourage swallowing.
  4. Pack in layers. Keep a small pouch of activities and snacks at your seat and the rest overhead.
  5. Rotate activities. Bring out one item at a time and save a surprise for the last hour.
  6. Download everything. Shows, music and games should be on the device before you leave home.
  7. Pack a spare outfit. For your toddler and for yourself.

Toddler carry-on checklist

  • Kids headphones and a charged tablet
  • Two or three quiet activities
  • Snacks and an empty sippy cup
  • Wipes, diapers or pull-ups, and a changing pad
  • A change of clothes and a light blanket
  • A favorite stuffed animal or comfort item
  • Any medicines and a small first-aid kit

Frequently asked questions

What do I need when flying with a toddler?

Pack a small bag of activities, kids headphones for a tablet, plenty of snacks, an empty water cup, wipes, a change of clothes for both of you, diapers if needed, and any comfort item like a blanket or stuffed animal.

How do I keep a toddler busy on a plane?

Rotate short activities: stickers, mess-free coloring, a doodle tablet, Wikki Stix, snacks and a few shows. Bringing out something new every 20 to 30 minutes works better than handing over everything at once.

Do toddlers need special headphones for flying?

Kids headphones with volume limiting are a good idea, since airplane noise tempts children to turn the sound up. The iClever HS19 can be limited to 85dBA, and it plugs into devices with a 3.5mm headphone jack.

Are these toys suitable for 2-year-olds?

Several are labeled for ages 3 and up, including the Crayola Color Wonder set, the LCD tablet and Wikki Stix. Check the age labels, and supervise younger toddlers with small parts and stickers.

Can you bring a tablet and headphones in carry-on luggage?

Yes. Tablets and wired headphones are allowed in carry-on bags. Download shows before you leave, since in-flight Wi-Fi can be slow or expensive.

The bottom line

The iClever HS19 kids headphones are the single most useful item when flying with a toddler, letting them watch shows at a safer volume. Pair them with a few mess-free activities, like the Crayola Color Wonder set, the EooCoo LCD tablets, Wikki Stix and the Cupkin sticker book, and rotate them through the flight. With snacks, a spare outfit and a little patience, you'll land with your sanity intact.
