How to choose a sleep mask for travel

  1. Shape: Contoured 3D masks seal out light and leave room for lashes. Flat masks are more packable but press on the eyes.
  2. Nose bridge: Most light leaks in around the nose. Raised or adjustable nose bridges help.
  3. Sleeping position: Side sleepers and window-seat nappers need thin sides that do not dig in.
  4. Fabric: Silky fabrics feel cool and smooth. Cotton feels soft and familiar. Silk is gentle on skin and night creams.
  5. Strap: An adjustable strap keeps the mask in place without squeezing your head.
  6. Care and packing: Check whether it is machine washable, and consider a mask that comes with a travel bag.

Tips for sleeping on a plane

  • Book a window seat: You get something to lean on and nobody climbing over you.
  • Stack your kit: Mask, earplugs and a neck pillow work better together than alone.
  • Dress warm: Cabins get cold, so pack a layer or a light scarf.
  • Help circulation: On long flights, consider compression socks for flying and stand up when you can.
  • Shift your clock: Start adjusting sleep a day or two before a big time zone change.
  • Skip the screen: Put devices away 30 minutes before you want to sleep.

Using a sleep mask to beat jet lag

Light is the main signal your body uses to set its clock, so a sleep mask is one of the simplest jet lag tools. On an overnight eastbound flight, wear your mask as soon as the meal service ends to sleep on destination time. When you arrive, get outside in daylight, then use the mask again at night if your hotel room has bright streetlights or a glowing smoke detector. Keep it in your personal item, not your checked bag, so it is always within reach.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best type of sleep mask for flying?

A contoured 3D mask with deep eye cups and a snug nose bridge blocks cabin light best without pressing on your eyes. Thin sides, like LitBear's, help if you lean against the window or a neck pillow.

Do 3D sleep masks block light better?

Often, yes. Molded 3D masks like MZOO and LitBear have contoured cups and a raised nose bridge that seal around the eyes, and the cups leave room to blink and protect lashes and makeup.

Are silk sleep masks better for skin?

Silk feels smooth and OLESILK says it does not absorb skin lotions, so it won't interfere with night eye creams or moisturizers. Many people with sensitive skin prefer it.

How do you wash a travel sleep mask?

Follow the label. LitBear recommends hand washing in water below 104°F and air drying, while MZOO says its mask can be machine washed.

Can I use a weighted eye mask on a plane?

Yes, if you sit back or lean on a pillow. The nodpod has no straps and drapes over your eyes, so it works best when your head is supported, and it can stay soft after time in the freezer at home.
