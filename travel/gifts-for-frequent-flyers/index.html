Frequently asked questions

What is a good gift for someone who flies a lot?

Pick something small that solves a common in-flight annoyance: a phone holder for the tray table, a Bluetooth adapter so they can use wireless earbuds with seatback screens, a good sleep mask, or a packable blanket.

Can you use AirPods with airplane entertainment?

Not directly, because seatback screens use a 3.5mm headphone jack. The Twelve South AirFly SE plugs into that jack and sends the audio to AirPods, wireless earbuds or headphones over Bluetooth, with over 20 hours of battery.

Will an airplane phone holder fit every tray table?

It needs a firm, flat edge. Perilogics says its clamp opens up to 1.6 inches and works on standard tray tables, desks and compatible luggage handles, but not on rounded, padded or irregular surfaces.

Is the MyHalos sleep mask good for lash extensions?

Yes, that is one of its main selling points. It has 16 mm deep contoured eye cups designed so the mask does not touch eyelids or lashes, and the listing says it blocks 100% of light.

Are travel blankets allowed in carry-on bags?

Yes, a fleece blanket is a soft item with no restrictions. The PAVILIA blanket folds into its own pillowcase and straps to a suitcase handle, so it does not take up bag space.
