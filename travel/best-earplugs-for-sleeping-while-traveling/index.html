How to choose travel earplugs for sleep

  1. Noise rating: NRR (US) and SNR (EU) show how much noise a plug can block. Higher numbers block more.
  2. Material: Foam blocks the most noise. Moldable silicone and wax cover the ear opening and suit side sleepers. Reusable silicone tips last longer.
  3. Fit: Small ears may find foam plugs too big. Moldable or multi-size options help.
  4. Sleeping position: Side sleepers press their ear into a pillow, so low-profile plugs are more comfortable.
  5. Reusability: Disposable plugs are hygienic for travel; reusable ones cut waste.
  6. Packing: A small case keeps plugs clean in a bag.

Tips for sleeping better on the road

  • Insert foam plugs correctly: Roll tightly, pull the top of your ear up and back, insert and hold while it expands.
  • Pair with a mask: Blocking light and noise together works better than either alone.
  • Use white noise: A fan app or hotel AC can mask sounds earplugs do not fully block.
  • Set a loud alarm: Earplugs can make alarms harder to hear, so use vibration or a louder tone.
  • Keep them clean: Store plugs in a case and wash hands before inserting.
  • Pack spares: Foam plugs are easy to lose on planes, so bring extra pairs.

Earplugs and long flights

On a long-haul flight, cabin noise sits around a constant hum for hours, which is tiring even if you are not trying to sleep. Frequently asked questions

What earplugs block the most noise for sleeping?

Foam earplugs usually block the most. Mack's Ultra Soft and Flents Quiet Time both list an NRR of 33 dB, the highest in this guide, compared with 22 dB NRR for Mack's silicone and 17 dB SNR for Loop Experience 2.

Are silicone or foam earplugs better for side sleepers?

Moldable silicone or wax earplugs sit flatter in the outer ear, which many side sleepers find more comfortable. Peace&Quiet says its wax plugs are designed for side sleepers, and Flents says its foam plugs are not recommended for frequent side sleepers.

Do earplugs help with ear pressure when flying?

Some can. Mack's says its Pillow Soft silicone earplugs are lab tested and proven to help relieve airplane ear pressure and pain due to flying.

Can you reuse foam earplugs?

Foam earplugs are generally meant for short-term or limited reuse because they collect dirt and lose their shape. Loop's silicone tips are reusable and easy to clean, and Peace&Quiet says each wax pillow can be split in half for up to 150 uses per pack.

Is it safe to sleep with earplugs every night?

Many people do, but keep them clean, insert them gently and replace disposable ones regularly. Stop and talk to a doctor if you notice ear pain, itching or a feeling of blockage.
