How to choose a universal travel adapter

  1. Plug coverage: Look for Type A, C, G and I outputs, and check special destinations like South Africa and India.
  2. Ports: Count your devices; USB-C ports are best for recent phones, tablets and laptops.
  3. Power: 20W charges a phone; 45W to 100W can handle many laptops.
  4. Size: Smaller adapters fit better in carry-ons and crowded outlets.
  5. Safety: Fuses, safety shutters and certifications add protection.
  6. Voltage: These are adapters, not converters; check that your devices say 100-240V.

Travel adapter tips

  • Check voltage labels: Look for "100-240V" on chargers before you go.
  • Leave the hair dryer: Many hotels provide one; single-voltage styling tools need a converter.
  • Pack a spare: A second small adapter is handy for families.
  • Charge overnight: Plug in everything at once with a multi-port adapter.
  • Carry backup power: A portable charger covers long travel days.

Adapter vs. converter

An adapter changes the plug shape so it fits the wall. A converter changes the voltage. Phones, laptops, tablets and most camera chargers work worldwide with just an adapter. Single-voltage appliances like many hair dryers, curling irons and straighteners need a converter or a dual-voltage version.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a voltage converter or just a travel adapter?

Most phones, laptops and camera chargers are dual voltage (100-240V) and only need an adapter. None of the adapters here convert voltage, so single-voltage items like many hair dryers and straighteners need a converter or a dual-voltage version.

Does a universal travel adapter work everywhere?

Most cover the common plug types A, C, G and I, which work in much of the world. EPICKA says its adapter covers more than 200 countries and regions but not South Africa or India, so check your destination before you go.

Can I charge a laptop with a travel adapter?

Yes, either by plugging the laptop's own charger into the AC socket or by using a high-power USB-C port. The Ceptics GaN 100W has a built-in 100W USB-C cable, and the Ceptics 45W lists 45W total output.

Can I use a travel adapter on a cruise ship?

Many cruise lines restrict surge protectors and some power strips. TESSAN says its adapter has no surge protection or cords and is designed for cruise ship use; always check your cruise line's rules.

How many travel adapters should I bring?

One multi-port adapter is enough for most solo travelers. Couples and families may want two, one for each side of the bed or each room.
