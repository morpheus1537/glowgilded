How to pack a garment bag

  1. Use good hangers. Sturdy, shaped hangers keep jacket shoulders in shape.
  2. Button and zip. Button jackets and zip dresses before hanging.
  3. Layer smart. Put the heaviest item at the back and lighter shirts in front.
  4. Use the straps. Interior straps stop garments sliding to the bottom.
  5. Fill pockets with soft items. Socks, ties and belts in side pockets keep the bag balanced.
  6. Hang it as soon as you arrive. Light wrinkles often fall out within an hour.

Frequently asked questions

Can you bring a garment bag as a carry-on?

Usually yes, if it fits your airline's carry-on size limits when folded. The ZEGUR measures about 22 inches square when closed, and Modoker says its bag meets a 51-inch (length + width + height) sizer. Always check your airline's rules before you fly.

How do you pack a suit in a garment bag without wrinkles?

Button the jacket, hang it on a sturdy hanger, and put the trousers on the same hanger folded along their creases. Use the bag's straps to hold the garments in place, fold the bag gently and unpack as soon as you arrive.

Is a garment bag better than a suitcase for a suit?

A garment bag keeps a suit or dress hanging flat for most of the trip, which usually means fewer creases than folding it into a suitcase. Convertible bags like the Modoker give you both a garment section and a duffel.

Can a garment bag go in the overhead bin?

Many folded garment bags fit in overhead bins. ZEGUR says its bag is designed to fit overhead bins at about 22 inches square with over 4 inches of depth, but bin sizes vary by aircraft.

What size garment bag do I need for a long dress?

Look for a longer bag. The Simple Houseware bag is 43 inches long and lists suits, dresses and long winter coats. For floor-length gowns, measure the dress on its hanger before buying.
