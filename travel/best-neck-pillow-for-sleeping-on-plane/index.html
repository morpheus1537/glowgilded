How to choose a neck pillow for plane sleep

  1. Chin support: Front support keeps your head from dropping forward.
  2. Side support: Raised sides or lobes stop your head from tipping into your neighbor.
  3. Adjustability: A cord lock or strap tightens the fit for different neck sizes.
  4. Filling: Memory foam holds its shape better than beads or air.
  5. Washable cover: Planes are not the cleanest places; removable covers help.
  6. Packability: A clip, strap or carry bag keeps it from taking up carry-on space.

Tips for sleeping on a plane

  • Pick a window seat: You get a wall to lean on and no one climbing over you.
  • Recline a little: Even a slight recline reduces forward head drop.
  • Block light and noise: An eye mask and earplugs or headphones make a big difference.
  • Dress in layers: Cabins swing from warm to cold. Wear socks and a soft layer.
  • Support circulation: On long flights, compression socks for flying can help, and get up to stretch when the seatbelt sign is off.
  • Pack smart: Clip the pillow outside your bag; see our carry-on packing essentials.

U-shape vs 360-degree pillows

Classic U-shaped pillows are compact and comfortable for side support. 360-degree and adjustable pillows add chin support, which matters most for people whose heads fall forward. If you always wake with your chin on your chest, choose chin support.

Frequently asked questions

What type of neck pillow is best for sleeping on a plane?

A memory foam pillow with chin support is usually best, because it stops your head from falling forward, the most common cause of waking up on a plane. An adjustable cord or strap helps you get a snug fit.

Are memory foam travel pillows better than inflatable ones?

Memory foam is more comfortable and holds its shape through a long flight; inflatable pillows pack smaller but feel firmer and can lose air. If carry-on space is tight, look for a compressible foam pillow with a carry bag.

How do I stop my head from falling forward on a plane?

Use a pillow with front chin support or turn a U-shaped pillow around so the thick part sits under your chin. Reclining slightly and choosing a window seat to lean on also helps.

Can I bring a neck pillow on a plane in addition to my carry-on?

Many airlines let you wear or carry a neck pillow without counting it as a personal item, but policies vary. Clipping it to your bag or wearing it through the airport is the safest bet.

How do I clean a travel pillow?

All five pillows here have removable, washable covers. Unzip the cover and follow the care label; do not machine-wash the memory foam core.
