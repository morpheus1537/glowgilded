What matters for work-from-home headphones

  1. Noise cancelling: Hybrid ANC with multiple mics usually handles a wider range of noise.
  2. Multipoint Bluetooth: Stay connected to your laptop and phone at once.
  3. Microphones: Beamforming or voice-targeting mics help your voice come through clearly.
  4. Comfort: Weight and cushion softness matter more than anything over an 8-hour day.
  5. Battery life: 24 hours or more covers several workdays between charges.
  6. Wired option: A cable is a handy backup when the battery runs low.
  7. Transparency mode: Hear the doorbell or a family member without taking them off.

Tips for better calls and focus

  • Test your mic: Record a short clip in your meeting app to hear how you sound.
  • Use multipoint wisely: Pair your laptop and phone so calls switch automatically.
  • Take breaks: Remove headphones for a few minutes every hour to rest your ears.
  • Keep volume moderate: ANC lets you hear clearly at lower volumes.

Frequently asked questions

Are noise cancelling headphones worth it for working from home?

For many people, yes. Active noise cancelling reduces steady background noise like fans, traffic, appliances and household chatter, which can make it easier to focus and less tiring to listen on long calls.

Which headphones connect to a laptop and phone at the same time?

Look for Bluetooth multipoint. The soundcore Q20i, Bose QuietComfort and JLab JBuds Lux ANC all list multipoint connections, so you can switch between your laptop and phone without re-pairing.

Do noise cancelling headphones help with calls?

ANC helps you hear, while the microphones determine how you sound. Sony lists beamforming mics with Precise Voice Pickup, and Beats lists voice-targeting mics that reduce background noise on calls.

Are over-ear headphones comfortable for an 8-hour workday?

Many are, especially light models with soft cushions. Sony says the WH-CH720N weighs just 192 grams, and Bose highlights plush ear cushions and a padded band for long sessions. Taking short breaks also helps.

Can I use wireless headphones with a wired connection?

Yes, with these models. Bose and soundcore include an audio cable, Beats supports USB-C and 3.5 mm wired listening, and JLab includes a USB-C cable for wired listening.
