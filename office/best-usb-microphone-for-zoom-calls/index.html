How to choose a USB microphone for Zoom calls

  1. Pickup pattern: Cardioid focuses on one speaker. Omnidirectional picks up a whole room, good for groups but noisier for solo calls.
  2. Dynamic or condenser: Dynamic mics reject more background noise. Condensers capture more detail in quiet rooms.
  3. Mute button: A physical mute with a light is one of the most useful features for meetings.
  4. Size and placement: Gooseneck and mini mics save desk space. Larger mics may need a boom arm.
  5. Headphone jack: Monitoring lets you hear yourself in real time and avoids echo.
  6. Compatibility: Check the listing for Windows and macOS, and any devices it does not support.

Tips for sounding better on Zoom

  • Get close: Place the mic 6 to 12 inches from your mouth and lower the gain.
  • Soften the room: Rugs, curtains and bookshelves cut echo more than any software setting.
  • Select the right input: In Zoom's audio settings, choose your USB mic as the microphone.
  • Wear headphones: They stop speaker sound from feeding back into the mic. See our noise cancelling headphones for working from home.
  • Use mute between turns: A physical mute button is faster than finding the on-screen one.
  • Test first: Record a short clip or use Zoom's test feature before important calls.

Zoom audio settings to check

Once your mic is plugged in, open Zoom's audio settings and select it as your microphone. Try turning off automatic volume adjustment and setting the input level manually, so your voice does not pump up and down. Zoom's background noise suppression can stay on low or auto for most rooms; raise it if you work near traffic, pets or a busy kitchen. If you use a mic with a headphone jack, plug your headphones into it so you hear the call and your own voice through the same device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a USB microphone for Zoom calls?

Not always, but it helps. Built-in laptop mics pick up keyboard noise and room echo, while a USB mic placed close to your mouth usually sounds clearer and plugs in without drivers.

What pickup pattern is best for video calls?

Cardioid is best for one person, since it focuses on sound in front of the mic. Omnidirectional mics like the TONOR G11 pick up from all directions, which suits several people around a table but also picks up more room noise.

Is a dynamic or condenser microphone better for working from home?

In a noisy or echoey room, a dynamic mic like the FIFINE AM8 tends to reject more background sound. In a quiet room, a condenser like the Blue Yeti captures more detail.

Do USB microphones work with Mac and Windows?

Yes. All five mics here list plug-and-play support for both Windows and macOS. CMTECK and JOUNIVO note their mics do not work with Raspberry Pi or Android.

How far should a microphone be from my mouth on Zoom?

Usually 6 to 12 inches for a desk mic, pointed at your mouth. Closer placement lets you lower the gain, which reduces room echo and keyboard noise.
