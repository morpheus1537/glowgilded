Gift etiquette for your boss

  • Check the policy: some companies limit gifts to managers.
  • Go group: a team gift avoids awkwardness and lets you buy something nicer.
  • Keep it professional: skip clothing, perfume and joke gifts.
  • Add a short note: a sincere thank-you card often means more than the gift.

Frequently asked questions

Is it appropriate to buy your boss a Christmas gift?

It depends on your workplace. In many offices, gifts usually flow down from managers to staff, not up. A group gift from the team or a modest, professional item is usually the safest choice. Check your company's gift policy first.

What is a good Christmas gift for a boss?

Choose something practical and professional that suits any taste: a quality tumbler, a nice pen, a notebook or a padfolio. Avoid anything too personal, like clothing, perfume or jokes.

Is a YETI tumbler a good gift for a boss?

Yes. It's useful, recognizable and suits almost anyone. The Rambler 20 oz is dishwasher safe and fits most standard cup holders. YETI notes the MagSlider lid isn't leakproof.

Does the Anker MagGo 3-in-1 charger work with Android phones?

It's designed as a 3-in-1 Apple charger for an iPhone, AirPods and Apple Watch, with Qi2 and MagSafe compatibility. If your boss uses Android, choose a different gift.

How much should you spend on a gift for your boss?
