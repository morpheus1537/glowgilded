Before you buy: measure these four things

  1. VESA pattern: Look at the back of your monitor for four screw holes. Most arms here fit 75x75mm and 100x100mm.
  2. Monitor weight: Check the spec sheet, without the stand.
  3. Desk thickness: Measure where you will clamp the arm.
  4. Space behind the desk: Arms need a little room behind the screen to swing and tilt.

Small desk setup tips

  • Clamp at the back corner: Mounting at a rear corner frees the most surface.
  • Set the top of the screen near eye level: Then adjust distance to about an arm's length.
  • Route cables along the arm: Use the built-in clips so cords do not drape over the desk.

Frequently asked questions

Is a monitor arm worth it for a small desk?

Yes, for many people. A monitor arm lifts the screen off the desk so the base no longer takes up space, giving you room for a keyboard, notebook or laptop. It also lets you set the screen at eye level, which many people find more comfortable.

How do I know if a monitor arm will fit my monitor?

Check three things: your monitor's VESA pattern (most arms here fit 75x75mm and 100x100mm), its weight, and its screen size. Then make sure the arm's listed range covers all three.

Will a monitor arm clamp fit my desk?

Measure your desktop thickness and check underneath for crossbars or drawers. Listed clamp ranges in this guide run from 0.39 to 2.56 inches on the ErGear up to 0.8 to 3.9 inches on the Amazon Basics arm.

Can a monitor arm damage my desk?

Clamps put pressure on one spot, so particle board or glass desks may not be a good fit. HUANUO and WALI both recommend wooden tabletops, ideally without beams underneath.

What is the difference between a free-moving arm and a pole mount?

Free-moving arms like the HUANUO FlowLift let you lift and lower the screen with one hand and hold it at any angle. Pole mounts like the VIVO adjust height along a center pole and are usually set once and left in place.
