What to look for in a webcam

  1. Resolution: 1080p is the sweet spot for calls; 2K and 4K add detail for recording.
  2. Microphones: Dual mics with noise reduction help if you do not use a headset.
  3. Low-light correction: Important in home offices with weak or uneven light.
  4. Field of view: 65 to 78 degrees frames one person; 90 degrees or more shows more of the room.
  5. Privacy cover: A sliding shutter blocks the lens between calls.

Setup tips for better calls

  • Eye level: Mount the camera at or slightly above eye level; a monitor arm helps.
  • Face the light: Sit facing a window or lamp, not with a window behind you.
  • Check the frame: Leave a little space above your head and keep the background tidy.
  • Use a headset for noisy homes: Built-in mics are good, but a headset isolates your voice best.
  • Close the shutter: Slide the privacy cover closed when you are done.

Frequently asked questions

Is an external webcam better than a laptop camera?

Usually, yes. Many built-in laptop cameras are lower resolution and sit at an unflattering angle. A 1080p or higher webcam on top of your monitor gives a sharper picture at eye level, and most add better microphones and a privacy cover.

Do I need a 4K webcam for Zoom or Teams?

No. Most video call apps compress video, so 1080p looks great for everyday meetings. A 4K camera like the EMEET NOVA is worth it for recording, streaming or cropping in without losing detail.

Do webcams need drivers?

Most do not. The EMEET C960, NexiGo N60 and Logitech Brio 101 are listed as plug-and-play over USB. Some, like the Anker C200, offer optional software to change settings like the field of view.

How can I look better on video calls?

Put the camera at eye level, face a window or light source, and keep a bright window out of the background. Good lighting improves image quality more than resolution. See our video call lighting setup guide for easy lights.

Can I use a webcam with a USB-C laptop?

Yes, with an adapter or hub if the webcam has a USB-A plug. Check the listing; some webcams include an adapter in the box.
