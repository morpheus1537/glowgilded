How to choose a desk mat

  1. Size: Measure your desk and how much room your keyboard and mouse need. Extra-large mats can also sit under a laptop or monitor.
  2. Material: PU leather wipes clean, cloth is soft and quiet, felt is cozy and absorbs noise, and cork adds a natural look.
  3. Mouse glide: Cloth surfaces like SteelSeries and Logitech are designed for smooth mouse tracking.
  4. Non-slip base: Rubber or suede backing keeps the mat from sliding around.
  5. Spill protection: Waterproof or spill-repellent surfaces protect the desk and are easy to wipe.
  6. Style: Pick a color and texture that suits your space. Many mats come in several colors.

Desk mat buying tips

  • Measure twice: Leave a little room at the edges of your desk so the mat lies flat.
  • Match your mouse: Cloth helps gaming and high-precision mice. Leather is fine for everyday office use.
  • Think about spills: If you keep coffee at your desk, a wipe-clean or spill-repellent surface saves headaches.
  • Flatten a rolled mat: New mats may arrive rolled. Lay them flat for a day or two, weighed down with books.
  • Tidy the rest: A mat looks best with tidy cables. See our desk cable management ideas.

Frequently asked questions

What size desk mat do I need for a keyboard and mouse?

Measure the space for your keyboard plus mouse movement. A mat around 28 to 36 inches wide, like the Logitech (28 x 12 in) or Aothia (31.5 x 15.7 in), fits a full keyboard and mouse. The SteelSeries QcK XXL (35 x 16 in) is large enough to sit under a monitor too.

Is a leather or cloth desk mat better?

PU leather mats like Aothia wipe clean and protect against spills, while cloth mats like Logitech and SteelSeries feel softer and quieter and give smooth mouse glide. Felt adds a cozy look and absorbs typing noise.

Are desk mats good for mouse tracking?

Yes. SteelSeries says its QcK surface is tested to optimize tracking for both optical and laser sensors, and Logitech says its fine-weave cloth gives an effortless, silent glide.

How do you clean a desk mat?

Wipe PU leather and cork with a damp cloth. Logitech's spill-repellent cloth also wipes clean. DAWNTREES says to brush its felt mat to remove debris.

Do desk mats protect the desk?

Yes, that is one of their main jobs. Aothia and DAWNTREES both list protection from scratches, stains, spills and heat, which helps keep wood and laminate desks looking new.
