How to choose a standing desk converter for two monitors

  1. Top surface width: Measure your two monitors side by side; 32-36 inches works for most pairs.
  2. Depth: A deeper surface keeps monitors farther from your eyes.
  3. Lift capacity: Add up monitors, laptop and accessories.
  4. Height range: Check that the top reaches your standing elbow height.
  5. Keyboard tray: Two-tier designs put the keyboard lower than the screens, which is better for posture.
  6. Assembly and warranty: Some arrive fully assembled; warranties range from 3 to 5 years.

Standing desk converter tips

  • Set your height: Elbows at about 90 degrees with wrists straight when typing.
  • Raise your screens: Top of the monitors at or slightly below eye level. A monitor arm can fine-tune height.
  • Alternate: Switch between sitting and standing throughout the day.
  • Check your desk: Make sure your desk can take the converter's weight and your monitors.
  • Manage cables: Leave slack so cables do not pull when you raise the riser. Our desk cable management ideas can help.
  • Support your back: See our ergonomic accessories for back pain.

Converter vs. full standing desk

Converters are the easier upgrade if you love your current desk, rent your home or need a quick setup. A full electric standing desk is better if you want a larger surface, motorized height presets and a lower sitting position.

Frequently asked questions

Can a standing desk converter hold two monitors?

Yes, if the top surface is wide enough and within the weight limit. All five here list room for dual monitors; check your monitors' combined width and weight against the listed surface size and lift capacity.

Is a standing desk converter as good as a standing desk?

A converter is cheaper and sits on your existing desk with little or no assembly. A full standing desk usually offers a larger surface and a lower minimum height, but a converter is the easier upgrade for most home offices.

How long should I stand at a standing desk?

Many people alternate between sitting and standing throughout the day rather than standing for hours. Start with short standing periods and add time gradually, changing position when you feel tired.

What is the weight capacity of a standing desk converter?

It varies by model. The VIVO 32-inch riser lists a 33 lb lift assist and FITUEYES and TechOrbits list up to 33 lbs; check the listing for your model.
