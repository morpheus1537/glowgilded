How to choose a wireless mouse for a laptop

  1. Connection: USB receivers are plug-and-play; Bluetooth saves a port.
  2. Port type: Receiver mice often need USB-A. USB-C-only laptops may need an adapter.
  3. Size: Compact for travel and smaller hands; full size for long desk days.
  4. Battery: Look for a long battery life or a rechargeable mouse.
  5. Extras: Adjustable DPI, side buttons, quiet clicks and fast-scroll wheels.

Tips for using a mouse with a laptop

  • Check your ports: USB-C-only laptops may need a USB-C hub for receiver mice.
  • Store the receiver in the mouse: Several mice have a slot so it does not get lost in your bag.
  • Use a mouse pad: It improves tracking and comfort, especially on glass or glossy desks.
  • Turn it off in your bag: An on/off switch saves battery when traveling.
  • Raise your screen: Pair a mouse with a laptop stand and keyboard for better posture.
  • Adjust DPI: Lower DPI is more precise; higher DPI moves faster across big screens.

Frequently asked questions

Is a mouse better than a laptop touchpad?

For long work sessions, many people find a mouse faster and more comfortable. Logitech cites a study in which laptop users with the M185 were more productive than with a touchpad.

Should I get a Bluetooth or USB receiver mouse?

A USB receiver is plug-and-play and very reliable, but it uses a port. Bluetooth frees up ports, which helps on thin laptops. The Logitech Signature M550 L offers both Bluetooth and a Logi Bolt receiver.

Will a wireless mouse work with a USB-C-only laptop?

Receiver mice like the TECKNET need a USB-A port, so you may need an adapter or hub. Bluetooth mice like the M550 L or Apple Magic Mouse connect without a receiver.

How long do wireless mouse batteries last?

The TECKNET and Logitech M550 L list up to 24 months, the Logitech M185 and M317 up to about a year, and the rechargeable Apple Magic Mouse about a month or more per charge.

What size mouse should I get?

Compact mice like the M185 and M317 suit small to medium hands and travel. Full-size mice like the Logitech Signature M550 L are designed for larger hands and long days at a desk.
