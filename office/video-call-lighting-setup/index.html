How to set up lighting for video calls

  1. Face the light. Sit facing a window or put your main light in front of you. Never sit with a bright window behind you.
  2. Put the light near the camera. Clip a ring light or LED panel at the top of your screen so light hits your face from roughly eye level.
  3. Raise the camera. Put a webcam on top of your monitor, or raise your laptop on a stand, so the camera is at eye level.
  4. Add fill. If one side of your face is darker, add a second light or turn a lamp's side bar toward you at lower brightness.
  5. Match color temperatures. Set your lights to match your room. Warm 3000K-ish light feels cozy; cooler light feels more like daylight.
  6. Light the background softly. A lamp behind you at low brightness adds depth so you don't look like you're floating in darkness.

Common video call lighting mistakes

  • Overhead light only. Ceiling lights cast shadows under your eyes. Add a light in front.
  • Too bright. Turn brightness down until your face doesn't look shiny or washed out.
  • Mixed colors. A blue-white ring light plus a yellow lamp can make you look odd. Match them.
  • Glare on glasses. Raise the light higher and angle it slightly to one side.
  • Messy cables in frame. Our desk cable management ideas can help tidy the view.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best lighting for video calls?

Soft, even light in front of you, at about eye level, is the most flattering. A window facing you works well during the day; a small ring light or LED panel near the top of your screen fills in at night. Avoid strong light behind you.

Where should I put a light for Zoom calls?

Place the light in front of you and slightly above eye level, close to the camera. Clipping a light to the top of your monitor or laptop screen is the simplest way to do this.

Do I need a webcam if my laptop has a camera?

Not always, but an external webcam like the Logitech C920x records Full HD 1080p at 30fps and automatically adjusts for lighting, which usually looks sharper than older built-in laptop cameras. It can also sit at eye level on top of a monitor.

Why do I look washed out or yellow on video?

Color temperature is usually the cause. Mixing warm room lamps with cool daylight confuses the camera. Lights with adjustable color temperature, like the ones in this guide, let you match the light to your room.

Does the Logitech C920x have a privacy shutter?

No. The listing notes that the C920x comes without a privacy shutter. It does include a 3-month XSplit VCam license for background removal and blur.
