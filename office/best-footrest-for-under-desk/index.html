How to choose an under-desk footrest

  1. Height: Pick a footrest that lets your feet sit flat with knees near hip level. Adjustable designs help.
  2. Firmness: Memory foam feels plush; high-density foam and wood stay firm.
  3. Movement: Rocking or tilting footrests encourage small leg movements.
  4. Size: Wider footrests give room to shift your feet.
  5. Grip: Non-slip bottoms keep it from sliding on hard floors and carpet.
  6. Cleaning: Removable, washable covers are easier to keep fresh.

Tips for a comfortable sitting setup

  • Set your chair first: Adjust chair height to your desk, then add a footrest to support your feet.
  • Keep knees near hip level: Avoid lifting your knees well above your hips.
  • Leave room to shift: Change foot positions throughout the day.
  • Pair it with a standing break: Alternate sitting and standing with a standing desk converter.
  • Stay warm: Cold floors make footrests even more welcome in winter.
  • Get up often: No footrest replaces regular movement breaks.

Frequently asked questions

Do footrests really help with posture?

A footrest helps when your feet do not rest flat on the floor, which is common for shorter people or when a desk is too high. Supporting your feet keeps your thighs level and reduces pressure under your legs, which many people find more comfortable.

How high should an under-desk footrest be?

Aim for a height that lets your feet rest flat with your knees at about hip level or slightly lower. Adjustable footrests like the ErgoFoam and BlissTrends, or the Everlasting Comfort with its added base, make it easier to find the right height.

Is a foam or rocking footrest better?

Foam footrests feel cushioned and steady, which suits long focused sessions. Rocking footrests like the StrongTek and EUREKA encourage small leg movements throughout the day. Everlasting Comfort and ErgoFoam offer both by flipping over.

Who needs a footrest under the desk?

Shorter people, anyone whose chair is raised to reach a high desk, and people who feel pressure behind the knees or thighs after sitting. A footrest is a simple fix compared with changing your desk or chair.

Can I wash a foam footrest?

Many have removable covers. Everlasting Comfort and BlissTrends list machine-washable covers, and ErgoFoam's cover is removable for cleaning.
