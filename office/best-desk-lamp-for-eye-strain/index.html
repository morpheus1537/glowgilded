How to choose a desk lamp for eye strain

  1. Flicker-free light: Look for lamps described as non-flickering.
  2. Diffused light: A frosted shade or light panel reduces glare.
  3. Dimming: Adjustable brightness lets you match the room.
  4. Color temperature: Cooler light for focus, warmer light for evenings.
  5. Coverage: A wide light bar lights more of the desk.
  6. Extras: USB ports, timers and clamps add convenience.

Tips for reducing eye strain at your desk

  • Light the room, not just the desk: Avoid a bright screen in a dark room.
  • Place the lamp to the side: This avoids glare on your screen.
  • Warm up the light in the evening: Switch to a warmer color temperature after dark.
  • Follow the 20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
  • Position your monitor: Keep it about an arm's length away and slightly below eye level. A monitor arm makes that easier.
  • See an eye doctor: Persistent strain can mean you need glasses or a new prescription.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of desk lamp is best for eye strain?

Look for a lamp with soft, even, flicker-free light, adjustable brightness and a choice of color temperatures. A wide light bar like the Lepro spreads light across the desk and reduces the contrast between a bright monitor and a dark room.

What color temperature is best for working at a desk?

Neutral to cool white, around 4000K-5500K, is good for focused work, while warm light around 3000K is easier in the evening. The Honeywell HWT-H01 offers 3000K, 4000K and 5500K modes.

What does CRI mean on a desk lamp?

CRI, or color rendering index, measures how accurately a light shows colors. Honeywell lists a CRI of 95 or higher for its HWT-H01, compared with 80-90 for most desk lights, which helps with reading, art and crafts.

Should I use a desk lamp when working on a computer?

Yes. Working at a bright screen in a dark room increases contrast, which many people find tiring. A desk lamp that lights the area around your monitor helps balance the light.

Can a desk lamp fix eye strain completely?

No. Good lighting helps, but also take regular screen breaks, position your monitor properly and see an eye doctor if strain, headaches or blurry vision continue.
