How to choose a label maker

  1. Tape system: Brother uses TZe tapes and DYMO uses D1 tapes. Check refill availability and colors.
  2. Tape width: 1/2 inch (12mm) covers most jobs; wider tape helps for bins and shelves.
  3. Keyboard or app: QWERTY keyboards are fastest; phone apps offer more designs.
  4. Fonts, frames and symbols: More options help for decorative labels.
  5. Memory: Saved labels make reprinting quick.
  6. Power: AAA batteries, AC adapters (often sold separately) or a rechargeable battery.

Label ideas for a home office

  • File folders: Label by year and category, such as "2026 Taxes" or "Insurance."
  • Cables: Wrap labels around chargers and cords, near the plug.
  • Storage bins: Use wider labels or two lines so you can read them from across the room.
  • Drawers: Label supplies like "Batteries," "Stamps" and "Spare cables."
  • Shelves: Label binder spines and reference books.
  • Pair with storage: Our small apartment organization ideas guide has more ways to tame clutter.

Tips for better labels

  • Keep spare tapes: Buy a refill before you run out mid-project.
  • Use high contrast: Black on white or white on clear is easiest to read.
  • Clean surfaces first: Labels stick better on dust-free, dry surfaces.
  • Save templates: Store repeated labels in memory for consistent folders.
  • Leave space: Add a little margin on bins so labels do not wrap around edges.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best label maker for home office organization?

For most home offices, a handheld Brother P-Touch like the PTH110BP is a great choice because it has a QWERTY keyboard, a preview display and laminated, water-resistant TZe tapes.

Are Brother and DYMO label tapes interchangeable?

No. Brother P-Touch models here use Brother TZe tapes, and the DYMO LabelManager 160 uses DYMO D1 tapes. Buy refill tapes that match your machine.

Do label makers need batteries?

The Brother PTH110BP and PTD220 use 6 AAA batteries or an optional AC adapter, each sold separately. The SUPVAN E11 has a built-in rechargeable battery.

Can I make labels from my phone?

Yes, with a Bluetooth model. The SUPVAN E11 prints from its keyboard or from an app over Bluetooth, with 30+ fonts and 660+ icons.

What size label tape is best for file folders?

About 1/2 inch (12mm) tape is a popular all-round size for folders, bins and shelves. The Brother PT-D410 can print up to about 3/4 inch (18mm) for bigger labels.
