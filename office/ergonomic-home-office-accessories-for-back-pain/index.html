How to set up your chair and desk in five minutes

Accessories work best when the basics are right. Use this quick order:

  • Chair height first: raise or lower the chair until your elbows are roughly level with the desk when your shoulders are relaxed.
  • Then your feet: if they no longer rest flat on the floor, add the footrest.
  • Then your back: strap the lumbar pillow at the small of your back, not your mid-back. Sit all the way back in the seat.
  • Then the screen: the top of the monitor should be around eye level. A laptop stand helps here; see our home office desk setup essentials.
  • Then your hands: put the wrist rest in front of the keyboard and rest on it between bursts of typing.

Buying tips

  • Fix one problem at a time: if your lower back aches, start with lumbar support. If your tailbone aches, start with a seat cushion. Buying all four at once makes it hard to tell what helped.
  • Watch the height change: a seat cushion raises you by an inch or more. Re-check desk and foot height afterward.
  • Choose washable covers: anything you sit or rest on every day gets grimy. All four picks here have removable or wipe-clean covers.
  • Move, too: no accessory replaces getting up. Stand and stretch every 30 to 60 minutes. Our guide to muscle recovery tools at home has ideas for easing tight muscles after a long week at the desk.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ergonomic accessory for lower back pain at a desk?

For most people a lumbar support pillow is the first thing to try, because it fills the gap between your lower back and the chair and helps you sit upright. Pair it with a footrest if your feet do not rest flat on the floor.

Do seat cushions actually help with sitting all day?

A memory foam seat cushion can make a hard or worn-out chair more comfortable. The Everlasting Comfort cushion has a U-shaped cutout meant to take pressure off the tailbone, and its cover is machine washable.

Should I use a footrest under my desk?

Yes, if your feet do not sit flat on the floor once your chair is at the right height for your desk. A footrest lets your feet rest flat with your knees at a comfortable angle, which many people find eases pressure on the lower back and hips.

Are keyboard wrist rests good or bad for your wrists?

They help most when you rest on them during pauses rather than pressing down while you type. The Gimars set uses 1.2-inch memory foam so your wrists sit level with the keys instead of bending up.

Can I use a lumbar pillow in the car too?

Yes. The Everlasting Comfort lumbar pillow has adjustable straps that fit seats up to 32 inches wide, and the listing names car seats along with office chairs, gaming chairs and recliners.

The bottom line
