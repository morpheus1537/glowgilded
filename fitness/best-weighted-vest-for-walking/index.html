How to start walking with a weighted vest

  • Start light: Choose a weight that lets you walk with your normal posture and stride. If you lean forward or shorten your steps, it is too heavy.
  • Shorten the walk at first: Use the vest for part of your usual walk and build up over a few weeks.
  • Keep it snug: A loose vest bounces and rubs. Tighten straps so it stays still.
  • Stay visible: Winter walks often happen in the dark. Reflective stripes help, and a headlamp or clip light helps more.
  • Listen to your body: Stop if you feel sharp pain in your back, knees or shoulders, and check with a professional if you have existing joint issues.

Buying tips

  • Fixed vs adjustable: Fixed vests are simpler and often slimmer. Adjustable vests grow with you.
  • Measure your chest: Every vest lists a chest range; check it before ordering.
  • Check washability: Daily walkers sweat. A washable vest stays fresher.
  • Look for pockets and reflectors: These small features make outdoor walks easier and safer.
  • Confirm the variant: Weighted vest listings have many weights and colors, and ratings may cover several versions.

Frequently asked questions

What weight vest should a beginner use for walking?

Start light. Many people begin with a vest that is a small share of their body weight and increase slowly over weeks. The Zelus Z-Fit listing suggests its 5 lb option for walks and yoga, and adjustable vests let you start with fewer weight packs.

Is walking with a weighted vest good for you?

Adding a weighted vest makes a walk more challenging, which is why many people use one to add intensity without running. Talk to a doctor or physical therapist first if you have back, knee, hip or balance problems.

How long should I walk with a weighted vest?

Begin with shorter walks than usual, such as 15 to 20 minutes, and build up as your body adapts. Stop if you feel pain in your back, shoulders or joints.

Can you wear a weighted vest under a jacket?

Slim vests are the easiest to layer. The Zelus Z-Fit listing describes it as slim enough to wear under a shirt. Bulkier iron-sand vests usually fit better over a base layer and under an open jacket.

Fixed or adjustable weighted vest, which is better?

Fixed vests are simpler and often slimmer. Adjustable vests let you add or remove weight packs as you progress, which saves buying a second vest later. Choose adjustable if you plan to use the vest for both walking and strength workouts.
