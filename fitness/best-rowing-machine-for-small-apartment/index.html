How to fit a rower into a small apartment

  • Measure length first: The rower's length is what matters most. Add a little room behind the seat for your arms.
  • Plan storage: Decide whether it will stand upright in a corner or slide under furniture, and choose a model that suits that plan.
  • Use a mat: An exercise mat protects floors and helps reduce vibration for neighbors.
  • Check your height: Rail length and listed height ranges matter. Tall users should look for a long rail; shorter users have more choice.
  • Start easy: Row at low resistance for 10 to 15 minutes, focusing on legs, then core, then arms.

Buying tips

  • Magnetic for quiet: Magnetic rowers are usually the quietest choice for shared walls.
  • Hydraulic for light weight: Hydraulic rowers tend to be lighter and smaller.
  • Look at weight limits: Ranges in this guide run from 300 to 350 pounds where listed.
  • Free apps help motivation: Both Sunny models connect to SunnyFit without a subscription, and Merach has its own app.

Frequently asked questions

What is the quietest type of rowing machine for an apartment?

Magnetic rowers are usually the quietest choice because resistance comes from magnets rather than air or water. The Sunny Smart Magnetic Rower, Merach Q1S and YOSUDA in this guide all use magnetic resistance and are described as quiet in their listings.

How much space do you need for a rowing machine?

Plan for the full length of the rail plus room to move. The Merach Q1S listing, for example, recommends a space of about 1.6 by 5.7 feet for use and 1.6 by 2.1 feet for upright storage.

Is rowing a good workout for beginners?

Rowing is low impact and works the legs, core, back and arms in one movement, which is why many beginners like it. Start with short sessions at low resistance and focus on pushing with your legs first.

Can you store a rowing machine upright?

Some models are designed for it. The Sunny Smart Magnetic Rower lists front wheels and vertical storage, and the Merach Q1S gives a storage footprint for upright use. Check each listing before buying if storage matters to you.

Will a rowing machine bother my downstairs neighbors?

A quiet magnetic rower on a thick exercise mat helps reduce noise and vibration. Avoid slamming the seat into the end stops, and row at reasonable hours.
