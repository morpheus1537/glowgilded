How to choose ankle weights for walking

  1. Weight range: For walking, lighter weights are easier on your joints. Adjustable sets let you start around 1 pound per ankle and build up.
  2. Adjustable or fixed: Removable weight packs let you progress without buying new weights. Fixed weights are slimmer and simpler.
  3. Strap and fit: Look for long hook-and-loop straps and a secure closure. Sportneer lists a fit for ankles about 7.5 to 12.5 inches around.
  4. Comfort: Soft, breathable, moisture-absorbing fabric helps on longer walks.
  5. Versatility: Many ankle weights also work on wrists or arms for upper-body moves.
  6. Fill: Most use iron sand or iron shot, which keeps the profile compact.

Tips for walking with ankle weights

  • Start light: Begin with 1 to 2 pounds per ankle and short walks, then build up slowly.
  • Keep your stride natural: Don't let the weights change the way you walk. If your stride shortens or your knees feel strained, go lighter.
  • Strap snugly: Fasten the strap just above the ankle bone so the weight does not bounce.
  • Mix in strength moves: Leg lifts, glute kickbacks and Pilates moves are where ankle weights shine. Pair them with resistance bands for beginners for variety.
  • Ask a professional: If you have joint issues or are recovering from injury, ask a doctor or physical therapist before adding weight.

Frequently asked questions

Is it good to walk with ankle weights?

Ankle weights add resistance to everyday walking and leg exercises, and many listings here mention walking. Start light, keep your normal stride, and check with a doctor or physical therapist first if you have knee, hip or ankle problems.

How heavy should ankle weights be for walking?

Lighter is better to start. Many people begin with 1 to 2 pounds per ankle and increase slowly. Adjustable sets like Sportneer, APEXUP and Fragraim let you add weight in small steps as you get stronger.

Are adjustable or fixed ankle weights better?

Adjustable ankle weights are better if you want to progress, because you add or remove weight packs instead of buying a new pair. Fixed weights are simpler and slimmer, and are fine if you know the weight you want.

Can ankle weights be used as wrist weights?

Many can. APEXUP, Fragraim and JFIT all say their straps fit arms or wrists as well as ankles, so you can use them for arm exercises too.

How do I keep ankle weights from slipping?

Tighten the strap snugly above the ankle bone. Sportneer uses a metal D-ring to hold the strap in place, and JFIT adds extra fabric to reduce chafing and keep the strap secure.
