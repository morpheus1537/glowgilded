How to choose a weight bench for your home gym

  1. Adjustable or flat: Adjustable benches add incline and decline angles. Flat benches are simpler and very stable.
  2. Weight capacity: Check the listed capacity and any maximum user weight.
  3. Angles: More backrest and seat positions mean more exercise options.
  4. Footprint and folding: Measure your space. Folding benches can live under a bed or against a wall.
  5. Padding and size: A wide seat and long backrest support your back during presses.
  6. Assembly: Some benches arrive almost ready, while others take a few minutes with tools.

Tips for training safely on a home weight bench

  • Check the pins: Make sure every adjustment pin is fully locked before each set.
  • Use a mat: A rubber mat protects floors and keeps the bench from sliding.
  • Mind the capacity: Add your body weight to the weight you lift and stay well under the listed capacity.
  • Start light at new angles: Incline and decline presses feel different. Build up gradually.
  • Keep your feet planted: Stable feet make presses safer and stronger.
  • Wipe the pads: Sweat wears upholstery faster, so wipe it after every workout.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an adjustable or a flat weight bench?

An adjustable bench lets you do incline, decline and seated exercises as well as flat presses, so it covers more of your body. A flat bench like the Marcy is simpler and sturdy, and works well if you mainly do flat presses, rows and step-ups.

How much weight capacity does a home weight bench need?

Add your body weight to the heaviest weight you plan to lift, then leave room to spare. The benches here list 600 to 827 lbs of capacity; YOLEO also lists a maximum user weight of 330 lbs.

Can a weight bench fit in a small apartment?

Yes, if you choose a folding model. NICEPEOPLE says its bench folds to 30.5 x 10.24 x 16.9 inches for storage under a bed or sofa, and YOLEO folds to 31.5 x 12.6 x 13 inches.

What exercises can you do with an adjustable bench and dumbbells?

Common moves include flat, incline and decline dumbbell presses, seated shoulder presses, one-arm rows, seated curls, Bulgarian split squats and decline crunches.

Is a weight bench hard to assemble?

It depends on the model. YOLEO says its bench ships 98% pre-assembled and you just insert two pins, while NICEPEOPLE says most users finish assembly in about 5 minutes with its instructions and video guide.
