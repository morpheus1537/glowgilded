Thick mat basics

  1. Thickness: 10mm (about 2/5 inch) is plush; 1/2 inch is very cushioned; 1 inch is closer to a floor pad.
  2. Firmness: Thicker and softer is not always better; a slightly firm foam keeps you more stable.
  3. Length: Taller people should look for 72-inch mats.
  4. Grip: Textured surfaces help on hard floors, but thick foam is generally best for dry workouts.
  5. Targeted padding: A knee pad can be moved under knees, elbows or wrists as needed.

Tips for kinder knee workouts

  • Fold for extra padding: Folding the end of your mat under your knees adds cushion in a pinch.
  • Use a firm base: Very soft mats on carpet can feel unstable; thick mats work best on hard floors.
  • Warm up first: Gentle movement before kneeling poses is easier on joints.
  • Add recovery tools: A foam roller can help after workouts; see our muscle recovery tools at home.
  • Clean regularly: Wipe foam mats with mild soap and water and let them dry fully.

Frequently asked questions

How thick should a yoga mat be for bad knees?

Many people with sensitive knees prefer 10mm (about 2/5 inch) to 1 inch of foam. Thicker mats feel softer under kneeling poses but can feel less stable in standing balance poses, so many people pair a medium mat with a separate knee pad.

Is a 1-inch yoga mat too thick?

For floor exercises, stretching and kneeling work, a 1-inch mat like the Retrospec Solana can be very comfortable. For flowing yoga or balance poses, some people find that much cushion wobbly, so a 10mm or 1/2-inch mat may be a better middle ground.

What is NBR foam?

NBR is a soft, cushiony synthetic foam used in many thick exercise mats, including the Gaiam, Fitvids and Gruper picks here. It is comfortable and lightweight, but it is usually less grippy and less firm than thin rubber studio mats.

Should I use a knee pad on top of my yoga mat?

A knee pad adds cushioning exactly where you need it without making the whole mat squishy. The ProsourceFit pad is 15mm thick and sized to match the width of a standard mat.

Why does my new yoga mat smell?

Foam mats often have an initial odor. Gaiam, Retrospec and Gruper all suggest unrolling the mat and airing it out for one to three days before use.
