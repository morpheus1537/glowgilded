How to layer for a winter run

  • Base layer: fleece-lined tights and a moisture-wicking top keep you warm and dry.
  • Outer layer: a light wind-resistant jacket on breezy days.
  • Extremities: gloves and a beanie make a big difference to comfort.
  • Visibility: a headlamp and reflective vest for any run in low light.

Buying tips

  • Don't overdress: you'll warm up after the first mile, so start a little cold.
  • Check sizing charts: gloves, tights and hats vary by brand.
  • Charge the night before: keep your headlamp on the charger so it's ready for early runs.
  • Watch for ice: slow down on bridges, shaded spots and crosswalks.
  • Check the current price on Amazon: winter gear often goes on sale around the holidays.

Frequently asked questions

What should I wear running in cold weather?

Dress in layers: a moisture-wicking base layer, fleece-lined tights or pants, a light jacket, gloves and a hat that covers your ears. A common rule of thumb is to dress as if it is about 15 to 20 degrees warmer than it is, because you heat up quickly once you start moving.

How do I stay visible when running in the dark?

Wear a headlamp so you can see the path and a reflective vest so drivers can see you. Bright or reflective clothing helps too. Run against traffic when there is no sidewalk.

Can you use your phone with these running gloves on?

Yes. SIMARI says its gloves have touchscreen fingers that work with phones, tablets, smartwatches and car GPS screens.
