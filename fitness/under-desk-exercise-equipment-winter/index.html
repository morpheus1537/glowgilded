How to choose the right under-desk exercise equipment

  • Sitting or standing? Sitting desks pair with the DeskCycle or Sunny elliptical. Standing desks pair with the balance board or walking pad.
  • Measure clearance: Measure from the floor to the underside of your desk, then subtract the device height. You need room for your knees at the top of each stroke.
  • Think about noise: Magnetic resistance devices are designed to be quiet, but test before important calls.
  • Plan for storage: The balance board and pedal devices tuck under a desk. A walking pad needs space to slide away.
  • Start slow: Begin with short sessions and low resistance, then build up over the week.

Frequently asked questions

Do under-desk exercise machines actually help?

They help you add movement during hours you would otherwise sit still, which is useful in winter when outdoor walks are harder. They are a supplement to regular exercise, not a replacement for a full workout.

Will an under-desk bike fit under my desk?

Check your desk height. The DeskCycle 2 adjusts down to 9 inches tall, which the listing says works with desks as low as 27 inches. Measure from the floor to the underside of your desk before ordering.

Are under-desk ellipticals quiet enough for video calls?

The Sunny pedal elliptical and DeskCycle are both described as quiet. Any machine still creates some motion and sound, so test it during a call and mute if needed.

Can I use a walking pad under a standing desk?

Many people do. The UREVO walking pad has a 0.6 to 4 mph walking mode, which suits slow walking while you read or take calls. Keep speeds low while typing.

Is a balance board good for a standing desk?
