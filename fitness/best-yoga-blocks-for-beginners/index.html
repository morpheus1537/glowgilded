How to choose yoga blocks

  1. Foam or cork: Foam is lighter and softer. Cork is denser, heavier and more stable.
  2. Size: The standard 9 x 6 x 4 inch block offers more height. A 3-inch block is lighter and easier to grip.
  3. Grip: Look for a non-slip or textured surface, especially if your hands get sweaty.
  4. Edges: Beveled or rounded edges are more comfortable to hold and to rest on.
  5. Density: A dense block will not squash under your weight in balancing poses.
  6. Extras: Some sets include a yoga strap or an exercise guide, which is helpful for beginners.

How to use yoga blocks as a beginner

  • Under your hands in forward folds: Place a block under each hand so you can keep your back flat.
  • In triangle and half moon: Rest your lower hand on a block instead of reaching for the floor.
  • Under your seat: Sit on a block in cross-legged poses or meditation to take pressure off tight hips.
  • Three heights: Turn the block on its flat side, its long edge or its end to raise or lower the support.
  • Bridge pose support: Slide a block under your lower back for a gentle supported bridge.

Frequently asked questions

Do beginners need yoga blocks?

Yoga blocks are one of the most useful props for beginners. They bring the floor closer in poses like triangle or half splits, so you can keep good alignment while your flexibility improves, instead of straining to reach.

Should I buy one yoga block or two?

Two is best for most people, because many poses use a block under each hand. Every pick in this guide comes as a 2-pack.

Is foam or cork better for yoga blocks?

Foam blocks are lighter, softer and gentler under knees and hips, which many beginners prefer. Cork blocks are denser, heavier and firmer, so they feel more stable for balancing and do not compress under body weight.

What size yoga block should I get?

The standard size is 9 x 6 x 4 inches, which gives you three heights to work with. Slimmer 9 x 6 x 3 inch blocks are lighter and easier to grip with smaller hands, but they give a little less height.

How do you clean yoga blocks?

Spot clean them with a damp cloth and mild detergent, then let them air dry. Gaiam recommends this method for its foam blocks.
