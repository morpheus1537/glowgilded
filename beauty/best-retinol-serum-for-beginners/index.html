How to start a retinoid routine

  1. Start low and slow: Use it a few evenings a week, then increase as your skin tolerates it.
  2. Use a pea-sized amount: More product does not mean faster results.
  3. Apply to dry skin: Then follow with a simple moisturizer.
  4. Keep the rest of your routine gentle: Avoid stacking strong acids or scrubs at first.
  5. Wear sunscreen every morning: This matters most of all.

Tips to avoid retinol irritation

  • Buffer if needed: Apply moisturizer first, then retinoid, if your skin feels tight.
  • Skip on irritated skin: Pause on sunburned, chapped or freshly shaved areas.
  • Do not combine retinoids: Use one retinoid product at a time.
  • Give it time: Many people need several weeks to see a difference in texture.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best retinol for beginners?

A gentle, encapsulated retinol serum with soothing ingredients is a common starting point. Both CeraVe serums in this guide pair encapsulated retinol with niacinamide and ceramides, are fragrance-free and are designed to be built up gradually.

How often should beginners use retinol?

Start slowly, such as two or three evenings a week, and increase as your skin adjusts. CeraVe recommends building up to daily use, and celimax suggests every other night for the first two weeks.

What is the difference between retinol, retinal and adapalene?

All three are retinoids. Retinol is the most common cosmetic form, retinal (retinaldehyde) is a related form used in products like the celimax Retinal Shot, and adapalene is an acne retinoid found in Differin Gel.

Should I use sunscreen with retinol?

Yes. Retinoids can make skin more sensitive to the sun, so wear a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning while using them.

Can I use retinol while pregnant?

Many skincare brands advise avoiding retinoids during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Talk to your doctor before using any retinoid if you are pregnant, trying to conceive or nursing.
