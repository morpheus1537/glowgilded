How to choose a niacinamide serum for pores

  1. Strength: 10% is a common starting point; 12-20% suits experienced users.
  2. Supporting ingredients: Zinc helps with oil; tranexamic acid and arbutin target dark spots.
  3. Texture: Watery, lightweight serums layer best under moisturizer and sunscreen.
  4. Fragrance: Fragrance-free formulas are gentler on sensitive skin.
  5. Size: Jumbo bottles save on reorders if a serum works for you.
  6. Skin type: Very oily skin may like a matte finish; dry skin needs hydration too.

How to use niacinamide for pores

  • Patch test first: Try a little on your jaw or behind the ear for a few days.
  • Apply to clean skin: Use after cleansing and toner, before moisturizer.
  • Less is more: A few drops covers the whole face.
  • Moisturize: Even oily skin needs hydration. Our hyaluronic acid serum guide has lightweight options.
  • Wear sunscreen: Sun damage stretches pores over time.
  • Add retinol slowly: Retinol also helps texture; see our retinol for beginners guide.
  • Be patient: Give a serum 4-8 weeks before judging results.

Other habits that help pores

Cleanse twice a day, avoid squeezing, use non-comedogenic products and remove makeup every night. A weekly exfoliating step can help clear congestion, but do not overdo it.

Frequently asked questions

Does niacinamide shrink pores?

Niacinamide cannot change the actual size of a pore, but it can make pores look smaller by helping control oil and smoothing texture. The Ordinary says clinical testing showed minimized pore appearance in 4 weeks.

What percentage of niacinamide is best for pores?

Many people do well with 5-10%. Higher strengths like 12% or 20% suit skin that already tolerates niacinamide; if you are new to it or sensitive, start at 10% or lower and build up.

Can I use niacinamide with vitamin C or retinol?

Yes. Niacinamide layers well with most ingredients, including vitamin C and retinol. If your skin feels irritated, use them at different times of day.

Should I use niacinamide in the morning or at night?

Either or both. The Ordinary suggests a few drops morning and evening, and Naturium suggests AM and PM use, increasing gradually as your skin adjusts. Always follow with sunscreen in the daytime.

Why is my skin purging or red after niacinamide?

Some people find high-strength niacinamide irritating. Patch test first, use fewer drops or every other day and switch to a lower strength if redness continues. See a dermatologist for persistent problems.
