If her vanity is full of serums and moisturizers, Protini feels thoughtful and on-brand for clean clinical routines. If she loves makeup but you do not know her lipstick shade, the Beauty Light Wand is the lower-risk glam pick.

You can also bundle two: Bum Bum Cream with the glow wand makes a body-and-makeup set, while Protini with the wand suits a skincare-and-finish routine. For a scent-focused Sol de Janeiro and Pillow Talk route instead, use our earlier discovery-set guide.
