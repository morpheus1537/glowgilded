Silk vs satin: the quick answer

Silk is a natural fiber made by silkworms. The best pillowcases use mulberry silk, often labeled Grade 6A, and are sold by weight in momme. Silk feels cool and smooth, is breathable, and typically costs more. It also needs gentler washing.

Satin is a type of weave, not a fiber. Most satin pillowcases are polyester, woven to be glossy and slippery on one side. Satin gives you a similar low-friction feel for a lower price, is easy to wash and is a vegan alternative to silk.

If you want the real thing, or are buying a gift, choose silk. If you want the smooth effect on a budget, or need several cases for the whole family, satin is a sensible choice.

How to get the most from a silk or satin pillowcase

  • Sleep with dry hair: Damp hair can still dent and frizz. Let it dry, then use a loose scrunchie or pineapple style if you have curls.
  • Let skincare absorb first: Blissy suggests letting creams and oils absorb before bed to avoid marks on the fabric.
  • Wash gently: Use cool water and a mesh bag for silk. Avoid high heat in the dryer.
  • Get the size right: A case that is too loose can wrinkle under your head. Match the case to the pillow, not the bed.
  • Pair it with a routine: A smooth pillowcase helps, but a good conditioner and less heat styling matter too.

Frequently asked questions

Is silk or satin better for your hair?

Both have a smooth surface that creates less friction than cotton, which is why people use them to reduce frizz and bedhead. Silk is a natural protein fiber and usually costs more; satin is a weave, usually polyester, that copies the slippery feel for less money. Many people are happy with either.

What does momme mean on a silk pillowcase?

Momme is a measure of silk weight and density. The Fishers Finery listing explains that higher momme means more silk. In this guide, Alaska Bear is 19 momme, Blissy is 22 momme and Fishers Finery is 25 momme.

Does a satin pillowcase help curly hair?

A smooth satin surface reduces the friction and tugging a cotton pillowcase can cause, which helps many people keep curls and blowouts looking neater in the morning. It is not a treatment, so keep up your usual hair care routine.

How do you wash a silk pillowcase?

Follow the care label. The Blissy listing, for example, recommends a cool delicate cycle in a mesh bag with a mild silk-safe detergent, then laying it flat to dry away from high heat.

Are satin pillowcases good for your skin?

Satin and silk both let your face glide rather than drag, which the brands say helps reduce sleep creases. Results vary from person to person, so treat it as a comfort upgrade rather than a skincare product.
