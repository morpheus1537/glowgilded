How to choose a tinted mineral sunscreen for your face

  1. SPF and broad spectrum: Choose broad spectrum SPF 30 or higher for daily wear.
  2. Mineral filters: Zinc oxide and titanium dioxide are the mineral (physical) filters.
  3. Shade range: Check whether it comes in multiple shades or blends into all tones.
  4. Finish: Dewy and moisturizing suits dry skin; matte suits oily skin.
  5. Skin type: Look for fragrance-free and non-comedogenic if you are sensitive or acne-prone.
  6. Makeup: Some double as primers under foundation.

How to use tinted sunscreen for the best results

  • Use enough: Apply a generous, even layer; a thin layer gives less protection than the label SPF.
  • Apply last in skincare: Put it on after serum and moisturizer, before makeup.
  • Reapply: Reapply every 2 hours outdoors and after sweating or swimming.
  • Do not forget your neck and ears: They get sun too.
  • Layer smart: A vitamin C serum under sunscreen is a classic morning combination.
  • Protect lips: Winter sun and wind dry lips; see our winter lip care essentials.

Mineral vs. chemical sunscreen

Mineral sunscreens use zinc oxide and/or titanium dioxide, which many people with sensitive skin prefer. Chemical sunscreens use filters that absorb UV and often feel lighter with no white cast. Both protect when used correctly. Tinted mineral formulas bridge the gap by hiding the white cast.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need sunscreen in winter?

Yes. UV rays reach your skin on cloudy and cold days, and snow can reflect sunlight. Dermatologists generally recommend daily broad spectrum SPF 30 or higher year-round.

What is the difference between tinted and regular mineral sunscreen?

Tinted mineral sunscreens add pigments such as iron oxides to offset the white cast zinc oxide and titanium dioxide can leave. CeraVe says its tint uses iron oxides for that reason.

Can tinted sunscreen replace foundation?

For many people, yes, on a light-coverage day. These give a sheer tint rather than full coverage; CeraVe says its formula can be worn alone or under makeup as a tinted primer.

Which tinted mineral sunscreen is best for sensitive skin?

CeraVe, EltaMD and Eucerin all describe their formulas as suitable for sensitive skin, and all three are fragrance-free according to their listings. Patch test any new product first.

Will tinted sunscreen match my skin tone?

It depends on the shade range. CeraVe and Supergoop offer three tinted shades, Eucerin says it blends into all skin tones, and EltaMD says it suits fair to medium tones.
