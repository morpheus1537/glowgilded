How to choose a hyaluronic acid serum for dry skin

  1. Supporting ingredients: Ceramides, vitamin B5 and glycerin help hold moisture in.
  2. Multiple molecular weights: Serums with several sizes of hyaluronic acid target hydration at different depths.
  3. Fragrance-free: Dry, sensitive skin usually does better without fragrance.
  4. Texture: Lightweight water-based serums layer easily; richer serums feel more cushioning.
  5. Extras: Some add vitamin C or anti-aging claims.
  6. Size: Check the bottle size if you use serum twice a day.

How to use hyaluronic acid serum for dry skin

  • Cleanse gently: Use a non-stripping cleanser. Our cleansing balm guide has gentle options.
  • Apply to damp skin: Pat on serum while skin is still slightly wet.
  • Seal with moisturizer: This locks in the hydration the serum draws in.
  • Use morning and night: Most hyaluronic serums are gentle enough for twice daily use.
  • Add SPF in the morning: Sunscreen is still essential in winter.
  • Add a humidifier: It helps keep moisture in the air, and your skin, overnight.
  • Patch test new products: Especially if your skin is sensitive.

Pairing hyaluronic acid with other actives

Hyaluronic acid layers well under most treatments. If you also use a vitamin C serum for dark spots or a retinol serum, add hyaluronic acid to help offset dryness.

Frequently asked questions

Is hyaluronic acid good for dry skin?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid is a hydration-attracting molecule that helps skin hold water, so it is a popular first step for dry, tight winter skin. Follow it with a moisturizer to lock in hydration.

Should you apply hyaluronic acid to damp or dry skin?

Many brands recommend damp skin. Good Molecules says to apply its serum to damp skin morning and night, then seal with moisturizer.

Can hyaluronic acid serum make dry skin worse?

On its own in very dry air, a serum can feel less effective, which is why brands recommend sealing it with a moisturizer. If you notice irritation, stop using it and patch test new products first.

Can I use hyaluronic acid with retinol or vitamin C?

Yes, hyaluronic acid pairs well with most actives. L'Oreal's Revitalift serum already combines hyaluronic acid with vitamin C. Use a gentle routine and introduce one new active at a time.

How many times a day should I use hyaluronic acid serum?

Most hyaluronic acid serums can be used morning and night. Naturium and Good Molecules both list morning and night use, followed by a moisturizer.
