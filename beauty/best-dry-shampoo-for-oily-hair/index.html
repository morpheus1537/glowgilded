How to choose a dry shampoo for oily hair

  1. Oil absorption: Look for formulas made to target oil and grease at the roots.
  2. Finish: Some add texture and volume; others aim for an invisible finish.
  3. Hair color: Tinted or tone-specific versions help avoid a white cast on darker hair.
  4. Scent: Fragrances range from strong and fresh to light and subtle.
  5. Format: Aerosols spray a fine mist; non-aerosols avoid propellant.
  6. Ingredients: Check for starches, keratin or other ingredients you want or avoid.

Matching dry shampoo to your hair

  • Fine, flat hair: Look for volume-boosting formulas like Amika Perk Up or Batiste Original that add texture.
  • Light or blonde hair: Moroccanoil Light Tones is formulated to help balance brassiness.
  • Dark hair: Choose a dark-tone or invisible formula, such as K18 AirWash with translucent microbeads, or a tinted version from the brand you like.
  • Sweaty or active days: K18's odor-trapping formula is aimed at sweat and odor as well as oil.
  • Sensitive about ingredients: MONDAY lists 0% SLES and 0% benzene and is dermatologically tested.
  • Patch test first: If you have a sensitive scalp, try a small amount before using a new product all over.

Dry shampoo tips for oily hair

  • Apply at night: Spraying before bed gives the formula time to absorb oil while you sleep.
  • Section your hair: Spray at the roots, section by section, where oil builds up.
  • Hold it back: Keep the can a few inches away to avoid concentrated patches.
  • Wait, then massage: Give it a moment to absorb, then massage and brush through.
  • Do not skip washing: Dry shampoo is for between washes; regular washing removes buildup.

Frequently asked questions

Does dry shampoo work on oily hair?

Yes. Dry shampoo absorbs excess oil at the roots so hair looks and feels fresher between washes. Moroccanoil says its formula removes up to 85% of oil from hair.

How do you use dry shampoo correctly?

Section dry hair, spray a few inches from the roots, wait a moment, then massage and brush through. K18 suggests 4 to 6 inches from the scalp, and Amika says to massage it in like shampoo.

How do I avoid a white cast from dry shampoo?

Use a light hand, hold the can at a distance and massage thoroughly. Formulas like K18 AirWash use translucent microbeads, and Moroccanoil makes Light Tones and Dark Tones versions for different hair colors.

Can I use dry shampoo every day?

It is made for use between washes, not as a replacement for washing. Wash regularly to remove buildup from your scalp.

Aerosol or non-aerosol dry shampoo: which is better?

Aerosols spray a fine, quick-drying mist. Non-aerosol sprays like K18 AirWash may feel slightly damp at first but are designed not to disturb your style.
