A simple winter routine for dry skin

  1. Cleanse gently. Use lukewarm water and a non-stripping cleanser.
  2. Moisturize on damp skin. Apply CeraVe or Kiehl's within a minute of washing.
  3. SPF in the morning. Winter sun still counts, especially on snow.
  4. Treat problem spots. Cicaplast on chapped cheeks, FAB on itchy patches.
  5. Seal at night. A thin layer of Aquaphor on hands, heels and lips before bed.

Buying tips

  • Look for ceramides. They help support the skin barrier, which is what winter weakens.
  • Go fragrance-free. Irritated winter skin is more reactive to fragrance.
  • Add humidity. A bedroom humidifier helps your skin overnight; see our best humidifiers for the bedroom.
  • Shorter, cooler showers. Hot water strips natural oils.
  • Patch test. Try a new product on a small area first, and see a dermatologist for persistent eczema or cracking.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best skincare routine for dry skin in winter?

Keep it simple: a gentle cleanser, a ceramide moisturizer applied to slightly damp skin, SPF in the morning, and a richer balm or ointment on the driest areas at night. Cut back on harsh exfoliants until your skin feels comfortable again.

Why does my skin get so dry in winter?

Cold outdoor air, low humidity and indoor heating all pull moisture from the skin, and hot showers strip away natural oils. The result is a weakened moisture barrier that feels tight, flaky or itchy.

Is CeraVe Moisturizing Cream good for the face in winter?

Yes, the listing says it is made for face and body, with three essential ceramides and hyaluronic acid, and it is fragrance-free and non-comedogenic. Very oily or acne-prone skin may prefer a lighter lotion.

Can I use Aquaphor on my face?

Many people use a thin layer as the last step at night, often called slugging, and the listing mentions face mask use. If you are acne-prone, apply it only to dry patches, lips and around the nose instead of the whole face.

What is the difference between Cicaplast Balm B5 and a regular moisturizer?

Cicaplast Balm B5 is a soothing barrier-repair balm with panthenol, shea butter and centella asiatica, designed for chapped, irritated skin and to protect against wind and cold. A regular moisturizer is lighter and meant for all-over daily hydration.
