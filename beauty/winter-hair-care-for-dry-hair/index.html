A simple winter hair care routine for dry hair

  1. Wash less often. Two or three washes a week help preserve natural oils.
  2. Use lukewarm water. Hot water strips oils from hair and scalp.
  3. Mask once a week. Apply the Arvazallia mask after shampoo and leave it on for a few minutes.
  4. Leave in moisture. Spray Redken One United on damp hair to detangle and protect from heat.
  5. Seal with oil. Smooth a few drops of Moroccanoil over mid-lengths and ends.
  6. Treat overnight. Use the Kerastase 8H Magic Night Serum a few nights a week.
  7. Sleep on silk. Swap to the ZIMASILK pillowcase to reduce friction and frizz.

More winter hair tips

  • Limit heat styling. Air dry when you can and always use heat protection.
  • Cover up. Wear a hat in cold wind, ideally lined with silk or satin.
  • Run a humidifier. Adding moisture to indoor air helps hair and skin.
  • Get regular trims. Removing split ends keeps hair looking healthier.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my hair so dry in winter?

Cold outdoor air holds little moisture, and indoor heating dries the air further. Hats, scarves and hot showers add friction and strip oils. The result is hair that feels rough, frizzy and prone to static and breakage.

How do I fix dry hair in winter?

Shampoo less often, use a moisturizing conditioner, add a weekly hair mask, use a leave-in conditioner or oil, protect hair from heat styling and sleep on silk. Lukewarm rather than hot water also helps.

Is hair oil good for dry winter hair?

Yes. A small amount of oil on the mid-lengths and ends helps smooth frizz and add shine. Moroccanoil Treatment is made with argan oil and is designed for medium to thick or curly hair.

How often should I use a hair mask in winter?

Once a week is a good starting point for dry hair. If your hair is very dry or damaged, you can use a mask twice a week, then cut back if it starts to feel heavy.

Does a silk pillowcase really help hair?

Silk has a smooth, low-friction surface. ZIMASILK says its mulberry silk pillowcase helps prevent frizz, split ends and bed head, which many people with curly or dry hair find helpful.
