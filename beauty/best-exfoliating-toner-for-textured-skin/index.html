How to choose an exfoliating toner

  1. AHA for surface texture: Glycolic and lactic acid smooth rough, dull skin.
  2. BHA for pores: Salicylic acid works inside pores to clear buildup and blackheads.
  3. PHA for sensitive skin: Polyhydroxy acids are often described as gentler.
  4. Strength: Higher percentages work faster but can irritate. Start slow.
  5. Formula extras: Look for soothing ingredients and what the formula leaves out, such as alcohol or fragrance.
  6. Routine fit: Plan how it fits with retinol, vitamin C and daily sunscreen.

Tips for using an exfoliating toner

  • Start slow: Use it two or three nights a week before moving to daily.
  • Apply to dry skin: Pat or swipe onto clean, dry skin after cleansing.
  • Do not stack acids: Avoid layering several exfoliants in one routine.
  • Moisturize after: Follow with a moisturizer to support your skin barrier.
  • Wear sunscreen daily: Acids can increase sun sensitivity; try our best tinted mineral sunscreen for face.
  • Back off if irritated: Stinging, redness or peeling means you should reduce use.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best exfoliating toner for textured skin?

For rough, dull texture, a glycolic acid (AHA) toner like The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% is a popular choice. For bumpy texture from clogged pores, a salicylic acid (BHA) exfoliant like Paula's Choice 2% BHA works inside the pore.

Should I use an AHA or BHA toner?

AHAs like glycolic and lactic acid work on the skin's surface to smooth and brighten, so they suit dry or dull texture. BHA (salicylic acid) is oil-soluble and works inside pores, so it suits oily skin, blackheads and clogged pores. Combination formulas like SOME BY MI include both.

How often should I use an exfoliating toner?

Start two or three times a week and increase slowly if your skin tolerates it. The Ordinary recommends once daily, preferably in the evening, and Thayers suggests up to twice daily after cleansing, but sensitive skin may need less.

Do I need sunscreen when using an exfoliating toner?

Yes. Acid exfoliants can make skin more sensitive to the sun, so wear a broad-spectrum sunscreen every day.

Can I use an exfoliating toner with retinol?

Many people use them on alternate nights to avoid irritation. If you are new to both, introduce one at a time and patch test first.
