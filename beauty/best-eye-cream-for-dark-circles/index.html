What causes dark circles?

  1. Pigmentation: Brownish circles from extra melanin; brightening ingredients help most.
  2. Thin skin: Bluish or purple tones from visible blood vessels; hydration and retinol can help over time.
  3. Puffiness: Swelling casts shadows; caffeine and cool temperatures help.
  4. Dryness: Dehydrated skin looks dull and crepey; hyaluronic acid and ceramides help.
  5. Lifestyle: Sleep, allergies and sun all play a part.

Tips for brighter under-eyes

  • Pat, do not rub: The skin around the eyes is thin and delicate.
  • Wear sunscreen: Sun makes pigmentation worse; use a gentle SPF around the eyes.
  • Cool down puffiness: Store your eye cream in the fridge for a cooling effect.
  • Sleep on your back: Elevating your head can reduce morning puffiness.

Frequently asked questions

Do eye creams really help dark circles?

They can help the look of some dark circles. Brightening ingredients like vitamin C and Thiamidol target pigmentation, caffeine helps with puffiness that casts shadows, and hydrators plump thin skin. Results build over weeks, and circles caused by genetics or bone structure may not change much.

What ingredients are best for dark circles under the eyes?

Look for vitamin C or Thiamidol for brightening, caffeine for puffiness, hyaluronic acid and ceramides for hydration, and retinol for thin, crepey skin. CeraVe's Vitamin C Eye Cream combines 5% vitamin C, caffeine, ceramides and hyaluronic acid.

How do you apply eye cream correctly?

Use a pea-sized amount for both eyes. Dab it with your ring finger along the orbital bone and gently pat, without rubbing or tugging. Apply morning and night unless the product says otherwise, and use retinol eye creams as directed.

Can I use retinol eye cream every day?

RoC says its Retinol Correxion Eye Cream is gentle enough for daily use. If you are new to retinol, start a few nights a week and build up, and wear sunscreen during the day.

Are these eye creams safe for contact lens wearers?

CeraVe says its Vitamin C Eye Cream is ophthalmologist tested, allergy tested and suitable for contact lens wearers. CeraVe Eye Repair and Eucerin Radiant Tone are also listed as ophthalmologist tested.
