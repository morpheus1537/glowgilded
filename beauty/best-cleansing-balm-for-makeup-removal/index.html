How to choose a cleansing balm

  1. Skin type: Dry and sensitive skin usually does best with fragrance-free, ceramide-rich balms. Oily and acne-prone skin may prefer a non-comedogenic formula that rinses clean.
  2. Fragrance: Some balms are scented with citrus or essential oils. Skip them if fragrance tends to irritate your skin.
  3. Makeup load: If you wear waterproof mascara or long-wear foundation, look for a balm listed as removing waterproof makeup.
  4. Rinse-off feel: Balms that emulsify into a milk when you add water usually leave less greasy residue.
  5. Size: Small jars are good for travel and trying a new formula. Larger jars last longer for daily use.

How to double cleanse with a balm

  • Start dry: Apply the balm to dry skin with dry hands. Water too early stops it from melting makeup.
  • Massage for a minute: Work it over your whole face, including the lash line with eyes closed.
  • Emulsify: Add a little lukewarm water and keep massaging until it turns milky.
  • Rinse well: Rinse thoroughly or wipe gently with a damp, soft cloth.
  • Second cleanse if you like: Follow with a gentle water-based cleanser, especially if you wore heavy sunscreen.
  • Keep the jar clean: Use the spatula instead of wet fingers so water does not get into the jar.

Tips for winter cleansing

  • Use lukewarm water: Hot water can leave winter skin feeling tight.

Frequently asked questions

Is a cleansing balm better than micellar water for removing makeup?

For long-wear, waterproof makeup and sunscreen, a cleansing balm usually works faster because its oils dissolve makeup as you massage it in. Micellar water is handy for light makeup or quick touch-ups, but it often takes more rubbing with a cotton pad.

Do I need to wash my face after using a cleansing balm?

Many people follow a balm with a gentle water-based cleanser, which is called double cleansing. Some balms, like HEIMISH All Clean Balm, are described as all-in-one cleansers, while e.l.f. suggests a second cleanse for an extra clean feel.

Can cleansing balms clog pores?

It depends on the formula and your skin. CeraVe lists its balm as non-comedogenic, and APRILSKIN lists its Carrotene balm as non-comedogenic. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water so no oily film is left behind.

How do you use a cleansing balm?

Scoop a small amount with the spatula, warm it between dry fingers and massage it over dry skin and makeup for about a minute. Add a splash of water so it turns milky, then rinse well with lukewarm water.

Is cleansing balm safe for the eye area?

CeraVe says its balm is effective on waterproof mascara, and HEIMISH describes its balm as safe for the delicate eye area with no stinging. Keep your eyes closed while massaging and rinse well.
