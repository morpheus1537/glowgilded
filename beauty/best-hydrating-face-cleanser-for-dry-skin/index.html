How to choose a cleanser for dry skin

  1. Texture: Cream, lotion and milky cleansers are gentlest. Cream-to-foam formulas suit people who like some lather.
  2. Hydrating ingredients: Ceramides, glycerin, hyaluronic acid, panthenol and niacinamide help skin feel comfortable.
  3. Fragrance-free: Fragrance can irritate dry, sensitive skin.
  4. Makeup and sunscreen: If you wear long-wear makeup, choose a cleanser that says it removes makeup or use an oil first.
  5. Your routine: A gentle cleanser at night may be enough; a water rinse in the morning works for many dry-skin types.

Cleansing tips for dry skin

  • Use lukewarm water: Hot water can leave dry skin feeling tighter.
  • Be gentle: Massage for 30 to 60 seconds with your fingertips, then pat dry instead of rubbing.
  • Moisturize fast: Apply serum and moisturizer while skin is still slightly damp. Our best hyaluronic acid serum for dry skin guide has good options.
  • Skip over-cleansing: Washing more than twice a day can make dryness worse.
  • Double cleanse on makeup days: Use an oil or balm first, then a gentle cream cleanser.
  • Watch for irritation: Stop using any product that stings or burns.

Cream, milky or oil cleanser: which texture suits you?

Cream and lotion cleansers like CeraVe Hydrating and La Roche-Posay Toleriane Hydrating are the classic choice for dry skin. They feel soft, rinse clean without foam and leave little tightness behind. Milky cleansers like Thayers are lighter and feel fresh, which some people prefer in the morning.

Cream-to-foam cleansers like Cetaphil Cream to Foam give a light lather while staying gentle, a good middle ground if you dislike the feel of non-foaming washes. Oil-to-foam cleansers like Cetaphil Nourishing Oil to Foam start as an oil that melts makeup and sunscreen, then rinse away as a foam, so they can replace a separate makeup remover on many days. Many people with dry skin keep two: an oil or balm for makeup nights and a cream cleanser for everyday use.

Frequently asked questions

What type of cleanser is best for dry skin?

Cream, lotion or milky cleansers are usually best for dry skin because they clean without stripping natural oils. Look for hydrating ingredients like ceramides, glycerin and hyaluronic acid and skip harsh sulfates and fragrance if you are sensitive.

Should people with dry skin use a foaming cleanser?

Harsh foaming cleansers can leave dry skin tight, but gentle cream-to-foam formulas like Cetaphil Cream to Foam are made for normal to dry, sensitive skin. If your face feels tight after washing, switch to a non-foaming cream cleanser.

How often should I wash my face if I have dry skin?

Many people with dry skin cleanse at night to remove sunscreen and makeup and simply rinse with water in the morning. Others use a gentle cleanser twice a day. Follow with moisturizer while skin is still slightly damp.

Is CeraVe or Cetaphil better for dry skin?

Both make well-reviewed cleansers for dry skin. CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser is a non-foaming lotion with ceramides and hyaluronic acid, while Cetaphil Cream to Foam turns into a light foam with glycerin, panthenol and niacinamide. Choose based on whether you prefer foam.

Can a cleanser remove makeup on dry skin?

Yes. CeraVe and La Roche-Posay say their cleansers remove makeup, and Cetaphil Oil to Foam is designed to melt away makeup. For waterproof makeup, a dedicated cleansing balm or oil first may help.
