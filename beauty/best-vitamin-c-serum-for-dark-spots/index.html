How to choose a vitamin C serum

  1. Form of vitamin C: L-ascorbic acid is the classic, pure form. Derivatives like SAP are often gentler.
  2. Strength: Around 10% is a common starting point. 20% formulas are more intense and better for experienced users.
  3. Supporting ingredients: Hyaluronic acid adds hydration, ceramides support the barrier, and vitamin E and ferulic acid are classic antioxidant partners.
  4. Packaging: Vitamin C can oxidize. Opaque or protective packaging helps.
  5. Sunscreen: Any brightening routine works best with daily SPF.

Tips for getting results from vitamin C

  • Wear sunscreen every day: Sun exposure darkens spots, so SPF is the most important step in any brightening routine.
  • Start slowly: Use your serum every other morning for the first week, then daily if your skin is happy.
  • Apply to clean, dry skin: Follow with moisturizer, then sunscreen.
  • Store it well: Keep the cap closed and store away from heat and sunlight.
  • Be patient: Expect to use a serum consistently for several weeks before judging it.

Frequently asked questions

Does vitamin C serum help with dark spots?

Many vitamin C serums are formulated to brighten skin and even tone, and several in this guide, such as TruSkin, say they help fade the look of dark spots. Results take consistent daily use over weeks, and daily sunscreen is essential.

What percentage of vitamin C is best for beginners?

Many people start around 10% or with a gentler vitamin C derivative. CeraVe uses 10% L-ascorbic acid, and TruSkin uses SAP, a gentler form it says is designed to be tolerated daily. Stronger 20% formulas like Timeless suit experienced users.

Should I use vitamin C serum in the morning or at night?

Many people apply vitamin C in the morning under moisturizer and sunscreen, because its antioxidant benefits pair well with daytime protection. TruSkin directs you to follow with moisturizer and SPF.

Can I use vitamin C with retinol?

Yes, many people do, often by using vitamin C in the morning and retinol at night to reduce the chance of irritation. Introduce one new active at a time and patch test first.

Is it bad if my vitamin C serum turns yellow?

Some vitamin C serums darken as they oxidize. La Roche-Posay says its 12% serum remains effective even if it turns yellow on exposure to air. Store any vitamin C serum tightly closed, away from heat and light.
