A simple winter lip care routine

  1. Exfoliate gently: once or twice a week, use a soft scrub on dry, flaky lips (not cracked ones).
  2. Moisturize daily: apply a balm in the morning and throughout the day.
  3. Protect in the sun: use SPF on sunny days, especially near snow.
  4. Heal overnight: apply a thick ointment before bed.
  5. Add shine for events: a lip oil adds gloss without drying.

Buying tips

  • Pick fragrance-free for sensitive lips: scents and flavors can irritate dry skin.
  • Keep one balm everywhere: bag, desk and nightstand.
  • Use a humidifier: dry indoor air makes chapped lips worse.
  • Stop licking your lips: saliva evaporates and leaves them drier.

Frequently asked questions

How do I heal chapped lips fast in winter?

Gently remove flakes, then apply a thick, fragrance-free ointment like Aquaphor Lip Repair and reapply often, especially before bed. Drink water, use a humidifier and avoid licking your lips.

Should you exfoliate chapped lips?

Only gently, and not when lips are cracked or bleeding. A soft scrub like Burt's Bees Conditioning Lip Scrub once or twice a week buffs away dry flakes. Follow it with a balm.

Do lips need SPF in winter?

Yes. Lips can burn in winter too, especially with sun reflecting off snow. Fresh Sugar Lip Treatment includes SPF 15 for daytime protection.

Is Summer Fridays Lip Butter Balm good for dry lips?

Summer Fridays says the balm deeply conditions and softens dry lips with shea butter and murumuru seed butter, and gives a sheer touch of color. For cracked lips, a thicker ointment like Aquaphor may work better.

Is Dior Lip Glow Oil moisturizing?

Dior describes it as combining the shine of a gloss with the comfort of lip care, with a non-sticky, non-greasy texture that forms a protective film against drying.
