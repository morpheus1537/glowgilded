The golden rule: thinnest to thickest

Apply products from the most watery to the richest. Light liquids absorb first; creams and balms seal everything in. In the morning, sunscreen is always the final skincare step.

Your routine at a glance

Morning: rinse or gentle cleanse, Marine Cream, sunscreen.

Evening: Cleansing Balm, BHA exfoliant (2-3 nights a week), Night Cream, Lip Sleeping Mask.

Give any new routine at least four weeks before judging results, and introduce one new product at a time so you know what is working.

Buying tips

  • Start with the cleanser and moisturizer. Add the exfoliant once your skin is happy.
  • Patch test new products on a small area for a few days.
  • Buy from official listings or items shipped and sold by Amazon to avoid fakes.

Why fewer, better products work

Long routines are hard to keep up, and layering many products increases the chance that something irritates your skin. A short routine of five well-made products is easier to follow every day, which matters far more than any single ingredient. Consistency is what most people see results from.

Each product in this routine has a clear job. The balm removes the day. The BHA exfoliant addresses texture a few nights a week. The night cream seals in moisture while you sleep, the lip mask takes care of the one area most routines forget, and the Marine Cream gives you a light morning base under sunscreen.

Adjusting the routine for your skin type

  • Oily or acne-prone: use the Marine Cream at night too, instead of the heavier Night Cream, and keep the BHA exfoliant as your main treatment.
  • Dry: use the Night Cream morning and night in winter, and exfoliate only once or twice a week.
  • Sensitive: start with only the cleanser and a moisturizer for two weeks, then introduce the exfoliant once a week.
  • Combination: follow the routine as written and apply the richer cream only to drier areas.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Exfoliating too often. More is not better; irritation can make texture look worse.
  • Skipping sunscreen. Especially important when using an exfoliating acid.
  • Changing everything at once. If your skin reacts, you will not know which product caused it.
  • Using too much product. A pea-sized amount of cream is usually enough for the face.

Frequently asked questions

What order should skincare go in?

Thinnest to thickest: cleanser, liquid treatments like a BHA exfoliant, then moisturizer. Sunscreen goes last in the morning.

How often should I use a BHA exfoliant?

Start with two or three evenings a week. Increase slowly only if your skin tolerates it, and stop if you notice irritation.

Do I need a separate day and night cream?

Not necessarily, but a lighter cream in the morning and a richer one at night works well for many people, especially in winter.

Can I use this routine with sensitive skin?

Introduce one product at a time and patch test. The BHA exfoliant and scented products are the most likely to bother sensitive skin; ask a dermatologist if unsure.
