A luxury routine does not need twelve steps. It needs a handful of products that each do one job well, used in the right order. Below is a simple morning and evening routine built on five widely loved products, plus the reasoning behind each step, so you can swap in what you already own.

The golden rule: thinnest to thickest

Apply products from the most watery to the richest. Light liquids absorb first; creams and balms seal everything in. In the morning, sunscreen is always the final skincare step.

How long until you see a difference?

Hydration you can feel immediately. Texture and glow usually take four to six weeks of consistent use, because that's roughly how long skin takes to renew its surface. Consistency matters more than any single product, so pick a routine you'll genuinely enjoy doing.
